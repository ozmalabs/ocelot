# Adversarial review: relational provenance foundation

## Scope and result

Reviewed the new foundational definition, status remark, evidence-relative recovery proposition, operator-supply connection, generated Statement, and inheritance in the mind and decision papers. Also checked the nearby evolution, woodlouse, autonomous-processing, and language-model qualifications. Manuscripts were read only; no builds or software tests were run by this reviewer.

The central principle survives review: actual causal relations occur independently of attribution; a frame recovers a provenance distinction through evidence and a model. The identifiability proposition is correct within its declared family. The attribution is not required to be conscious. The contribution is appropriately identified as an established concept given a relational treatment, rather than a newly discovered concept.

Two substantive corrections and one clarification are warranted before treating the changes as settled.

## Findings

### 1. High: the mind application overstates the human mechanism and excludes explicit records

**Location:** `01b_graphic_equalisation_mind_and_self_grounding.tex`, `rem:detection`, especially the statements that reality judgment is a subjective-strength threshold, signal strength is the proxy human brains use, and a sorting frame holds no provenance label. Also inspect the corresponding operational prediction around line 1530.

**Problem:** Dijkstra and Fleming's visual imagery experiment supports a strength-threshold account in the studied paradigm. It does not establish that every human source judgment uses that cue or that all source-monitoring systems lack explicit labels. The newly cited Johnson et al. framework instead recognises perceptual, contextual, semantic, affective, and cognitive-operation information. The universal label exclusion also conflicts with the foundational remark's explicit allowance for provenance records. A label can be present and useful while requiring an evidential relation to its source.

**Fix:** Say that the studied perceptual reality-monitoring task supports a strength-based mechanism. Describe source monitoring generally as attribution using available cues, records, and processes. Permit explicit grounding tags; their interpretation and reliability are model-dependent. GE predicts evidence dependence, not this particular neural proxy or a universal threshold. Restrict matching-strength misclassification to the conditions where strength dominates the judgment. Keep first-order misclassification distinct from metacognitive awareness of possible error.

**Primary sources checked:** [Johnson et al., original author-hosted article](https://onlineacademiccommunity.uvic.ca/lindsaylab/wp-content/uploads/sites/4861/2020/07/JohnsonHashtroudiLindsay1993_0.pdf); [Dijkstra and Fleming, original article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-37322-1).

### 2. High, inherited tension exposed by the new link: general evidence dependence does not prove unconditional operator nonclosure

**Location:** Core `prop:leak`, `prop:runner-opaque`, following runner discussion, and falsifiability item “Provenance is never closed by inference.” The new foundation links operator supply as an application; the Statement likewise contrasts local attribution with operator provenance closure.

**Problem:** The foundational theorem carefully allows a singleton compatible provenance set under a declared model. Later, operator discussion says “partial always, complete never” and that two operators supplied by one or different providers are indistinguishable without explicit evidence-equivalence assumptions. Non-self-transparency of the presently running process does not, by itself, show that a finite declared provider map cannot be identified. Nor does dependence on primitive operations invalidate every inference made with those operations. This stronger claim is not a consequence of the new identifiability theorem.

**Fix:** Distinguish (a) identification of a declared local provider map, which may be possible, from (b) unconditional certification that no further provider or alternative realisation exists, which the declared model alone cannot supply. For a precise non-identifiability claim require two admissible supply histories with identical available evidence and different requested provider provenance. Treat nonclosure of an unrestricted total supply chain as the separately argued foundational commitment, not a new formal consequence. Reflection can expose local provider structure without creating a self-authenticating guarantee of exhaustive supply history. Qualify the operational falsifier to target that unrestricted claim, not a finite local attribution.

This correction preserves the author's principle and the existing argument about epistemic frontiers while preventing the new conditional theorem from being used as an unconditional impossibility proof.

### 3. Medium: make the derivational dependency explicit rather than leaving “derivation” to a slogan

**Location:** Core `def:provenance`, `rem:provenance-status`; decision `rem:decision-provenance`; derivation record.

**Problem:** The no-view-from-nowhere axiom establishes that operational characterization occurs in a frame. On its own it does not entail that every object lacks an intrinsic historical property; that conclusion also uses GE's relational ontology and the stipulated distinction between actual causal relation and attribution. The set-valued identifiability argument derives an epistemic consequence after those definitions, not the entirety of the ontology from the axiom alone.

**Fix:** Add a short chain: relational ontology supplies the account of origin as a relation; relative grounding places its characterization in a frame; the declared evidence map then yields the recovery/non-recovery result. State that the backing is conditional on those foundational commitments. Preserve the existing statement that neither the concept nor the standard identifiability argument is new. This makes the contribution more defensible without weakening it to merely a metaphor.

### 4. Low: “no intrinsic certificate” should mean no self-authentication

**Location:** Decision `rem:decision-provenance` and any abbreviated summaries.

**Problem:** A response can contain an explicit source field, signature, audit record, or process annotation. The current following paragraph allows records, but the categorical opening can sound like a prohibition on carrying them.

**Fix:** Prefer “no self-authenticating certificate independent of the relations supporting it.” Reliable certificates constrain local attribution under stated recording, calibration, and trust assumptions. Their existence is compatible with the principle.

## Findings that do not warrant changes

- `prop:provenance-inference` correctly separates singleton identification from completeness of the candidate history family. Its empty-set case and same-evidence argument are sound. Statistical uncertainty would need an extended observation model, but the exact model is a legitimate limited specification.
- The three prior-work descriptions are appropriately differentiated. Shpitser and Pearl concern identification of causal queries under graphical models, not direct universal recovery of individual event origins. Buneman et al. concern contributing input data and source locations for query outputs, not the whole metaphysical causal history. The manuscript currently marks these scopes; retain that language. [Shpitser and Pearl's original article record](https://jmlr.org/papers/v9/shpitser08a.html) was checked directly. The Buneman author-hosted PDF timed out during this pass; no additional source-content assertion is made from that failed retrieval.
- The definition does not identify inference with deliberation. Johnson et al.'s automatic and deliberate source judgments support this distinction.
- The decision paper distinguishes prior thought from operative causal influence and allows redundant sufficient causes. It does not infer deliberation merely from successful action, a verbal rationale, evolution, or a woodlouse turn.
- The woodlouse discussion explicitly leaves neural control possible and does not assert a demonstrated minimum-energy mechanism. The LLM discussion distinguishes absence of a required explicit explanation from absence of all internal models. These qualifications should remain.
- Leaving `00_is.tex` unchanged is appropriate: its structural floor deliberately does not supply causal dynamics or epistemic machinery. Actual histories are not made dependent on a measuring observer by the new principle.
- The generated Statement includes the same qualified definition and recovery proposition as the core. No semantic discrepancy was found. Any core corrections should be propagated through `01s.spec` generation, rather than editing the generated manuscript independently.

## Recommended disposition

Retain the foundation and prior-work framing. Correct the mind mechanism overclaim, qualify operator closure, and make the derivational dependency explicit. Those corrections address the substantive vulnerabilities while keeping **provenance is inferred, not inherent** as the common relational principle.
