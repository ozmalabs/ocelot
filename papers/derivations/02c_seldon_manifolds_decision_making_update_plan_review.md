# Adversarial review of the decision making update plan

Reviewed: `02c_seldon_manifolds_decision_making_update_plan.md`, current Paper 02c, Paper 02's cost-field construction, and Paper 03's Narrative Momentum cost hypothesis.

Verdict: **Implement after the corrections below.** The plan preserves the author's intended identity claim and contains appropriate checks against an unsupported universal optimisation theorem. Its remaining problems concern the scope of that identity, the causal status of the new notation, and the proposed cognitive mapping. These can be corrected without returning to an independent chooser that merely favours a trajectory.

## 1. Major: distinguish the identity from its domain

“A realised decision is a continuation” does not entail “every continuation is a decision.” The plan's proposed thesis can otherwise classify a falling rock, cellular maintenance, and hypothetical calculation as decisions merely because they undergo transitions. Conversely, if a represented intention is called a decision, it can occur before the action that fails to implement it. The manuscript must avoid switching silently between these uses.

**Fix:** define a realised decision at a declared action scale as the action-bearing transition or history of an embedded agent. Represented selection, intention, deliberation, and execution are distinguishable processes within its causal organisation. A realised decision has no further chooser outside that organisation. The scope of what counts as an agent or action must be declared in an instance rather than inferred solely from change. State the identity as an ontological interpretation; it does not, by itself, predict which transition occurs.

This preserves the user's “they are it” claim. It does not require consciousness, a separate observer assigning value, or a uniquely biological implementation.

## 2. Major: the proposed Type 1/Type 2 equation is not literally coherent

System 2 is also present operative organisation continuing. Therefore equating System 1 with *all* operative continuation leaves no distinct referent for System 2. Autonomous processes can also update learned structure and change behaviour; hypothetical thinking can fail to change behaviour. “Continuation versus revision” is not an exhaustive empirical partition into two mechanisms.

**Fix:** preserve “System 1 simply is” as shorthand for the installed organisation producing autonomous action without a separate act of conscious choosing. Treat hypothetical modelling as an embedded operation that can alter subsequent continuation. Use Type 1/Type 2 in research attribution, and explicitly mark this as an interpretive mapping, not a literal identification of every manifold revision with Type 2. Avoid implying that Type 1 cannot learn or revise, or that Type 2 sits outside current dynamics.

The plan correctly notes overlapping modes, cost, failure, and the absence of a compulsory sequence. These qualifications should appear near the mapping itself, not only in the final limits.

## 3. Major: actual continuation and minimisation need separate specifications

An arbitrary deterministic transition can always be represented as cheapest by assigning its selected successor cost zero and other successors cost one. That proves representability, not an explanatory cost law. The plan recognises this danger but its notation still allows a reader to mistake the supplied minimum rule for the derivation.

**Fix:** separate:

1. The actual transition description, including the declared boundary and any unresolved influences.
2. The independently grounded candidate domain and edge costs.
3. The cheapest-successor hypothesis or implemented finite policy.

The identity locates the decision within item 1. Items 2 and 3 supply the additional explanatory claim. A finite construction may implement item 3 exactly without validating it for human agents. Do not call its correctness a proof that humans universally minimise energy.

In each numerical example, give the action domain, costs, tie rule, and revision transition before reporting the realised action. A missing cost is undefined; it must not silently become infinity or zero. State the policy's domain: nonempty successor set with usable costs and an adequate comparison rule. A terminal state should terminate, or receive an explicitly declared self-loop; an empty argmin has no successor.

## 4. Major: keep causal sufficiency conditional and visible

The schematic equation `X_(t+1) = T(X_t, B_t)` is valid as a deterministic description only when `X_t` and `B_t` contain everything the declared instance requires. Apparent unpredictability does not prove a separate chooser; neither does an unobserved complete state entitle the observer to a unique predicted action.

**Fix:** identify the deterministic equation as a supplied finite grounding. Where the chosen state description is not sufficient, use an explicitly declared transition relation or probabilistic kernel rather than pretending to know a unique successor. Stochastic selection is compatible with the identity claim: the realised transition is still the system's continuation. It is not compatible with a claim that every realised successor must be a strict unique minimum unless the stochastic rule is restricted to minima and stated that way.

Keep actual operative organisation distinct from its measured description and the agent's own representation. The latter can influence continuation without being complete, and external causes can change actual continuation without entering representation. The observer's inability to recover the mechanism is an epistemic limit, not a missing causal agent.

## 5. Major: specify what a changed manifold means

If a manifold means only a reachable set, weights can change action while the set remains identical. If every state change is counted as a manifold change, “a decision changes only by changing the manifold” becomes true by bookkeeping and supplies no distinct prediction.

**Fix:** explicitly define an enriched operative description containing reachability, admissibility, fields, standing structure, capacity, and transition rules. Use it consistently when stating the author's manifold claim. A changed action under identical independently specified operative state, boundary, and deterministic rule would refute the declared instance or demonstrate insufficient state description. It must not be rescued by inventing an unrestricted hidden field after the event.

Do not describe an unchanged menu with changed valuation as a changed *bare reachable set*. It is a changed field over that set. The plan's reappraisal example is especially useful for establishing this distinction.

## 6. Important: prospective deliberation is not a claim of temporal separation

One can make a present commitment about the next instant, revise one's interpretation of the past, or deliberate during an ongoing action. “Only future decisions” should not imply that current commitments or concurrent control are impossible.

**Fix:** define the object of prospective deliberation as a not-yet-realised continuation at the declared causal resolution. The process of deliberating is present and itself realised. Reviewing the past concerns its present model and future consequences, rather than altering its realised history. Do not assert that every agent explicitly represents a future before every action.

## 7. Important: preserve the series' conditional cost results

Paper 02 distinguishes local edge cost from accumulated cost-to-reach and supplies basin definitions only after a successor policy is declared. Paper 03 requires an independently measured baseline and alteration charge, plus equal baseline costs for its cheapest-reuse comparison. Neither establishes a universal strict state-potential descent.

**Fix:** retain these conditions next to the relevant formulas. Provide a numerical local/global counterexample: a first edge of cost 1 followed by cost 10 can be locally preferred over a first edge of cost 2 followed by cost 0, despite total costs 11 versus 2. The admissible paths and horizon must be stated. The policy is locally minimum, not globally cheapest.

Likewise, a cycle under a cheapest-successor rule cannot be described as strict descent of a single state potential. Ties, cycles, finite horizon, and capacity constraints should be recognised rather than erased. Do not interchange physical expenditure, preference score, potential, cost-to-go, and Narrativium.

## 8. Important: revise inherited claims everywhere they occur

The plan properly targets the draft notice, abstract, scope, operational commitments, and limits. Two additional inherited statements need direct correction:

- The decision-domain definition presently restricts evaluation to represented landscape. Preserve it for represented evaluation, not for actual consequences or actual dynamics.
- The target section states that standing Narrative remains selected regardless of visible alternatives when there is no surprise or meta-analysis. Preserve this only as the declared grammar's retention rule, under unchanged operative conditions. External intervention, capacity loss, or automatic adaptation can otherwise change continuation.

The surprise/meta implication is a reopening taxonomy, not a proved complete taxonomy of every cause of action change. Avoid conflating surprise with Type 2: a represented mismatch can have an already installed autonomous response.

## 9. Empirical interpretation and contribution

The new ontology is not validated by the existence of dual-process research. The finite minimum policy is not validated by defaults, nor does the antecedent-state constraint solve transformative choice. A clear separation among interpretation, implemented instance, and independently tested human hypothesis is necessary throughout.

**Fix:** state one concrete discriminating research proposal. Hold action availability fixed, predeclare a measurement or experimental manipulation of a comparison field or revision cost, and predict a change in action under a specified model. Compare against an appropriate fixed-frame baseline on observations not used to assign the costs. State what would count against the declared grounding. No claim of universal recoverability or exact neural implementation is required.

The warranted contribution is a coherent treatment of decision as embedded continuation, deliberation as paid restructuring, and represented futures as causally effective but incomplete. Priority over all planning, metareasoning, dynamical cognition, or dual-process accounts would require a separate literature argument.

### Warranted remaining corrections

1. **Residual identity contradiction in the quality section:** “Implementation may fail while the decision remains unchanged” uses the old represented-selection sense of decision after the new definition identifies a realised decision with the actual action-bearing history. Replace with “Implementation may fail while the represented selection or commitment remains unchanged.” Keep the following distinction between carrying out the selected action and its undesirable consequences. This is a material consistency correction.

2. **Non-occurrence proof qualification:** The definition requires aperture adequate to distinguish non-occurrence from an undisclosed or unobserved occurrence, while the proof lists “an observation failure not excluded by aperture” as a surviving cause. Clarify that false-negative or otherwise unreliable observation belongs to a weaker evidence model and does not warrant categorical elimination. The categorical case can retain changed boundary, intervention, coupling, or recurrence as alternatives. The manuscript's new probabilistic-expectation qualification is otherwise sound.

3. **Finite example accounting clarity:** In the horizon example, explicitly declare that the path costs and the one-unit planning charge share an additive accounting convention for that example. The earlier general examples separate effective score from physical expenditure; the reader should not infer that adding computation expenditure to an arbitrary preference score is universally valid. The existing phrase “same accounting currency” substantially addresses this, but naming the convention would remove the ambiguity.

The study is correctly described as a proposed discrimination among grounded instances, not unique validation of the ontology.
