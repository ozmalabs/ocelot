## 2. The common relational object

### 2.1 Definition: relational realization situation

For a frame-indexed system, define the **relational realization situation**

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak R_t
 = (W, G_t, \Gamma_t, \mathcal P_t, \mathcal H_t, \mathcal C_t, \mathcal O_t)
}
\]

with the following typed components:

- \(W\): an individuating frame/boundary relation, in the sense of 01; it is not presumed to be a coordinate chart, physical observer, Hilbert factor, or gauge-invariant subsystem.
- \(G_t=(g_t,r_t,\Gamma_t)\): the present graph, transformation structure, and admissibility grammar of 01.
- \(\mathcal P_t:=\operatorname{Poss}(G_t)\): the family of immediate admissible continuations under the current antecedent and grammar.
- \(\mathcal H_t\): the realised history available to \(W\), when it is represented. It supports the 03 distinction between realised history and presently operative history-bearing structure; it is not automatically a Hilbert space.
- \(\mathcal C_t\): any cost/change bookkeeping associated with an admissible continuation. At corpus level this is a typed cost relation, not an energy, action, entropy, or stress tensor.
- \(\mathcal O_t\): the record/readout relations held by the relevant frame. At corpus level these are frame-relative representations; they are not yet physical gauge-invariant observables.

A realization is the local relation

\[
\operatorname{Coll}^{W}_t:\mathcal P_t\rightsquigarrow G_{t+1},
\]

where the superscript makes the frame index explicit. This is a direct typed restatement of 01’s frame-relative collapse rather than a new physical dynamics.

### 2.3 Internal consequence: realization is relative, not a global update

**Proposition P1 (typed relativity of realization).** Given 01’s frame definition and collapse definition, `Coll` is a relation indexed to the frame that holds the relevant interaction/record. There is no corpus-level frame-independent global realization time.

**Derivation.** In 01, a frame is an instantiated boundary relation and collapse is a local realization/write, not a universal tick. The domain \(\mathcal P_t\), the grammar \(\Gamma_t\), and the successor represented by \(G_{t+1}\) are all defined relative to an antecedent graph held through a frame. Erasing \(W\) would erase the relation that supplies the relevant distinction, contradicting the no-view-from-nowhere axiom. ∎

**Limit.** P1 is not a covariant quantum measurement theorem. It does not prove no-signalling, consistency between observers, decoherence, complete positivity, gauge invariance, or compatibility with a relativistic causal structure.

## 4. What follows from existing premises

### 4.1 Possibility is not amplitude

**Proposition P2 (non-derivation of amplitudes).** From \(G_t\), \(\Gamma_t\), and \(\mathcal P_t=\operatorname{Poss}(G_t)\) alone, no complex amplitude representation follows.

**Derivation.** The data specify membership/admissibility of alternatives. They do not specify a scalar field, an additive operation on alternatives, a phase group, an inner product, normalization, composition, or dynamics. Many inequivalent probability theories can be defined over the same set of alternatives. Therefore no map \(A_{W,t}\) is fixed. ∎

**New-hypothesis label H-QA.** To use amplitudes, add a physical representation \(A_{W,t}\) with a stated operational state space and composition law. If it is complex Hilbert space, that is an admitted contingent rule-content choice in the present corpus, not an output of P2.

**Falsifier.** H-QA fails for a proposed realization if its stated relational update rules cannot be represented in the claimed amplitude structure while preserving its declared compositions and measurable probabilities, or if a non-equivalent probability theory satisfies all proposed premises.

### 4.2 Frame-relative realization does not recover Born weights

**Proposition P3 (conditional Born boundary).** If a proposed realization supplies (a) complex Hilbert state structure, (b) a noncontextual probability measure over the applicable measurement structure, and (c) the additional dimensional and composition assumptions of a relevant reconstruction/Gleason-type result, then Born weights may follow under that result’s stated scope. The corpus does not establish premises (a)–(c) in full.

**Derivation.** 01d maps free continuation, phase, no privilege, and superframe structure to candidate operational axioms. It explicitly rates the rigorous inference from uncoupled continuation to linearity/norm preservation and the composition seam as unresolved. 01a likewise states that phase averaging selects the square among natural candidates but relies on Gleason for uniqueness after vector-space and context-independence premises. Thus the implication is conditional on additional premises, rather than a derivation from \(\mathfrak R_t\). ∎

**Breakpoint B1 — Born circularity.** Quantum-gravity tools such as holography, QEC, information geometry, and GME protocols presuppose standard quantum states and Born probabilities. They cannot close H-QA or P3 without circularity.

**Falsifier.** A candidate Born bridge fails if it uses Hilbert linearity, the trace rule, or standard quantum composition in an intermediate step while claiming those same facts as conclusions; or if a competing non-Born measure satisfies its published premises.

### 4.3 Change-cost does not become a gravitational source

**Proposition P4 (non-identification of load).** The 03 cost form and 01a change-load language do not define a physical energy density, momentum flux, stress tensor, modular energy, entropy, or source for metric curvature.

**Derivation.** 03 explicitly allows the currency, payer, and location of cost to vary by grounding; its mechanical impulse correspondence is a constrained grounding, not a universal unit assignment. 01g requires a comparison class and an order/units structure even to define cross-frame cost. 01a calls \(G\) a proposed change-load–geometry coupling but supplies no map to a tensor, conservation equation, or field equation. Therefore neither dimensional type nor coupling dynamics is fixed. ∎

**New-hypothesis label H-CL.** A candidate physical realization must define a dimensioned, local-or-explicitly-nonlocal source \(T_{ab}^{(\mathrm{eff})}\), its operational measurement, and all exchange terms. It must state whether its load is matter energy, modular energy, entropy production, a new variable, or a functional of a quantum state.

**Dimensional/units breakpoint B2.** A generic alteration cost \(C_{\mathrm{alt}}\) cannot be inserted into Einstein’s equation. The equation requires a source of stress-energy dimensions (or a declared replacement) and a coupling with correct dimensions. Setting dimensional constants to one does not remove the need to specify which dimensioned observable is being mapped.

**Falsifier.** H-CL fails if no source can satisfy the required conservation/Bianchi compatibility in the claimed covariant regime, or if its predicted coupling is nonuniversal in a way excluded by equivalence-principle and local-Lorentz tests.

### 4.4 Cycle ordering does not produce physical proper time

**Proposition P5 (non-derivation of dilation).** The existence of local realization order or closure-cycle counting does not entail either the special-relativistic Lorentz factor or gravitational redshift.

**Derivation.** An order can be embedded in many inequivalent clock functions. 01a’s “budget spent on motion and change-load” provides an interpretive shape for both effects, but it gives neither a metric \(g_{ab}\), worldline functional, transformation group, source equation, nor a derivation of the exact tested factors. Without \(\Theta_W\), \(\Phi_\epsilon\), and dynamics, a local cycle rate is not comparable to the proper time measured by physical clocks. ∎

**New-hypothesis label H-CT.** Define a clock observable and prove that in an appropriate regime it obeys both

\[
\frac{d\tau}{dt}=\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\frac{d\tau_1}{d\tau_2}=\sqrt{\frac{-g_{00}(x_1)}{-g_{00}(x_2)}}
\]

(or state and test a different measured law). The first relation is a target special-relativistic limit; the second is a stationary-metric redshift target, not an existing corpus result.

**Falsifier.** Any realized model fails H-CT if it disagrees with the tested Lorentz dilation, gravitational redshift, Shapiro delay, PPN bounds, or precision-clock comparisons in the regime it claims.

### 4.5 Relations do not by themselves recover geometry

**Proposition P6 (non-derivation of geometry).** The relational/coherence base of 00 and the graph/frame structures of 01 do not determine a Lorentzian geometry or gravitational dynamics.

**Derivation.** 00 deliberately excludes rule, cause, and time. 01 admits arbitrary typed relations and does not furnish a continuum limit, causal-order reconstruction, dimension, signature, volume assignment, metric, connection, curvature, or action. Even a partial order plus cardinality would require additional assumptions and a continuum theorem; the present graph contains less specified physical structure than a causal-set realization. Hence neither \(\Phi_\epsilon\) nor \(\mathcal D\) follows. ∎

**New-hypothesis label H-GEO.** Specify a concrete realization and a controlled map to physical observables, including the domain in which geometric approximation error is bounded.

**Falsifier.** H-GEO fails if the proposed continuum limit lacks stable causal/metric observables, violates local Lorentz invariance in its stated domain, or cannot reproduce the weak-field/GR limit as its control parameter tends to the relevant limit.

## 5. Conditional reconciliation theorem: strongest currently warranted form

### 5.1 Required premises

Let the following be explicit premises of a future candidate realization, not claims already obtained from the corpus.

**Quantum premises**

- **Q1:** Operational physical state/effect spaces and normalized affine probabilities are defined.
- **Q2:** Sequential and composite composition are associative and physically specified; any failure of naive factorization from gauge constraints is handled explicitly.
- **Q3:** The model derives or admits a complex Hilbert/C\(^*\)-algebraic quantum structure, with reversible transformations and composition.
- **Q4:** Born weights are derived from Q1–Q3 plus fully declared assumptions, or openly admitted as an input law.
- **Q5:** Records and realization events are represented by gauge-invariant relational observables/instruments.
- **Q6:** The unitary or nonunitary domain is specified, including normalization and black-hole/strong-gravity scope.

**Gravity premises**

- **G1:** A controlled coarse-graining \(\Phi_\epsilon\) produces a Lorentzian continuum or explicit alternative with causal observables.
- **G2:** \(\Phi_\epsilon\) provides metric/connection data and their transformation law.
- **G3:** H-CL identifies a dimensioned source and conservation/exchange equation.
- **G4:** A field equation or alternative dynamics is derived, with an error term and domain.
- **G5:** Universality/equivalence and local Lorentz behavior are derived or parameterized and tested.
- **G6:** H-CT defines proper time and obtains both required clock limits.
- **G7:** Entropy/area and black-hole behavior are derived or explicitly excluded from the claim domain.
- **G8:** The model identifies how relevant no-go and gauge-observable assumptions are satisfied or evaded, without merely asserting emergence.

### 5.2 The theorem

**Theorem candidate T0 (conditional compatibility, not yet proved).** Let \(\mathfrak S\) be a concrete realization of \(\mathfrak R\). If \(\mathfrak S\) satisfies Q1–Q6 and G1–G8, and if its maps preserve the stated operational predictions in their shared domain, then \(\mathfrak S\) is a candidate reconciliation of quantum and gravitational descriptions in that domain. It must still publish a discriminator from GR plus standard QFT effective field theory before it can claim additional empirical content.

**Proof skeleton.**

1. Q1–Q4 specify the quantum predictive domain and prevent the Born rule from being silently imported.
2. Q5 makes the claimed realization/record physically meaningful rather than gauge dependent; Q6 bounds quantum dynamics.
3. G1–G4 provide a physical spacetime/dynamics target, source, and controlled approximation rather than a graph analogy.
4. G5–G6 establish compatibility with the existing local-Lorentz, equivalence, and clock regimes in which both descriptions overlap.
5. G7–G8 state the strong-gravity and no-go/gauge domain rather than exempting the model from known consistency conditions.
6. These conditions jointly yield compatibility **conditional on the named premises**. They do not prove any premise from the current corpus, and no concrete \(\mathfrak S\) has been supplied. ∎

**Status.** T0 is a theorem candidate specification. It is not a theorem of 00, 01, 01a, 01d, 01g, 03, or this artifact.

2. Frame-relative record \(\neq\) diffeomorphism-invariant local observable.
3. Graph or partial order \(\neq\) Lorentzian manifold.
4. Statistical/Fisher metric \(\neq\) dynamical spacetime metric.
5. Entanglement pattern \(\neq\) metric dynamics.
6. Error-correcting encoding \(\neq\) proof that generic spacetime is a code.
7. Cost/change-load \(\neq\) stress-energy conservation, equivalence principle, or curvature source.
8. Closure/phase language \(\neq\) complex Hilbert structure.
9. Proposed Born recovery \(\neq\) independent Born derivation.
10. GME witness, if observed \(\neq\) a graviton detection, a unique quantum-gravity theory, or evidence for this substrate.
11. Cross-modal recurrence \(\neq\) a shared physical mechanism.

Each is a type check. A derivation that crosses one without an explicit map fails even if its conclusion resembles a known result.

## 9. Empirical obligations and no-invention rule

No numerical prediction is present because no concrete realization fixes a numerical parameter, a source normalization, a continuum scale, or an observable. A later paper may not infer a number by dimensional rhetoric alone. It must first provide:

1. a defined physical quantity and units;
2. a model parameter or a derivation fixing it;
3. an observable relation and uncertainty/control analysis;
4. a comparison to GR+QFT EFT in the same domain; and
5. a result whose null/contrary outcome rejects an identified mechanism or parameter region.

A GME-type experiment can become relevant only if the candidate model computes a modified or recovered entangling phase in a stated regime. Standard GME predictions test nonclassical mediation under their own assumptions; they do not test a generic information-gravity vocabulary.
