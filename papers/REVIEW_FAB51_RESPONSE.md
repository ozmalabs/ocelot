# Response to review_fab51

Assessment and edits: 10 October 2026. Review source: `/tmp/review_fab51/`, including its overall review and all 27 individual reviews.

## Decisions

The review identifies substantial errors. The revisions correct recoverable dates, misleading source attributions, missing mathematical assumptions, several incorrect physics and finance claims, and inconsistencies between companions. Claims supported only by a proposed correspondence now state that correspondence's assumptions and outstanding construction requirements. Historical measurements retain their original scope; no new empirical results are asserted.

Two general recommendations require qualification:

- A proposition can legitimately be a consequence of definitions. Mathematical corollaries do not need independent empirical content. Valid conditional results are retained; circular empirical tests and unsupported interpretations are qualified or relabelled individually.
- Consolidating, retiring, or renaming papers is an editorial choice, not a consequence of the technical findings. The current document structure and stable labels are retained. The executor gap is addressed with a finite construction rather than by removing the construction claim wholesale.

The composition challenge previously called “equal facility” lacked a measurement rule. Paper 01 now offers a candidate comparative description-length protocol, including the mapping and auxiliary rules, with a nonpositive control-versus-target advantage as a challenge. It explicitly states that the protocol has not been registered or run. This supplies a reviewable proposal, not fabricated validation.

## Verification behind the nuanced changes

### Primary sources

- [Bennett, Logical Reversibility of Computation](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/fall06/cos576/papers/bennett73.html): reversible computation and uncomputation do not require a permanently retained full execution history. A bijective operation is not automatically logical erasure.
- [Hardy, Quantum Theory From Five Reasonable Axioms](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0101012): the continuity axiom concerns reversible paths between pure states; global-phase rotation does not connect distinct rays.
- [Chiribella, D’Ariano and Perinotti, Probabilistic theories with purification](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.81.062348): purification includes essential uniqueness, not just placement in a larger system.
- [Masanes, Galley and Müller](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-09348-x) and [Schlosshauer and Fine](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0312058): Born reconstruction needs stated quantum/probability premises; the cited results do not establish the proposed substrate mapping.
- [Ozawa, Universally valid reformulation of the Heisenberg uncertainty principle](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0307057): measurement error/disturbance and state-preparation uncertainty require distinct statements.
- [Bousso, Holographic probabilities in eternal inflation](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0606114) and [Bousso and Yang, Global-local duality in eternal inflation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.2783): local/model-relative measure proposals exist, so lack of an external vantage does not exclude the entire programme.
- [New York Fed term-premium documentation](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/data_indicators/term-premia-tabs), [Treasury auction documentation](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/help-center/faqs/auction-faqs/), and [Federal Reserve SCF](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/files/scf23.pdf): distinguish duration-risk compensation, auction allocations and household debt burdens.
- [Stanford AI Index 2025](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/ai-index-2025-state-of-ai-in-10-charts): the cited MMLU-threshold inference-price comparison is $20 to $0.07 per million tokens, November 2022 to October 2024. Other ledger components remain unmeasured.
- [Oizumi et al., IIT 3.0](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003588): a strictly feed-forward causal system does not form a conscious complex under that theory. A software graph alone does not specify the complete physical system.
- [Ćwiek et al., cross-cultural bouba/kiki experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8591387/) and [Nagel's original paper](https://home.csulb.edu/~cwallis/382/readings/482/nagel.what.is.it.like.to.be.a.bat.1974.pdf): qualify universality claims and identify the subjective-character objection to the phenomenal premise.
