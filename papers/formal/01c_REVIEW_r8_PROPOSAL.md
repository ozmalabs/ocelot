## B. One proposition without a falsifier, and one pointer

1. `prop:totality` has no entry. Add `[shape]` **No individuated
   totality.** Fails if a frame is exhibited that is individuated and has
   no container — a boundary relation with nothing on its other side —
   which would make the universe as a whole an object after all.
2. `prop:copernican`'s exposure is item 1 (an aperture wider than
   assumed would let the inference be made from inside), and item 3 says
   only what it does *not* touch. One clause in the note after the list:
   "Proposition~\ref{prop:copernican} is exposed through item~1: a
   dimension read from inside would be the inference made."
