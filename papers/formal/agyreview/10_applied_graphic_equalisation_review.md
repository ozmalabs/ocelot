## 2. Core Applied Formal Analysis

### 2.1 Why the US Dollar Frame? (The Centrality of Conversion)
- By Proposition `NV-prop:no-sum`, distinct sovereign frames are incommensurable without an explicit conversion relation $\kappa$.
- The dollar is not merely one currency among equals; it is the **global conversion relation itself**.
- A change in domestic US fiscal or regulatory policy is not an isolated local transition; because third parties (e.g., a Korean exporter and a Brazilian importer) settle in dollars, alterations to dollar rails directly rewrite the admissible action spaces of external frames.

### 2.2 The Institutional Revision Horizon
- The paper models nation-states and international alliances as complex nested frames carrying massive historical Narrative Momentum ($\mathcal{N}$).
- As sovereign debt levels, demographic dependency ratios, and infrastructure maintenance backlogs accumulate, the ongoing alteration cost $C_{\text{alter}}$ required to sustain the regime rises exponentially.
- The paper calculates the impending **Revision Horizon** ($H_{\text{rev}}$): the point at which available fiscal and political Narrativium ($N$) is fully consumed by non-discretionary debt service and institutional preservation, eliminating the capacity to adapt to external geopolitical or technological shocks.

### 2.3 Sanctions and Network Weaponization
- Analyzes the weaponization of SWIFT and reserve confiscation through the lens of frame revocation: revoking access to the conversion frame forces excluded participants to construct alternative, non-dollar bilateral conversion rails, permanently fragmenting the global Seldon manifold.

## 3. Rated Issues & Deficiencies

### [MAJOR] Issue 10-1: Sensitivity to Geopolitical Black Swans and Non-Economic Agency
- **Location:** Section 6 (`sec:geopolitical-dynamics`), lines 820–980.
- **Description:** The paper’s formal pricing models assume that nation-states and central banks act as cost-minimizing or capacity-conserving frames. While this accurately models peacetime economic governance, it under-weights non-rational, ideological, charismatic, or revanchist motivations that historically drive state actors to accept catastrophic economic costs in pursuit of nationalistic or territorial goals (e.g., major wars).
- **Proposed Remediation:** Formally incorporate a "non-economic ideological utility weight" or threshold within the frame's loss function, acknowledging that certain cultural and religious Narratives possess near-infinite alteration resistance, overriding standard fiscal optimization.

### [MINOR] Issue 10-2: Rapid Obsolescence of Macro-Economic Data
- **Location:** Section 2 (`sec:cog`), lines 90–105.
- **Description:** The paper cites specific empirical data points from Q1 2026 (e.g., IMF COFER reserve shares at 57.1%, US equity market shares at 43.7%). In macroeconomic research, high-frequency currency reserve data can shift significantly over short periods.
- **Proposed Remediation:** Supplement specific point-in-time figures with trailing 10-year trend intervals, establishing that the structural dominance of the conversion frame remains valid across cyclical fluctuations.
