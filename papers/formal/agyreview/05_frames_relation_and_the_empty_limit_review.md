## 1. Executive Summary

`05_frames_relation_and_the_empty_limit.tex` is a brief (283 lines), elegant metaphysical coda that explores two foundational philosophical questions generated, but deliberately left open, by Graphic Equalisation:
1. Does the progressive optimization of a frame toward maximal well-formedness converge toward Nagel’s celebrated "View from Nowhere"?
2. Are relations ontologically prior to relata, or relata prior to relations?

The paper delivers deflationary, mathematically disciplined answers to both questions. It establishes that **objectivity is a direction rather than a destination or standpoint**. As a frame strips away unearned, idiosyncratic structure (maximizing well-formedness), it converges toward a shared, optimal representation for a given purpose—not toward a perspectiveless absolute. The absolute limit point of complete de-particularization is proved to be the empty set ($\emptyset$), which does no work and represents metaphysical dissolution.

## 2. Formal Analysis & Core Arguments

### 2.1 Objectivity as a Vector Gradient
- **The Optimization Gradient:** A frame becomes "better formed" by purging structure that buys no distinction (`GE-def:well-formed`).
- **Convergence Without a God's-Eye View:** Two independent frames optimizing for the same task converge toward an identical structural minimum (`GE-prop:convergence-observed`). This convergence creates the phenomenological sensation of approaching "objective reality."
- **The Purpose Constraint:** Convergence is strictly task-relative. Frames serving different purposes do not converge on each other, because "purpose is a somewhere."

### 2.2 The Empty Limit Theorem
- **Remark (`rem:empty-limit`):** If all particularity, purpose, and aperture constraints are stripped from a frame, what remains? The mathematical answer is the empty set $\emptyset$.
- The empty set possesses no relata, instantiates no boundary, and performs no transitions. Hence, achieving a true "view from nowhere" is not an epistemic triumph; it is the annihilation of the observer.

### 2.3 Underdetermination of Priority (Relations vs. Relata)
- The paper resolves the perennial scholastic debate regarding whether edges precede vertices or vertices precede edges.
- In mathematical construction (graph theory, model theory), vertices must be declared first to define edges as pairs $E \subseteq V \times V$. But this is merely an artifact of human linear notation and mathematical scaffolding.
- Ontologically, at the floor of reality, relations and relata are co-primitive: an edge cannot exist without ends, and an end cannot exist without standing in a relation (`00_is`).

- **Location:** Section 2 (`sec:direction`), lines 66–99.
- **Description:** The paper discusses "approaching a limit" and "convergence" in prose without specifying the topological or metric space in which this convergence occurs. In formal topology, defining a limit point requires a topology $\tau$ or metric $d(F_1, F_2)$ over the space of admissible frames $\mathfrak{F}$.
