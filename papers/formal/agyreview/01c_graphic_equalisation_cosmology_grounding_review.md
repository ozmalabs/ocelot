By demonstrating that a particle horizon is an instantiated boundary relation ($\mathcal{B}_{W:G}$) and that a frame-free totality represents one of the degenerate zero-information limits identified in `00_is`, the paper reframes standard cosmological debates with exceptional philosophical rigor.

## 3. Rated Issues & Deficiencies

### [MAJOR] Issue 01c-1: The Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) Horizon Problem and Inflation
- **Location:** Section 4 (`sec:inflation`), lines 210–260.
- **Description:** The paper critiques inflation theory for generating the measure problem, but fails to give an adequate account of why cosmic inflation was proposed in the first place—namely, the *horizon problem* (the extreme uniformity of the CMB temperature across causally disconnected regions in standard Big Bang cosmology). If the universe beyond our horizon is merely a superframe dimension, how does the Graphic Equalisation substrate explain the $10^{-5}$ thermal equilibrium across $4\pi$ steradians without an early inflationary expansion or a shared antecedent causal fixed point?
- **Proposed Remediation:** Explicitly address the CMB horizon problem: show how superframe coupling or an antecedent shared seed at the Big Bang entry point accounts for large-scale homogeneity without requiring eternal inflation and its attendent measure catastrophe.

### [MINOR] Issue 01c-2: Tension with Observational Cosmic Topology
- **Location:** Section 2 (`sec:not-an-object`), lines 103–132.
- **Description:** The paper asserts that any individuated universe must have an outside container. However, in standard general relativity, a spatially closed universe (e.g., a 3-sphere $S^3$ or a 3-torus $T^3$) is compact and without boundary ($\partial M = \emptyset$), requiring no embedding space $\mathbb{R}^4$ or exterior container to be mathematically determinate and complete. The author should address whether a compact, boundaryless spatial topology is admissible under the graph substrate.
- **Proposed Remediation:** Clarify that "container" in graph theory refers to the abstract containing frame of rules and relation-types, not an extrinsic spatial embedding dimension.

### [EDITORIAL] Issue 01c-3: Tone and Stated Empirical Boundary
- **Location:** Section 1 (`sec:scope`), lines 66–71.
- **Description:** The text states: *"This paper derives no cosmological parameter, predicts no observation, and settles nothing empirical."* While this epistemic humility is commendable, it leaves the paper vulnerable to being dismissed by practicing astrophysicists as pure meta-philosophy.
- **Proposed Remediation:** Frame the negative results as constructive constraints: eliminating ill-posed parameter searches directly saves observational and computational resources.
