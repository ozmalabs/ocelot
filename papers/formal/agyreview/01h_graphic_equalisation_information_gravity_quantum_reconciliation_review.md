## 3. Rated Issues & Deficiencies

### [MAJOR] Issue 01h-1: The Undischarged Bridge Paradox
- **Location:** Section 3 (`sec:bridges`), lines 240–380.
- **Description:** While the ledger is brilliant in its diagnostic precision, none of the 14 bridges (Q1–Q6, G1–G8) are discharged within Paper 01h itself (or anywhere else in the circulating corpus). By setting the bar for reconciliation so extraordinarily high and discharging none of the required mathematical maps, the paper leaves the Graphic Equalisation corpus in a state of self-acknowledged suspension regarding physical gravity.
- **Proposed Remediation:** Provide at least one toy mathematical model (e.g., a $1+1$ dimensional discrete lattice with explicit Hamiltonian and graph curvature) where at least one subset of bridges (e.g., Q1–Q3 and G1–G3) is discharged constructively, demonstrating proof-of-concept feasibility.

### [MINOR] Issue 01h-2: Tension with Paper 01a’s Optimistic Tone
- **Location:** Comparison between `01a.tex` Section 4 (`prop:sr-gr`) and `01h.tex` Section 1.
- **Description:** Paper `01a` claims that "Special and General Relativity are reconciled as one change-budget spent two ways" (`prop:sr-gr`). Paper `01h` explicitly warns that a change-budget is *not* a metric or an Einstein tensor and does not constitute a reconciliation. This creates a severe tonal and epistemic tension between the two companions.
- **Proposed Remediation:** Back-port `01h`'s terminology guards into `01a`, ensuring that `01a`'s Proposition `prop:sr-gr` is explicitly labelled as an intuitive change-budget heuristic rather than a completed physical reconciliation.
