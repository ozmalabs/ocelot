## 3. Systemic Rated Issues Across the Corpus

Cross-evaluating all 27 documents reveals several critical structural issues that span multiple papers:

### [CRITICAL] Issue GLOBAL-1: Concurrency Confluence vs. Non-Monotonic Deletion
- **Involved Papers:** `01` (Section `sec:causal-order`), `A1` (Section `sec:merge`).
- **Nature of Defect:** Paper 01 asserts that distributed uncoupled frames execute asynchronously without locks and achieve deterministic confluence by re-running to a fixed point. Paper A1 invokes Tarski’s and Kleene’s Least Fixed Point theorems to guarantee order-independent determinism. However, Graphic Equalisation fundamentally permits destructive node/edge deletions ($\Delta G^-$). In formal rewriting theory and deductive logic, **deletion is non-monotonic**. Non-monotonic operators destroy lattice continuity, permitting race conditions that generate diverging terminal states (breaking `prop:determinism`).
- **Required Strategic Fix:** Formally introduce **stratified graph rewriting** (analogous to stratified Datalog). Prove that graph rewrite rules are partitioned into ordered strata, where destructive deletions depend strictly on lower-stratum preconditions, restoring monotonic lattice convergence.

### [CRITICAL] Issue GLOBAL-2: Discrete Lattice Anisotropy vs. Continuous Lorentz Invariance
- **Location:** `01` (`hyp:discrete`), `01a` (`sec:c-rate`, `sec:sr-gr`).
- **Nature of Defect:** The corpus adamantly defends "discrete sufficiency" (Axiom `hyp:discrete`), postulating that physical state lives on a discrete relational graph. Yet Paper 01a derives the continuous Lorentz metric $ds^2 = c^2 dt^2 - dx^2$ and claims exact compatibility with Special and General Relativity. Standard lattice field theory dictates that any regular discrete graph breaks $SO(3,1)$ continuous boost invariance, inducing energy-dependent dispersion relations that are tightly constrained by astroparticle observations (e.g., Fermi-LAT gamma-ray burst limits).
- **Required Strategic Fix:** Acknowledge this fundamental tension directly. Either:
  1. Formally adopt random graph topologies (Poisson sprinkling as in Causal Set Theory) to suppress preferred-frame artifacts; or
  2. Formally declare high-energy Lorentz-invariance violation (LIV) as a concrete, quantitative empirical falsifier in Paper 01a.

### [MAJOR] Issue GLOBAL-3: The Undischarged Rank-2 Tensor Bridge in Gravity Groundings
- **Location:** `01a` (`prop:sr-gr`), `01h` (`sec:bridges`), `01i` (`sec:bridges`).
- **Nature of Defect:** There is a severe internal tension between Paper 01a and Papers 01h/01i. Paper 01a optimistically asserts that "SR and GR are one change-budget spent two ways," implying that gravitational curvature is already derived from graph rewrite loads. However, Paper 01h and 01i ruthlessly admit that a scalar rewrite cost cannot generate the divergence-free symmetric rank-2 Einstein tensor $G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$ without 14 undischarged mathematical bridges.
- **Required Strategic Fix:** Reconcile the sequence: back-port 01h’s strict terminology guard into 01a, clearly labeling 01a’s Proposition `prop:sr-gr` as an intuitive heuristic and designating 01h/01i as the authoritative technical ledger.

### [MAJOR] Issue GLOBAL-4: The Metric Topology of Abstract State Spaces
- **Location:** `01f` (Qualia-space), `02` (Muchness $\mathcal{M}$), `03` (Alteration cost $C_{\text{alter}}$), `04` (Narrativium $N$).
- **Nature of Defect:** Across the middle sequence, several fundamental quantities are introduced as continuous metric fields, but their underlying topology remains under-specified:
  - Muchness $\mathcal{M}$ oscillates between a discrete state count and a continuous volume.
  - Narrative alteration cost $C_{\text{alter}}$ lacks a formal graph-edit metric space.
  - Narrativium capacity $N$ collapses a multi-dimensional controllability ellipsoid into a scalar.
  - Qualia-space is posited as a coordinate manifold without an explicit Riemannian metric tensor.
- **Required Strategic Fix:** Formally ground each space in recognized mathematical topology: define Muchness via Kolmogorov metric capacity, alteration cost via weighted Graph Edit Distance (GED), and Narrativium via Controllability Gramian determinants.

### [MAJOR] Issue GLOBAL-5: The Evidentiary Bottleneck vs. The Mandatory Substrate Embargo
- **Location:** `06` (`sec:record-wrong`), `07` (`sec:moved`), `08` (`sec:audit`), `09a` (`sec:benchmarks`), `02b` (`sec:scope`).
