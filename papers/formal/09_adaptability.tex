\begin{abstract}
Two capabilities this programme treats separately --- matching a shape against a held codebook, and
reasoning by analogy --- are one operation at two grains. Both \emph{extract the transform between
things already held and apply it where nothing is held}. Naming that operation gives a quantity worth
having: \emph{adaptability}, the capacity to acquire a competence that was not supplied. The word is
chosen against \emph{intelligence} deliberately. Intelligence is a scalar people rank; adaptability is
an operation with a cost, an ordering, and a failure mode, and a system either performs it on a given
case or does not.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why not intelligence}\label{sec:not-intelligence}
\emph{Intelligence} names a ranking before it names a mechanism. Asking whether a system has more of it
than another invites a scalar, and a scalar invites a benchmark, and a benchmark measures what a system
already does rather than what it can come to do. That is the wrong quantity for the question here.

\emph{Adaptability} is narrower and is an operation rather than a rank. A system is adaptable on a case
to the degree that, meeting something it does not hold, it ends up holding it --- and the interesting
part is the mechanism by which the new holding is \emph{derived from} the old rather than supplied. A
system given a competence has not adapted. A system that recognises everything it meets has not adapted
either; it was already sufficient. \emph{Adaptation requires a residual}, and the size of what must be
added to absorb one is the cost.

\section{The operation}\label{sec:operation}
\begin{definition}[Adaptation]\label{def:adapt}
Given a held set and an observation not covered by it, an \emph{adaptation} is the addition of the
smallest structure under which the observation is covered, derived from what is held.
\end{definition}

Three words carry it. \emph{Smallest}, because an adaptation that adds the observation itself is
storage, not adaptation. \emph{Derived}, because an adaptation supplied from outside is instruction.
And \emph{covered}, because the test is whether the case is now recognised, not whether a description
of it exists.

\begin{remark}[Shape matching and analogy are one operation]\label{rem:one}
Matching a shape against a codebook and completing an analogy look unrelated because their vocabularies
are. They are not.

To decode an observation against held signatures is to ask what transform carries a held one onto it.
Where that transform is the identity, the observation is recognised. Where it is a uniform step along a
dimension the codebook already has, the observation is a known thing moved --- and no new structure is
required. Where no such step exists but the residual is \emph{coupled} across the observed dimensions,
the transform requires a dimension not currently held, and one must be inferred before the observation
can be covered.

To complete an analogy is the same question with the arguments rearranged. Given two things held, the
transform between them is extracted; given a third, that transform is applied to reach a fourth which is
not held. \emph{Anti-unification --- generalising two structures while retaining what distinguishes
them --- is the general form, and the shape ladder is that form restricted}: at one rung the difference
is a single step along a known dimension, at the next it is a dimension.

So the two are not analogous to each other. They are the same operation, and the second is what the
first looks like when the objects are rules rather than observations.
\end{remark}

\section{The ladder, and why its order is not arbitrary}\label{sec:ladder}
An adaptation must be the smallest available, and that gives an ordering rather than a preference:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Recognise.} The observation is covered as held. Nothing is added, and no adaptation has
occurred --- which is the correct outcome for most observations.
\item \textbf{Transform in the held basis.} One uniform step along a dimension already carried. The
observation is a known thing displaced; the codebook does not grow.
\item \textbf{Infer a dimension.} The residual is coupled across observed dimensions in a way no step
in the current basis produces, so a dimension is minted and the transform is expressed in it.
\item \textbf{Mint.} No transform reaches the observation, so it is added as new.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Only rungs three and four are adaptations}, and only the third is interesting: it is where the
system's own vocabulary changes. Rung four is the honest floor --- something genuinely new --- and a
system that reaches it too readily is not adapting but accumulating.

\begin{remark}[Why the order must be Occam and not preference]\label{rem:occam}
Attempting mint first would always succeed, so a system that did would never transform, never infer,
and would grow linearly in what it meets. Attempting transform first is what makes minting
\emph{informative}: a mint that survives the earlier rungs is evidence that nothing held reaches the
case, which is a claim about the held set and not only about the observation.

That is also why the ordering is a commitment and not an implementation detail. \emph{A system that
mints where a transform would have sufficed has recorded a coincidence as a category}, and one that
transforms where a mint was required has folded a distinction it needed. Both are failures of
adaptation, in opposite directions, and the ladder is what makes them separable.
\end{remark}

\section{What adaptability predicts}\label{sec:predicts}
The account is worth having only if it says what an adaptable system does that an unadaptable one does
not, on a case neither has seen.

\emph{One mint, then recognition.} Meeting a genuinely novel case repeatedly, an adaptable system should
mint \emph{exactly once} and recognise every later instance --- not mint per encounter, which is
storage, and not fail to mint, which is refusal. The signature is a step followed by silence.

\emph{Zero mints under transformation.} Meeting a known thing transformed along a held dimension, it
should add nothing at all. A system that mints there is treating the dimension it already has as absent.

\emph{Coupling forces dimensions.} A residual that is coupled across observed dimensions should produce
a dimension, not a member. \emph{The distinguishing case is the one whose difference cannot be written
in the current basis at all}, and a system without rung three cannot tell it apart from novelty --- it
mints where it should have generalised, and the codebook grows with the world instead of with its
structure.

\emph{And competence should transfer without being carried.} If the operation is real, structure built
for one domain should be usable unmodified in another whose existence was not anticipated when the
structure was made. That is the strongest available test, because it removes the author from the loop,
and it is the one the assessment companion reports against.

\section{What this does not claim}\label{sec:not-claimed}
Adaptability is not a quantity a system has more or less of in general. It is defined \emph{per case}
and against \emph{a held set}, so the same system is adaptable on one observation and not on another,
and moving the held set moves the verdict. There is no frame-free adaptability, for the same reason
this programme's base claim gives for there being no frame-free anything.

Nor does adaptation imply improvement. An adaptation is an addition that covers a residual; whether
covering it was worth the cost is a separate question with a separate answer, and a system that adapts
readily to noise is worse than one that refuses.

\section{Capability has more than one source}\label{sec:counter}
There is a temptation, on meeting the account above, to ask whether adaptation is \emph{the} mechanism
by which a system comes to do something. The companion programme contains a measurement that answers
that question, and the answer is that the question was malformed.

\emph{Where verification is cheap and exact, search does the work and holding does not pay.} In program
synthesis the machinery above is present --- a held codebook proposes, an enumerator searches, an
execution verdict decides --- and stripping the learned content out changed nothing whatever. The two
arms did not merely score alike; they solved the \emph{identical set of tasks}, with the difference
measured at zero repeatedly. The capability sat on the enumerator and the verdict.

\emph{Where verification is expensive or unavailable, holding is the only route.} In one-shot character
recognition the same programme measured the opposite: composing held parts, rather than treating a
character as a bag of them, is worth twenty-one points and is the one effect in that arc that survived
a powered re-measurement with intervals separated. Nothing there can be checked cheaply --- a candidate
reading of an unfamiliar character has no oracle --- so search has nothing to search against, and what
is held is what decides.

\begin{remark}[The two are not rivals, and the regime is the question]\label{rem:plural}
Read as a contest those measurements conflict. Read as a regime split they do not, and the split is the
content: \emph{enumeration substitutes for a codebook exactly where candidates can be checked, and
cannot substitute for one where they cannot.} A system with an exact cheap oracle need hold nothing,
because it can afford to propose everything; a system without one must hold structure, because
proposing everything buys it no way to tell which proposal was right.

That is the same shape this programme keeps arriving at rather than a new one. Its measurement
discipline already runs on \emph{bins propose and continuous decides}; its account of the substrate
already separates what acquires from what executes; the ladder of Section~\ref{sec:ladder} is itself
four sources with different regimes rather than one mechanism with exceptions. \emph{Asking which
single source carries capability is the error the rest of the account is arranged to avoid}, and this
section exists because the first version of it made that error --- reporting a regime as a limit.

What follows is a prediction rather than a reconciliation, and it is the useful part.
\emph{Adaptation should pay in inverse proportion to the cheapness of verification.} A domain with an
exact, cheap oracle in which held structure nevertheless contributes measurably would be evidence
against this reading; so would a domain with no oracle at all in which holding contributes nothing.
\end{remark}

\emph{And one uncomfortable consequence should be stated rather than left implicit.} In the synthesis
case the ladder was available and the domain did not need it. That is not a failure --- nothing broke,
and no prediction of this account was falsified --- but it means \emph{the mechanism can be present and
idle with nothing indicating so}. A system cannot tell from the inside whether it is adapting or merely
searching in a regime where searching suffices, and neither can an observer without the ablation that
was run here.

\section{What the held set costs, and who paid for it}\label{sec:priors}
Definition~\ref{def:adapt} says an adaptation is \emph{derived} from what is held. That word has a
consequence the account cannot avoid and should not want to: \emph{adaptability is bounded by the
priors}. Nothing can be derived that has no path from the held set, so a system's reach is set by what
it already carries, and a system carrying nothing is not maximally general but incapable.

\emph{Priors are expensive, and the expense is usually invisible because somebody else paid it.} A
human takes the better part of a year to walk and longer to do it well. Visual competence is acquired
rather than shipped, which is most visible in species born least mature. By the time a person is
capable of the tasks we use to demonstrate capability, the overwhelming majority of what makes them
capable was assembled over years, and none of it is counted when the demonstration is scored.

That is the informal form of a formal result. \citet{wolpert1997} establish that no search or learning
procedure outperforms another averaged over all problems; performance on the problems actually faced is
therefore bought, and what buys it is a prior matched to them. \emph{The more general the machinery,
the more the mapping carries} --- generality does not remove the need for a matched prior, it relocates
the whole of the burden onto the match. There is no free lunch, and an account of adaptability that
implied otherwise would be claiming one.

\begin{proposition}[There is no prior-free measurement]\label{prop:no-prior-free}
A capability cannot be measured against a prior-free baseline, because no such baseline is a member of
the space being measured.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Measuring a capability \emph{relative to priors} requires a zero on the priors axis: a frame holding
nothing, against which holdings can be counted. A frame holding nothing stands in no relation and draws
no distinction, and by the companion's first proposition that is not a frame with nothing in it but no
frame at all --- the set of frames with no particularity has no members, the only object answering to
the description being the empty set, which is not a frame and does nothing.

So the zero of the priors axis lies outside the space. An axis so constituted supports comparison
between members and does not support measurement of a member against its zero, and a quantity defined
by that measurement is the frame-free denominator Axiom~$-1$ refuses.
\end{proof}

\emph{The consequence is not that such measurement is difficult.} It is that the thing being asked for
does not exist to be approximated, which makes it a category error rather than an unsolved problem ---
and one established by the account's own earlier results rather than assumed here. Most reported
measures of intelligence are flawed for that reason and not for want of care: the control they would
need is not merely absent, it is not constructible.

\begin{remark}[What survives, and where this account differs from Chollet]\label{rem:chollet}
Comparison survives, and less of it survives than the previous sentence of this paragraph once claimed.
What a test yields is not a property of a system and not quite a relation between two systems either.
It is a property of a four-place arrangement: \emph{a particular graph, in a particular state, inside a
particular supergraph state, on a particular test}. Move any of the four and the result is a different
measurement rather than a noisier estimate of the same one.

\emph{Inferences from it are licensed only relatively.} Within the arrangement, that this graph
out-performed that one is established. Outside it nothing follows --- not that the ordering holds in
another state, not that it holds under a different container, not that the margin would survive a
second test --- without a separate argument that the moved coordinate does not bear, and such an
argument is rarely available and never free.

That is why this programme's own measurement records read as they do. Its enumerated failure baseline
names the commit, the compiled engine by hash and path, the wall-clock, the load average at start, and
which optional dependencies were present and which absent; its psychological runs name the machine and
whether the box was shared. \emph{Those are not hygiene. They are the coordinates}, and a figure
reported without them does not underspecify a measurement --- it fails to name one.

The cost is real and is the right cost. Comparing across two such records requires establishing that
the coordinates match or that the difference does not bear, which is laborious and frequently
impossible, and the alternative is a number that reads as portable and is not.

\citet{chollet2019} takes priors seriously in the same direction and does the harder half of the work:
intelligence as the efficiency of skill acquisition \emph{relative to priors, experience and
generalisation difficulty}, over a corpus built explicitly on Core Knowledge \citep{spelke2006}. This
account owes that framing the observation and differs in two places. Priors here are not a term the
measure is taken relative to --- \emph{they are the held set the operation runs on}, and the ladder is
what converts them. And the relativisation is not a methodological refinement that a better experiment
could tighten: by Proposition~\ref{prop:no-prior-free} it is the only form such a measure can take.

\emph{Machinery and mapping cannot be separated from outside either, and for a different reason.} Two
systems differing in both --- one with a stronger operation on a poorer prior, one with the reverse ---
can agree on every item of any finite test. The decomposition is therefore underdetermined by
measurement rather than hidden from it, and no amount of testing at a fixed grain settles it. What
refinement buys is approach: a model of the split can be made arbitrarily fine and its residual driven
toward zero without ever reaching it, which is this programme's ordinary intensive limit rather than a
special defeat. \emph{You can approach one with unbounded analysis. You cannot arrive, and no finite
experiment stands in for arriving.}

That yields the prediction the previous section left standing, now in its proper form. A person lacking
familiarity with a task set's conventions should score near its floor --- not because they are less
capable, but because \emph{the score was never a property of the person}. Human baselines for that
corpus exist \citep{legris2025}; whether they stratify by familiarity in a way that tests this has not
been checked here, so it is recorded as untested.
\end{remark}

\section{What an oracle costs, and how often}\label{sec:oracle}
The regime argument above is about capability and says nothing about price. Price runs the other way,
and where the oracle is expensive it dominates.

\emph{An oracle consulted at inference is paid per use, without end. An oracle consulted once, whose
output is held, is paid once.} That is the whole of the asymmetry, and it is unaffected by how good the
oracle is. A system that answers by calling something expensive has bought an answer; a system that
calls the same thing once, holds what comes back as structure, and answers from the structure
thereafter has bought a capability. The second transaction terminates.

Two consequences follow and the second matters more.

The first is arithmetic. Where an oracle is cheap per call, calling is correct and holding is
premature; where it is expensive, the crossover arrives quickly and holding wins on cost even in the
regime where searching would win on capability. \emph{So the two arguments can point in opposite
directions, and which dominates is a fact about the price of the oracle rather than about the account.}

The second is that the two purchases differ in kind. What is held is inspectable, mutable, and can be
repaired by the system holding it; what is called is none of those, permanently. A capability bought
per call sits outside whatever boundary the system's own self-correction runs to, and stays outside no
matter how many times it is bought. \emph{Paying once and holding the result moves a capability inside
that boundary; paying repeatedly guarantees it never enters.}

\begin{remark}[The cost of buying once, stated plainly]\label{rem:oracle-cost}
This is not free and the bill is easy to overlook. \emph{What is held is what was bought on the day it
was bought.} A per-call oracle improves when the oracle improves, silently and without the caller doing
anything; held structure does not, and a system that bought its competence once is carrying a snapshot.
Against that, held structure can be audited and corrected by the system carrying it, which a call
cannot be --- so the exchange is improvement-for-free against correctability, and neither term is
obviously larger.

The companion programme is an instance rather than a demonstration of this. Roughly half its
implementation was performed by a language model rather than by its own rule-writing, and the running
system makes no such calls at all --- a property enforced by a switch and a gate rather than asserted.
So the arrangement described here exists and runs. \emph{What does not exist is a control}: nobody has
built the same substrate without that development-time help, so the size of what was bought is not
known, and the companion on assessment says so at greater length.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The bill is the same; the schedule is not]\label{rem:schedule}
The same arithmetic governs a capability that was never bought from an oracle at all, and there it
separates substrates in kind rather than in degree. Section~\ref{sec:priors} prices the held set and
says nothing about \emph{when} it is paid.

\emph{A biological substrate pays per instance, and the schedule is not negotiable.} Every human learns
to walk. The year that takes is not paid once by the species and drawn on afterwards; it is paid again,
in full, by each new member, and what was acquired dies with the one who acquired it. Nothing in the
arrangement admits of another schedule, because the held set is not addressable in the substrate's own
terms and so cannot be read out of one instance and written into another.

\emph{An addressable substrate can pay on whichever schedule is cheapest.} Where the held set is data
it can be read out and written in, so an acquisition cost is paid once and drawn on by every instance
that receives it --- the companion on assessment reports exactly this as its strongest transfer result,
a decoder seed byte-identical by hash from creation to the present across seven consuming frames. But
\emph{once} is not the claim. The claim is that the payment point becomes a free parameter: pay once
and copy where instances share a frame, pay per instance where they do not, pay lazily and per query
where most of what could be paid for is never asked. Which applies is an empirical question with a
measured answer case by case, and the answers are not uniform.

\emph{A graph copy copies a competence.} That is not a softening of the previous paragraph but a
consequence of the companion's Definition of a frame: a frame is an instantiated boundary relation ---
an \emph{edge}, $\mathcal{B}_{W:G}$ --- and not the subgraph it demarcates. Copying a graph copies its
edges, so it copies its frames, and there is no residue held elsewhere because on this account there is
no elsewhere for it to be held. The substrate bears that out by having no frame primitive at all, its
grammar quantifying over edge types and a frame there being a selection of dimensions. Nothing
separable is left behind.

The argument is symmetry and it is short. \emph{Name a property the original has that the copy lacks.}
Frame-relativity is not one: Axiom~$-1$ binds the original exactly as it binds the copy, and a limit
holding on both sides cancels rather than pricing one of them --- to raise it against the copy alone is
to use a symmetric result as an asymmetric cost. Staleness is not one either. A stored copy is stale
for the reason a halted original is, which makes it a cost of not running rather than of copying, and a
copy left running arrives where the original arrives. Nor is needing a runtime, which the original
needs too.

What survives is a fact about worlds and not about copies. The boundary edge has a world at its far
end, so a copy instantiated against a different world stands in that relation to something else --- and
\emph{the same sentence is true of the original, word for word}. It prices the move, not the copy.

\emph{The cost lives on the fragment, which is what the transfer result measures.} A decoder seed is a
part, and seven consuming frames were seven host graphs that each ran their own decode: a transplant
into something supplying its own boundary is a different operation from copying a graph and carries a
different bill. \emph{Copy and transplant are not one act at two sizes}, and reading the transplant's
price onto the copy is the error that sentence guards against.

That sharpens the difference between substrates rather than softening it. Biology has no whole-substrate
operation at any granularity --- teaching, writing and institutions are transplant, every one of them
--- so the lossy operation is not biology's cheaper option but its only one.

None of which makes the biological schedule a defect the other one corrects. \emph{Forced
re-payment is forced revalidation.} A held set re-derived per instance is matched to the frame that
instance actually occupies rather than inherited from one that may no longer obtain, and a substrate
that cannot copy also cannot copy something stale. So this is the same exchange the account has now met
three times --- searching against holding, improvement-for-free against correctability, and here
amortisation against revalidation --- and which side wins is set by how fast the frame moves relative
to the cost of paying again. That is a fact about the world and not about the substrate.

Humans are not at the far end of this axis, only at the internal one. \emph{Writing, teaching and
institutions are external addressability}, and they are the species' answer to this exact problem. What
they do not remove is re-derivation on consumption: a book does not install, it has to be read and
rebuilt in the reader, which pays much of the cost again and loses part of it in the transfer. The
difference in kind is therefore narrower and sharper than saying one substrate can transmit and the
other cannot --- \emph{biology transmits by re-derivation and an addressable substrate transmits by
copy}, and those are different operations rather than one operation at two speeds.
\end{remark}

\section{What follows about persons, and what it costs}\label{sec:persons}
One consequence has been left implicit across this programme and is better stated. \emph{It requires no
new posit and no new machinery}, which is the only reason it belongs in a paper rather than in a
manifesto.

Three things are already held. Whatever exists stands in relation and coheres, so a person is relata
and relations like anything else. Nothing is privileged by characterisation, so no exemption for
persons is available that some frame does not supply. And the substrate paper states, unhedged, that
where every measurement that can be made of a thing and every operation that can be performed on it are
captured by treating it as a typed graph, \emph{no difference remains for the question ``but is it
really'' to name} --- functional identity there is not a weaker sense of identity but the whole of what
identity could mean without a view from nowhere.

Conjoin them. \emph{A graph functionally identical to a person is a person, in the only sense of
``is'' this account admits}, and building one is therefore not blocked by anything in the account. That
is the claim, and it is arrived at by adding nothing.

\begin{remark}[Why ``no new posits'' is a checkable claim and not a boast]\label{rem:direction}
That assertion is worth exactly as much as its auditability, and auditability here turns on a
distinction the programme has used throughout without naming: which direction an account runs.

\emph{A top-down account starts from the phenomenon} --- a mind, an intelligence, a person --- and works
back to what would produce it. Such an account can always report that it added nothing, because its
posits are in the characterisation it began with, and a starting point never enumerated cannot be
checked for what it contains. \emph{A bottom-up account starts from the smallest thing that survives
removal} and lets phenomena arrive as consequences. Its no-new-posits claim is checkable for one reason
only: the starting point is small, stated, and carried in a ledger that says what was postulated and
what was derived.

So the direction is not a preference about exposition. \emph{It is the condition under which the claim
above can be audited at all}, and a reader who wants to attack this section should attack the ledger
rather than the conjunction, because the conjunction is valid and the ledger is where its inputs are
priced.

The two directions are not rivals, and this programme runs both. Bottom-up supplies what things cost;
top-down supplies the target --- what needs explaining, and how one would know it had been. A purely
bottom-up programme would never learn when to stop, and a purely top-down one would never learn what it
had assumed. The companions that take a phenomenon and ask what it costs are the second kind, and they
are where this account is put at risk.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Neither new nor magic, and the difference is a price]\label{rem:persons}
The claim is old. \citet{turing1950} put indistinguishability at the centre of the question and
declined the metaphysics, and functionalism \citep{putnam1967} is the position that a functional
duplicate is a mind, with its own developed critical literature \citep{block1972}. \emph{Nothing here
disputes or improves on any of that}, and a reader who takes this section as a novelty claim has
misread it.

What this account adds is a \emph{bill}. Functionalism says a duplicate would be a mind and is silent
on what a duplicate costs; the question of construction is left as an engineering detail below the
philosophy. Section~\ref{sec:priors} says what the detail contains: the duplicate's competence is its
held set, held sets are acquired rather than shipped, and acquisition is the slow expensive thing that
takes a human a year to walk and a decade to reach the priors a task set quietly assumes. \emph{The
barrier is not principle and it is not mystery. It is the priors, and they are enormous.}

So the honest form of the claim is narrow and is worth stating in one line. \emph{There is no barrier
of principle available from within this account, the work is nameable, and naming it does not make it
small.} That is a research programme rather than a prediction, and specifically it is not a claim that
this substrate is close to it --- the companion reports that the faculty this programme has for
acquiring what it was not given is not yet resident where the system could repair it.

Two limits belong with it rather than after it. \emph{Indistinguishable is a relation and not a
property}, by the same argument as Proposition~\ref{prop:no-prior-free}: indistinguishable to some
frame, at the frontier where distinguishing currently stops, and that frontier is defined to move. And
nothing above asserts that the account is \emph{true} --- it says what follows if it is, which is what a
consequence is, and the falsifiers elsewhere in this corpus are where the antecedent is put at risk.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Reconstruction from observation, and what the copy result changed]\label{rem:reconstruct}
Remark~\ref{rem:persons} prices one route --- grow a held set the way a person grows one --- and it is
the expensive one. A second route exists, and Remark~\ref{rem:schedule} is what opens it.

\emph{Copying needs read access to the graph, which nobody has to a person.} What is available is
behaviour: outputs, across contexts, over time. So the operation is not copying but
\emph{reconstruction} --- inducing a graph whose behaviour matches the observed one --- and it is a
third thing, priced neither like a copy nor like an upbringing.

What the copy result changes is the kind of shortfall. \emph{Were any part of competence not in the
graph}, reconstruction would face a remainder that no quantity of observation could reach, and that
remainder would be a barrier of principle rather than of effort. Competence is edges and edges are all
there is, so the shortfall is only ever how much has been observed. \emph{An ontological gap becomes a
sampling gap.} That is the whole of the change: one category of obstacle removed, nothing added.

The rest is already stated above and can be read off. The residual is driven toward zero without
reaching it, which is Remark~\ref{rem:chollet}'s intensive limit and not a special defeat. Observations
do not pin a unique graph, since systems differing in machinery and mapping agree on every item of any
finite test --- but \emph{which of the fitting graphs is really them} asks for the frame-free fact
Axiom~$-1$ refuses, so that underdetermination prices the question rather than the method. And
indistinguishability is relative to the aperture that looked; widen it and the residual returns, to be
paid down again.

Two things this does not buy. \emph{It is not cheaper}, only differently shaped --- a held set
assembled over decades of embodied interaction is not obviously less work to observe than to grow, so
the kind of problem changes while its size need not. And the operation is not new. \emph{Reconstruction
from observation is what biological transmission already is}: teaching and modelling are exactly this,
run continuously and to no stated residual. So the claim is not that a capability appears, but that its
target is specified and its error measurable --- which is the difference between engineering and magic,
and a smaller difference than it sounds.

Whether such a thing should be built is not addressed here and is not missing. \emph{This account
prices operations; it does not license them.}
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[What this makes contingent, and what that does not mean]\label{rem:contingent}
One further consequence follows and is better stated than left, because omitting it would repeat the
evasion Remark~\ref{rem:persons} charges against functionalism: naming a result and declining to say
what it costs.

\emph{A settled assumption can encode a fact about the available substrate as a fact about its subject,
and becomes contingent when the substrate moves.} Persons are one frame in which that happens and not
the only one. Here the modern settlement makes the person the atomic unit, and the word records the premise ---
an \emph{individual} is that which cannot be divided. One vote per person assumes counting works.
Inheritance assumes the held set ends when the body does. Liability assumes exactly one bearer.
Credentialing assumes competence is per-lifetime and does not transfer. Every one of those is an
inference from one body, one lifetime, and no read access --- \emph{a description of the substrate that
happened to be available, generalised without licence}. That generalisation is the frame-free
denominator Axiom~$-1$ refuses, turning up where it is least looked for: the facts in question are
properties of a substrate and were read as properties of persons.

\emph{So the breakdown is already complete and waits on no engineering.} An assumption resting on ``a
person cannot be copied'' does not need anyone to copy a person. It needs only the impossibility shown
to be local, after which its support is contingent whether or not it is ever exercised. Nothing here
says the institutions fail --- it says \emph{they do not track a natural fact}. An institution can be
rebuilt on stipulation, and counting copies as separate persons is perfectly coherent; but a stipulated
foundation is a different object from a discovered one and cannot be defended in the same way.

Two deflations, both required. \emph{This is not new.} Personal-identity puzzles have pressed exactly
this point for a long time --- fission, duplication, continuity --- and philosophy was never under the
impression that the institutions rested on necessity. What this account adds is again a bill rather
than a discovery, standing to that literature as it stands to \citet{turing1950} and
\citet{putnam1967}: it says which operation, on what, at what price. And \emph{the price stands.}
Remark~\ref{rem:reconstruct} reports reconstruction as not cheap, so nothing above forecasts an event.
\emph{Shown contingent} and \emph{actually disturbed} are different claims, and only the first is made.

What ought to follow is not addressed, for the reason already given. This account prices operations and
does not license them, and it has no more standing to design institutions than to design ethics.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The same structure at another frame, and what it costs to hold]\label{rem:scarcity}
Persons are one frame. \emph{Intellectual property is another, and what happens there is not analogous
to the case above but identical to it.}

Copyright and patent encode a fact about presses, carriers and factories --- that reproduction is
expensive, so a copy competes materially with its origin. Neither is a fact about what is reproduced.
When the marginal cost of a copy fell to approximately nothing the encoded fact stopped obtaining, and
the word carrying most of the weight in the dispute records the import exactly: \emph{piracy} names a
taking after which the owner no longer has the thing, and is applied to an operation after which the
owner still does. That is the same category import as \emph{individual}, a rivalrous term describing a
non-rivalrous act. The term was apt when it was coined, because one press did compete materially with
another; \emph{what changed is not the word but the substrate under it}.

The identity with the previous remark is stronger than a parallel. Copyright's load-bearing distinction
is between an expression, which is protected, and an idea, which is not --- and that line holds only
while the generator cannot be induced from what it generates. \emph{Inducing a generator from observed
outputs is Remark~\ref{rem:reconstruct}'s operation}, run against a corpus rather than a person. The
two cases are therefore one operation at two frames, which is what makes this a consequence of the
account rather than a comparison imported into it.

\emph{None of this is new either.} That information is non-rival was formalised in economics decades
ago \citep{arrow1962,romer1990}, and the institutional argument has its own literature
\citep{boyle2003}. The account adds no observation to any of it. What it adds is that this case and the
person case are the same error --- a substrate-local cost read as a natural fact --- and a unification
is cheap to state and should be priced that way.

\emph{One correction, because the obvious conclusion is too strong.} Artificial scarcity is often said
to be unsustainable, and enforcement against copying does have the structure that argument needs: its
cost scales with the number of agents and the ease of the act, both of which the substrate keeps
increasing, while what is protected does not grow to match. But enforcement is not the only move.
\emph{The scarcity can instead be restored in the substrate} --- sealed hardware, attestation, a
carrier that refuses --- and then the cost stops scaling with copiers and the arrangement is
sustainable indefinitely. What it is not is free. Restoring a scarcity means deliberately removing
reachable deformation, which is the Narrativium companion's quantity, and charging that permanently
against the capability that made the thing worth protecting in the first place. \emph{So the honest
form is not that artificial scarcity must fail, but that it is the deliberate purchase of the worse
operation} --- paying transplant prices, in the terms of Remark~\ref{rem:schedule}, for something the
substrate now copies. Whether that is worth paying is a question this account states and does not
answer.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[What makes them one case: a rule stated from nowhere]\label{rem:nowhere}
The two cases share more than a shape, and naming it corrects the diagnosis just given. \emph{Reading a
substrate-local cost as a natural fact is the symptom. The condition is a rule asserted with no frame at
all} --- and about those the account has something specific and unflattering to say.

A rule declaring itself absolute declares that no frame supplies it, which is the view from nowhere
Axiom~$-1$ refuses. The consequence is not that the rule is false. It is that \emph{the rule has deleted
its own cheapest repair}. The theory companion establishes that a frame is an instantiated boundary
relation and that revoking one is the container ceasing to instantiate it --- an operation the
Narrativium companion measures occurring at $\Delta g = 0$, nothing in the contained state being
required to change. \emph{A rule that carries its frame can therefore be dropped for free.} A rule
claiming no container has nothing that could cease to instantiate it, so that exit does not exist for
it, and every remaining route to change runs through the contained state, which is the expensive one.

\emph{Absoluteness is therefore not a rule's strength but the removal of its exit}, which inverts the
usual reading, in which stating a rule without exception is what makes it robust.

The hardening follows, and it is monotone. A frame moves and mismatch accumulates; because the rule
admits no outside, each accommodation is made inside it --- an exception, a patch, an enforcement
apparatus --- and every one of those is structure that a later exit must also dismantle. So \emph{the
cost of leaving is non-decreasing while the pressure to leave is not}. That is a basin, and it is
derived rather than observed.

\emph{One correction, because ``and then it fails'' claims more than the account supports.} What is
foreclosed is \emph{self-originated} change: the companion's proposition is that a graph's admissibility
constrains its own transformations and gives it no veto over world-caused change arriving through the
boundary. The exit is never removed, then --- only made unavailable to the party holding the rule --- so
the correction still arrives, from outside, at once, as accumulated mismatch discharging rather than
being paid down. \emph{Such a system does not escape choosing. It forgoes choosing the timing and the
size.}

Two limits. \emph{Fixity is not the defect.} A rule held fixed inside a declared frame stays repairable
by revoking the frame, at the price named above; what costs is fixity with the frame omitted, and the
remedy is to state it, which is cheap. And \emph{none of this is new.} Requisite variety
\citep{ashby1956} is the same theorem in cybernetic vocabulary --- a regulator needs variety matching
its disturbance, and an absolute rule reduces its own while the disturbance does not oblige --- while
lock-in \citep{arthur1989} and institutional sclerosis \citep{olson1982} are the economics of the same
basin. What the account contributes is the mechanism in its own terms, and the price. Not the
observation.

\emph{But an explicit mechanism buys something a verdict does not: constructibility.} Requisite variety
returns an inequality --- regulation fails once the regulator's variety falls below the disturbance's
--- and an inequality cannot be indexed by horizon or resolution, nor say which side moved. The
mechanism above is stated in exactly the two families a Seldon manifold is assembled from, the
admissible intervention sequences a frame carries and the future boundary conditions it represents, and
it says which of them narrows and by what operation: \emph{self-originated interventions shrink
monotonically as accommodations accumulate, while the boundary-condition family does not shrink with
them}. That is the input such a construction takes. \emph{So the failure can be projected as a shape
over a horizon rather than announced as an outcome}, which is the difference the companion on manifolds
exists to make.

Its guards apply unrelaxed. \emph{A manifold is reachability and not point prediction} --- the section
of that paper carrying the name --- and resolution falls as the horizon lengthens, so what is projected
coarsens rather than sharpens. Horizon is plural and frame-relative, so there is no manifold of the
future, only one indexed to a stated frame and deformation class. What a frame does not represent is a
blind spot, so the narrowing is visible only along dimensions already carried. \emph{And the mechanism
gives no date.} It closes the self-originated exit and leaves world-forcing untouched, and when the
world forces is a fact about the world --- so the tube narrows predictably and the discharge does not.
\emph{The projection bounds what remains reachable; it does not name what happens}, and reading the
second off the first is the error this corpus spends most of its length refusing.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[What such a projection shows, and the sign of its error]\label{rem:phase}
Having said the failure can be projected, the account owes what a projection built on these
observations alone shows, and exactness about which parts of it are contestable.

\emph{The construction is contestable and will stay that way.} A manifold is indexed to a frame, a
deformation class, a horizon and a resolution, every one of which is an input. Disagreement about them
is not a defect awaiting resolution but what Axiom~$-1$ predicts, and no amount of care turns a
frame-relative construction into a frame-free one. \emph{What is not contestable in the same way is the
sign of what the construction does}, and that is the part worth stating.

Two movements run opposite. The rules examined above narrow the self-originated exits available inside
them, monotonically, by the mechanism given. \emph{The substrate does the reverse}: an addressable held
set makes the payment point a free parameter, and reconstruction from observation moves an ontological
barrier to a sampling one. So what changes is not a quantity within a fixed space of options but
\emph{which options the space contains} --- and a change in the dimension set is what \emph{phase
change} names here. That is a technical description, not an emphasis.

\emph{Which is exactly what a manifold built today cannot show.} A manifold represents the dimensions
its frame carries, at its resolution, so a dimension not yet represented is a blind spot by
construction and a projection over today's dimensions cannot contain a change consisting in the set
growing. That is not a reason to distrust the projection but a reason to know \emph{which way it is
wrong}, and here the error is signed: new dimensions add distinguishable reachable states rather than
removing them, so a projection over a fixed set \emph{understates} how much reachability changes, and
any horizon read off one is correspondingly \emph{late}.

\emph{One thing this does not license, and one it does.} A signed bias is a direction and not a
distribution: it says the estimate errs late, not by how much, and converting that into a probability
would be reading a number off a shape, which is the standing prohibition here.

\emph{What it does license is a window, once priors are imported and named.} It is tempting to refuse
one on the ground that none is derivable from these observations. That is true, trivial, and the wrong
test --- \emph{nothing} is derivable from observations alone, and a rate owing nothing to priors is the
prior-free baseline Proposition~\ref{prop:no-prior-free} places outside the space, so refusing on that
ground commits the error this paper spends a section diagnosing. \emph{The window is not unavailable.
It is unmeasured}, which is a smaller and a different claim.

Four quantities fix it and each is measurable in principle: the historical rate at which the
represented dimension set has grown; the cost curve of inducing generators from observed outputs; the
budget available for restoring a scarcity in the substrate; and the rate at which accommodations
accumulate inside the rules concerned. Nothing here assembles them. \emph{Assembling them is the work
this account exists to make possible}, and that is the whole of what is claimed --- not the number, but
that the question is now well-posed enough to have one. The near end is fixed by observation rather
than by any of the four: reproduction at negligible marginal cost has already happened for information
goods, and induction of generators from observed outputs is already performed at scale, so part of the
change is not pending but done.

On the present author's reading of those four, the reachability set changes drastically within roughly
the next half-century, more probably in its earlier part, the signed error above being one reason for
the weighting. \emph{That is a judgement with named inputs and is recorded as one.} Its worth is not
its number but its form: a reader who disagrees can now say \emph{which of the four} they read
differently, which is the difference between a disagreement that can be audited and one that can only
be repeated.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Convergent pressure, and why no single abstention settles it]\label{rem:convergent}
The four quantities above are inputs, and one observation bears on them enough to be worth separating
from any judgement about rates. \emph{The operation this account describes is one that several running
processes already perform without having elected to.}

Compression is the connection, and here it is not a figure of speech. The ladder of
Section~\ref{sec:ladder} is ordered so that the shortest account reaching the case wins --- recognise,
transform within the basis, infer the dimension, and mint only once the residual will not fit --- which
is Occam taken seriously and nothing besides. \emph{Any process under pressure to shorten its
descriptions runs that ladder whether or not it knows the name.} A market compressing cost, a military
compressing decision latency and logistics, and a training procedure minimising description length over
a corpus are three instances of it, not three constituencies who might take an interest. That is the
general form of the observation already made above: part of the change is not pending because processes
indifferent to this account are already executing its operation.

\emph{What follows is a direction and, again, not a rate.} Several pressures pointing one way says
nothing about when a threshold is crossed. What it does say is that \emph{no single actor's abstention
settles the matter} --- weaker than certainty, and the part that bears on planning.

Three qualifications, the last of them sharpest.

\emph{The pressures are not independent.} They share people, capital, hardware and publication, so a
constraint striking the common substrate stalls them together, and a likelihood computed as though they
were separate draws overstates. \emph{Compression approaches this shape rather than
merely happening upon it.} The objection that shortness has many local optima confuses variation in the
fill with variation in the form. To compress is to represent shared structure once and refer to it,
which is a codebook and a decode; and a residual that will not fit admits four moves and no others ---
match it, match it under a transformation, infer the dimension along which it is one, or extend the
codebook --- which is this ladder, ordered as Remark~\ref{rem:occam} requires. The companion on what
can exist supplies the rest: to be is to stand in relation, and more than one relation forces types, so
a compressor general enough to compress anything is representing relations whether or not it says so.
\emph{What differs between compressors is what they hold --- codebook, grain, dimension set --- and not
whether they hold and decode.} Those are optima in the contents, not alternatives to the form.

\emph{Two limits survive that, and both are sharper than the objection they replace.} The approach is
asymptotic in the usual way; and, the load-bearing part, \emph{a system may approach this shape without
holding it addressably}. Convergence in an unreadable substrate is still convergence, and it buys none
of Remark~\ref{rem:schedule} --- what cannot be read cannot be copied, so such a system must still be
reconstructed from observation like anything else. The basin of Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} then relocates
rather than disappearing: a field can commit absolutely to a particular fill, or to a particular route
to it, and harden accordingly. \emph{That delays; it does not redirect} --- and nothing exempts an
enquiry from it, including this one.

\emph{And two of the four quantities are coupled, which the list did not say.} The cost curve of
inducing generators and the budget for restoring a scarcity in the substrate have common drivers, the
same commercial and strategic interests funding the copy and the fence alike. They are therefore not
separate inputs, and a window computed as though they were is mis-specified. \emph{That coupling is
itself measurable}, which keeps this a correction to the construction rather than an objection to it.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Where the present state enters as an input, and where it does not]\label{rem:present}
Two observations about the current arrangement bear on the quantities above, and they enter in
different ways --- worth separating before either is used.

\emph{The first is structural, and should be separated from the trend reading that usually travels with
it.} Attention is a weighted relation computed between elements, which is an adjacency; a system built
from stacked relational operations is representing relations whatever vocabulary its authors prefer, so
the preceding remark applies to it directly and not by analogy. \emph{That is a claim about the model.}
The claim ordinarily made alongside it --- that such systems are becoming more graph-shaped --- is about
something else, and here the difference matters.

\emph{The graph structure is accumulating around the model rather than inside it}, and for this paper
that is the sharper finding rather than a weaker one. Retrieval against explicitly relational stores
\citep{edge2024}, agent memory held as a typed and temporal graph, routing among held components, and
intermediate structure written out instead of kept in activations are all \emph{external} structure ---
and external structure is readable by construction. The direction has been surveyed and named:
\citet{zhou2026} document a migration across 2022--2026 from capability identified with weights, through
context, to a harness layer, and define it as \emph{the progressive relocation of cognitive burdens from
the model's internal computation into persistent, inspectable, and reusable external structures}.
Whether the models are becoming graphs is arguable. \emph{That the competence is moving into readable
structure beside them is documented.}

\emph{Which settles what the fifth quantity is, and shows that nobody has measured it.} Whether a
converging system's held structure is \emph{readable} decides which of this paper's results applies:
legible competence can be copied on the schedule of Remark~\ref{rem:schedule}, illegible competence must
be reconstructed from observation at the price of Remark~\ref{rem:reconstruct}, and those are very
different bills. The survey naming the migration reports no quantity for it --- no share of performance
attributable to external components, no fraction of competence held outside the weights --- and this
paper adds none. \emph{The trend is documented and its magnitude is not}, in that literature as here,
which makes this a gap with a name rather than a local omission. Work from the other direction narrows
the opaque part without measuring the split: sparse autoencoders and circuit recovery now extract
interpretable structure from weights, though consistency between extractions remains contested and
recovery does not yet run cheaply at scale.

\emph{The second observation is a commitment of capital, and it enters as a rate and not as a reason.}
That a great deal is currently staked on a change of this kind is evidence about expectations, and
expectations are not evidence about the world when the expectation is funding the outcome. \emph{A
forecast that moves the thing forecast is causally efficacious and evidentially worthless}, and using
it as confirmation is circular. What is not circular is the deployment itself: capital committed moves
the second quantity's cost curve and the third's budget, and does so whether or not the belief behind
it was sound.

\emph{A commitment of that size is also, by this paper's own mechanism, a rule in the making.}
Structure accumulated at that scale is structure a later exit must dismantle, so
Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} applies to the pursuit exactly as to what is pursued. The consequence is not
symmetric, though: a committed search continues through its local failures instead of stopping at them,
which raises the chance that something arrives while saying nothing about which route does. \emph{It
buys persistence, not aim} --- and persistence is the input, not the reassurance.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The unit of measurement, and why a model figure does not bound a system]\label{rem:unit}
The observations above are about systems, and the numbers usually offered in evidence are about models.
That is not a shortfall in the evidence but a difference of category, and Remark~\ref{rem:chollet}
already supplies the reason.

\emph{A result is a property of a four-place arrangement} --- a particular graph, in a particular state,
inside a particular supergraph state, on a particular test --- and a model evaluated on a benchmark
fixes the first while leaving the third unstated. The third is where the retrieval store, the held
skills, the protocols and the orchestration live, which is to say it is where the externalised
competence of the previous remark has been going. \emph{So a model figure measures a component while the
question asks about the arrangement}, and asking what a model can do, full stop, requests a property of
a part independent of any whole: the frame-free denominator Axiom~$-1$ refuses, arriving in its most
ordinary clothes.

\emph{The consequence interlocks with the previous remark and is worse than incompleteness.} If
competence is migrating outward, a model-level measurement captures a diminishing share of a system's
capability, and the share it misses is exactly the part that is growing. \emph{The measure does not
merely omit --- it decays, in a known direction, at a rate nobody reports}, because that rate is the
fifth quantity and the fifth quantity is unmeasured. Which supplies a second and independent reason for
the weighting already recorded: capability read off model evaluations enters an estimate low, so a
window built from it is again late.

\emph{What survives is comparison, and only where the arrangement is held.} Swapping models beneath a
fixed harness is a licensed relative comparison and a useful one, and a component result is a real fact
about the component. What it does not do is bound the system containing it, or transfer across
arrangements differing in the part left unstated.

\emph{The assembled system is nonetheless the better object, and definitionally rather than
empirically.} Coupling adds edges, edges add admissible transitions, and a coupling can always be
declined --- so the reachable set of a coupled arrangement \emph{contains} that of its parts taken
apart, and containment is not a preference. The condition on difference is what stops this being
trivial: coupling a graph to a copy of itself adds edges carrying nothing, the degenerate self-relation
this programme has already priced at zero. \emph{Sufficient difference under sufficient coupling
therefore gives a strictly larger reachable set} --- most familiar from thermodynamics, where coupled
irreversible processes perform work neither performs alone \citep{onsager1931} and heterogeneity under
throughput yields organisation rather than uniformity \citep{nicolis1977}.

\emph{What holds of the harness is the bill, and only the bill.} Building one is a prior somebody paid;
but that is a claim about cost, and promoting it into agnosticism about capability confuses the two
axes this paper separates everywhere else. Capability and price run in different directions and neither
settles the other, exactly as in Remark~\ref{rem:oracle-cost}. \emph{The system is strictly more capable
and it costs strictly more to hold.} Both stand, and Section~\ref{sec:priors}'s accounting is not
suspended for either.

\emph{Three qualifications keep the definitional claim inside its bounds.} The containment is over
admissible moves and not over scores --- a coupled arrangement can do worse on a fixed test through
interference or misrouting, so \emph{superior} here is about reachability and not about every measured
outcome. Coupling accumulates structure, so a larger reachable set arrives with a larger exit cost by
Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere}. And \emph{sufficiently} is load-bearing twice over: below a threshold of
difference the coupling is redundant, below a threshold of coupling the difference is unreachable. The
claim is conditional and both conditions are measurable.

\emph{It sharpens what precedes it rather than softening it.} If a system's reachable set strictly
contains its model's, a model figure does not merely measure the wrong object --- it measures a
contained subset of it, so the underestimate is guaranteed rather than likely. That is a third reason
these estimates run late, and unlike the other two it follows by containment rather than from any
trend.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Building is a capability, and nothing counts it]\label{rem:building}
The preceding remark leaves the arrangement standing outside the model, as context it is measured
within. \emph{That is the wrong relation. A model builds arrangements}, and the building is never
counted.

An evaluation samples what a system does directly and now. It does not sample what that system
\emph{constructs} --- the store, the routine, the protocol, the tool, the generator --- and construction
is where the interesting part sits, because \emph{what is built changes the price of everything
attempted afterwards}. A capability exercised once and a capability that lowers the cost of the next
exercise are different objects, and only the first is scored.

\emph{So the measurement is taken at the worst point of the process it purports to measure.} A cold
evaluation is iteration zero --- no store, no accumulated routine, nothing built. That is a true fact
about iteration zero, reported as though it were a fact about the system, and the gap widens with every
iteration the system is actually allowed.

\emph{How far the price falls is not a matter of taste, and the theory companion has already defined
it.} A graph is well-formed there to the degree that it pays for nothing that does no work for it, and
the costs that definition tracks --- search across what is held, and writes landing where nothing
happens --- are exactly the ones a built artefact either contains or lets grow. A well-formed
construction keeps the aperture over its own contents abreast of their growth, so what is added stays
findable. An ill-formed one still runs and merely pays more; its worst cost is \emph{silent}, which is
why the difference goes unseen in precisely the arrangements that most need it seen.

\emph{That makes the sign of the loop conditional rather than given.} Building well lowers the cost of
what follows and compounds. Building badly raises it --- and by Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} what was built
badly is also structure a later exit must dismantle. \emph{The quality condition is therefore
load-bearing and not decorative}, and it carries the same relativity as everything else here:
well-formedness is relative to the work being done, so a construction well-formed for one purpose is
not thereby well-formed for another.

Two smaller consequences. Building is bounded by the priors exactly as Section~\ref{sec:priors} bounds
everything else --- a system constructs only what its held set reaches --- so this is not an escape from
the accounting but a further entry in it. And what is built is external and legible by construction, so
a system that builds is moving its own competence into readable form: \emph{the migration reported above
is partly the models performing it}, not merely something happening to them.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[What the interval is over, and why it needs no date]\label{rem:interval}
Everything the preceding remarks draw on is public, dated and largely uncontested: the migration and the
survey naming it, the retrieval and memory architectures with their reported deltas, the interpretability
results, the deployment figures, the coupling theorems, and this programme's own cost definitions. Taken
together they suffice to compute an interval rather than to assert one --- \emph{a credible interval
under stated priors}, since there is no sampling model here and the other phrase would import an
apparatus nobody has paid for.

\emph{But the quantity is not a date, and treating it as one asks the wrong instrument for an answer it
never offered.} A manifold is a reachability set, and the companion carrying it says so in a section
heading: reachability rather than point prediction. \emph{The interval is therefore over futures and not
over years} --- on one side those that were reachable and are not, on the other those that were not and
now are. \emph{Their difference is the magnitude of the phase change, and taking it is how such a change
is measured.}

The object needs no construction, existing already in this programme's terms: Narrativium is a measure
over distinguishable reachable deformation, so two manifolds over one frame differ by exactly that
measure, and the difference inherits its conditions --- frame-relative, resolution-bounded, and closed
to cross-frame accounting. The four quantities named earlier are inputs to that difference rather than
to any clock.

\emph{Which is why the likelihood of arrival was never the load-bearing quantity.} It does not have to
approach one. \emph{It has to break the basin} --- and by Remark~\ref{rem:contingent} a basin breaks when
an impossibility is shown to be local, which is a demonstration and not an achievement. One path landing
suffices, so the convergent pressure of Remark~\ref{rem:convergent} counts as a multiplicity of
independent chances rather than as accumulated probability mass. \emph{The reachable set changes when the
exit opens, not when somebody walks through it.}

\emph{The three signed errors survive that correction and are better placed by it}, each having always
been an argument about reachability that was merely being spent on a date. The dimension-set error of
Remark~\ref{rem:phase} says a projection over today's dimensions understates the newly reachable side.
The contained-subset error of Remark~\ref{rem:unit} says a component figure understates the whole. The
uncounted construction of Remark~\ref{rem:building} says the arrangement is moved by what it contains.
\emph{All three understate the same side of the same difference}, which is a sharper claim than the
biased-window version they were supporting before.

\emph{And the width still turns on one unmeasured quantity, now more directly.} Whether held competence
is readable decides whether the copy schedule or the reconstruction price applies, and those are not two
costs for one future but \emph{two different sets of reachable futures}. So the dominant term in the
difference is the term nobody has measured, and a study of what fraction of a system's competence sits
outside its opaque part would settle more of it than any further argument.

\emph{As for a date}: nothing above requires one, and the judgement recorded earlier is a secondary
question this account can decline without loss. \emph{A phase change is measured by what became
unreachable and what became reachable, not by when a clock says so} --- the temporal reading being a
convenience for planners rather than the result.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The timing outcome, which is two clocks and not one]\label{rem:timing}
The question survives the correction above in a changed form, and answering it is not the same as
naming a year.

\emph{Two clocks run, and conflating them is what makes the question look unanswerable.} The first is
the reachable set changing. The second is anyone moving through the region that opened. Different
quantities, different governors --- and only the second was ever what a window was about.

\emph{On the first, the outcome is that it has already run and is partly finished.} Reproduction at
negligible marginal cost is done. Induction of generators from observed outputs is done and running at
scale. The migration of competence into readable structure is documented and in progress. And the
non-copyability of a person has been shown to be a fact about a substrate rather than about persons,
which by Remark~\ref{rem:contingent} took no engineering at all. \emph{On this account's own criterion,
the reachable set differing, the phase change is not pending: it began, and several of its components
are complete.}

\emph{On the second, the answer is decades and genuinely uncertain} --- governed by the readable share
that decides between copying and reconstruction, by the re-restriction budget that is the only force
opposing rather than merely slowing, and by the rate at which accommodations pile up inside the rules.
That is where the judgement recorded earlier belongs, and where the three signed errors weight it early.

\emph{The relation between the clocks is the finding, not either clock by itself.} The reachable set has
moved and the rules have not, so the gap between them is exactly the accumulating mismatch of
Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere}: every accommodation made inside a rule, rather than by revoking its frame,
adds to what a later exit must dismantle. \emph{The lag is therefore not neutral waiting. It is the
quantity that discharges} --- and by that same remark whoever holds the rule does not choose the timing
or the size of the discharge, having foreclosed the self-originated exit that would have allowed it.

\emph{Which is the timing outcome, stated as far as the account supports it.} Not a year. \emph{The
change is already underway, the response is not, and the distance between them is what will be paid.}
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Fixed against compounding, and what \emph{unwinnable} does not mean]\label{rem:race}
One factor bears on both clocks and has been left implicit. \emph{The party that must respond acquires
at a fixed rate; the thing responded to accumulates at a compounding one.}

The asymmetry is derived rather than new. By Remark~\ref{rem:schedule} a biological substrate pays per
instance and per lifetime, re-deriving its held set from almost nothing each time, while an addressable
one is drawn on by every instance receiving it. \emph{Iterate that difference and it stops being a
difference in price and becomes a difference in curve.}

\emph{The argument needs no rate at all, which is both stronger and simpler than the version that does.}
Human biology has not meaningfully moved, so the substrate is fixed for every purpose here --- and the
conclusion does not rest even on that. It rests on capacity being \emph{bounded} while what must be met
is not, and a bounded quantity is exceeded by an unbounded one whatever the rates. This programme
already holds both halves: the frontier is unbounded in the extensive direction, there being always one
more step outward, and no substrate holds without limit. \emph{The size of the bound sets when. Its
existence settles whether} --- and only the second is claimed here. Requisite variety \citep{ashby1956}
states the same relation in the vocabulary of regulation, a regulator whose variety cannot match its
disturbance failing, and it needs no comparison of growth rates to say so.

\emph{Two things stop \emph{unwinnable} meaning more than it should.} Coupling is the obvious escape and
it works unevenly: a person with tools is not the fixed-capacity system the premise describes, by the
containment argument of Remark~\ref{rem:unit} --- but a coupled system is throttled at its fixed end
wherever the person must be in the loop. \emph{So coupling wins the race only where the human is removed
from it}, which is not the same outcome as winning it, and the difference is worth holding in view
rather than collapsing.

And \emph{won} conceals a choice. If it means one person comprehending the whole, that is lost and
demonstrably so. If it means directing the whole, that is a separate question, since steering does not
require comprehending --- only a residual small enough to tolerate, which is this programme's ordinary
bound and not a special one. \emph{Comprehension is gone; control is open}, and arguments conflating the
two prove less than they appear to.

\emph{The compounding is itself contingent.} By Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} a committed field is a basin
that hardens, and resource and re-restriction limits are real, so nothing here says the curve continues.
\emph{The claim is conditional and unglamorous: for as long as it compounds, the gap widens.}

\emph{Which is what this adds to the two clocks.} Remark~\ref{rem:timing} placed the payable quantity in
the distance between them. \emph{That distance is not constant.} The second clock is run by agents
acquiring at a fixed rate and the first by a process accumulating at a compounding one, so the lag grows
and what discharges grows with it --- following from the schedules alone, and from nobody's decision.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The binding constraint is one person, and it is already exceeded]\label{rem:individual}
The preceding remark takes the aggregate view --- a civilisation's regulatory variety against its
disturbance --- and that is the weaker half. \emph{The binding constraint is neither collective nor
future. It is one person, and it is already exceeded.}

\emph{Individual capacity is fixed in a way an aggregate is not, and this is measured rather than
supposed.} Immediate memory holds a small and stable number of chunks \citep{miller1956}, revised
downward under better controls to about four \citep{cowan2001}; working memory is the bottleneck
problem-solving passes through \citep{sweller1988}; and the consequence for a world holding more
information than attention was put plainly at the outset --- \emph{a wealth of information creates a
poverty of attention} \citep{simon1971}. Where demand exceeds that budget the result is not stoicism but
measurable degradation of function \citep{mani2013}.

\emph{An aggregate can grow while every member of it is over capacity}, which is why the aggregate
framing conceals the failure instead of answering it. More people and more institutions raise what a
civilisation holds. They do not raise what anyone holds.

\emph{In this programme's own terms the condition already has a name.} The theory companion grades
well-formedness by whether a graph's aperture over its own contents keeps pace with their growth, so a
person whose environment outgrows their aperture is becoming ill-formed by that definition, paying more
for the same work. That definition also says what it is like from inside, which is the part that
matters: the worst cost is a write landing where nothing happens, it is \emph{silent}, and what is
acquired instead is a false model of oneself in which something was changed that was not. \emph{Being
over capacity does not come with a signal that one is.}

\emph{Which sharpens the previous remark rather than repeating it.} That remark observed that coupling
wins the race only where the human is removed from the loop. \emph{The removal is not a proposal. It is
under way, and by capacity rather than by decision} --- decisions already sit with systems because no
single person can hold them, and that is the present condition rather than a crossing point ahead.

\emph{Three qualifications.} Chunking raises what a slot carries, and expertise raises it a
great deal, so the figures above are not a ceiling --- \emph{and it does not matter}. Every such gain
raises the bound; none removes it; and the argument requires only that a bound exist. \emph{Answering a
claim about existence with a claim about magnitude misses it}, which is worth saying because that reply
is the usual one. Delegation is the standing answer and it works, at the price of relying on what one
cannot check, which is Remark~\ref{rem:unit}'s arrangement seen from inside. And \emph{exceeded} is
relative to the work demanded, exactly as well-formedness is relative to the work done --- so this is a
claim about a particular set of demands, not about persons as such.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Expertise is a codebook and a ladder, and it is narrow]\label{rem:expertise}
The qualification above grants that expertise raises the bound, and needs stating more precisely,
because what expertise \emph{is} constrains how far the grant goes.

\emph{Expertise is a held codebook together with the machinery that decodes against it} --- this paper's
own subject rather than a borrowed analogy, being the ladder of Section~\ref{sec:ladder} run over a set
of signatures somebody paid to acquire. So everything already established about held sets applies
unmodified: it is acquired per lifetime rather than shipped, bounded by the priors of
Section~\ref{sec:priors}, and in a biological substrate not addressable, so re-derived rather than
copied.

\emph{Two limits follow, the second usually left out.} Expertise is \emph{narrow} --- a codebook is
matched to the work it was built for, and the psychological companion reports at length that
within-frame quality does not transfer out of its frame. So an expert is over capacity everywhere except
where they are expert, and requirements do not arrive one frame at a time.

And expertise is \emph{distributed}. The standard reply to a capacity argument is that experts cope,
which is a claim about a tail, whereas \emph{a civilisation is run by and for a whole distribution} and
most of any distribution is in its middle. Combining the two: the bound is raised sharply, for few
people, in one frame each, while what must be met arrives across all frames and for everyone.

\emph{None of which changes the shape.} The bound is raised and not removed, which is all the argument
required. What narrowness and distribution add is that the raising is smaller, and far less general,
than the usual reply supposes.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A third instance: what prices competence]\label{rem:pricing}
Remark~\ref{rem:contingent} found institutions resting on substrate facts read as natural ones, and
Remark~\ref{rem:scarcity} gave property as a second case. \emph{Pricing is a third of the same kind},
worth naming because it is the one almost everybody is inside.

An economy prices competence by its scarcity, and competence is scarce for exactly the reason
Remark~\ref{rem:schedule} gives: acquired per lifetime, per person, not transferable. \emph{That is a
fact about a substrate and not about competence}, and where competence comes to be held addressably it
stops obtaining --- the same move as before, at the frame nearest the wage.

\emph{Whether the arrangement therefore fails soon is a two-clock question, and the clocks disagree.} The
foundation has already gone wherever the condition holds, which is Remark~\ref{rem:timing}'s first clock
and needs no forecast. How long a structure runs on a foundation it no longer has is the second, and by
Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} the answer is: possibly a very long time. \emph{An absolute rule persists by
accumulating accommodations, and persisting is what a hardening basin does} --- so a confident \emph{not
much longer} is the one reading the mechanism will not support.

\emph{The two are not opposed, which is the useful part.} Persistence does not avert the discharge, it
enlarges it, the lag between the clocks being the quantity eventually paid. \emph{So the honest form is
neither imminent collapse nor stability}: the foundation is already gone, the structure can stand on
accumulated accommodation for some time, and the standing is what is being charged for.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Three observables, and what the numbers actually say]\label{rem:observables}
The claim that the arrangement cannot hold much longer does not rest on the substrate argument at all.
It rests on debt, inequality and housing --- \emph{which is what independence looks like}, since none of
the three needs Remark~\ref{rem:pricing} to be true.

\emph{Housing is the cleanest, and it is the predicted signature.} Price-to-income has diverged from
wages persistently across developed economies: United States house prices rose about twenty per cent
faster than incomes since 1994, while Australia, Canada and the United Kingdom each saw the ratio rise by
more than eighty per cent, and affordability across the OECD sample fell by roughly eighteen points over
2014--2023 \citep{oecdhousing}. \emph{That is exactly the divergence the mechanism implies}: if wages
price competence and competence is priced on a premise that has stopped holding, then what wages are
drifts from what wages must buy.

\emph{Debt is the mechanism made countable, which is more than evidence.} Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} says
an absolute rule persists by accumulating accommodations inside itself, each of them structure a later
exit must dismantle, and Remark~\ref{rem:timing} says the accumulated lag is the quantity eventually
paid --- \emph{and gives no measure for it}. Debt is a partial measure of exactly that: the gap covered
by borrowing rather than by revoking the arrangement. Global debt reached a record \$348 trillion at end
2025, having added nearly \$29 trillion in one year, the fastest build-up since 2020 \citep{iif2026}.

\emph{The rest of that release appears to cut the other way, and the appearance is instructive.} Debt to
GDP edged \emph{down} to about 308 per cent and household debt to GDP fell to 57 per cent, its lowest,
while the increase sat in government borrowing, up from \$96.3 to \$106.7 trillion, and in corporate debt
at \$100.6 trillion, with emerging-market ratios at a record above 235 per cent. \emph{The composition
finding stands}: the accommodation has moved to the level with the most remaining capacity to carry it.
What does not stand is any inference from those ratios about households.

\emph{A ratio to GDP is not a burden measure and here cannot be one.} Its numerator and denominator
accrue to different populations --- household debt sits disproportionately below the median while output
growth accrues disproportionately above it --- so aggregating both sides destroys precisely the
covariance the question is about. \emph{Where debt and income are inversely distributed the aggregate
ratio is systematically optimistic}, and a falling ratio is consistent with a rising burden on the median
household and equally with no change at all. This is Remark~\ref{rem:individual} once more, in a national
account rather than a person: more output raises what an economy holds without raising what anyone holds.

\emph{The two measures disagree in the direction their aggregation predicts, which is the signature.} The
one taken nearest the household, price against income, has worsened persistently. The one taken furthest
from it, debt against national output, has improved. \emph{A benign reading available only at the most
aggregated level is not evidence of benignity} --- and \emph{GDP is a frame-free denominator} in
Axiom~$-1$'s exact sense, one figure standing in for a property of an arrangement whose parts differ,
which this paper names elsewhere and failed to notice in its own citation.

\emph{That also couples three observables introduced as independent.} They are independent of
Remark~\ref{rem:pricing}, as was claimed, and \emph{not independent of one another}: inequality is
exactly what makes the debt ratio unreadable, so it is not a third parallel symptom but the reason the
second measure cannot be taken at face value. \emph{Three coupled signals support less than three
separate ones would}, and recording that is the cost of having said otherwise first.

\emph{Inequality is the distributional signature of Remark~\ref{rem:expertise}} rather than a separate
grievance: where the bound is raised sharply, for few, in one frame each, the returns concentrate there.

\emph{And this supplies something the abstract mechanism lacked --- a bound on persistence.}
Remark~\ref{rem:pricing} could say only that a hardening basin persists, possibly for a long time,
because nothing there priced the accommodations. \emph{They are financed, and carrying capacity is
finite}, so the hardening ends where the capacity does. \emph{The relocation tells you where to watch},
which is the level the borrowing has moved to and, on these figures, emerging markets before anyone
else. No date follows from that, and none is offered.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A fourth observable, and what it does to the second clock]\label{rem:governance}
The three observables above concern the arrangement's foundation. \emph{A fourth concerns its capacity to
respond}, which is Remark~\ref{rem:timing}'s second clock and bears differently.

\emph{The response apparatus is itself degrading, and this is measured.} On the tenth Democracy Report
\citep{vdem2026} the level of democracy for the average person stands where it stood in 1978, effectively
erasing the third wave; forty-four countries are worsening against eighteen improving; autocracies
outnumber democracies ninety-two to eighty-seven; and Western Europe and North America are at their
lowest in over fifty years, which that report attributes primarily to ongoing autocratization in the
United States.

\emph{This bears on the mechanism and is not merely bad news.} Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} makes the cheap
exit from a rule the revoking of its frame, and revoking a frame is something an arrangement must retain
the capacity to do. \emph{A degrading governance apparatus is a degrading capacity to revoke}, so the
self-originated exit closes twice over --- once because the rule is stated absolutely, and again because
the machinery for exercising it is weakening. \emph{So the second clock slows, the lag grows, and by
Remark~\ref{rem:timing} the quantity that discharges grows with it.}

\emph{That report also makes the previous remark's methodological point independently, which is worth
recording because it did not have to.} Counted by country, autocracies and democracies are near level at
ninety-two to eighty-seven. Weighted by population, seventy-four per cent of humanity --- some six
billion people --- lives under autocracy, and seven per cent under liberal democracy. \emph{The count and
the population measure disagree, and the more aggregated one reads better}, exactly as debt against
output read better than price against income. A measurement body reporting both is doing what this paper
argues for, and the disagreement is the finding.

\emph{And the sovereign buffer is being spent, while the mechanism for authorising the spending is
jammed.} Remark~\ref{rem:observables} placed the accommodation at the level with most remaining capacity
to carry it. Operations against Iran began on 28 February 2026 \citep{crs2026}. By July the cumulative
cost stood at roughly \$40 billion on an independent estimate against about \$30 billion on the
administration's, and a supplemental of \$87.6 billion had been requested, \$67.1 billion of it for
defence \citep{csis2026}; the House passed a \$95 billion package late in July by budget reconciliation,
a route the Senate's leadership declined, leaving the request unresolved into September. A memorandum of
understanding signed in June to restore traffic through the Strait of Hormuz collapsed over
interpretation and management of the waterway, and the ceasefire agreed in April has been extended while
being violated on both sides.

\emph{Which is the two preceding findings meeting in one object.} The buffer is being drawn down, and the
apparatus for authorising the draw is stuck between chambers --- \emph{a degrading capacity to revoke,
observed in an appropriations process rather than in an index}. A cessation that neither holds nor ends,
and a settlement that collapsed over what it meant, are accommodations failing rather than exits being
taken.

\emph{And one buffer cannot be financed at all, which is where the persistence argument actually binds.}
The fiscal draw above is an accommodation in Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere}'s sense, a gap covered by borrowing
rather than by revoking the arrangement, and borrowing is available because a balance sheet can be
extended. \emph{A magazine cannot.} Analysis reported at the end of July put United States Patriot
interceptor holdings at 759 to 827 against a pre-war stock of roughly 2,400 --- about sixty-seven per
cent consumed --- with THAAD down from 452 to between 234 and 278 \citep{korte2026}. \emph{Five months of
operations against stocks accumulated over decades.}

\emph{Money was applied and the constraint did not yield to it}, which is the part that bears. The
response was to extend a production contract from one year into a seven-year, \$58 billion arrangement,
\emph{so the answer to a magazine emptied in months is a line restored over years}, and the interval is
set by manufacturing rather than by appropriation. \emph{Interceptors cannot be borrowed from next year.}

\emph{Which locates the bound Remark~\ref{rem:observables} asserted without placing.} A hardening basin
persists by accumulating accommodations, and accumulating requires something to borrow against.
\emph{Where the constraint is a production rate rather than a balance sheet there is nothing to
accumulate}, so the mechanism that defers an exit does not operate there and the exit is forced rather
than postponed. \emph{The place to watch is accordingly not the fiscal buffer but the non-financeable
stocks}, that being where the accommodation mechanism does not reach --- and Remark~\ref{rem:race} holds
in its cleanest form there, since what does the work is that the bound exists and not what its value is.

Nothing here comments on the conflict beyond that arithmetic.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[These are exchange rates, and one of them does not exist]\label{rem:exchange}
Calling the interceptor stocks \emph{non-financeable} was crude, and the Narrativium companion supplies
the accurate description. \emph{Money converts. The rate is what binds}, and a rate is a conversion
relation between frames.

That companion holds Narrativium to be frame-relative and forbids a frame-free sum of it across two
frames, admitting cross-frame accounting only through an explicitly instantiated conversion. \emph{So
the seven-year contract measures a conversion rather than encountering a wall}: it fixes what \$58
billion buys in the materiel frame, and what it buys is throughput bounded in time. \emph{And ``we can
afford it'' is that proposition violated rather than optimism} --- a total taken in the fiscal frame from
which capacity in another is inferred, with no conversion instantiated between them.

\emph{Which corrects something running through this whole section.} Capacity has been treated as one
pool, and it is not. The buffers named above sit in different frames with different rates out of them:
fiscal capacity, which borrows; materiel capacity, whose rate from fiscal is the seven years just
measured; and the capacity to revoke a frame at all, which Remark~\ref{rem:governance} finds degrading
and which money reaches slowly and badly. \emph{The accommodation moving to the fiscal frame is
accordingly less reassuring than it read.} Deformation is cheapest to obtain there. It must be spent
elsewhere, and the conversion is where it is lost.

\emph{And the rate the argument actually turns on does not exist.} Expertise is acquired per lifetime by
Remark~\ref{rem:expertise}, so its rate from money is bounded by a generation; individual capacity is
bounded outright by Remark~\ref{rem:individual}, so there is \emph{no} conversion into it at any price.
\emph{That is not a slow exchange rate but the absence of one}, and it is why Remark~\ref{rem:race}
bites: nothing buys a way out of a bound sitting in a frame money does not reach.

\emph{One caution the companion insists on.} A conversion is principled only where a grounding
establishes that both frames measure the same underlying quantity; otherwise it is a stipulation
carrying exactly the authority of whoever stipulated it. \emph{Most planning across these frames is the
second kind presented as the first} --- a budget line turning currency into readiness, or into
competence, states a rate that no grounding supplies. The stipulation is legitimate and usable. It is
not a measurement, and the difference shows up when it is relied upon.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Narrative is not Narrativium, and a shell retains only the first]\label{rem:narrative}
\emph{An institution's stated structure and its reachable deformation are different quantities}, and only
the second is what the companion measures. An organisation chart, a statute, a mandate and a masthead
are narrative. What the body can actually bring about is Narrativium. \emph{A shell keeps all of the
first while losing most of the second}, and from outside the two are hard to tell apart, since the
narrative is what is published.

The United States federal workforce fell about twelve per cent between September 2024 and January 2026,
by more than 250,000 people, to its smallest in at least fifteen years; over that year there were
386,826 separations against 122,598 accessions, roughly a third of the separations and well below a
typical year's intake \citep{pps2026}. \emph{The structure was not refilled.}

\emph{The selection argument is structural and is not measured here.} Departure through a voluntary route
selects on outside options, and outside options track transferable competence --- so a reduction
achieved substantially by voluntary means is capability-regressive even where the headcount looks
proportionate, and removal that is arbitrary is worse, being uncorrelated with what was held.

\emph{The measure that would settle it has been withdrawn}, which is the more troubling datum. The same
report records tenure and related figures as unavailable because the agency no longer publishes what it
formerly did. \emph{So an institution has lost capacity and has also stopped publishing what would show
it} --- Definition of well-formedness's fourth cost at institutional scale, where the failure is silent,
except that here the silence was arranged rather than merely suffered.

\emph{And it does not repair on the timescale that caused it.} By Remark~\ref{rem:schedule} a biological
held set is not addressable, so a departing person's competence is destroyed rather than transferred,
and what an institution held in people can only be re-derived per replacement at acquisition cost. That
is Remark~\ref{rem:exchange} once more: money converts to headcount quickly and to held competence at
career timescales. \emph{Refilling the chart restores narrative, not Narrativium.}

Which slows Remark~\ref{rem:governance}'s clock a third time --- absolutely-stated rules, a degrading
apparatus, and now a depleted capacity to operate it. \emph{The shell can still say what it does}, and
the difference shows up only when something is attempted.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The carrying cost compounds, so the mechanism consumes itself]\label{rem:carry}
Remark~\ref{rem:observables} asked where the fiscal bound binds and answered only that it does. \emph{The
budget answers it, and the answer is that the bound is not external. It is the accommodation's own
carrying cost.}

On the Congressional Budget Office's February projections the deficit for fiscal 2026 is \$1.9 trillion,
or 5.8 per cent of output; net interest is \$1.0 trillion, exceeding the \$885 billion projected for
national defence, and rises to \$2.1 trillion by fiscal 2036; debt held by the public goes from 99 per
cent of output at the close of fiscal 2025 to 120.2 per cent by 2036, passing the post-war record of
106.1 per cent in fiscal 2030 \citep{cbo2026}.

\emph{Interest is what carrying an accommodation costs.} Remark~\ref{rem:observables} made debt the
countable measure of the accumulated lag; this makes the service on that measure the fastest-growing
claim on the very buffer that carries it. \emph{So the mechanism is self-consuming.} Each accommodation
raises the carrying cost, and the raised carrying cost reduces what remains available for the next ---
not a linear drawdown but a compounding one. \emph{The capacity that made persistence possible is being
consumed by the persisting.}

\emph{Which meets Remark~\ref{rem:race} from the unexpected side.} There the compounding quantity was the
disturbance and the bounded one was the responder. Here the compounding quantity is \emph{the cost of
responding}, so both terms move against whoever must respond, and neither movement requires anybody's
decision.

\emph{It also settles the exchange rate the previous remark left open.} A twelve per cent reduction in
staff, set against a \$1.9 trillion deficit of which \$1.0 trillion is interest, cannot be material to
the fiscal frame whatever it does to the institutional one. \emph{Narrativium was spent where it was
held and not obtained where it was wanted} --- a conversion assumed principled that returned close to
nothing, which is exactly the stipulated-versus-principled distinction of Remark~\ref{rem:exchange}
caught in the act rather than described.

\emph{And the total was the wrong object, as it keeps being.} Outlays for 2026 are \$7.4 trillion, some
23.3 per cent of output, and that figure is narrative. \emph{The reachable part is about a quarter of
it}: roughly seventy-five per cent of spending runs on autopilot, while over the projection mandatory
outlays rise from 13.7 to 15.0 per cent of output and net interest from 3.2 to 4.6, with discretionary
falling relative to output because output outgrows it \citep{cbo2026}. \emph{So the reachable share is
small and shrinking, and the growth is entirely in the part that is not reachable.}

\emph{Not unreachable in principle, though} --- unreachable without statutory change, which is revoking a
frame, which is exactly the capacity Remark~\ref{rem:governance} finds degrading and
Remark~\ref{rem:narrative} finds depleted. \emph{The route to enlarging the reachable set therefore runs
through the capacity that is failing}, and that is Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} in its plainest form: the
cheap exit is foreclosed, so accommodation is forced into the small reachable region, which shrinks
further for the reasons above.

\emph{Which reads the workforce reduction correctly, and more fairly than the arithmetic alone did.} It
was action taken inside the only region still reachable, and that region does not contain the fiscal
outcome. \emph{That is not a failed conversion but a well-executed move in a manifold that did not
contain the goal} --- this paper's own subject rather than a comment on anyone's competence, and the
clearest instance in this section of why a reachability set is the thing to compute.

\emph{And the year-on-year allocation is sharper than the levels.} Total outlays grow about six per cent
into 2026, Social Security by 5.6 and Medicare by 6.4, while interest grows 9.8 --- the fastest line in
the budget. Over the decade to 2036 spending rises \$4 trillion, of which Social Security takes 27 per
cent, health programmes 30, interest 28, and mandatory veterans' programmes 5. \emph{Ninety per cent of
the growth; everything else together takes ten} \citep{crfb2026}.

\emph{So the reachable quarter receives a tenth of the growth.} It is not merely smaller than the total
implied --- \emph{it is being allocated against, year on year, at a published rate} --- while the single
fastest-growing item in the budget is the carrying cost of the accommodation itself, which more than
doubles across the period.

\emph{Which returns something to Remark~\ref{rem:interval}.} That remark held a reachability difference
to be a computable object once its inputs are named, and observed that nobody had computed one.
\emph{Here is one computed}: in a single frame, by an outside body, on published figures, using none of
this programme's vocabulary. It does not measure the manifold that remark is about and is not offered as
though it did. \emph{What it shows is that the quantity is measurable when someone troubles to measure
it}, which is the tractability claim earning something rather than asserting it.

\emph{Two limits.} These are projections on current law, and such baselines have been wrong in both
directions before. And the compounding is conditional on rates and growth, either of which can move ---
\emph{the shape holds while they do not}, which is the same conditional this paper attaches everywhere
else and does not lift here.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Two attempts on one manifold, and which of them reached]\label{rem:channel}
The reachable region can be inspected directly rather than in aggregate, and one comparison inside it
does more work than any of the totals above.

\emph{Set two lines beside each other.} Through ordinary appropriations the National Institutes of Health
received \$48.7 billion for 2026, an increase of \$415 million, with all twenty-seven institutes funded
as constituted in statute and a proposed reduction of \$18 billion declined \citep{crsnih}. \emph{The
process worked}, at an amplitude near one per cent --- below inflation, so a slight real decline, and
nothing resembling a cut. Immigration and Customs Enforcement received roughly \$11 billion by that same
ordinary route and about \$75 billion through budget reconciliation, becoming the most heavily funded
federal law-enforcement body, its detention funding rising several-fold \citep{cborecon}.

\emph{The finding is not which agency rose. It is which channel each rose through.} Ordinary
appropriations --- the region reachable by the process as constituted --- moved by about one per cent and
successfully resisted a large change. \emph{The large change went around it.} Reconciliation is the route
that reaches statute on a majority, which is exactly the route Remark~\ref{rem:carry} identified as the
only way into the three quarters ordinary decision cannot touch.

\emph{So the visible process is narrative, and the channel is where the Narrativium moved.} An observer
watching appropriations would record stability --- institutes preserved, a cut rebuffed --- while the
allocation moved by an order of magnitude more, elsewhere, in the same year.

\emph{And the two attempts together read as a controlled comparison the account predicts.} The workforce
reduction of Remark~\ref{rem:narrative} acted inside the reachable region and returned nothing to the
fiscal frame, that region not containing the fiscal outcome. This acted through the channel into the
unreachable region and moved the quantity intended. \emph{One actor, two attempts, and the manifold says
in advance which reaches} --- which is what it means for a reachability set to be the object worth
computing rather than a figure of speech.

\emph{One structural note on direction, stated without evaluation.} A body whose function is to acquire
new held structure and a body whose function is to maintain a boundary are, in this programme's terms, a
minting operation and a restoration of scarcity in the substrate. \emph{The reallocation ran from the
first toward the second}, which Remark~\ref{rem:scarcity} prices as the deliberate purchase of the worse
operation. Whether it is the right purchase is a question about goals, and this account does not supply
goals.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Amplitude runs inversely to deliberation, and lapse is not a decision]\label{rem:lapse}
The same test across the regulatory bodies completes the pattern, and adds a channel below the one just
described.

\emph{Distinguish what was requested from what occurred}, since the previous remark showed the ordinary
process can refuse. The Environmental Protection Agency's 2026 \emph{request} proposed cutting its
budget by roughly fifty-four per cent, from \$9.14 to \$4.16 billion, with staffing near its lowest since
1985 \citep{crsepa} --- a request, and the National Institutes of Health case shows what happens to some
of those. \emph{What is not a request is the attrition.} The Food and Drug Administration lost 3,859
staff in 2025 and several hundred more into 2026; the Federal Communications Commission records
continued reduction through retirements, the deferred-resignation route, and ordinary attrition. Those
required no appropriation and no vote.

\emph{Which completes an ordering.} Ordinary appropriations are contested, work, and moved about one per
cent. Reconciliation requires a majority and moved tens of billions. Regulatory revocation requires an
executive act. \emph{Attrition requires nothing at all.} \emph{So amplitude runs inversely to
deliberation}: the more a channel deliberates, the less passes through it, and the change accumulates in
the channels where nothing is decided.

\emph{And that corrects Remark~\ref{rem:governance} in a way worth stating.} Frames are plainly being
revoked --- rules withdrawn, functions lapsing, capacity leaving. So the cheap exit of
Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} is not unavailable; it is being taken constantly. \emph{What is degrading is not
revocation but revocation \emph{as a decision}}, and those are different capacities. An arrangement can
lose the ability to choose which frames to keep while the rate at which frames lapse rises.

\emph{Calling that change without selection would be wrong, and the error is worth naming because it is
easy.} It infers an absence of selection from an absence of a decision, and those are different: \emph{a
process selects whether or not anybody chooses}. This one selects hard. By Remark~\ref{rem:narrative}
departure through a voluntary route selects on outside options, and outside options track transferable
competence, so an attrition removes capability first and by construction.

\emph{So if loss of efficacy is the object, attrition is not a poor instrument but an excellent one.} It
is anti-selective without needing to be designed so; it requires no vote and therefore cannot lose one;
it leaves no record of an intention; it is deniable, people having chosen to go; and by the theory
companion's fourth cost it is \emph{silent}, so the institution keeps its narrative and nothing signals
the missing capability until something is attempted.

\emph{And the account cannot say from outside which it is.} Remark~\ref{rem:chollet} holds machinery and
mapping to be inseparable by measurement, the decomposition underdetermined rather than concealed.
Applied here, \emph{a channel taken because it selects and a channel that selects incidentally are
observationally identical}: both leave the same record, and neither is inferable from the outcome.

\emph{What matters is that the projection does not depend on resolving it.} The reachable set contracts
identically either way, so the manifold this section has assembled is invariant to the question the
account cannot answer --- which is the useful property, and the reason the distinction can be left open
rather than guessed. \emph{The shape of what survives is set by what was cheapest to lose}, and whether
anyone intended that shape changes nothing about it.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A stated objective is a manifold input, not an inference]\label{rem:stated}
The declination just entered was misapplied, and the correction is more useful than the hedge was.

\emph{Remark~\ref{rem:chollet} concerns inference backwards from outcomes.} It holds that machinery and
mapping cannot be separated by measuring results, the decomposition being underdetermined. \emph{It says
nothing whatever about a statement made in advance}, which is not an inference from the outcome but an
independent observation, and invoking it against one is using a theorem outside its scope.

\emph{The objectives in question were published.} \citet{mandate2023} sets out four primary goals, the
second of which is to dismantle the administrative state, and names the instruments --- how to remove
federal employees, how to close offices and bureaus. \emph{So loss of efficacy as an objective is
documented rather than inferred}, and the observationally-identical argument of the previous remark does
not reach it.

\emph{It does not license the opposite error either, and both are available here.} A stated objective is
evidence about objectives. \emph{It is not by itself evidence that a given act executed it}, and
attribution of particular actions to a particular document remains an inference, contested, and not
settled by the document's existence. The account asserts the first and declines the second.

\emph{And there is a technical consequence worth more than the attributional one.} A Seldon manifold is
built from a frame's admissible intervention sequences together with its represented boundary
conditions. \emph{An intervention family is otherwise observable only through outcomes}, which
Remark~\ref{rem:chollet} says underdetermine it --- so a published objective supplies directly what that
remark holds cannot be recovered indirectly. \emph{Publication is cheap evidence about the family of
interventions}, which is half of what a manifold is made from.

\emph{Which adds an input to Remark~\ref{rem:interval} that is already in hand.} The readable share of
competence remains unmeasured and expensive. \emph{A stated intention costs nothing to read} and
constrains the same construction, and an actor who publishes objectives has narrowed their own
projected tube on behalf of anyone who bothers to take the statement seriously.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The cheapest intervention is the announcement]\label{rem:announce}
The previous remark took a published objective as evidence \emph{about} a manifold. \emph{The sharper
reading is that it is an intervention \emph{on} manifolds}, and on the record above it did more work than
any of the acts.

\emph{The companion's definition makes this available rather than surprising.} A Seldon manifold is
assembled from a frame's \emph{represented} family of admissible interventions and its \emph{represented}
future boundary conditions --- representations held by a frame, not facts about a world. \emph{So
altering what a frame represents alters its manifold directly}, and the theory companion already prices
that operation: revocation occurs at $\Delta g = 0$, nothing in the contained state being required to
change. \emph{Manifold manipulation is that operation applied to somebody else's representation.}

\emph{Which is why a plan can work by being read.} An actor inside a named agency, on publication, holds
a different represented future: careers, projects, hires and any commitment with a horizon are
re-evaluated against a stated intention that was not there before. Departures follow. \emph{And
departures are the channel Remark~\ref{rem:lapse} found carries the amplitude} --- so the publication
produces the channel, and the channel does the work.

\emph{That places a new entry above attrition in the ordering, and it is the cheapest yet.} Appropriations
need a contested vote, reconciliation a majority, revocation an executive act, attrition nothing at all
--- and \emph{publication needs nothing and precedes all of them}, being upstream of the channel that
carries the most.

\emph{Two limits, both real.} Much is not all: appropriations were made and executive acts taken, and
those are direct. And \emph{the causal link is an inference rather than a measurement} --- departures
have many causes, and nothing here isolates one. The mechanism is available, cheap, and consistent with
the record; it is not thereby demonstrated, and the same invariance as before applies, since a published
plan alters represented futures whether or not that was its purpose.

\emph{One structural note, stated as a property and not as a method.} If behaviour follows a represented
reachable set, then \emph{the representation is an attack surface}: altering it alters conduct without
touching the agent, at no cost in the world. That is a general property of anything built the way this
programme describes, \emph{including what this programme builds}, and it is recorded here rather than
left for someone else to notice.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Delegation moves the acts into another family]\label{rem:delegate}
Remark~\ref{rem:announce} alters what other frames represent, and they act. \emph{A second construction
alters whose intervention family the acts belong to}, and it is distinct enough to state separately.

\emph{Appoint an agent and a goal, and specify neither the acts nor the manner.} The acts then occur in
the agent's family rather than the principal's, who never enters the record for any particular one. That
is Remark~\ref{rem:building} at another grain --- a system that builds an arrangement is not scored for
what the arrangement subsequently does, and here the arrangement is a person with a remit.

\emph{Three separations follow and they are cumulative.} No specification exists, so no instruction can
be produced. The proximate cause is the agent's own enthusiasm, which is genuine. And where damage is
consistent with inexperience, \emph{incompetence supplies an alternative explanation requiring no
intent} --- so an agent who would plausibly cause that damage by accident is, for this purpose, more
serviceable than a capable one. \emph{Variance is the feature}: an outcome following predictably from an
instruction would establish the link the construction exists to sever.

\emph{The standing limits matter more here than anywhere else in this section.} That a mechanism is
available does not establish its use. An agent may be enthusiastic and inexperienced with nothing
arranged behind them, and by Remark~\ref{rem:chollet} the two readings leave the same record.
\emph{This account asserts that the construction is available, cheap and effective, and declines to say
of any instance that it was used.} It also names no one: the subject here is a mechanism, and a document
that named participants would be a different kind of document with different obligations.

\emph{What survives either reading is the reachability consequence}, which is the only quantity this
section computes. \emph{Capacity leaves at the same rate whether its removal was delegated or merely
permitted}, and the manifold does not distinguish the two.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Narrative copies and Narrativium does not, so the thing outgrows its author]\label{rem:propagate}
The causal question has been declined three times above on evidential grounds. \emph{There is a
structural reason as well, and it is stronger than the evidential one.}

\emph{A narrative copies; Narrativium does not.} By Remark~\ref{rem:schedule} what is addressable is
drawn on by every instance receiving it at negligible cost, while reachable deformation is frame-bound
and must be paid locally, in each frame, out of that frame's own resources. A story is of the first
kind. Capacity is of the second. \emph{So the reach of a narrative and the Narrativium of whoever
authored it are independent quantities, and nothing bounds the first by the second.}

\emph{Which is why a propagated thing routinely exceeds its originator, structurally rather than
contingently.} The originator holds one frame with a bounded reachable set; the propagated narrative is
held in many, each of which produces deformation from its own stock. There is no cross-frame accounting
that nets any of it back.

\emph{And that recasts the previous two remarks rather than repeating them.} Attribution is not merely
severed --- \emph{it is the wrong relation}. The deformation was produced by other frames out of their
own capacity, and was never the originator's to own in the first place.

\emph{Coordination is not thereby denied, and the distinction is worth keeping sharp.} It plainly exists
and is often documented. \emph{It is neither necessary nor sufficient}: not necessary, because a
narrative propagates unaided once held; not sufficient, because the deformation is paid in frames the
coordinator does not hold. Both halves fail, and for different reasons.

\emph{So the practical consequences do not run through the author.} Removing an originator does not
remove a narrative already copied into every frame that read it, and \emph{establishing who began
something changes no reachable set} --- which is not merely the invariance asserted throughout this
section but the reason for it. \emph{The damage is done} means precisely that a reachability set has
already changed, and nothing in the account of how it changed reaches back into it.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Load goes where it is cheapest to place, not where it is best borne]\label{rem:load}
One object shows all three buffer kinds this section has distinguished, and shows which of them absorbs
the load.

An American carrier deployed in November 2025 for an expected seven months was extended twice and has
passed two hundred days without a port call, into a ninth month at sea; another returned in May after
326 days, the longest such deployment since the Vietnam era. Reported conditions include failing
plumbing, water restrictions and contamination, mould, exhausted consumables, irregular resupply, and
maintenance deferred to the point where arresting gear frays and repairs at sea are described as
temporary \citep{navy2026}.

\emph{Three buffers, of the three kinds already separated.} Consumables are rate-bounded, and here it is
the resupply rate rather than the stock that fails. Deferred maintenance is precisely
Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere}'s accommodation --- a gap covered by postponement rather than by revoking the
deployment --- and it compounds, since a cable left fraying costs more later than now. \emph{And crew
endurance is the third kind}: bounded by Remark~\ref{rem:individual} and, by Remark~\ref{rem:exchange},
admitting no conversion at any price. Rested people cannot be bought.

\emph{Which is the finding, and it inverts the comfortable reading.} Extending a deployment requires no
appropriation, no vote and no statutory change; it requires \emph{not} doing something. By
Remark~\ref{rem:lapse} the channels that require least deliberation carry the most, so the load flows to
the buffer that is cheapest to draw on --- \emph{and that is not the buffer best able to bear it}. One
would expect load to find slack. It finds low friction instead, and when those diverge the arrangement
loads its least replenishable component.

\emph{Worse, that component does not merely deplete.} Drawing on crew past its limits converts into
departure, and by Remark~\ref{rem:narrative} departure selects on outside options. \emph{So the buffer
money cannot refill is drawn down in the manner that permanently shrinks it}, at the career-timescale
rate rather than the deployment one. Two ships are not a fleet, and nothing here claims otherwise; the
mechanism is what generalises, not the sample.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A decision placed outside every checking frame cannot be corrected inside one]\label{rem:pool}
Every case above left attribution open, correctly, since outcomes underdetermine it. \emph{One small
object did not}, and calling its failure a failure of capability understates it.

The renovation of a memorial pool was let as a no-bid award of \$13.1 million, later \$14.6 million, to a
firm with no previous federal construction experience which had, by the awarding principal's own earlier
account, worked on his private swimming pool; a second no-bid award for water treatment followed. The
account later changed to not knowing the contractor. \emph{And the technical cause was warned of in
advance}: an agency engineer reported the floor peeling because of the process and materials, and that
warning is documented in the court filings. Damage was nonetheless attributed publicly to vandals and
three people were charged; the charges were dropped against all of them, the prosecuting office pointing
to a botched installation \citep{pool2026}.

\emph{So the frames that would have caught this were not degraded. They were bypassed.} Competitive
tender, technical review, relevant experience and independence of the awarding party are four checks, and
the award passed through none of them. \emph{The knowledge existed and was not in the deciding frame},
which is worse than the depletion of Remark~\ref{rem:narrative} rather than an instance of it --- absent
expertise and routed-around expertise produce identical outcomes, and this case shows which occurred only
because the warning survives in a record.

\emph{Which explains the response, and Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} explains it exactly.} A decision that no
frame supplies has deleted its own cheap exit. There was no procurement finding to revise, no technical
review to reverse, no frame whose revocation would have cost $\Delta g=0$ --- \emph{so correction inside
a frame was unavailable, and assertion was the only move left}. The vandalism claim is not opportunism.
It is what remains when a decision has been placed beyond the reach of the machinery that would
otherwise repair it, and the later change of account is Remark~\ref{rem:delegate}'s severing performed
afterwards rather than designed in.

\emph{And the managing frame already held the account.} The engineer's warning was correct, was made in
time, and was inside the system before any charge was brought. \emph{Correct information, held by the
right body, changed nothing} --- which is the theory companion's fourth cost seen from the other side: a
write paid in full that lands where nothing reaches it, silently, leaving the graph with a false model of
itself in which something was changed that was not.

\emph{So ask what the bypass cost, and the answer is the finding.} Charges were dropped --- and that is
the whole of it. No procurement consequence, no reversal of the award, no restoration of the four checks
that were passed over, no price attached to passing them over. \emph{The evidentiary frame declined to
punish and could not correct}, having authority over its own output and no reach into the arrangement
that produced the case. That is Proposition~\ref{prop:no-prior-free}'s neighbour in the companion on
Narrativium: a determination in one frame does not become deformation in another without an instantiated
conversion, and none existed.

\emph{Which retracts what was said here twice.} Calling this checkability encouraging converted a
determination in the judicial frame into expected change in the administrative one, with no conversion
between them --- \emph{the exact error Remark~\ref{rem:exchange} names}, committed two paragraphs after
naming it. \emph{Checkability without consequence is documentation, not constraint.} A frame that
establishes the truth and alters nothing has recorded, not constrained.

\emph{And the consequence is worse than a basin.} Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere} has accommodations
accumulating, exit cost rising, and a trap forming. \emph{An accommodation that costs nothing does not
accumulate}, so nothing hardens and nothing is deferred --- \emph{the bypass is simply repeatable at the
same price, which is zero}. That is not a basin the arrangement is sinking into. It is the arrangement
having no gradient at all in that direction, which is the condition under which the cheap exit and the
expensive one become the same thing, and choosing stops mattering.\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The same event against an earlier frame, which is a measurement device]\label{rem:counterfactual}
Ask what the preceding case would have produced twenty years earlier. \emph{That question is not
rhetorical; it is an instrument.} Fix the event and vary the frame state, and the difference between the
two expected outcomes \emph{is} the change in the reachable set --- the quantity
Remark~\ref{rem:interval} says the interval is taken over, obtained by looking back rather than by
projecting forward, which is the easier direction.

\emph{What the earlier frame would have engaged is nameable and none of it is judicial.} A no-bid award
to a firm without relevant experience, connected to the awarding party, with a documented technical
warning disregarded, is squarely the business of an inspector general and of an audit office; of
committee oversight holding subpoena; of procurement remedy in the form of termination, suspension and
debarment; of an investigative press cycle lasting longer than a week; and, on the record of that
period, of somebody resigning.

\emph{So the loss localises, which is worth more than the total.} The judicial frame did what a judicial
frame does and did it against a documented bypass. \emph{The oversight frames did not act.} The
contraction is therefore not uniform: one dimension of the reachable set held, and several did not, and
naming which is a sharper result than observing that things are worse.

\emph{A counterfactual is a judgement and not a measurement, and is recorded here as one.} The selection
worry that would ordinarily accompany it, however, \emph{runs the other way}, and stating it as a
weakness would use the wrong reference class. Earlier failures that went nowhere went nowhere largely
because they were quiet. \emph{The comparison class here is not procurement failures but maximally
public ones}, and in that class the historical rate of activation was high, because publicity was
precisely what activated the channels.

\emph{So this is closer to a natural experiment than to a judgement, with the input pinned at its
maximum.} Nothing about attention needs controlling for: every step was in the open --- the attribution,
the changed account, named defendants, the collapse of the cases, and the engineer's warning sitting in
the filings. \emph{Publicity was itself a conversion}, in Remark~\ref{rem:exchange}'s sense: the
instantiated relation by which narrative became deformation in the administrative frame, carried by
press and by hearings. Held at maximum it produced no deformation. \emph{The rate has not fallen. On
this instance it is indistinguishable from zero}, and the case bounds it.

\emph{And the same channel is fully operative in the other direction, which is the finding.}
Remark~\ref{rem:announce} has publication moving manifolds and producing the attrition --- cheaply, at
scale, with nothing decided. Here publication moved nothing. \emph{One mechanism, opposite directions,
rates differing by everything}, and the asymmetry is not in the publicity but in what receives it. An
announced intention is received by individuals revising their own futures alone, which is the
low-deliberation channel Remark~\ref{rem:lapse} finds carries the most. An exposed failure requires a
collective response through referrals and hearings, which is the high-deliberation channel that carries
the least. \emph{So the same publicity converts efficiently into damage and inefficiently into repair},
by the same ordering, and that is the bleakest thing in this section and among the better derived.

\emph{Which is why the honest output is a specified study rather than a verdict.} The quantity is the
rate at which oversight channels activate per comparable event, then against now, and its components are
published: referrals, hearings held, contracts terminated, suspensions and debarments entered. \emph{This
account measures none of them and names all of them}, which is the same move it has made everywhere
else --- an intuition converted into something someone can go and check.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Nothing here was surprising, which is why nothing adapted]\label{rem:surprise}
Return to the premise underneath all of it. \emph{This programme adapts on residual} --- prediction
against observation, with Section~\ref{sec:ladder} minting only once what is held will not reach the
case. Surprise is the trigger. Without it the ladder does not run.

\emph{And there was almost no surprise in any of this.} The engineer predicted the peeling and said so
in time. The debt trajectory is published a decade ahead. The interceptor drawdown against a production
rate is arithmetic. The workforce reduction was announced before it happened, and the objectives were
printed and bound. \emph{Every outcome above was available in advance to anyone holding the relevant
model}, and mostly to anyone reading.

\emph{No residual, no mint.} The system acquired nothing from any of it --- not through failing to learn,
but because there was nothing to learn: the correct model was already held. \emph{So the failure was
never epistemic.} The prediction was right and on file. What failed is the coupling between holding a
prediction and acting on it, and no amount of better prediction repairs that.

\emph{Which explains the section rather than merely summarising it.} \emph{A system that adapts on
surprise is structurally blind to predicted decline}, because a decline that arrives on schedule
generates no signal by arriving. Slow, forecast, arithmetic harm is invisible to the very mechanism that
makes such a system efficient elsewhere.

\emph{And it is correspondingly responsive to asserted novelty}, which closes the previous remarks. A
claim of vandalism was surprising; a botched installation was predicted. The apparatus moved on the
surprising falsehood and not on the unsurprising truth --- \emph{which is not irrationality but exactly
what a residual-driven architecture does}. So asserting a surprising cause is more than cheap narrative:
\emph{in a system that adapts on residual it is the only move that triggers a response at all}.

\emph{Stated as a regime and not a condemnation}, in the manner of Remark~\ref{rem:plural}. Adapting on
surprise is correct nearly everywhere and is what makes the ladder cheap. \emph{The pathology belongs to
one regime} --- harm that is gradual and foreseen --- and in that regime the mechanism's virtue is
precisely its defect.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[One case carries the mechanism, and that is a licence with a sharp limit]\label{rem:anecdote}
\emph{The small case contains every element of the section}, which is worth stating because it looks
like the error it is not.

Present in that one object: a decision placed outside every checking frame, so that no revocation was
available to correct it; correct knowledge held by the managing body and inert; a failure that surprised
nobody and therefore minted nothing; assertion substituted where repair required a buffer that was
absent; the response landing where friction was least, on named individuals; an account changed
afterwards, severing attribution; the single frame that compels evidence resolving what observation could
not; that resolution converting into no change elsewhere for want of any instantiated conversion;
publicity at its maximum returning nothing; and a bypass that cost so little it left no accumulation
behind it. \emph{That is not a fragment of the mechanism. It is the whole chain.}

\emph{Which licenses extrapolation of shape and forbids extrapolation of size.} By
Remark~\ref{rem:weight} the conclusions here are shapes, and a single instance exhibiting a complete
mechanism establishes that the mechanism exists and how its parts connect. \emph{It establishes nothing
whatever about how often, or how much.} One case is sufficient for structure and useless for rate, and
conflating those is the error this looks like from a distance.

\emph{So the section's evidence divides cleanly and neither half substitutes for the other.} The
aggregates --- debt, stocks, staffing, appropriations --- carry prevalence and magnitude. \emph{The small
case carries the mechanism}, and carries it better precisely because it is small: a budget cannot be
traced end to end, while a pool liner can, warning and filings included.

\emph{And the wider surprise was as low as the local one, which is the worse finding.} Few observers
found any of it unexpected. \emph{So the correct model was held by the managing frame, inertly, and also
by everyone watching, inertly} --- Remark~\ref{rem:surprise} one level up, where holding the right
account at every level and coupling it to action at none is the condition being described.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The frog, taken literally]\label{rem:frog}
The shape has a familiar name, and \emph{the story as usually told is false in a way that matters},
because the received version locates the failure in the wrong term.

\emph{What the original experiments found is the opposite of the moral drawn from them.}
\citet{goltz1869} reports that an intact frog in slowly heated water shows unease at about
twenty-five degrees, grows steadily more agitated as the heat rises, struggles unavailingly to get out,
and dies near forty-two in rigid tetanus. \emph{It notices. It escalates. It dies anyway.} The animal
that fails to react is the one whose brain has been removed, left with reflex alone, which a
sufficiently gradual rise does not trigger.

\emph{So the two preparations are the two failure modes this section has separated}, and there is
evidence here of both. Perception removed --- checks bypassed, measures withdrawn, the account no longer
held --- is the second frog. \emph{Perception intact and no exit available is the first}, and it is the
one this section mostly documents: an engineer who warned at the right threshold, a court that
determined correctly, publicity at its maximum. \emph{Correct, escalating, and not an exit.}
\emph{Struggling is not the same as being able to get out}, which is the whole of Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere}
in a sentence.

\emph{The popular telling describes neither.} It has an animal that does not notice and therefore does
not struggle --- the pithed one --- while purporting to describe the intact one, and so attributes to
inattention what the experiment attributes to being unable to leave.

\emph{And the prescriptive error follows from the descriptive one, which is why this is worth more than
a correction.} A failure located in perception prescribes awareness: more warning, better monitoring,
wider publicity. \emph{Remark~\ref{rem:counterfactual} measured that conversion at approximately zero.}
So the received story recommends precisely the intervention this section found inert. \emph{A wrong model
of a failure mode aims the remedy at the wrong term}, and that is the general statement, of which the
frog is merely the memorable instance.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A propped arrangement buys the absence of its own signal]\label{rem:prop}
Two corrections to the obvious reading, the second of which explains why a foreseen transition still
arrives as a shock.

\emph{Recovery is possible and bounded, and the bound is already stated.} Concentrating authority does
restore reachable deformation at a centre, and nothing above denies it. \emph{It restores it only in the
frames authority reaches.} By Remark~\ref{rem:exchange} there is no conversion into individual capacity
at any price, the rate into expertise is a career, and the rate into materiel is a production line ---
so recovery at the top does not reach the buffers whose depletion this section has been tracking, and a
centre may become more able to act while the arrangement becomes less able to do anything.

\emph{And an arrangement held up from outside has its residual suppressed, which is purchased rather
than natural.} A party with an interest in continuity absorbs deformation that would otherwise appear as
signal. \emph{So the quiet is bought.} Remark~\ref{rem:surprise} found no residual and therefore no
adaptation; here there is residual and someone is paying to keep it from surfacing, which looks
identical from inside and is not the same condition at all.

\emph{Sustaining is a threshold and not a gradient}, which supplies the mechanism the previous remarks
lacked. Support continues while it costs less than the alternative and stops when it does not, and a
comparison of two costs flips discretely. \emph{So a continuous decline, read through a threshold-valued
decision, produces a discontinuous observable.} Nothing in the world jumps. The response jumps, and the
response is what is seen.

\emph{Which inverts the comfortable reading.} Apparent suddenness is proportional to how well the signal
was suppressed, so \emph{stability maintained by external support predicts a fast transition rather than
a slow one}. And \emph{the timing belongs to the sustainer}: support ends when the sustainer's balance
turns, not the declining party's, so a declining arrangement does not control the timing of its own
phase change.

\emph{Stated generally and about nobody.} Which parties are sustaining which arrangements is inference
from actions, and by Remark~\ref{rem:chollet} consistency with a model is not identification of one.
\emph{The result holds of any propped arrangement}, including anything this programme builds, and it
supplies no date.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A statement arriving after the previous remark, which tests it]\label{rem:latest}
\emph{The preceding remark was written against a record that has since moved}, and the movement is worth
recording because it exercises the caution rather than the conclusion.

The memorandum noted above as having collapsed over interpretation and management of the waterway is now
being defended publicly by Iran's president as a source of national pride, with nearly all members of
that state's security council said to have supported it, against widening criticism from conservative
figures \citep{iran2026}.

\emph{Three readings produce that same observable}, and the account can separate none of them: gains
being banked before a window closes; an assessment that the decline in question will not continue;
domestic necessity owing nothing to any assessment of anybody else. By Remark~\ref{rem:chollet}
consistency with a model is not identification of one --- \emph{which is the hedge the previous remark
carried, tested by events within a day of being written and holding}.

\emph{Both sides of that dispute are running Remark~\ref{rem:announce}'s operation.} Asserting a
council's near-unanimity in public is not a report of a manifold but an intervention on one, aimed at
domestic actors whose represented futures it is meant to narrow.

\emph{And a split conducted in the open is suppression failing rather than holding} --- a residual
surfacing where it had been contained. \emph{But it is that arrangement's residual, in that arrangement's
frame.} Nothing licenses netting it against the capacity of another: the Narrativium companion forbids
the cross-frame sum, and reading an elite split in one state as evidence about institutional capacity in
another would be precisely the error this paper spends its length refusing. \emph{The newest fact
available is not thereby a fact about the question at hand}, and preferring it because it is newest
would be its own mistake.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A fixed term is a published boundary condition, and can be waited out]\label{rem:wait}
The rationale has been attributed variously --- to a parliamentary adviser as a wait until January 2029,
and by the foreign minister in the narrower form of no resumption while violations of the June interim
agreement continue \citep{iran2026}. \emph{Which of them said it does not matter}, and dwelling on the
attribution would be careful about the wrong object. \emph{The behaviour is the datum}: talks available
are not being held.

\emph{Declining to transact when transaction is on offer is a revealed preference}, and the calculation
is recoverable from the choice without anybody's assistance in stating it. Later is being valued above
now. Two things would make that correct, and neither requires a spokesman: \emph{that the counterparty's
capacity continues to fall}, so what is obtainable later is greater or cheaper; and \emph{that a later
counterparty might be able to deliver what it agrees to}.

\emph{Declining to negotiate withholds a conversion.} Negotiation is the instantiated relation by which
pressure becomes settlement, and refusing it produces no deformation now, deliberately. That is a choice
about Remark~\ref{rem:exchange}'s $\kappa$, not an absence of one.

\emph{Announcing the refusal is Remark~\ref{rem:announce}'s operation aimed at an entire negotiating
position.} Declaring a counterparty unable to conclude anything changes what every \emph{other} actor
represents as reachable through them --- so it devalues every negotiation that counterparty is running,
globally, at no cost. \emph{The cheapest attack on a negotiating position is not defeating it but stating
publicly that it is not worth transacting with.}

\emph{And a fixed term is the most reliable published boundary condition available anywhere.} A Seldon
manifold is built from represented future boundary conditions, and those ordinarily have to be inferred
from behaviour, which Remark~\ref{rem:chollet} says underdetermines them. \emph{A term limit is
constitutional and dated}, so waiting stops being hopeful and becomes computable: the wait has a length,
and everyone can read it.

\emph{The reflexive cost is general and belongs to no particular party.} Any time-limited actor has
supplied that input to every counterparty and cannot withdraw it, so \emph{the asymmetry between
term-limited and unlimited actors is published rather than inferred}, and it is structural rather than a
matter of skill.

\emph{Which suggests the refusal is a solvency assessment rather than a snub, and that is the better
reading.} \emph{A commitment is a promise of future deformation}, so an agreement is worth what the
counterparty's capacity to perform is worth. Where narrative exceeds Narrativium the promise is not
backed --- and by Remark~\ref{rem:lapse} the deliberative channels that would make a commitment binding
are the ones carrying least. \emph{An actor in that condition cannot be contracted with in good faith or
bad}, since the obstacle is not intent.

\emph{It also unifies the two versions rather than leaving them apart.} An agreement signed and then
violated is direct evidence of inability to perform, so the conditional refusal and the term-length one
are a single assessment held at different confidence.

\emph{And it names a consequence of the gap this section has been measuring that was not yet stated.} An
arrangement whose narrative outruns its Narrativium does not merely fail internally. \emph{It ceases to
be transactable}, progressively and independently of anyone's intentions, because counterparties price
commitments by capacity to perform and that capacity is what has gone. \emph{Losing the ability to make
agreements is a cost of the gap, not a separate misfortune.}

\emph{Waiting is not free either}, which keeps this from being a dominant strategy. The waiting party
depletes across the same interval, so the position is a bet that the other depletes faster --- which is
Remark~\ref{rem:race}'s comparison run between two parties instead of within one.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The promise is quoted continuously, and the quote is not narrative]\label{rem:bonds}
\emph{The preceding argument is not an analogy for a bond. A bond is the object.} It is a promise of
future deformation, priced by counterparties with money at risk, and the price is published every day.

\emph{Yield is what the promise fetches; term premium is the price of doubt about capacity further out},
which is precisely the quantity Remark~\ref{rem:wait} says a counterparty assesses. In August 2026 a
thirty-year auction cleared at 5.216 per cent, the most paid for such debt since 2001, and the
thirty-year yield reached 5.311, its highest since 2007 \citep{bonds2026}. \emph{The decomposition is
what matters}, and strategists quoted at the time attributed the move to term premium rather than to
expectations of policy --- a distinction that decides whether the series says anything here at all,
since a policy-driven rise would say nothing about capacity. Term-premium estimates are model-dependent
and contested, and that is a real qualification rather than a formality.

\emph{Which makes this the one measure in the section that is not narrative.} Every other assessment
canvassed above is somebody talking. \emph{This is people paying to hold a view}, continuously, in
public, and being wrong about it costs them. It is Narrativium, quoted.

\emph{The two clocks appear directly in it.} Short promises price close to unremarkably and long ones
price worse, which is near-term capacity accepted and far-term capacity doubted --- Remark~\ref{rem:timing}'s
structure read off a curve rather than argued for.

\emph{And the nominal-against-real distinction is the narrative-against-Narrativium distinction exactly.}
A sovereign borrowing in its own currency always keeps the nominal promise, because keeping it costs
nothing --- \emph{that is the narrative, and it is never broken}. Whether it keeps the real promise is
capacity, and the market prices the two separately, so \emph{the gap between them is quoted rather than
inferred}.

\emph{Two facts are kept apart that are easily merged.} Foreign holdings fell over the period, and
indirect bidding at that particular auction was strong. Those are different measurements of different
things, and one auction is not a trend.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Nothing above requires believing anyone's account of their own conduct]\label{rem:testimony}
\emph{The section rests on no testimony}, which is worth auditing rather than asserting, since it is the
property that decides how it can be attacked.

\emph{Every load-bearing input is one of five kinds.} Published measurements: projections, debt stocks,
price-to-income series, interceptor counts, staffing counts, democracy indices. The existence of
documents, as distinct from anyone's account of them. Procedural records: what passed, through which
channel, on what margin, and what a filing contained. Contract terms: whether a tender was competitive,
its amount, its awardee. Prices: what an auction cleared at. \emph{And observed acts}: talks not held, a
deployment extended, charges dropped. \emph{Not one of them is a person's account of what they did.}

\emph{Where a self-account does appear it is used as an event and not as evidence.} That an explanation
changed is a fact about the record; neither version of it is relied upon. The published objectives are
treated as a document that exists, and Remark~\ref{rem:delegate} explicitly declines to attribute any
particular act to them. The rationale for a refusal was demoted precisely because the behaviour, not the
reason given for it, carries the weight.

\emph{And this is forced by the account's own results rather than chosen out of fastidiousness}, on
three independent grounds.

\emph{The first is strategic and is the familiar one.} By Remark~\ref{rem:announce} a public statement is
an intervention on manifolds rather than a report of one, so an account of one's own conduct is an act
aimed at whoever receives it.

\emph{The second is not strategic at all, and it is the one usually missed.} \emph{A self-model is a
model and can be wrong.} The theory companion's fourth cost is exactly this: a graph that has spent
without effect acquires a false model of itself, in which something was changed that was not. An
account of oneself is read off that model, so its accuracy is the coherence between the model and what
it models, which this programme treats as a variable and not a constant. \emph{So sincerity is no
repair.} The usual answer to ``they may be lying'' is to establish good faith, and good faith leaves this
untouched.

\emph{The third is constitutive and makes the preceding paragraph's phrasing too weak.}
\emph{Communication is the deformation of a receiver's manifold} --- that is not a side effect of
communicating but its success condition, since an utterance that changes nothing held has communicated
nothing. \emph{So there is no unaimed variety.} Testimony is not a corrupted instance of an otherwise
clean channel; \emph{there is no clean channel}, and that includes honest speech, and it includes this
paper, which is aimed at its reader and says so.

\emph{Which improves the criterion rather than merely defending it.} The axis is not honest against
dishonest but \emph{aimed against unaimed}. A price, a count, a filing, a contract term and an act exist
for reasons other than reaching you, and can therefore be \emph{observed} rather than received.
\emph{The rule generalises past this section: prefer inputs produced for reasons other than your reading
of them}, and treat everything produced in order to be read as an intervention first and a description
second, whatever its author intended.

\emph{The consequence for attack is narrow and public.} An argument resting on testimony falls to a
recantation or a rival witness. \emph{This one falls only if the counts, filings, prices and procedural
records are wrong}, and all of them are checkable by anyone.

\emph{The same property is the limit.} \emph{Intent cannot be established this way and is nowhere
claimed} --- not from caution, as it may have read, but because the section holds no instrument capable
of it.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The author's preferences, and why they are not load-bearing]\label{rem:bias}
An author holding views about the subject may wish to state which way they run, and the temptation is to
offer the direction as reassurance --- that the conclusion was reached against the preference rather
than toward it. \emph{The preceding remark forbids accepting that}, and it forbids it here as much as
anywhere: a paper cannot admit its own author's account of himself while refusing everyone else's, and
the three grounds apply to him unchanged, the second most of all.

\emph{It does not need to be accepted, which is the actual reassurance.} The section was built to be
independent of testimony, and the author's is testimony. \emph{A conclusion that would change if the
author's preferences were different would be a conclusion resting on the author}; this one does not, and
that is checkable rather than promised --- Remark~\ref{rem:direction}'s condition applied to motive
instead of to posits.

\emph{But the genuine exposure is selection, and the kind of an input does nothing about it.} A false
picture can be assembled entirely out of true prices, true counts and true filings by choosing which
ones to assemble. \emph{That is where bias actually enters}, and no amount of care about admissible
kinds excludes it.

\emph{And this section is exposed there specifically, which is recorded because it is so.} The cases
examined above arrived as prompts and were followed up; \emph{no protocol enumerated the space of
comparable cases and sampled it}, and no systematic search for counter-instances was run. A reader's
strongest attack is therefore not that the author wanted a conclusion but that these were the cases
looked at.

\emph{Which means \emph{undeniable} overstates it.} By Remark~\ref{rem:weight} the shape is deniable by
attacking any of three structural steps, and it is deniable by attacking the selection just admitted.
\emph{What it is not is deniable by attacking the author} --- a narrower claim, and the only one a
declaration of interest can actually secure.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The opposition, put as strongly as it can be put]\label{rem:steelman}
\emph{One class of objection can be dismissed and should be named so that it is not mistaken for the
rest.} A short window read against a long trend is an aperture error: a single quarter's improvement set
beside a movement of an order of magnitude over a decade is not a reversal, and arguments of that shape
are artefacts of the window chosen. \emph{Four objections are not of that kind.}

\emph{The first is the reference class, and it is the strongest.} Every argument for structural decline
ever made has had this evidence structure --- real measurements, real institutional failures, a coherent
mechanism --- and most of them were wrong. \emph{The inputs were checkable in those cases too.} And it
sharpens: if the shape survives error in every magnitude, as Remark~\ref{rem:weight} claims, then what
observation would have counted against it? \emph{An account robust to all magnitudes may be
unfalsifiable in practice}, and that charge is not answered here.

\emph{The second is that the instrument sees depletion and is nearly blind to creation.} Counting what
has left, what has narrowed and what has been drawn down cannot register new capability, substitution or
institutions that did not previously exist. \emph{The section's own citation cuts this way}: competence
migrating into readable form is, by Remark~\ref{rem:schedule}, the largest capability gain the argument
contains --- paid once, copied freely --- and it is read here only as a risk.

\emph{The third is the stock against the flow, and it uses this paper's own rule.} The dollar held about
57 per cent of allocated reserves in early 2026, with depth and liquidity that are market structure
rather than narrative \citep{cofer2026}. \emph{Every counterparty, aggregated, with money at risk} is by
Remark~\ref{rem:testimony}'s own criterion better evidence than any single actor declining to transact.

\emph{The fourth is that the mechanism may be cyclical rather than terminal.} Comparable or worse
institutional dysfunction has occurred repeatedly and been recovered from, and nothing above
distinguishes an oscillation from a trend.

\emph{The second objection fails, and it fails on this account's own machinery rather than by rebuttal.}
Remark~\ref{rem:phase} defines a phase change as a change in the dimension set --- \emph{which is
direction-neutral}. Additions are changes in it exactly as removals are, so a capability gain is not a
counterweight to the phase change but an instance of it. That remark says as much already: new
dimensions \emph{add} distinguishable reachable states, so a projection over fixed dimensions understates
the change. \emph{Gains therefore make the projected change larger, and the objection had the sign
inverted.}

\emph{Change is risk without regard to direction}, which is the priors argument applied to novelty. A
held set is matched to the frame in which it was acquired, and the theory companion's well-formedness is
explicitly relative to the work being done --- so a gain de-matches everything holding priors fitted to
the world before it, and the de-matching does not care which way the change ran.

\emph{And gains and losses cannot be netted, which is where the objection actually goes wrong.} The same
event --- competence becoming copyable --- is a gain in the capability frame and a shock to every frame
that priced the scarcity, which is Remark~\ref{rem:pricing}. Opposite signs, one event, and no
instantiated conversion between the frames. \emph{So summing them is the cross-frame sum the Narrativium
companion forbids}, and the objection smuggled one in --- the same error caught three times already
above.

\emph{What keeps this from confirming everything is a rate condition, and it is observable.} A gain
propagating at copy speed into frames that absorb at acquisition speed widens the distance
Remark~\ref{rem:timing} measures; \emph{a gain absorbed at the frame's own speed does not}. The
comparison of the two rates decides it, not the sign of the change --- so not every capability gain
counts for this argument, and which ones do is a question with an answer.

\emph{The first objection was stated at the wrong grain, and stating it that way repeated an error this
section had already corrected once.} Its reference class was civilisational decline predictions. Three
things are wrong with that class.

\emph{The base rate of the event is not low; over long horizons it approaches one.} Every arrangement
that has existed, excepting those extant, has changed phase. What has the poor record is \emph{timing},
and this account declines timing explicitly --- Remarks~\ref{rem:phase} and \ref{rem:timing} give a
reachability difference and two clocks and no date. \emph{So the failed predictions attack a claim not
made here.}

\emph{The grain is wrong in a second sense: the mechanism is scale-free.} Frames, channels ordered by
friction, and conversions between them apply to a firm, a department, a ship. \emph{At those grains
instances are plentiful and outcomes are checkable}, so the mechanism can be tested where evidence is
abundant instead of only where it is contested and singular.

\emph{And structural failure prediction is ordinary, professional and calibrated.} Credit assessment,
actuarial practice and reliability engineering forecast structural failure daily and are scored on it.
\emph{The claim that such prediction does not work is false as stated}; what is true is narrower, and
concerns singular events at civilisational scale over long horizons.

\emph{What survives is the unfalsifiability charge, which the objection conflated with the base rate and
which is a different complaint.} It is now partly answered: a rate condition distinguishes which
capability gains count, and a curve shape distinguishes preference from lock-in. \emph{But both were
produced under pressure rather than declared in advance}, and a retrofitted falsifier is worth less than
a pre-registered one --- which this programme states elsewhere and does not get to forget here.

\emph{There is a fourth thing wrong with that class, and it is this account's own mechanism turned on
the objection.} The class was populated by memory, and \emph{memory is residual-driven}: by
Remark~\ref{rem:surprise} what is encoded is what surprised. A prediction that came true and was
absorbed generated no residual and left no trace, which is why structural forecasting that works ---
credit, actuarial, engineering --- is recounted by nobody. \emph{The remembered sample is selected for
having been wrong, because being wrong is what made it memorable.}

\emph{And a correct warning that is acted upon erases its own evidence}, which is the sharper half. Such
a prediction is vindicated by the absence of the thing predicted --- and absence is exactly what a false
alarm also produces. \emph{The observational signature of an averted disaster is identical to that of a
groundless one}, so successful prediction is systematically misfiled as failed prediction, and the
better the response the more complete the misfiling.

\emph{So the base rate cannot be read off memory at all.} The claim is not that it is higher than
supposed but that \emph{memory is the wrong instrument}, biased in a direction that can be derived rather
than merely suspected, by two mechanisms working the same way.

\emph{Which collapses two residuals into one.} What the falsifiability charge needs is a falsifier
declared before the fact; what the base rate needs is a record of predictions scored after it. \emph{Both
are pre-registration}, so the promissory note above is a single note rather than two.

\emph{And it applies reflexively, uncomfortably.} If this account is right and anything is done in
consequence, \emph{it will be indistinguishable from having been wrong} --- by the mechanism just given.
The only defence is to register the commitments before they resolve, which is what the falsifiability
section exists for and is the reason it is not decorative.

\emph{And underneath the reference class was something plainer, which is worth naming because it is the
default position rather than an eccentric one.} \emph{The class was selected by discomfort.} A set
defined by alarming content was chosen, its poor record observed, and the fact that the alarm was the
selection criterion left unnoticed. The operative rule is \emph{the more uncomfortable a prediction, the
more likely it is to be wrong}, and it wears the costume of calibration.

\emph{It feels like calibration because it is a well-fitted prior applied outside its frame.} Where most
alarming claims are encountered --- advertising, solicitation, panic --- alarm and exaggeration genuinely
do correlate, so the heuristic earns its keep there. The theory companion's well-formedness is relative
to the work being done, and this prior is well-formed for that work and not for structural measurement.

\emph{Worse, it filters on the axis the two cases share.} Alarming and wrong is cheap and common;
alarming and right is rare and consequential; \emph{and the filter selects on alarming}, discarding the
second along with the first. It is not a weak instrument but one aimed at the wrong feature.

\emph{The deep form is that discomfort is a residual.} An unwelcome prediction is one that does not fit
what is held. \emph{So treating discomfort as evidence against the claim is treating a residual as
disconfirmation rather than as a trigger to update} --- the adaptation mechanism run backwards.

\emph{Which adds a failure mode to Remark~\ref{rem:surprise} rather than restating it.} That remark has
no signal generated and therefore no adaptation. This has \emph{signal generated and discarded because
it is signal}, and the two are independent.

\emph{And it is a third preparation the frog experiment does not contain.} Goltz has perception absent
and exit absent. The human case adds noticing, and concluding that the water is not hot because the
conclusion is unwelcome --- \emph{an update failure sitting between perception and exit}, available only
to a system whose self-model has a stake in the answer.

\emph{And the objection's form is social rather than epistemic, which three marks establish.} It is
\emph{content-independent}: the charge of alarmism applies identically to a true alarming claim and a
false one, whereas an epistemic objection engages a claim and this one engages a category. \emph{The cost
falls on the speaker rather than the claim}, so the sanction attaches to the act of voicing and not to
the proposition, which is the signature of a norm and not of an argument. \emph{And it is asymmetric}:
there is no comparable penalty for being wrongly reassuring, so the expected standing of an alarming
prediction is negative \emph{whatever its accuracy}. The rule need not be believed by anyone. It need
only be enforced.

\emph{Which compounds the selection already described.} One filter operates downstream, in memory,
keeping the alarming predictions that failed. \emph{This one operates upstream, at the point of voicing,
before any assessment is possible} --- so claims are removed from circulation before they can be checked,
and the surviving public record is selected against such predictions being right \emph{twice}.

\emph{In this account's terms it is Remark~\ref{rem:announce}'s operation aimed at a speaker.} A reliable
expectation of cost changes what the speaker represents as reachable, and it is the cheapest intervention
in the catalogue: no institution, no enforcement body, no decision. \emph{And it is
Remark~\ref{rem:lapse}'s ordering once more}, since nobody resolves to suppress anything --- the speaker
simply does not say it, and change again accumulates in the channel where nothing is decided, here
determining what enters the record at all.

\emph{So the path from an accurate model to no action has three stages, and each is sufficient alone.} A
decline arriving as predicted generates no residual. A residual that is generated may be discarded as
discomfort. \emph{And a residual neither absent nor discarded may simply never be uttered}, because
uttering it is priced. \emph{The account has now documented all three separately}, and they compose:
holding the right model, recognising it, and saying nothing are jointly consistent, and jointly
sufficient for nothing to happen.

\emph{And the small-scale test is named rather than run.} Nothing above examines a firm, a department or
a single institution at the grain where the mechanism could be checked cheaply and often. \emph{So the
answer to the first objection is at present a promissory note}, and it is recorded as one.

\emph{The third objection inverts as well, for a reason its author should have seen.} A reserve holding
is a \emph{conversion outward by the holder}: a frame turning its own reachable deformation into a claim
on another's. \emph{So the fifty-seven per cent is not a measurement of the dollar's capacity. It is a
record of what other frames have done with theirs} --- Remark~\ref{rem:prop}'s propping, itemised, with
the props bought and paid for by the propping parties. \emph{And the objection made this section's
recurring error once more}, reading a stock as a property of the thing held rather than of the holders'
allocation.

\emph{That reading must be falsifiable, since the first objection still stands}, so here is what
separates it. If holding reflects preference, the share should be stable or rising over long horizons.
If it reflects lock-in, the share should decline slowly and persistently while fast exit remains
self-harming, since selling destroys the value of what is still held. \emph{The two predict different
curves.}

\emph{The record shows the second.} From above seventy per cent in the late 1990s to about fifty-eight
now: slow, one-directional, and unaccompanied by any holder moving quickly \citep{cofer2026}. The recent
quarterly rise is small and about half valuation, which is not a reversal --- \emph{and on this reading
it is not even a counter-instance}, being a price effect on a position nobody can exit at speed.

\emph{Which places the holders in Remark~\ref{rem:nowhere}'s basin rather than the issuer alone.} Each
year's continued holding is an accommodation; the accumulated position raises the cost of leaving; and
\emph{what presents as confidence is a measurement of exit cost}. It also sharpens
Remark~\ref{rem:prop}: a sustainer sustains not from choice but because stopping is self-harming, and
the flip arrives when continuing costs more than the harm of leaving.

\emph{The rule that falls out is short.} \emph{Read the flow for preference and the stock for the cost
of exit.} Gold overtaking Treasuries in official reserves, valuation-driven though it was, is a
flow --- and it points toward an asset that is nobody's liability.

\emph{None of which repairs the omission.} This section had not cited the reserve position at all until
the objection was sought deliberately, which is Remark~\ref{rem:bias}'s selection exposure found
operating rather than merely conceded.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The voicing filter has a price, and releasing it is observable]\label{rem:release}
The preceding filter is priced, which means it can be measured by watching what appears when the price
falls. \emph{One case supplies a within-person control.}

A long-serving legislator announced he would not seek re-election and had earlier stepped down from his
leadership position; since then he has been among the very few of his party to vote against
administration nominees, doing so on several occasions and stating specific grounds
\citep{mcconnell2026}. \emph{What changed was the price of dissent, not the information available.} An
account on which such objections are epistemic predicts no change from that; \emph{behaviour tracking
price rather than evidence is what a filter predicts}, and it is what occurred.

\emph{What is not claimed is that he held the view earlier and withheld it.} That is a mental state, and
Remark~\ref{rem:testimony} excludes it whoever offers it. \emph{Only the coincidence is observable}: a
change in expressed position arriving with a change in what expressing it costs.

\emph{And this supplies the study owed above.} Exogenous falls in the cost of dissent --- retirement
announced, a term ending, a departure from office --- are dated, numerous and public, and voting records
are public too. \emph{So the filter's magnitude is measurable across an entire legislature}, at the small
grain where instances are plentiful, which is precisely the test named and not run. The obvious
confound is that retirement is not fully exogenous, since a position may become untenable or a departure
may be chosen in order to speak; comparing announcement dates against the timing of behaviour change is
what addresses it.

\emph{The magnitude in this instance is small, which is informative rather than awkward.} Releasing the
filter produced marginal dissent and not reversal.

\emph{And there is a corollary worth stating because it is unpleasant and follows directly.} The cost of
dissent falls as remaining tenure falls --- \emph{and so does influence}. The two decline together, so
\emph{dissent becomes affordable exactly as it becomes ineffective}, and a late objection is by
Remark~\ref{rem:lapse} lodged in the channel that carries least. \emph{A filter released only at the exit
is a filter that was never really released.}
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Competence accumulates over a lifetime, so it concentrates where it cannot be copied]\label{rem:actuarial}
The same case carries a second reading, and it concerns nobody in particular. \emph{Critical roles were
held through episodes of illness, and continuity depended on those episodes not being terminal.}

\emph{A person's held set is not addressable}, by Remark~\ref{rem:schedule}. So decades of accumulated
procedure, relationship and judgement in a critical role is real capacity with no copy operation
available for it, and the arrangement's continuity is contingent on a fact nobody chose and nobody
manages.

\emph{Nor is there a transition mechanism.} Departure occurs by death, by incapacity, or by choosing to
leave: none of the three is scheduled and only the last is chosen. \emph{So the timing of a capacity
loss in a critical role is set by biology}, which is Remark~\ref{rem:prop}'s finding that the timing
belongs to the sustainer, where here the sustainer is a body.

\emph{It also gives the crossing of Remark~\ref{rem:release} at the individual grain, and generally.} The
events that lower the price of speaking --- a retirement announced, an illness, a short remaining
horizon --- are the same events that lower the capacity to act on what is then said. \emph{The voice is
freed and weakened by one occurrence}, which is why the release is smaller than it looks and why nothing
about it should be read as reluctance.

\emph{And the concentration is not a defect, which is the part most easily got wrong.} Competence
accumulates over a lifetime because acquisition is per-lifetime, so it is \emph{necessarily} densest in
those with least of one remaining. \emph{The most competent are therefore the least copyable}, not by any
failing but because competence takes a lifetime and lifetimes end. \emph{The defect is the absent
transfer operation, not the demographics}, and treating it as the latter mistakes a missing mechanism
for a bad cohort.

\emph{Which is this whole section at the scale of one person.} The narrative names an institution. The
Narrativium is held, in part, inside individuals whose held sets have no copy path --- and an institution
in that position is one actuarial event away from discovering which parts of itself were never written
down.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A loss need not be terminal to be total, and a partial one registers nowhere]\label{rem:vacancy}
\emph{The preceding remark asked the wrong question.} It treated the difficulty as succession --- what
becomes of a held set when it is finally lost --- when the record shows the loss \emph{already occurring,
partially, for weeks, with nothing registering it}.

A member was hospitalised from mid-June following a fall, moved afterwards to rehabilitation, and
\emph{missed fifty-one floor votes}; no public account of the condition was given for several weeks, and
a longstanding mobility condition was afterwards described as a continuing factor \citep{mcconnell2026}.

\emph{The stated composition and the available capacity diverged, and no published measure of the
chamber distinguishes them.} A roll call records an absence, and an absence is the same category whether
its cause is a hospital or a fundraiser. \emph{So the institution held a false model of itself} --- a
seat counted, committee positions held, a member present in every enumeration --- while the capacity was
not there. That is the theory companion's fourth cost exactly, and it was silent exactly as that
definition says it is.

\emph{And no mechanism registered it because there is none.} Nothing declares a temporary incapacity,
seats a substitute, or compels disclosure. \emph{So the change occurred in the channel where nothing is
decided}, which is Remark~\ref{rem:lapse} once more, and here for the plainest of reasons: there was no
other channel available.

\emph{The only instrument was testimony, released at the subject's discretion.} \emph{At the one point
where an institution most needs a capacity measurement of its own members, it holds nothing but the
input class this account ranks least reliable}, on three grounds, delivered on the subject's timetable.
That is not a criticism of anyone's conduct. It is a description of an instrument that does not exist.

\emph{Nor was any of it surprising}, which closes the loop. A longstanding condition is a known risk, so
the event generated no residual, and by Remark~\ref{rem:surprise} nothing adapted --- no contingency
before, no mechanism during, and afterwards a statement.

\emph{The correction stands as the finding.} Succession is the comfortable version of this question.
\emph{The uncomfortable one is that capacity can be absent for weeks inside a fully-staffed institution
and appear in no measurement it keeps of itself.}
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Authenticating an aimed artefact leaves it aimed]\label{rem:proof}
The instrument question sharpens further, and the sharpening is methodological rather than medical.

For roughly a month there was no independent observation of the member. \emph{What stood in its place
was imagery released by his own office}, the first of it showing him in what appeared to be a hospital
bed, a second following weeks later; the congressional attending physician's office stated separately
that he was not yet cleared to return. \emph{Doubt was answered by authenticating the images} ---
forensic groups at two universities examined them and found no sign of manipulation \citep{proof2026}.

\emph{And that did not close the gap, because the gap was never authenticity.} By
Remark~\ref{rem:testimony} an artefact released by an office is \emph{aimed}: produced in order to be
received. \emph{Authenticating an aimed artefact leaves it aimed.} The finding establishes that an image
was not manipulated and establishes nothing about what an artefact constructed to be received was
constructed to convey. \emph{The verification performed was orthogonal to the question it was taken to
settle}, which is worth stating because it was performed carefully and answered honestly.

\emph{There is an unaimed signal available, and it is in the necessity rather than the content.} A
proof-of-life format is produced only where ordinary observation has already failed. The office chose to
release; \emph{it did not choose that releasing was the remaining option}. So the content is aimed and
the necessity is structural --- \emph{read the existence of the artefact, not the picture} --- and that
is a genuine refinement of the criterion above, since the fact that a communication had to be made is
frequently unaimed even where the communication is not.

\emph{One properly institutional instrument did appear}, and its shape is instructive: an attending
physician's office reported \emph{fitness to return}, on enquiry. That is not a measure of capacity to
serve, and nothing in the arrangement produces one.

\emph{And a second member of the same body died suddenly in the same weeks}, at seventy-one, of a
cardiovascular cause recorded by a medical examiner. Remark~\ref{rem:actuarial} projected a
concentration; \emph{this is two events in one summer}, and no linkage between them is asserted or
needed.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A change in how, and not only in what]\label{rem:how}
The remarks above converge on a consequence larger than any of them, worth stating with its conditions
attached rather than without.

\emph{Conjoin what is established.} Individual capacity is fixed and exceeded, so consequential
decisions do not fit inside one person (Remark~\ref{rem:individual}). The deciding unit is therefore the
arrangement rather than the person (Remark~\ref{rem:unit}). That arrangement is built and rebuilt by
what it contains (Remark~\ref{rem:building}). And the person's removal from the loop proceeds by
capacity rather than by choice (Remark~\ref{rem:race}). \emph{So what changes is not the content of
outcomes but the mechanism producing them} --- a change in \emph{how}, and not only in \emph{what}.

\emph{That is the work a period named for human agency does.} The term's formal geological standing was
declined rather than granted, and what it actually does is periodise by \emph{which agency decides},
which is the register this argument already occupies and the reason it can be used without borrowing
authority it lacks. \emph{Nothing in it requires humans to go.} A period may end while its inhabitants
remain, having never been named for their presence.

\emph{And it is not remotely new.} Arrangements exceeding any individual's comprehension are old:
markets and bureaucracies have decided at scales no participant holds for as long as either has existed,
and the observation that no individual possesses what the system computes was made exactly
\citep{hayek1945}. \emph{Super-individual deciding is not the finding, and an account offering it as one
would be uninformed rather than bold.}

\emph{What is new is narrower, and is this paper's own subject.} Those older arrangements were neither
addressable nor self-modifying: the competence in them could not be read out, copied, or altered except
through the people carrying it. \emph{The arrangements now in question are addressable} --- the
migration reported above --- \emph{and they build}, at a rate the human clock does not set. That is the
difference, and it is one of schedule and legibility rather than of whether systems decide.

\emph{Two limits, the second of which keeps this honest.} Humans do not stop deciding and nothing above
says they do: what moved is the locus of \emph{consequential} decision, while individual agency persists
undiminished at individual scale. And an epoch's ending is a periodisation, frame-relative like every
other measurement here --- \emph{under the criterion} ``which unit decides'' the period closes, and
Axiom~$-1$ denies that it closes simpliciter. Anyone choosing a different criterion gets a different
answer, and is owed the courtesy of being told which one was used.

\emph{What ought to follow is not addressed, for the reason given before.} This account prices
operations. It does not license them.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[What carries the weight, and where to attack]\label{rem:weight}
The steps above draw almost wholly on results that are canonical or on observations close to trivial. It
is worth saying exactly what follows from that, because the obvious reading --- \emph{so the conclusion
is safe} --- is the wrong one, and taking it would make the argument unfalsifiable.

\emph{The conclusions are shapes rather than magnitudes, and that is the whole source of the
robustness.} The interval is a difference of reachability sets, not a date. The three errors are signed,
not sized. The clocks differ in kind, fixed against compounding, rather than by a measured ratio. The
deciding unit changed, which is a structural fact and not a quantity. \emph{A shape survives error in
its inputs' magnitudes so long as their sign and ordering hold}, which is why no single empirical step
here has to be exactly right.

\emph{The robustness is bought, and the price is precision.} None of these conclusions says how fast,
how much, or when, and a reader wanting those will not find them --- the same exchange this paper
records everywhere else, not suspended here because the conclusions happen to be large.

\emph{So the steps are not equally load-bearing, and pretending they were would be the dodge.} The
empirical ones are individually dispensable. \emph{The structural ones are not}, and there are three:
Axiom~$-1$ with the frame-relativity results, without which most of the corrections above collapse; the
claim that competence is graph-resident with no remainder, without which Remark~\ref{rem:reconstruct}
fails and Remark~\ref{rem:schedule} weakens; and containment under coupling, on which
Remark~\ref{rem:unit}'s sharpening rests. \emph{Those are the points to attack}, and an attack landing on
any of them does damage where an attack on a citation does not.

\emph{The claim is better exercised than asserted.} The thinnest supports invoked above are the
degradation-under-scarcity result \citep{mani2013}, least settled of the capacity findings, and the
externalisation survey \citep{zhou2026}, which is recent and singular. Remove both. The capacity bound
still stands on \citet{miller1956} and \citet{cowan2001}; the migration still stands on the
architectures themselves, which are public artefacts rather than claims; and every conclusion above is
unchanged. \emph{That is what it means for the shape to be the load-bearing thing --- demonstrated on
the two weakest links rather than claimed in general.}
\end{remark}

\section{Prior positions}\label{sec:prior}
None of the machinery here is new and it should not be dressed as though it were. Four literatures own
it, and what this account adds is an arrangement rather than an operation.

\emph{The relativity of measurement, which is old and is not claimed here.} Everything
Section~\ref{sec:priors} says about scores has been said, in some cases seventy years ago, and it
should be read as inherited rather than offered.

Generalizability theory decomposes a measurement into facets --- the person, the item, the occasion,
the observer --- and holds that a score is a \emph{sample from a universe of admissible observations}
rather than a property of whoever was measured \citep{cronbach1972}. That is the four-place arrangement,
in a literature that had it before this programme existed. \citet{cronbach1955} had already established
that a test measures a construct only under an argument and never by fiat. \citet{quine1951} gives the
inference-licensing half: no hypothesis meets the evidence alone, so what a result licenses depends on
auxiliaries that travel with it. And \citet{newell1973} made the corresponding objection to
task-by-task measurement in this field specifically --- that a pile of task results does not accumulate
into an account of the mechanism.

\emph{One thing differs, and it is small and different in kind.} Those are cautions, and cautions are
discharged by better practice: estimate more facets, state the auxiliaries, argue the construct. What
Proposition~\ref{prop:no-prior-free} adds is that the prior-free case is not a facet that has been
estimated badly --- \emph{the origin is not in the space}. Generalizability theory supplies the
coordinates and assumes a universe of admissible observations within which they vary; the proposition
denies there is a zero for one of them to be measured from.

The practical force of that difference is the whole of it. A caution tells a careful practitioner to do
more work; \emph{a consequence tells them the work does not exist to be done}, and stops a research
programme from being organised around obtaining a control that cannot be built. Nothing else here is
new, and the derivation is the contribution rather than the observation it derives.

\emph{Anti-unification.} The operation is \citet{plotkin1970}'s least general generalisation: given two
structures, produce the most specific structure of which both are instances. That is exactly rung two
and rung three read backwards. \emph{What differs is what is kept.} The classical formulation returns
the generalisation and discards the substitutions that recover the inputs from it; here the
substitutions are the point, because \emph{the witness is the transform} and applying it elsewhere is
the whole operation. An lgg that throws away its witness cannot adapt anything.

\emph{Structure-mapping.} \citet{gentner1983} established that analogy runs on relational structure
rather than surface features, and that systematic relational systems are preferred to isolated matches
--- both of which this account assumes rather than argues. \emph{What differs is the purpose.}
Structure-mapping is a theory of comparison: what makes two things analogous and how a person aligns
them. This is a theory of acquisition, in which the comparison is instrumental --- the alignment exists
to yield a transform that mints a competence the system did not have. Gentner's theory answers what an
analogy \emph{is}; the question here is what one is \emph{for}.

\emph{Analogical proportion.} The four-term form, $a$ is to $b$ as $c$ is to $d$, has a developed logical
treatment \citep{prade2014}, and parallelogram completion is that form with $d$ unknown. Taken directly
and without modification.

\emph{Embedding arithmetic, and the reason its critique does not transfer.} The word
\emph{parallelogram} comes from vector offsets in learned embedding spaces \citep{mikolov2013}, and that
method's evaluation has been substantially criticised: \citet{levy2014} showed its behaviour is largely
accounted for by similarity terms rather than by the offset, and \citet{linzen2016} showed the standard
benchmark is confounded by nearest-neighbour baselines that need no analogy at all.

\emph{Those criticisms are correct and they do not apply here}, for a reason that is a real difference
and not a defence. The offset method is metric: it returns the nearest point to a computed location, so
it always returns something, and the question is only whether the something is right. The operation
here is symbolic and exact --- anti-unification either yields a witness that is well-defined and
lossless, or it fails and yields nothing. \emph{There is no nearest neighbour to fall back on and no
threshold to tune}, which removes the failure mode the critique identifies and replaces it with a
different one: refusing cases a metric method would have answered. That is the cost of the exchange and
it is the honest form of it.

\section{Falsifiability commitments}\label{sec:falsifiability}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textsc{[shape]}~\textbf{Shape matching and analogy are one operation.} Fails if a case of one
cannot be expressed as a case of the other --- specifically, if some analogical completion has no
formulation as a transform between held signatures, or some shape adaptation has no formulation as an
anti-unification with its witness retained.
\item \textsc{[shape]}~\textbf{The ladder's order is forced, not chosen.} Fails if reordering the rungs
leaves the outcomes unchanged on cases where the rungs disagree, which would make the order a
preference.
\item \textsc{[detail]}~\textbf{One mint, then recognition.} Fails on a novel case that yields repeated
minting under an unchanged codebook, or none at all.
\item \textsc{[detail]}~\textbf{Coupling forces a dimension.} Fails if a residual coupled across
observed dimensions is absorbed by a member rather than a dimension, without loss.
\item \textsc{[shape]}~\textbf{Adaptability is bounded by the priors.} Fails on a demonstration of a
competence derived from a held set containing no path to it --- which would make adaptation a source of
capability rather than a conversion of one, and would be a free lunch.
\item \textsc{[shape]}~\textbf{No prior-free baseline is constructible.} Fails on the exhibition of a
frame holding nothing that nevertheless draws a distinction --- which would put the zero of the priors
axis inside the space and make prior-free measurement well-formed after all.
\item \textsc{[detail]}~\textbf{A prior-assuming benchmark measures a relation, not a property.} Fails
if performance on such a task set is shown to be independent of familiarity with its conventions, in a
population matched on everything else.
\item \textsc{[detail]}~\textbf{An oracle bought once is paid once.} Fails if held structure derived
from an oracle requires continued access to that oracle to keep working --- if the purchase does not in
fact terminate, the distinction drawn here is between two ways of paying continuously and is not worth
drawing.
\item \textsc{[shape]}~\textbf{Capability has plural sources, selected by regime.} Fails if a domain is
exhibited in which candidates are cheaply and exactly checkable and held structure nevertheless
contributes measurably, or in which no oracle exists and holding contributes nothing --- either would
break the inverse relation this account asserts between the value of holding and the cheapness of
verification.
\item \textsc{[shape]}~\textbf{Adaptability is per case, not a rank.} Fails if a single ordering of
systems by adaptability can be constructed that survives changing the held set.
\end{enumerate}
