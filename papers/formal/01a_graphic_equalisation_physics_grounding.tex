\begin{abstract}
This companion to Graphic Equalisation develops the physics groundings of the
change-substrate. Its central move is that a frame's time is an inferred count of
\emph{cycles} and that the invariant speed $c$ is the frame's own
change-throughput unit, $1$ by construction rather than by postulate; from that
one economy special and general relativity, the dimensional constants, and the
second law follow as \emph{shapes}. Special relativity's $c$-invariance becomes a
theorem; special and general relativity reconcile as one fixed change-budget
spent two ways, on motion or on change-load; the dimensional constants
($c,\hbar,k_{\mathrm B},G$) are each a frame's own base unit and their magnitudes
are cross-frame artefacts; the uncertainty principle is the finite-aperture
floor, the Landauer bound the information--energy conversion, quantization the
aperture cell, and the Planck scale the frontier. The second law is no-copy
accumulated, with entropy frame-relative, the demon exorcised by cost, and local
order affordable as paid minting. The claims are information-level, physics taken
as an embodiment: the paper mechanises the shapes and explicitly does not derive
the field equations, the dimensionless constants, or a theory of quantum gravity.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope}\label{sec:scope}
This paper is a grounding, not a physical theory. It inherits Graphic
Equalisation's position that the claims are about information --- distinction,
frame, admissibility, realisation --- of which physical theory is one embodiment,
and that a physical result bears on an information-level claim when it settles an
informational fact. Everything below is at that level. The strong claims are
structural: that there is a $c$, a $\hbar$, an uncertainty principle, a Landauer
floor, a second law, and that special and general relativity reconcile as one
change-economy. The weak part is anything carrying a number: no field equations,

exposed to falsification, in the manner of Graphic Equalisation's collapse
bridge, not a result asserted from within.

\section{$c$ as the change-to-time rate}\label{sec:c-rate}
Graphic Equalisation establishes that time is not a substrate dimension but is
\emph{inferred} by a frame, whose unit is a \emph{cycle} --- one traversal of a
closed rule-loop, of no fixed length. Once time is the inferred count of cycles,
the invariant speed $c$ has a natural reading, consistent with the
missing-denominator result (there is no rate \emph{in} substrate-time, since
there is no substrate-time to divide by). $c$ is the fixed \emph{rate} at which
change is realised --- per unit of the substrate's throughput, a cycle consuming
a variable amount of it --- from which a frame's time is inferred. So $c$ is
prior to time: the conversion between change-count and inferred duration, which
is exactly what a constant relating the temporal and spatial parts of an interval
is.

\begin{proposition}[$c$ is each frame's own unit, hence invariant by construction]\label{prop:c-unit}
$c$ is each frame's own base rate, its change-throughput, so every frame measures
its own $c$ as the unit, $1$ --- and a unit cannot be measured as anything else.
Its invariance is therefore a theorem, not a postulate: not that all frames
happen to agree on a speed, but that each is reporting its own unit.
\end{proposition}
A value \emph{other} than $1$ is a cross-frame quantity: the familiar number
appears only when one frame is compared with another --- change-throughput
against a spatial metric, seconds against metres --- which is where the
continuous lives, in the maps between frames rather than in a frame's own
discrete states. One can give time a number at all only by comparing frames; in
its own frame $c$ is the unit, and its particular value ($299\,792\,458$ in
SI) is an artefact of the comparison, not a fact about the substrate. And $c$ is
a \emph{limit} because a signal is a sequence of changes and none can be realised
faster than the frame's throughput.

\section{The change-budget: speed, dilation, mass, aperture}\label{sec:budget}
Speed is a quantum of that unit. The fixed throughput $c$ splits between
\emph{spatial motion} and \emph{internal change} (proper time), and a speed is
the fraction spent on motion. At rest the whole budget is internal change and the
clock ticks fully; approaching $c$ moves budget to motion, leaving less for
internal change, so the clock slows --- time dilation as budget re-allocation ---
and at $c$ the whole budget is motion and no proper time remains. Mass is the
budget \emph{reserved} for internal change, so a massive graph can never put all
of it into motion: it asymptotes to $c$ and the energy to approach diverges,
while a massless one reserves none and \emph{is} at $c$, with no proper time.

Because observation is funded by internal-change throughput, approaching $c$
coarsens a frame's \emph{aperture}. The transformation between frames in relative
motion --- time dilation and length contraction --- is an aperture transformation
between different budget-splits, and each frame's aperture self-calibrates so that
its own $c$ still reads $1$. The Lorentz factor is the aperture change that holds
$c=1$ across the split. (The constant-magnitude four-velocity of Minkowski
geometry \citep{minkowski1908} is this budget-split stated as a metric fact
rather than as a mechanism.)

\section{Special and general relativity reconciled}\label{sec:sr-gr}
The two relativities are one mechanism, because the one fixed budget $c$ can be
drained two ways.

\begin{proposition}[SR and GR are one change-budget spent two ways]\label{prop:sr-gr}
Special relativity is the budget spent on \emph{motion}: a moving graph puts
throughput into spatial motion, leaving less for internal change, so its clock
slows. General relativity is the budget spent on \emph{change-load}: mass is
throughput tied up in internal change, and concentrating change-load (dense mass)
consumes the local budget, so local clocks slow. Both dilations are the same act
--- the one fixed budget drained, by motion or by mass, each leaving less for
proper time.
\end{proposition}
The equivalence principle is the seam. Accelerating (changing one's motion) and
standing in a gravitational field (change-load) draw on the budget identically,
so inertial mass (resistance to a change of motion --- more state to re-write,
hence more change and more cost, the impulse relation of the Narrative-momentum
paper with mass as the exchange rate) and gravitational mass (change-load
consuming throughput and slowing local time) are one quantity, ``how much change
this matter loads onto the ruleset,'' measured two ways. General relativity
assumes their equality and does not explain it; here they coincide by
construction.

\begin{corollary}[Relativity reconciles because $c$ is defined, not postulated]\label{cor:reconcile}
Because $c$ is here \emph{defined} (Proposition~\ref{prop:c-unit}) rather than
postulated, special relativity's postulate of $c$-invariance becomes a theorem,
and its effects follow as consequences rather than as separate surprises, with
the classical paradoxes dissolving into aperture differences --- each moving clock
right, each reading its own $c=1$. Special and general relativity then reconcile
(Proposition~\ref{prop:sr-gr}) as velocity- and gravity-dilation of one budget,
joined at the equivalence principle.
\end{corollary}

\section{The measurement problem is frame-relative collapse}\label{sec:measurement}
Collapse in Graphic Equalisation is a local write that realises one continuation
\emph{relative to the frame that interacts}. It is not a physical process needing
a mechanism --- no dynamical collapse, no spontaneous localisation --- but the
in-frame realisation that occurs when frames couple. So there is no
frame-independent ``when'': each frame realises its own outcome upon interaction,
and a frame that has not interacted holds no collapse. This is Axiom~$-1$ and
relational quantum mechanics \citep{rovelli1996} --- facts indexed to
interactions, not assigned from nowhere.

\begin{proposition}[Wigner's friend is the map and the event of two frames]\label{prop:wigner}
The friend measures the system and realises an outcome --- collapse relative to
the friend, who interacted. Wigner, not having interacted with friend-and-system,
holds relative to himself the continuous unitary \emph{map} (Section~\ref{sec:collapse}).
Both are correct: the friend holds the in-frame \emph{event}, Wigner the
cross-frame \emph{map}, and these are the two sides of the distinction that
reconciles general relativity with collapse. The apparent contradiction requires
a single frame-independent fact about whether collapse occurred, which
Axiom~$-1$ forbids; without that demand the friend's definite result and Wigner's
superposition coexist without strain.
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Any coupling is a measurement; outcomes are definite; no special observer]\label{cor:observer}
Any coupling is a measurement relative to the coupled frames, so no conscious or
privileged observer is required and none is distinguished --- a plate, a rock, or
a friend measure relative to whatever they couple to. Outcomes are definite
because collapse realises \emph{one} continuation, and the basis in which they are
definite is set by the distinctions the interaction makes --- the aperture of the
coupling, which is decoherence selecting the pointer basis. There is no cut and
no collapse mechanism to locate.
\end{corollary}

This dissolves the measurement \emph{problem} --- the when, the how, the who, and
the definiteness --- but it does \emph{not} derive the Born rule. That outcomes
are realised frame-relatively is the shape; that their probabilities go as the
squared amplitude is a number, and the framework says nothing about it. The same
shape/number division applies: the paradoxes are mechanised, the quantitative
probabilities handed back untouched.

\section{Entanglement is dimensional access through frames}\label{sec:entanglement}
An entangled pair is one system in the superframe, described by a joint dimension
--- the correlation --- that is a first-class quantity of the superframe and
\textsc{null} in each subframe individually (Graphic Equalisation's higher-frame
first-class structure). Each subframe has dimensional access to it only through
its own boundary, and only in part; the full dimension lives one level up, along
the structural-depth axis rather than the spatial one.

\begin{proposition}[A subframe's local state is mixed because the purity is a superframe dimension]\label{prop:mixed}
Described alone, an entangled particle has no pure state --- it is mixed --- because
its pure description requires the joint dimension, which is \textsc{null} in its
subframe. Tracing out the partner is not discarding locally-available
information; the missing information was never a subframe dimension. The reduced
state is mixed exactly because the purity is a superframe dimension, inaccessible
from below.
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Nonlocality is up, not across; Bell forbids only subframe-local variables]\label{cor:nonlocal}
The connection between the parties is not \emph{across} the space between them but
\emph{up}, to the superframe they are both subframes of. No cross-frame write
passes between the subframes; each accesses the same superframe dimension
locally, and the joint fact appears only when their accesses are compared (a
further coupling), so no signal is sent. Bell forbids \emph{local} ---
subframe-level --- hidden variables \citep{bell1964}, and the framework has none:
the correlating variable is a superframe dimension, so the violation is expected,
not paradoxical. Kochen--Specker contextuality \citep{kochen1967} is the access
itself --- measuring projects the superframe dimension into the subframe's
aperture, so a realised value is context-dependent by construction.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Spookiness is an aperture artefact: the light-switch identity]\label{rem:light-switch}
The confusion entanglement provokes is identical in structure to that of a person
who has never seen a light switch and does not understand electricity: flip a
switch, a far light obeys, with no connection inside the aperture, so action at a
distance is inferred. In both cases the inference is wrong for the same reason ---
there is a connection, through a dimension the observer cannot see: the wiring in
the walls for the switch (the opaque runner), the superframe's joint dimension
for entanglement (structural depth). Spookiness is therefore a property of the
aperture, not of the phenomenon; a switch is mundane once one sees the wiring,
entanglement once one sees the superframe. This is the sequence science has run
before --- magnetism was action at a distance until the field, gravity until
curvature, disease until germs --- each ``spooky'' a real connection through a
not-yet-visible dimension, each dissolved by the aperture widening rather than
the phenomenon changing. ``We do not understand entanglement'' is structurally
``I do not understand the light switch.''
\end{remark}

Honest boundary: seeing the connecting dimension dissolves the \emph{spookiness}
--- the qualitative action-at-a-distance mystery --- not the \emph{numbers}. The
Tsirelson bound, the correlation strength, and the Born weights are not derived;
they become ordinary physics questions rather than spooky ones, the same
shape/number division as elsewhere.

\section{Amplitudes are a rule on Narrative; the Born rule's frontier}\label{sec:amplitudes}
An amplitude is not a foreign number-structure but the directional Narrative of
the Narrative-momentum paper, acted on by a rule. That paper's directional
momentum $p=\phi_W(\mathcal N)\,\hat{\mathbf d}$ is a magnitude $\phi(\mathcal N)$
--- the operative-history measure --- times a direction $\hat{\mathbf d}$, which
is a magnitude times a phase: a complex number. The complex amplitude is
therefore the directional Narrative, its dynamics a rule applied to it, and its
phase the direction $\hat{\mathbf d}$ read as position in the cycle
(Section~\ref{sec:c-rate}). The complex structure of quantum amplitudes is thus
grounded, not imported.

\begin{proposition}[The Born square is a candidate via closure]\label{prop:born}
A realised outcome is a \emph{closed} cycle --- the realised ``now'' is one
traversal of the closure loop, and a loop that closes. A probability is the
weight of a realised outcome, hence of a closed loop: the Narrative carried
forward and carried back to close it, the amplitude and its return. The weight is
then the amplitude applied twice, amplitude times its conjugate,
$|\text{amplitude}|^{2}$. The Born square would on this reading be the closure
itself --- a realisation must close its loop, and closing requires the
directional Narrative both ways.
\end{proposition}

This is a candidate, not a derivation, and a real advance on leaving the
amplitude a blank. The \emph{complex} structure is grounded (directional
Narrative); the \emph{square} has a mechanism (closure); what is not yet done is
the rigour --- tying closure-as-conjugate to the full Hilbert inner product and
Gleason's uniqueness theorem \citep{gleason1957}, and showing the phase
composition reproduces interference quantitatively. So the Born rule moves from
``a structure the framework lacks entirely'' to ``the directional Narrative for
the amplitude, closure for the square, the formal identification still to make.''
The honest frontier of the physics arc is therefore a \emph{proof} --- the
rigorous tie to the Hilbert formalism --- not a missing object: the framework
reaches the amplitude (Narrative), the phase (cycle/direction), and a candidate
for the square (closure), and stops at the quantitative formalism, as it stops at
every number.

\subsection*{An attempt to close it}
The candidate can be pushed further than a candidate, though not all the way. Take
the amplitude as a directed magnitude --- a Narrative's size $r$ times a phase ---
and follow four steps, marking what each earns. \emph{This subsection is an attempt,
flagged as such, not a claimed result.}

\emph{Complex (motivated).} The phase is position on the realisation cycle, and a
cycle's traversal parameter is one-dimensional: $S^1=U(1)$. So an amplitude is
$r\,e^{i\theta}$, a complex number --- real would be a phase-less (cycle-less)
magnitude, and the one-dimensional closure loop gives a single angle, not the three
of a quaternionic phase. Motivated by closure, not forced.

\emph{Linear (a candidate mechanism, not a proof).} Independent contributions to one
outcome superpose by addition. This has a candidate mechanism rather than a bare posit.
Free evolution is the amplitude's own Narrative, and a Narrative is what a subgraph runs
on an \emph{empty} rule set --- the free continuation carried forward when no rule fires
(the momentum of Narrative-momentum, the free-continuation default of
Section~\ref{sec:noether}). With no rule present, nothing \emph{couples} the components of
a superposition; each free-continues independently, and uncoupled independent continuation
\emph{is} linear superposition, since a nonlinearity would need a rule reading one
component and acting on another --- exactly what the empty set lacks. Linearity is on this
reading the signature of the empty rule set. What stays a residual is the rigorous step
from ``uncoupled'' to full additivity and norm-preservation (unitarity), and the
linearity of the imposed supergraph map is not handed over for free: the mechanism
motivates linearity, it does not yet prove it.

\emph{Phase-independent (from no privilege).} A cyclic direction has no privileged
origin, so a global phase rotation is a change of nothing (Graphic Equalisation's
no-privilege principle applied to the phase). The realised probability must
therefore be invariant under a global $U(1)$, hence a function of the modulus alone,
$P=f(|a|)$. This step is earned, and it is the no-privilege thesis of the mind
companion doing physics work.

\emph{Squared (forced, given the above).} Two alternatives with no privileged
relative phase --- decohered, their relative phase averaged over --- are classically
exclusive, so their probabilities must add. Averaging the combined modulus over the
relative phase,
\[
\big\langle f(|a\,e^{i\theta}+b|)\big\rangle_\theta = f(|a|)+f(|b|),
\]
since the cross term $2\,\mathrm{Re}(a^\ast b\,e^{i\theta})$ averages to zero. The
unique natural $f$ satisfying this is the square, $f(x)\propto x^{2}$: a quartic
overshoots by the surviving $\langle\cos^{2}\rangle=\tfrac12$ term, the linear
undershoots ($a=b=1$ gives $4/\pi\neq2$), the square lands exactly; normalisation
fixes the constant. So the Born square is not merely plausible --- given complex,
linear amplitudes and no privileged phase, $P=|a|^{2}$ is forced --- and it is real
because closure (a returned cycle) is net-zero phase.

\emph{Bottom line, honestly.} The attempt \emph{reduces Born to the linearity of free
continuation}. Given that amplitudes superpose linearly, the squared modulus follows from
the framework's own no-privilege principle (phase-independence) and the classical
additivity of decohered alternatives (the square); and linearity itself now has a
candidate mechanism rather than a bare posit --- free evolution is the empty-rule-set
Narrative, whose components, uncoupled by any rule, superpose. What is \emph{not} closed:
the rigorous additivity and norm-preservation of that free map, and Gleason's
multi-context uniqueness \citep{gleason1957} --- that $|\cdot|^{2}$ is the \emph{unique}
measure consistent across \emph{all} measurement contexts on a Hilbert space of dimension
$\ge 3$, the rigorous form of the phase-average. The frontier has moved twice, and shrunk
each time: from ``why the square'' to ``why linear,'' and from ``why linear'' to ``why the
free map is exactly additive and norm-preserving'' --- each a smaller residual than the
last, none yet zero.

\emph{The Gleason residual, reconsidered.} The internal Narrative is relative: it is the
vector of the frame's \emph{current} rule set (the state read in the basis that rule set
fixes), so a superposition is rule-set-relative --- definite in one rule set, superposed in
another --- as relational quantum mechanics \citep{rovelli1996} already holds. Because no
rule set is privileged (the no-privilege principle again), the probability rule must be the
\emph{same} across every rule set, which is exactly the context-independence Gleason's
theorem assumes. The framework thus supplies Gleason's premises --- a vector-valued state
(the linearity step above) and a context-independent measure (no privileged rule set) ---
from its own principles, and Gleason \citep{gleason1957} supplies the uniqueness:
$|\cdot|^{2}$ is the only such measure in dimension $\ge 3$. The residual is then no longer
``why context-independence'' but only the cited theorem itself and the vector-structure
rigour of the linearity step --- the last physics gap reduced to a citation and one
posit.

\emph{The Hilbert posit, reconsidered: linearity as projection through frames.} That one
posit --- the state lives in a complex Hilbert space and free evolution is unitary --- is
what treating Hilbert space \emph{as a frame} is meant to reach. Read the inner product
$\langle a\mid b\rangle$ as the projection of one frame's Narrative into another's rule set:
how much of $b$ shows up when $b$ is read in the rule set $a$ fixes. So the inner product is
not imported structure but the framework's own cross-frame reading. Linearity is then that
projection distributing over components: during a free (empty-rule-set) segment no rule
couples the Narrative's parts, so reading a superposition is superposing the readings --- the
same uncoupling, applied to the projection. Unitarity is that a free continuation is a
\emph{no-write}: it creates and destroys no distinction, so it preserves the Narrative and
every cross-frame projection of it, norms and inner products conserved (the free-continuation
conservation of Section~\ref{sec:noether}). The Narratives, directional magnitudes that
superpose, form the complex vector space; the inner product, a cross-frame map, makes it
Hilbert; and its \emph{completeness} --- the defining Hilbert property --- is the continuum
living in the maps between frames, $\mathrm{Hom}(\text{frame},\text{frame})$, exactly where
Graphic Equalisation places the continuous and never in a frame's own stepped states
(Section~\ref{sec:c-rate}).

This meets the informational reconstructions of quantum theory, which \emph{derive} the
complex Hilbert space and the Born rule from a few operational axioms
\citep{hardy2001,chiribella2011}. The framework appears to supply those axioms from its own
principles: purification --- every mixed state is pure in a larger system --- is entanglement
as a superframe dimension (Section~\ref{sec:entanglement}); continuous reversibility is free
continuation (a no-write is reversible); context-independence is no privileged rule set; and
composition with local tomography is subframe nesting. If that mapping holds, Hilbert space
and the Born rule follow by the cited reconstructions, as $|\cdot|^{2}$ followed from Gleason.

Honest boundary: this is the same move again, one level deeper --- the framework supplies the
premises and a cited theorem supplies the structure. What is \emph{not} done: the mapping from
these principles to the reconstruction axioms is claimed, not verified in detail, and the
rigorous additivity of the projection is still owed. The residual is a mapping to check and
two cited theorems, not a structure assumed --- the smallest the frontier has been, and still
not zero.

\section{Interference and the double slit}\label{sec:interference}
The double slit is obvious once two established facts are held together: causality
is ordering \emph{in a frame}, and a superframe applies changes to a subframe
without passing through the subframe's admissibility.

\begin{proposition}[Superposition is the possibility structure; interference is amplitudes combining by phase]\label{prop:interference}
Superposition is Graphic Equalisation's possibility structure $\mathcal P(G)$ ---
the admissible continuations a graph holds before collapse --- so a particle ``in
superposition'' is a graph whose $\mathcal P$ has more than one admissible
continuation, none yet realised. Each continuation carries a directional Narrative
(Section~\ref{sec:amplitudes}), an amplitude with a cyclic phase, and before
collapse the alternatives' amplitudes combine, constructive where the phases align
and destructive where opposed. The outcome weight is the combined closed loop,
$|\sum\text{amplitude}|^{2}$.
\end{proposition}

Two established facts make this un-mysterious. First, causality is ordering
\emph{in a frame}: there is no global fact about ``which path the particle really
took,'' because which-path is \emph{undetermined}, not \emph{undisclosed}, until a
coupling realises it --- a cross-frame question with no in-frame answer, like the
value of $c$. The ``both paths'' is not a particle in two places but the frame's
$\mathcal P$. Second, a superframe applies changes to a subframe \emph{without}
passing through the subframe's admissibility (Graphic Equalisation: admissibility
is self-governance, not armour --- a graph's grammar governs its self-originated
transformation, not world-caused forcing). So the interference is the superframe
applying its combined-amplitude structure to the particle; the particle does not
select a path by its own rules, and cannot veto the combination.

\begin{corollary}[The double slit, duality, and delayed choice]\label{cor:doubleslit}
Both paths are admissible continuations in $\mathcal P$; the superframe applies
their combined amplitudes at the screen, giving fringes. A which-path coupling
collapses before the screen --- one continuation realised, the other no longer in
the sum --- so the superframe applies a single-path change and the fringes vanish.
Wave--particle duality is the map/event distinction: the ``wave'' is the amplitudes
combining (the map, the held $\mathcal P$), the ``particle'' the realised outcome
(the event, one continuation collapsed), and ``which is it really'' names no
difference. Delayed choice adds no retrocausality: collapse attaches to the actual
interaction, not a retroactive rewrite (cost is a local write; no write reaches
the past), so interference-or-not depends only on whether the which-path coupling
occurs.
\end{corollary}

Honest boundary: this grounds the shapes --- superposition as possibility,
interference as phase-combination, the double slit, duality, and delayed choice
without retrocausality. The quantitative fringe pattern needs the amplitude phases
computed, i.e.\ the Hilbert formalism, which is the Born-rule frontier of
Section~\ref{sec:amplitudes}.

\section{The accelerator: forced concurrency and decoherence}\label{sec:accelerator}
The same $c$ bounds concurrency, and this gives the high-energy regime a reading.
Within a frame, change is self-change and strictly ordered, so forcing two
changes onto one rule-step is the paid exception; a particle accelerator is the
machine that pays it, spending energy to push particles' interactions to the same
step, where the frame --- built to sequence --- cannot order them and the
interaction is \emph{decoherent} in that frame. The energy divergence as a
particle approaches $c$ is the change-rate ceiling: two changes cannot be crowded
closer than a step, and forcing them there costs more the closer one gets. The
strong reading is ``energy is the price of forced concurrency, and forced
concurrency is frame decoherence''; anything with a number is weak.

\section{Dimensional constants are frame-units; dimensionless ones are not}\label{sec:constants}
The reading of $c$ as a frame's own base unit generalises. Each dimensional
constant is the base unit of one of a frame's own economies --- $1$ in-frame, a
number only cross-frame.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item $c$ is the change-rate unit (Section~\ref{sec:c-rate}).
\item $\hbar$ is the \emph{aperture quantum}, the smallest resolvable cell:
observation is funded by finite internal-change throughput, so fine resolution in
one dimension trades against another and the trade floors at a minimum cell ---
which is the uncertainty principle, $\Delta x\,\Delta p\ge\hbar/2$ being the
finite-aperture budget stated as a bound, with $\hbar$ its quantum.
\item $k_{\mathrm B}$ is the information--energy conversion: Landauer's
$k_{\mathrm B}T\ln 2$ \citep{landauer1961} --- the floor cost of change of
Graphic Equalisation's economics --- is one bit's change converted from the
information frame into the energy frame at that rate.
\item $G$ is the change-load--geometry coupling, mass deforming the containing
frame.
\end{itemize}
That physics sets $c=\hbar=k_{\mathrm B}=G=1$ (natural units) is then not a
convenience but a recognition: these are frame-units, so they read $1$ in a
frame's own terms, and their familiar magnitudes are cross-frame artefacts ---
the frame's unit against a human metre, second, kilogram, kelvin. ``Why is $c$
that number'' is a question about the comparison, not the substrate.

\begin{proposition}[The dimensional/dimensionless boundary]\label{prop:dim-boundary}
This dissolves the \emph{dimensional} constants and touches the
\emph{dimensionless} ones not at all. The fine-structure constant
$\alpha\approx1/137$ and the mass ratios cannot be set to $1$ by any unit choice;
they are genuine cross-frame \emph{ratios}, real numbers with no dimension to
absorb, and the framework says nothing about their values.
\end{proposition}
It sharpens the real question rather than answering it: the numbers that need
explaining are the dimensionless ones, while the pseudo-mysteries --- why $c$,
why $\hbar$ --- are unit conventions. This is the standard observation that the
number of fundamental \emph{dimensional} constants is a matter of convention
\citep{duff2002}, landing on the side that they are units; the dimensionless
remainder is left exactly as open as it was found.

\section{The Planck scale, quantization, and fine-tuning}\label{sec:planck}
Three consequences follow. First, the \emph{Planck scale} is where the
dimensional units meet at $1$ simultaneously ($\ell_P=\sqrt{\hbar G/c^{3}}$ and
its companions): the aperture cell ($\hbar$), the change-rate ceiling ($c$), and
the change-load--geometry coupling ($G$) reach their unit at once. Below it a
frame cannot resolve, cannot order, and horizons its own geometry, so nothing
there is representable --- the ``\,?\,'' of Graphic Equalisation's epistemic
boundary with a location. The Planck scale \emph{is} the frontier, which is why
physics predicts its own breakdown there: not a failure of theories but the
exhaustion of a frame's units, below which the pre-relational substrate begins.

Second, \emph{quantization} is the aperture being cellular: because $\hbar$ is a
minimum cell rather than an infinitesimal, action and energy come in
$\hbar$-steps, so the ``quantum'' of quantum mechanics is Graphic Equalisation's
discrete sufficiency read at the resolution axis --- the granularity of the
aperture, not a separate granularity of the world.

Third, the \emph{dimensionless} constants are contingent rule-content: the
framework cannot derive $\alpha$ or the mass ratios, but it can say what kind of
thing they are --- the contingent specifics of this substrate's rule-set (Graphic
Equalisation's nothing-compulsory principle), so ``why $\alpha$'' is not ``why
this necessary value'' but ``why this rule-set,'' a question answered with
contingency, since a different rule-set gives a different $\alpha$ at no extra
machinery. This relocates fine-tuning at its root: the inference ``the constants
are tuned, therefore design or necessity'' assumes they are the kind of thing
with a necessary value, whereas the dimensional ones are units with no value to
tune and the dimensionless ones are contingent, so their being tunable is
expected rather than remarkable; and the ``why this rule-set'' is not merely
deferred but answered by self-location (Section~\ref{sec:cosmology}) --- we are
the observers this rule-set produces.

\section{Symmetry and conservation}\label{sec:noether}
A symmetry is a transformation that produces no residual: a change the structure
does not feel, free and reversible, a no-op in the container's grammar. Noether's
theorem, symmetry $\leftrightarrow$ conserved quantity, is then the cost side of
that. A quantity is conserved precisely when altering it would cost --- produce
residual, require a paid write --- so the symmetric direction is free and the
quantity perpendicular to it cannot move without paying. What a transformation
leaves untouched is what it costs to change.

\begin{proposition}[Momentum conservation is the free-continuation default]\label{prop:momentum-cons}
Space-translation symmetry --- the rules the same everywhere --- produces no
residual under translation, and momentum is conserved. But momentum conservation
is already the free-continuation default of Graphic Equalisation's momentum: the
un-rewritten Narrative, Newton's first law, continuation costing nothing.
Noether's momentum case and inertia are one statement --- momentum is conserved
because only changing it costs.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Energy conservation is contingent on time-translation symmetry]\label{prop:energy-cons}
Time-translation symmetry --- the rules the same each cycle --- conserves the
change-budget, which is energy. The failure case is checkable: where the rules
effectively change across cycles there is no time-translation symmetry and energy
is not globally conserved. This is the standard general-relativistic result that
a time-dependent spacetime has no global energy conservation --- in an expanding
universe photons redshift their energy away --- here from the same principle:
rules changing across cycles (cosmic expansion, or minting) means no conserved
energy.
\end{proposition}
Charge follows from gauge symmetry, which is a redundancy of description: a
freedom in the representation that changes no physics, in the framework a
cross-frame re-description (in the maps, where the continuous lives) producing no
in-frame residual, so charge is conserved.

\begin{remark}[The gauge group is contingent rule-content]\label{rem:gauge-contingent}
The framework grounds \emph{that} a symmetry yields a conservation law --- the
shape, residual-free $\leftrightarrow$ cost-to-change --- but \emph{which}
symmetries hold, the $\mathrm{U}(1)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{SU}(3)$ of
the Standard Model, is a fact about this substrate's rule-set, not a necessity.
``Why this gauge group'' is ``why this rule-set,'' the same relocation as ``why
$\alpha$'' (Section~\ref{sec:planck}): both are contingent specifics of this
universe's grammar, answered with contingency, not derived. The conservation
\emph{structure} is universal; the particular symmetries are this universe's.
\end{remark}

Honest boundary: this gives why symmetry yields conservation, why momentum
conservation is inertia, and why energy conservation fails in cosmology ---
shapes. It does not derive the Standard Model gauge group, the Noether currents
quantitatively, or any number; those are contingent rule-content or ordinary
calculations, handed back.

\section{The second law}\label{sec:second-law}
Entropy and its arrow follow from the same economy.

\begin{definition}[Entropy is what a frame does not distinguish]\label{def:entropy}
The entropy a frame holds is the log-count of the microstructure it carries but
cannot tell apart --- what falls below its aperture. Entropy is therefore
frame- and aperture-relative from the start: a finer aperture resolves more and
reads less entropy, a coarser one more.
\end{definition}
This is the Gibbs--Jaynes reading, in which entropy is a property of the
observer's coarse-graining rather than of the world in itself \citep{jaynes1957};
here it is grounded rather than assumed --- entropy is \emph{what this frame does
not distinguish}.

\begin{proposition}[The second law is no-copy accumulated]\label{prop:second-law}
Every change discards its predecessor --- the prior state goes \textsc{null}
unless paid to keep --- so each change pushes structure below the aperture, into
the un-distinguished. Change therefore accumulates entropy, because change forgets
by default and forgetting is exactly this demotion. The arrow of entropy is the
no-copy asymmetry, which is the same asymmetry as accumulated change-cost (the
Landauer floors summing) and as causation (a change enables its successor, not
its predecessor). Equivalently, entropy is accumulated un-absorbed residual: what
no grammar mints stays un-distinguished, so residual piling up is entropy rising.
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[The demon is exorcised, and local order is affordable]\label{cor:demon-life}
Because $k_{\mathrm B}$ converts entropy to energy, Landauer's
$k_{\mathrm B}T\ln 2$ is the price of \emph{resolving} one bit --- dragging one
distinction up out of the un-distinguished, or erasing one back down. Maxwell's
demon is exorcised by exactly this: it must pay to distinguish, and that payment
is the entropy it thought it removed for free \citep{bennett1982}. There is no
free distinction, hence no free entropy reduction. Yet local entropy reduction is
possible: minting resolves residual and builds structure, but it is a write, so
it is paid, and the payment is Landauer heat exported to the surroundings ---
local order bought by global entropy increase. This is Schr\"odinger's ``life
feeds on negentropy'' \citep{schrodinger1944} grounded: an organism, a learner, a
growing graph is a region that pays to mint local structure and dumps the cost as
heat. Life does not violate the second law; it is the second law with a subsidy.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[The arrows unify]\label{rem:arrows}
The thermodynamic arrow (entropy up), the causal arrow (change enables forward),
and the psychological arrow (memory is present structure of a discarded past) are
not three arrows that happen to align; they are one, the no-copy accumulation of
change. Every ``why do they agree'' puzzle in the arrow-of-time literature is
asking why three descriptions of a single asymmetry point the same way, and they
do because the asymmetry is one: change does not keep its predecessor.
\end{remark}

\section{Cosmology: the initial state, the seed, and self-location}\label{sec:cosmology}
By Graphic Equalisation's no-start-end result the event order has no intrinsic
first, so the beginning of the universe is not an intrinsic edge but a
\emph{chosen entry} --- a boundary condition imposed from the containing frame.
This is why physics supplies initial conditions separately from its laws: the
laws are the ordering, which fixes no start, so the entry must come from outside.
The Big Bang is that entry, the seed; what is ``before'' it is not an earlier
time but the frontier, the pre-relational substrate, unrepresentable from within,
the same ``\,?\,'' that sits below the Planck scale (Section~\ref{sec:planck}).

\begin{proposition}[The Past Hypothesis is a seed, hence a hypothesis]\label{prop:past-hyp}
The low-entropy initial state from which the second law takes its direction ---
the Past Hypothesis --- is a seed: the initial rule-and-state configuration
installed from the container. Its long-standing status as an assumption rather
than a derivation is therefore correct in kind, since a seed cannot be derived
from within (a mint cannot authorise its own installation). The arrow of time
then splits cleanly: its \emph{mechanism} is no-copy accumulation
(Section~\ref{sec:second-law}), and its \emph{origin} --- that there is a
low-entropy state to rise from --- is the seed. The direction points away from
the imposed low-entropy start; the running is no-copy.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Why this seed: self-location]\label{prop:self-location}
Why \emph{this} seed --- and, equally, why this $\alpha$, this gauge group --- is
answered, not declined, and the answer is self-location. A map is referenced from
``you are here,'' and the seed is the here: the origin the frame is drawn from,
as $c=1$ is the unit it is measured from. The observer is not independent of it
--- by frame-relative identity a different seed is a different observer --- so
``why this seed rather than another'' assumes a trans-seed observer who could
compare, and there is none (Axiom~$-1$). It is that way because we are this way,
and we are this way because it is that way: one determination read from inside,
not two facts one explaining the other.
\end{proposition}
This inverts the fine-tuning inference. The constants are not tuned \emph{to} a
pre-existing observer; the observer is what those constants produce, so the
apparent coincidence is the co-determination of observer and seed mistaken for a
tuning aimed at a target. It is the same move as being surprised that the Graph
Law applies to everything: it applies because that \emph{is} what everything,
measured, is --- a constitutive identity, not a contingent breadth, the
discriminator being independent re-derivation (Graphic Equalisation's composition
falsifier). What remains out of reach is only \emph{derivation}: no formula
computes $\alpha$ or the seed. The \emph{why} is answered; the \emph{value} is
not derived. Reference points take no ``why'' --- they are where the why is
asked.

\section{Why 3+1 dimensions, and why a rock has no time}\label{sec:dimensions}
Time is not a must. A frame whose rules are not firing --- inert, a rock --- has
no time at all: nothing happens in it, there is no succession, and in its own
frame it merely \emph{is}. What an external clock calls ``later'' is, for the
rock, only another \emph{is}; the weathering it undergoes is the \emph{supergraph's}
succession applied to it from above (admissibility being self-governance, not
armour), not the rock's own. Time belongs to active, inferring frames --- those
with a firing closure loop to iterate (Graphic Equalisation's cycle-unit) and the
inference to represent the succession. The rock has neither, so it has no time;
its rules exist but are inert.

\begin{proposition}[The observed time is one-dimensional]\label{prop:one-time}
Every time we have observed is one-dimensional, and self-change gives a reason it
tends that way: change is self-change on one structure, strictly ordered --- no
two of a frame's own changes are concurrent --- so a frame lays down one
succession at a time. But this is a tendency, not a theorem. Independent
successions are no more prohibited than four spatial dimensions are; what selects
the single observed time axis is the same filter that selects three spatial
dimensions --- decoherence and stability --- not a necessity. So one-dimensional
time is observed and selected, not forced; multi-dimensional time is unobserved,
not forbidden. Time itself remains optional, absent from every inert frame.
\end{proposition}

Space is $N$-dimensional by contingency: the number of positional coordinates the
rules address is rule-content, so ``why $N$'' is ``why this rule-set,'' answered
by self-location (Section~\ref{sec:cosmology}). Why $N=3$ specifically is that
self-location filtered by stability --- Ehrenfest's result that stable orbits and
stable atoms exist only in three spatial dimensions \citep{ehrenfest1917}, so
observers occur in the three-dimensional rule-sets and we are the observers such a
rule-set produces. And the $3+1$ \emph{signature}, time set apart from space, is
the succession/position distinction: time is the change-axis, the one succession,
space the config-axes, the many positions; one time and $N$ space is
one-succession-and-many-positions read as a signature.

Honest boundary: time is not necessary (an inert frame has none); the time we
observe is $1$-dimensional, decoherence-selected rather than forced
(multi-dimensional time is unobserved, not prohibited); space is $N$-dimensional
contingently, $N=3$ by self-location and stability, not by necessity; and the
metric signature is the succession/position distinction as a shape, not a derived
quantity.

The contingent-not-necessary status of these verdicts --- and of the gauge group,
the dimensionless constants, the arrow, and the value of $c$ --- is licensed by the
minimal-universe argument: a rock is a universe, and the minimal universe does
without them, so none can be compulsory. That argument, together with the run/record
axis, free will, personal identity, and death, is developed in the companion paper
\emph{The Law of Graphic Equalisation --- Mind and Self Grounding}, which treats
philosophy of mind as applied physics of the run.

\section{Identical particles are functional identity}\label{sec:identical}
Graphic Equalisation's Axiom~$-1$ holds that two things with no difference that
makes a difference are the same, and its no-frame-free-identity theorem that one
cannot say which of two is which independently of an individuating frame. This is
the quantum treatment of identical particles exactly: two electrons carry no
label, and ``which is which'' names no difference and so has no content.
Classical physics labels identical objects --- treats two identical bodies as
distinct in principle --- and that label is a fiction, a distinction imposed
where none exists.

\begin{proposition}[Quantum statistics, and the Gibbs paradox, are functional identity]\label{prop:gibbs}
Classical Maxwell--Boltzmann counting labels identical particles and over-counts
arrangements; quantum Bose--Einstein and Fermi--Dirac counting treats them as one
where they are indistinguishable. The Gibbs paradox --- the spurious entropy of
mixing two identical gases --- resolves by not labelling them, which is
Axiom~$-1$, and ties to Section~\ref{sec:second-law}: entropy is what a frame
distinguishes, so identical particles, having nothing to distinguish, add no
mixing entropy. The $1/N!$ Gibbs correction is functional identity made
quantitative.
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Pauli exclusion is ``distinct things need a distinction'']\label{cor:pauli}
If two fermions were in the same state --- the same in every quantum number ---
they would carry no distinguishing distinction, and by Axiom~$-1$ would be one,
not two. To be two they must differ somewhere. The exclusion principle is
therefore the requirement that distinct things be distinguishable: there is no
such thing as two indistinguishables in one state, because that ``two'' names no
difference. Exclusion is the arithmetic of identity, not a force.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[The honest limit: spin--statistics]\label{rem:spin-stat}
Indistinguishability and fermion exclusion are clean here; the boson side is not.
Why bosons pile into one state as a count while fermions cap at one, and the
minus sign under exchange tied to a $2\pi$ rotation, is the spin--statistics
theorem, which needs structure this account does not supply. The framework
grounds \emph{that} particles are indistinguishable and \emph{why} fermions
exclude, and hands the boson/sign/spin--statistics content back untouched --- the
same shape/number division as elsewhere.
\end{remark}

\section{Positioning and prior art}\label{sec:prior-art}
The pieces are individually established; the contribution is the conjunction
under one change-economy and the mechanisation of shapes prior art postulates,
geometrises, or offers heuristically.

\paragraph{Deriving $c$, and mass as internal motion.} That $c$ should be
\emph{derived} rather than postulated has precedent by a different route.
Ignatowsky \citep{ignatowsky1911} and, cleanly, L\'evy-Leblond
\citep{levyleblond1976} obtain the Lorentz transformation from the relativity
principle together with homogeneity, isotropy, and group structure alone, with an
invariant speed \emph{emerging} rather than assumed and its value left empirical;
those derivations use symmetry as the engine where the present one uses the
change-substrate --- $c$ as each frame's own change-throughput unit, $1$ by
construction. The reading of mass as the budget reserved for internal change has
two standing precedents, offered heuristically rather than mechanised:
Schr\"odinger's \emph{zitterbewegung}, the electron's rest energy as internal
motion at $c$ \citep{schrodinger1930}, developed as a rest-mass-from-internal-
circulation interpretation by Hestenes \citep{hestenes1990}; and the elementary
result that confined massless radiation --- light in a box --- carries rest mass
$E/c^{2}$, internalised $c$-motion appearing as inertia. The budget-split itself
is the constant-magnitude four-velocity of Minkowski geometry
\citep{minkowski1908}.

\paragraph{Relativity as postulate.} Einstein's special \citep{einstein1905} and
general \citep{einstein1916} relativity take $c$-invariance and the equivalence
principle as postulates; the present account renders the first a theorem
(Proposition~\ref{prop:c-unit}) and the second a common cause
(Section~\ref{sec:sr-gr}), at the cost of claiming only the shapes.

\paragraph{Entropy and the demon.} The observer-relative reading of entropy is
Jaynes's \citep{jaynes1957}; the resolution of Maxwell's demon by the cost of
information is Landauer's and Bennett's \citep{landauer1961,bennett1982}; life as
locally paid negentropy is Schr\"odinger's \citep{schrodinger1944}. The present
account ties all three to one substrate law --- cost is the write.

\paragraph{Dimensional constants.} That the count of fundamental \emph{dimensional}
constants is conventional is Duff's position in the Duff--Okun--Veneziano exchange
\citep{duff2002}; the present account gives the reason (they are frame-units) and
draws the boundary at the dimensionless remainder.

\section{Falsifiability commitments}\label{sec:falsifiability}
Each is exposed to attack; the scope caveat of Section~\ref{sec:scope} applies
throughout.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{$c$ as frame-unit.} Fails if a frame can measure its own base
change-rate as anything other than $1$, or if $c$-invariance requires a postulate
that cannot be recovered as the unit reading.
\item \textbf{One budget, two dilations.} Fails if velocity time dilation and
gravitational time dilation cannot be exhibited as the same fixed budget spent on
motion and on change-load respectively --- if the equivalence principle is not
their common cause here.
\item \textbf{Dimensional/dimensionless boundary.} Fails if a genuinely
dimensionless constant can be set to $1$ by unit choice, or if a dimensional
constant carries an irreducible value not absorbable into a frame's units.
\item \textbf{Second law as no-copy.} Fails if entropy can rise in a frame whose
changes preserve their predecessors, or fall in one without a paid write
exporting cost.
\item \textbf{Indistinguishability is functional identity.} Fails if identical
particles carry a frame-free label distinguishing them, or if the Gibbs $1/N!$
correction and Fermi exclusion cannot be read as ``distinct things require a
distinguishing distinction.'' (The boson/spin--statistics content is not
claimed.)
\item \textbf{Measurement is frame-relative collapse.} Fails if a
frame-independent fact about whether collapse has occurred is required to
describe a Wigner's-friend scenario --- if the friend's outcome and the outside
frame's superposition cannot both stand as the event and the map of two frames.
(The Born-rule probabilities are not claimed.)
\item \textbf{Entanglement is a superframe dimension.} Fails if the correlation
of an entangled pair is a subframe-local quantity rather than a joint dimension
\textsc{null} in each subframe, or if a reduced single-particle state can be pure
--- i.e.\ if the mixedness is not the inaccessibility of a superframe dimension.
(The Tsirelson bound and correlation strengths are not claimed.)
\item \textbf{Conservation is the cost side of symmetry.} Fails if a continuous
symmetry (a residual-free transformation) carries no conserved quantity, or if a
conserved quantity persists across a genuine change in the rules --- in
particular, if global energy is conserved in a time-dependent (rules-changing)
regime such as cosmic expansion. (The gauge group is claimed to be contingent,
not derived.)
\item \textbf{The initial state is a seed, answered by self-location.} Fails if
``why this seed / constant / gauge group'' can be posed from a trans-frame
standpoint that could have obtained a different one --- if there is an
observer-independent ``we'' separable from the seed. (The claim is that the
\emph{why} is self-location and the \emph{value} is not derived; both together,
not either alone.)
\item \textbf{Amplitude is directional Narrative; Born reduces to linearity.} Fails
if the complex amplitude cannot be identified with a magnitude-times-direction
operative-history structure, or if the Born square admits no closed-loop (conjugate)
reading. The attempted closure predicts more sharply: given complex, \emph{linearly
superposing} amplitudes and no privileged phase, $|a|^{2}$ is forced (by
phase-independence plus the additivity of decohered alternatives), so the claim
fails if any of those premises holds yet the probability is not the squared modulus.
What is \emph{not} claimed: linearity is derived --- it has a candidate mechanism
(free evolution is the empty-rule-set Narrative, whose components are uncoupled by any
rule, hence superpose), but the rigorous additivity and norm-preservation of the free
map, and Gleason's multi-context uniqueness, remain the residue. The open question has
moved from ``why the square'' to ``why the free map is exactly additive and unitary,'' and
the Hilbert structure itself is routed through the informational reconstructions (the inner
product as cross-frame projection, purification as entanglement-as-superframe): the residue
is then a mapping to those axioms plus cited theorems, not an assumed structure. It fails if
that mapping cannot be made good.
\item \textbf{Interference is possibility plus superframe forcing.} Fails if
interference requires the particle's own admissibility to select among paths, or
if a which-path coupling that collapses before the screen does not remove the
fringes --- i.e.\ if superposition is not the possibility structure and the
superframe does not apply the combination without passing through the subframe's
grammar.
\item \textbf{Time is optional; the observed time is one-dimensional.} Fails if
an inert frame (no firing rules) nonetheless has an in-frame succession. (That the
observed time is $1$-D is claimed decoherence-selected, not forced ---
multi-dimensional time is unobserved, not prohibited; that space is $N$-D with
$N=3$ is likewise contingent and self-located. The minimal-universe licence, the
block universe, free will, personal identity, and death are treated in the
companion mind paper and carry their falsifiers there.)
\item \textbf{Scope.} The paper claims shapes only; it is falsified as
\emph{overclaiming} if any passage is read to derive a field equation, a
dimensionless constant, or a quantum-gravity result --- none is asserted.
\end{enumerate}
