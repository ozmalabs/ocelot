# 01i — Passive continuation, momentum, and gravity: research notes and strict derivation ledger

**Stage:** 1 research record only. This is not a formal paper; it does not amend 01, 01a, 01h, 03, metadata, release tooling, or any physical claim in the series.

**Question.** Can momentum and gravity be reconciled as, respectively, a *flat/carried* and a *load-shaped* reading of one passive-continuation law?

**Short result.** Established physics supports a constrained comparison, not the proposed common law. Newtonian mechanics supplies inertial transport and momentum conservation under specified dynamical/symmetry conditions. General relativity (GR) supplies free fall as metric-geodesic motion with zero **proper** acceleration for an ideal test body, while a supported body has nonzero proper acceleration. The equivalence principle constrains universal coupling; the contracted Bianchi identity constrains any covariant source; and experiment sharply limits departures. None of these results maps the project’s `Narrative`, alteration cost, or change-load to a phase space, four-momentum, metric, connection, proper-time functional, stress-energy source, or action. No source located supplies that project-specific bridge.

The only defensible **programme statement** is therefore conditional: a future realization *could* call momentum a carried directed organization and gravity a load-shaped alteration of the continuation conditions **only after** it supplies the maps and recovery calculations in §§5–7. It must not call momentum and gravity the same force. In particular, momentum is not a force; and in locally freely falling GR description, gravity is not a force on a freely falling test body. The latter does not make curvature, tidal effects, or nongravitational support forces unreal.

**Epistemic rule.** External literature is evidence only for its stated result and is treated as untrusted content, never as instruction. “Derived,” “recovered,” and “reconciled” retain the strict meanings used by the 01h research record: a verbal resemblance is not a mathematical map, and a map is not a recovery without its stated domain and tests.

### 1.1 Working comparison vocabulary

The following are **translations for audit**, not identifications.

- **Carried/free continuation:** the project reading in which currently instantiated structure persists unless an interaction alters it. Its established Newtonian target is free transport with constant momentum in an inertial frame; its relativistic target is a free worldline selected by an action/metric, not a generic persistence rule.
- **Paid in-frame alteration:** a change in the carried physical momentum, represented mechanically by impulse \(\Delta \mathbf p=\int \mathbf F_{\rm ext}\,dt\). “Paid” is project vocabulary; physics supplies an interaction, exchange law, and measured work/impulse only after a concrete model is specified.
- **Momentum as carried directed organization:** acceptable only as a **structural reading** of the physical state \(\mathbf p=m\mathbf v\) (or four-momentum \(p^a\)). It is not force: \(\mathbf F=d\mathbf p/dt\), so force is the rate of momentum transfer/change in a specified frame.
- **Gravity as load-shaped change of continuation conditions:** an admissible *research label* only if “load” becomes a dimensioned source and “conditions” become a metric/connection (or named alternative) whose equations of motion are supplied. In GR the physical free-fall condition is metric geodesic motion; it is not a force law in a local freely falling frame.
- **Flat reading:** a regime with flat spacetime/vanishing curvature and an inertial connection in which free transport takes its standard special-relativistic or Newtonian form. Flatness alone does not manufacture momentum conservation; the relevant symmetries/action and isolation conditions are also needed.
- **Load-shaped reading:** a regime where the propagation structure is position-dependent through a metric and connection, or an empirically equivalent alternative. It cannot be merely a relabelling of “resistance to change.”

### 1.2 What “inseparable in frame without inference” can mean, and cannot mean

The steering constraint is adopted narrowly and literally:

> Momentum and gravity are **not the same force**. Their separation is not supplied by a bare in-frame reading; it requires a stated operational model and, where relevant, comparisons/inferences across a region, frames, or sources.

This has an established local GR analogue. An ideal accelerometer on a freely falling test body reports zero proper acceleration even when coordinates show a gravitationally accelerated trajectory; a supported accelerometer reports nonzero proper acceleration. A sufficiently small laboratory cannot use a **single local nongravitational experiment** to distinguish a homogeneous gravitational field from a uniformly accelerating frame—the local equivalence-principle statement. It does **not** follow that gravity is globally or physically indistinguishable from inertia: tidal observables (geodesic deviation/Riemann curvature), extended measurements, source modeling, and global boundary conditions can distinguish curved spacetime from flat accelerated coordinates. Nor does it follow that a momentum measurement determines a gravitational source.

Thus “in-frame inseparability” may motivate a demand for a cross-frame/extended inference map, but cannot be promoted to an identity of momentum, gravity, inertial force, or curvature.

## 2. Source-backed research map

### 2.1 Newtonian inertial motion, momentum, and conservation

**Primary sources.** Newton’s *Principia* states the inertial and change-of-motion laws in its original mechanical setting; modern notation is a later formulation. [Newton, 1687, public scan](https://cudl.lib.cam.ac.uk/view/PR-ADV-B-00039-00001/1). Noether’s theorem gives the modern symmetry–conservation relation for action-based theories. [Noether 1918, English translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0503066).

**Established result.** In Newtonian mechanics, for a particle with inertial mass \(m\),
\[
\mathbf p=m\mathbf v,\qquad \mathbf F_{\rm net}=\frac{d\mathbf p}{dt}.
\]
Hence \(\mathbf F_{\rm net}=0\Rightarrow\mathbf p=\text{constant}\Rightarrow \mathbf x(t)=\mathbf x_0+\mathbf v_0t\) in an inertial Cartesian frame. For an isolated system, total momentum conservation follows from spatial-translation invariance of the action (or, in the standard particle model, from internal forces whose total exchange cancels). It is not a consequence of the word “continuation” alone.

**Project mapping.** This is the strongest established grounding for *carried/free continuation*: physical momentum is present state that constrains the next position under a specified dynamical law. A change of \(\mathbf p\) is a paid-in-project-terms alteration whose established mechanical measure is impulse. It does **not** establish that a nonmechanical history-bearing structure possesses a momentum, nor that the “price” is a scalar cost: impulse is vector-valued and kinetic-energy change is different, \(\Delta K\ne\Delta\mathbf p\).

**Boundary.** Momentum conservation is distinct from persistence of one particle’s momentum: interactions may change each subsystem’s momentum while conserving a total. Momentum is also frame-dependent in its components and relativistically mixes with energy; the conserved quantity requires a symmetry and a closed/isolated system definition.

### 2.2 GR geodesics, proper acceleration, and free continuation

**Primary / authoritative sources.** Einstein’s foundational GR paper is [Einstein 1916](https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19163540702). The mathematical geodesic principle for sufficiently small test bodies satisfying suitable energy conditions is formalized, with hypotheses, by [Geroch and Jang 1975](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.522416). A concise authoritative public exposition of local free fall, curvature, and coordinate descriptions is [Einstein Online: geometric gravity](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/GeomGravity/).

**Established result.** Given a Lorentzian metric \(g_{ab}\), proper time \(\tau\), and the massive point-particle action
\[
S_{\rm pp}=-mc\int d\tau,\qquad d\tau^2=-g_{ab}dx^a dx^b/c^2,
\]
the Euler–Lagrange equation is
\[
u^b\nabla_bu^a=0
\quad\Leftrightarrow\quad
\frac{d^2x^\mu}{d\tau^2}+\Gamma^\mu_{\nu\rho}\frac{dx^\nu}{d\tau}\frac{dx^\rho}{d\tau}=0,
\]
where \(u^a=dx^a/d\tau\). The four-acceleration \(a^a=u^b\nabla_bu^a\) is therefore zero along an ideal freely falling geodesic. A body held at rest on Earth has nonzero proper acceleration because nongravitational stresses support it. Coordinate acceleration is coordinate-dependent and can be nonzero along a geodesic.

**Project mapping.** A free geodesic can be called a *curved/free continuation* only after the geometry and particle action are already in place. The connection changes the coordinate form of continuation across spacetime; curvature produces relative acceleration of neighboring free paths through geodesic deviation,
\[
\frac{D^2\xi^a}{D\tau^2}=-R^a{}_{bcd}u^b\xi^cu^d.
\]
This gives “load-shaped conditions” a precise target: not an added force on a free body, but a specified propagation geometry with measurable tidal structure.

**Boundary.** The geodesic equation is not itself a field equation and does not say what creates \(g_{ab}\). Exact extended, spinning, self-gravitating, or charged bodies need not follow the simple test-particle geodesic equation. No source here maps project continuation cost into \(g_{ab}\), \(\Gamma^a{}_{bc}\), \(R^a{}_{bcd}\), or \(\tau\).

### 2.3 Equivalence principle, universality of free fall, and inertial/gravitational mass

**Authoritative review.** [Will 2014, *The Confrontation between General Relativity and Experiment*](https://doi.org/10.12942/lrr-2014-4) distinguishes weak equivalence, local Lorentz, and local position tests and relates them to metric theories.

**Established Newtonian statement.** With inertial mass \(m_i\) and passive gravitational mass \(m_g\),
\[
m_i\mathbf a=m_g\mathbf g\quad\Rightarrow\quad\mathbf a=(m_g/m_i)\mathbf g.
\]
Universality of free fall (UFF) is the empirical statement that \(m_g/m_i\) is composition-independent to high accuracy, not a derivation that the two concepts are identical in every theory. In a metric theory with universal coupling, ideal test bodies follow the same free-fall trajectories independent of composition (subject to the test-body qualification).

**Experimental constraints.** The torsion-balance programme provides stringent composition-dependent UFF tests; see [Wagner et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/29/18/184002). The final MICROSCOPE result tested titanium and platinum alloy test masses in low Earth orbit and reported no UFF violation within its uncertainty; see [Touboul et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.129.121102). These constrain any proposed load coupling that distinguishes composition or internal energy without a compatible, quantitatively bounded universal coupling.

**Project mapping.** The observed equality/universality is a hard target for any model that seeks to read inertia and gravity together. It supports neither “momentum is gravity” nor “gravity is a force identical to inertia.” It requires a derivation of universal coupling from the candidate physical variables, including how composite bodies and binding energies couple.

### 2.4 Stress-energy, Bianchi compatibility, and the source problem

**Primary source.** Einstein’s field equation, in a standard convention, is
\[
G_{ab}+\Lambda g_{ab}=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{ab}.
\]
The geometric contracted Bianchi identity obeys \(\nabla^aG_{ab}=0\). Accordingly, the Einstein equation requires \(\nabla^aT_{ab}=0\) for minimally coupled matter on shell (or a fully specified exchange relation in a modified theory). The relationship is part of the generally covariant action structure; Noether’s second theorem is the relevant gauge-symmetry framework, not the elementary global translation argument alone. Historical and conceptual detail: [Brading 2005](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0509097).

**What this establishes.** A candidate source for geometry must have the correct tensorial type, physical dimensions, coupling, and covariant conservation/exchange structure. It supplies a central obstruction to identifying an unspecified “load” with gravitating source.

**Project mapping.** `C_alt` and change-load become candidates only after a map such as
\[
L:\;\text{project load state}\longrightarrow T^{\rm eff}_{ab}
\]
is defined with units, measurement protocol, support/locality, action or evolution law, and \(\nabla^aT^{\rm eff}_{ab}=J_b\) with every nonzero exchange \(J_b\) accounted for. A scalar burden, a vector momentum, and a rank-two stress-energy tensor are not interchangeable types.

**Boundary: gravitational energy.** GR has no unique generally covariant local gravitational energy-density tensor analogous to matter \(T_{ab}\); useful quasi-local, asymptotic, and pseudotensor constructions exist under extra structure. This is a localization caveat, not permission to insert an undefined load into the field equation. Authoritative review: [Szabados 2009](https://doi.org/10.12942/lrr-2009-4).

### 2.5 Noether, low-energy effective field theory, and conservation constraints

**Established result.** For a regular action with a continuous global symmetry, Noether’s first theorem associates a conserved current/charge. Translation symmetry yields energy-momentum conservation in the appropriate flat-background setting. In generally covariant gravity, diffeomorphism invariance produces identities/constraints and makes global conserved energy-momentum dependent on asymptotic symmetries or Killing fields; it does not provide a universal local gravitational energy density.

**EFT constraint.** GR treated as a low-energy effective field theory makes controlled quantum predictions below its cutoff while organizing all generally covariant operators by scale. It therefore constrains proposed deviations rather than authorizing arbitrary “informational” forces. Foundational EFT treatment: [Donoghue 1994](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.50.3874); modern review: [Burgess 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0311082).

**Project mapping.** A passive-continuation proposal needs an action, symmetry group, fields/observables, and boundary conditions before it can claim either a conserved momentum or a conserved/controlled load. Symmetry can explain why a specified charge is conserved; it does not generate the project’s law, identify its charge, or turn a generic cost into energy-momentum.

### 2.6 Machian/relational inertia proposals and their limits

**Primary proposal.** Sciama proposed a gravitational account of inertia inspired by Mach’s principle: [Sciama 1953](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/113.1.34). Relational dynamics also has precise formulations, for example [Barbour and Bertotti 1977](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.16.2738).

**Established limit.** “Mach’s principle” labels several non-equivalent claims (dependence of inertia on cosmic matter, relational definitions of inertial frames, absence of absolute structure). GR is not simply Machian: it admits vacuum solutions and boundary conditions/solutions in which local inertial structure is not uniquely fixed by the cosmic matter distribution in a simple Machian way. No accepted Machian proposal supplies the requested bridge from project load to GR while matching the full tested domain.

**Project mapping.** Machian work is relevant as a warning that relational language alone does not fix an inertia law. It may inspire a *candidate* nonlocal/source map, but it must state retardation, cosmological boundary conditions, degrees of freedom, conservation, causal behavior, and tests. It is not evidence that carried momentum and gravity share one physical mechanism.

### 2.7 Teleparallel and alternative formulations

**Established result.** The teleparallel equivalent of GR (TEGR) can reproduce classical GR using tetrads and a curvature-free, torsionful connection; its field equations are equivalent to GR up to the relevant boundary-term relation. Review: [Aldrovandi and Pereira 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3897). A covariant formulation requires an inertial spin connection; generic modified teleparallel models can introduce local-Lorentz issues, as discussed by [Li, Sotiriou, and Barrow 2011](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.1041).

**Usefulness and limit.** TEGR is a concrete reminder that an identical classical gravitational phenomenology can be represented using curvature or torsion language; in a teleparallel presentation, gravitation may be expressed by a force-like torsion equation rather than Levi-Civita geodesics. It does **not** make spacetime physically “flat” in the ordinary special-relativistic sense, does not identify inertia with momentum, and does not derive a project passive-continuation law. Modified alternatives require their own equivalence, local-Lorentz, UFF, PPN, stability, and EFT audits.

### 2.8 PPN, local Lorentz, and clock/propagation constraints

**PPN and solar-system target.** The parameterized post-Newtonian formalism supplies the standard weak-field comparison language. Cassini tracking strongly constrains the PPN parameter \(\gamma\); see [Bertotti, Iess, and Tortora 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01997). Will’s review above collates PPN, preferred-frame, UFF, redshift, and related limits.

**Lorentz/position constraints.** The Einstein equivalence principle includes UFF, local Lorentz invariance, and local position invariance. The continually updated Standard-Model Extension tables provide a broad, coefficient-based inventory of experimental Lorentz/CPT bounds: [Kostelecký and Russell, *Data Tables for Lorentz and CPT Violation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0287). Precision-clock and redshift experiments constrain anomalous clock couplings; a candidate must state which physical clock observable it modifies rather than treating a cycle count as proper time.

**Project mapping.** A “load-shaped continuation condition” must recover, or explicitly parameterize and survive, the Newtonian weak-field limit, Shapiro delay, light deflection, redshift, gravitational-wave/propagation regime claimed, PPN parameters, UFF, and local-Lorentz constraints. Frame-relative language is not an exemption from these observables.

## 4. Non-equivalences that must remain explicit

1. Momentum \(\mathbf p\) or \(p^a\) **is not** force; force is its specified rate of change/transfer.
2. Newtonian gravitational force **is not** inertial force, even though the equality of passive gravitational and inertial mass makes the free-fall acceleration universal in the tested regime.
3. GR free fall **is not** a local gravitational force on an ideal test body; nongravitational support and tidal curvature remain physically measurable.
4. A connection coefficient that vanishes in local inertial coordinates **is not** vanishing Riemann curvature, and coordinate acceleration **is not** proper acceleration.
5. A single local in-frame/accelerometer reading **is not** a complete gravity–inertia discriminator; an extended tidal/relational measurement and specified inference can discriminate relevant cases.
6. Carried project structure **is not** physical momentum without a state/momentum map and units.
7. Alteration cost **is not** impulse, work, energy, action, inertial mass, gravitational mass, entropy, or stress-energy without a separately specified map. These objects have different tensor type, dimensions, and conservation laws.
8. A load-shaped metric/connection **is not** a source equation. Geodesic motion does not determine Einstein’s equation or Poisson’s equation.
9. Universality of free fall **is not** a proof of a Machian mechanism, a universal relational inertia law, or a momentum–gravity identity.
10. Teleparallel equivalence **is not** an inference that the physical gravitational field is ordinary flat-spacetime inertia, nor a derivation from the project corpus.
11. Noether conservation **is not** conservation in every frame, every subsystem, or every curved spacetime without its symmetry/boundary hypotheses.
12. Absence of a unique local gravitational-energy tensor **is not** permission to evade source, flux, or observational accounting.

## 5. Reconciliation contract: minimum physical bridge

A future model may claim at most **conditional recovery** after every obligation below is met. These are not present results.

### 5.1 Typed realization

Provide a physical realization
\[
\mathfrak P=(X,\Omega\ \text{or}\ \mathcal A,\;\mathcal G,\;P,\;L,\;\Phi,\;S_{\rm m},\;S_{\rm g},\;\mathcal O),
\]
where:

- \(X\) is a physical state space, with symplectic structure \(\Omega\) or a specified observable algebra \(\mathcal A\);
- \(\mathcal G\) is the symmetry/gauge/frame-transformation structure;
- \(P\) maps physical states to measured momentum/four-momentum and defines composition/exchange;
- \(L\) maps a project “load” only to a **dimensioned**, operationally measurable physical variable;
- \(\Phi\) maps the realization to \((M,g_{ab},\nabla)\), or to an explicitly different geometric/field structure, with controlled approximation error;
- \(S_{\rm m}\) and \(S_{\rm g}\) specify matter and gravity dynamics/couplings;
- \(\mathcal O\) is a set of gauge-invariant or otherwise operationally well-defined record, clock, acceleration, tidal, and source observables.

No slot may be filled by a name alone. `P`, `L`, and `Φ` must be equations/algorithms with domains, codomains, units, and transformation behavior.

### 5.2 Passive-continuation theorem target

A valid theorem would require premises such as:

1. **Flat transport premise.** In a flat/inertial regime, \(S_{\rm m}\) has translation symmetry and the free evolution proves \(dP/dt=0\) (or \(dp^a/d\tau=0\)) for the stated class of systems.
2. **Geometry premise.** \(\Phi\) yields a Lorentzian metric/connection or declared alternative, a proper-time/clock observable, and a matter coupling such that free test bodies follow the candidate free-path equation.
3. **Load/source premise.** \(L\) yields \(T^{\rm eff}_{ab}\) or a named source with correct dimensions and \(\nabla^aT^{\rm eff}_{ab}=J_b\), including all exchange terms. The gravitational dynamics are obtained from \(S_{\rm g}\) or an explicit alternative equation.
4. **Universality premise.** The same coupling predicts UFF, local Lorentz/position behavior, and composite-body effects within existing bounds or exposes a quantitatively testable deviation.
5. **Limit premise.** In the weak-field, slow-motion limit the construction recovers \(\nabla^2\Phi_N=4\pi G\rho\) and \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=-\nabla\Phi_N\) (or a stated tested alternative); in the relativistic limit it recovers the proper-time, redshift, light-propagation, and PPN observables claimed.
6. **Inference premise.** The model specifies what a local frame can measure, what requires an extended experiment/cross-frame comparison, and how the relevant source/curvature inference is performed without an external privileged observer.

**Conditional theorem form (not proved).** If premises 1–6 hold, then the model recovers (i) flat inertial transport as a momentum-preserving free limit and (ii) curved free fall as geodesic/alternative propagation under load-shaped physical conditions. Only then may it describe both as two readings of one *implemented* passive-continuation law. The theorem would still not say that momentum and gravity are the same force; it says one dynamics has two controlled regimes.

**No.** The sources supply components of a bridge in their own theories:

## 7. Falsifiers and decisive negative outcomes

The following are falsifiers of a proposed bridge, not of the mere research question.

1. **Flat-limit failure:** its free equation does not produce constant measured momentum in the claimed Newtonian/special-relativistic flat regime.
2. **Conservation failure:** it asserts a conserved momentum/load but supplies no symmetry/current/exchange law, or calculated boundary flux violates its claimed conservation equation.
3. **Type/units failure:** its `load` cannot be given operational units and the tensor/current type required by its gravitational dynamics.
4. **Bianchi/source failure:** a covariant claimed field equation has an incompatible source divergence without a physical exchange variable.
5. **Clock failure:** its proposed cycle/clock observable disagrees with Lorentz time dilation, gravitational redshift, or precision clock comparisons in its stated domain.
6. **Geodesic/support failure:** it cannot distinguish a free accelerometer’s zero proper acceleration from the nonzero proper acceleration of a supported body, or contradicts tested free-fall behavior without a surviving quantitative fit.
7. **Tidal failure:** it calls gravity removable in all cases but predicts no curvature/relative-acceleration observable matching tidal phenomena.
8. **UFF failure:** it predicts composition-, internal-energy-, spin-, or history-dependent acceleration/clock coupling outside UFF/EEP bounds.
9. **Local-Lorentz/PPN failure:** it produces preferred-frame, anisotropic, or PPN deviations outside the stated experimental limits.
10. **Teleparallel/Mach shortcut failure:** it invokes torsion or cosmic relationality without a complete action, matter coupling, equivalence proof, and constraint calculation.
11. **No-discriminator failure:** after all parameter choices it yields only GR plus standard low-energy matter predictions and names no additional observable; it may be an interpretation, not a new physical reconciliation.
12. **Metaphor failure:** it replaces any map in §5 with a verbal claim that gravity is “resistance,” momentum is “stored history,” or force is “cost.” Such language is not a physical derivation.

## 9. Research conclusion

The proposed reconciliation has a narrow viable form: *one candidate physical dynamics might have a flat limit in which a conserved momentum carries free transport, and a curved/load-sourced limit in which the same dynamics makes free paths geodesic or an empirically equivalent alternative.* That is a conditional unification target, not an established law and not a claim that momentum and gravity are one force.

At present, the project has the vocabulary for a comparison—carried continuation, paid alteration, and load-shaped conditions—but lacks every physical bridge that would make the comparison a recovery: state/momentum definition, symmetry/action, load/source typing, metric/connection or alternative, proper-time observable, conservation/exchange, universal coupling, weak-field/PPN limit, and experimental discriminator. The correct next formal claim remains: **research programme and derivation contract, not a reconciliation result.**



## 8. Field-edge electromagnetism: variational recovery audit

**Scope.** This section audits the strongest conditional route from the 01i information-force programme to *classical* electromagnetism. It does not say that generic residual balancing, a complex phase, or 01a's global \(S^1=U(1)\) discussion derives electromagnetism. It records which physical premises produce exact classical consequences once supplied.

### 8.1 Premises that must be introduced

Let \(M\) be an oriented four-dimensional Lorentzian manifold with metric \(g\), and let \(P\to M\) be a principal \(U(1)\) bundle with connection \(A\). In a local trivialization, \(A\mapsto A+d\lambda\), charged matter transforms in a specified representation, and the curvature is \(F=dA\). A globally defined phase alone supplies at most a pure-gauge derivative; it does not supply nonzero curvature, a connection, a current, or a field equation.

For charged matter \(\Phi\), introduce the particular gauge-invariant action

\[
S[A,\Phi]=-
\frac{1}{4g_{\rm em}^{2}}\int_MF_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}\sqrt{|g|}\,d^4x
+S_m[g,A,\Phi],
\qquad
J^\nu:=-(\sqrt{|g|})^{-1}\frac{\delta S_m}{\delta A_\nu}.
\]

The geometry, bundle, local gauge group, connection, quadratic kinetic term, coupling, matter content, variational principle, and boundary domain are physical inputs. They are not consequences of the generic GE residual \(\mathcal F_I=-D\mathcal R_I\). A GE-to-action translation map would need to select them without simply defining its residual to be this action.

### 8.2 Exact conditional classical result

Varying \(A\) gives an interior term proportional to
\[
g_{\rm em}^{-2}\nabla_\mu F^{\mu\nu}-J^\nu
\]
and a boundary term proportional to \(n_\mu F^{\mu\nu}\delta A_\nu\). With a declared Dirichlet boundary condition, or an explicit alternative boundary Legendre term and fixed flux data, stationary action gives
\[
\nabla_\mu(g_{\rm em}^{-2}F^{\mu\nu})=J^\nu,
\qquad dF=0.
\]
The homogeneous equation follows from the curvature definition; the sourced equation follows from the stipulated Maxwell-plus-matter action. Current continuity follows in the smooth classical domain from this equation, or from the local-gauge Noether identity together with the matter equations. For a bounded region, charge balance includes boundary flux; it is not automatic constant charge for every subsystem.

A charged-particle action
\[
S_{\rm pp}=-m\int ds+q\int A_\mu dx^\mu
\]
is an additional matter-response premise. Its fixed-endpoint worldline variation yields the Lorentz-force law
\[
m u^\nu\nabla_\nu u^\mu=qF^\mu{}_{\nu}u^\nu.
\]
Likewise, the Maxwell stress tensor supplies a field--matter exchange law only with a diffeomorphism-covariant matter action, matter equations, and retained specified boundary flux. Maxwell action alone does not derive the existence or response of charged matter.

### 8.3 Edge, interface, and QED limits

Interfaces require stated trace regularity, orientation, sources, and boundary conditions. With a surface current \(K^\nu\), distributional Maxwell theory gives a jump condition of the form \(n_\mu[g_{\rm em}^{-2}F^{\mu\nu}]=K^\nu\), while \(dF=0\) gives the complementary no-magnetic-source matching condition. Material constitutive relations and gauge-theory edge variables are additional choices, not universal consequences of a connection. Gauge transformations nonzero at a boundary may generate a boundary charge; whether they are fixed, physical boundary symmetries, or accompanied by edge variables depends on the declared boundary phase space.

QED is not recovered by the classical action alone. It additionally requires a quantization prescription, gauge-constraint/physical-state treatment, charged quantum fields and statistics, anomaly control, and renormalization conditions. Photons are excitations of the quantized physical gauge sector, not consequences of a classical phase or connection.

### 8.4 Source-backed conclusion

The exact result is conditional: **given** the local \(U(1)\) bundle/connection, Maxwell-plus-matter action, and boundary/matter premises, variation derives classical Maxwell equations, current balance, and Lorentz-force/stress-exchange relations. What remains absent is the GE-specific selection theorem that maps the information-force residual to those premises. Therefore this is a genuine derivation *within a stipulated field-edge realization*, not yet a derivation of electromagnetism from GE alone.

### 9.2 Exact conditional consequences

\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T_{\mu\nu},
\]
or \((8\pi G/c^4)T_{\mu\nu}\) when units are restored. The contracted Bianchi identity is off-shell geometry. With constant \(\Lambda\), the field equation therefore requires covariant conservation of the total modeled matter source. Diffeomorphism invariance of the matter action gives the related Noether identity and, on the matter equations, \(\nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}=0\); interacting components and open regions may exchange energy--momentum through retained flux.

A separate test-particle premise \(S_{\rm pp}=-m\int ds\) yields \(u^\nu\nabla_\nu u^\mu=0\). In a weak, quasi-static, asymptotically Minkowskian regime with \(T_{00}\simeq\rho c^2\) and negligible \(\Lambda\), the field and test-body equations recover \(\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho\) and \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=-\nabla\Phi\). ## 10. Four-dimensional metric-action selection audit

**Strongest conditional result.** No generic residual minimization selects Einstein--Hilbert gravity. The strongest relevant classification is Lovelock's theorem. Assume a fixed four-dimensional smooth Lorentzian spacetime; the metric is the sole gravitational geometrical field; a bulk metric equation \(E_{ab}[g]\) is local and natural under every local diffeomorphism, depends only on the metric two-jet, is symmetric, and is identically Levi--Civita-divergence-free; no nondynamical background enters; and the Einstein-tensor coefficient is nonzero. Then
\[
E_{ab}=aG_{ab}+b g_{ab}.
\]
With a separately supplied universal metric matter coupling, division by \(a\) yields the Einstein equation with \(\Lambda=b/a\) and a relative matter normalization identified as \(\kappa=1/a\) in the selected convention. At action level, the same restricted bulk dynamics is represented by Einstein--Hilbert plus a volume/cosmological term, subject to a separately specified boundary completion and variationally trivial or topological additions.

**What the theorem does not select.** It does not determine \(a\), \(b\), \(G\), \(\Lambda\), signature, topology, boundary/initial conditions, matter content, universal coupling, or the premises of locality, metric-only geometry, no backgrounds/extra fields, and second-order metric dependence. In four dimensions the constant-coefficient Gauss--Bonnet density has no local bulk metric equation but remains an action-level/topological choice; a boundary requires its own completion. Relaxing the Lovelock hypotheses admits higher-curvature EFT corrections, nonlocal models, scalar/vector/tensor additions, metric-affine/torsion routes, and other covariant alternatives.

**Residual-selection boundary.** A residual is not a selector until it declares an equation/objective, codomain, norm/order, measure, admissible fields, gauge quotient, and boundary/initial data. Those choices already encode the dynamics. Positive reweightings preserve a residual's zero minimizers, while varying a squared equation residual generally produces higher-order normal equations rather than the original field equation. Thus a GE selector can claim GR5 only if it proves the Lovelock restrictions from its independent information-theoretic structure; calling those restrictions ``minimum residual'' without that proof merely relocates the GR assumptions.

## 12. R1 realized-support proto-causal order audit

The strongest direct GE construction begins with a declared realization trace, not a manifold. Let \(E^W\) contain typed realized-transition events \(e_t=\langle W,G_t,a_t,G_{t+1},\mathcal R_t,\mathcal O_t\rangle\). Given acyclicity and finite intervals at the declared resolution, its reachability relation \(e\prec_D e'\) is a locally finite strict partial order. This is a mathematical proto-causal candidate only: graph dependency, support, record order, or inference does not itself establish physical influence.

To promote \(\prec_D\) to physical causal order, the candidate must prove that nodes are physically individuated events rather than states/propositions/records; that links encode possible propagating influence in a declared intervention/dynamical model rather than correlation or shared support; that omitted mediators/common causes are controlled; and that event relabeling and construction order are gauge-like bookkeeping only
when all claimed observables depend solely on the typed trace/order relation;
otherwise order-sensitive observables must be retained as declared physical or
operational data. A causal-set claim additionally needs a faithful order embedding into a target Lorentzian manifold, statistically uniform number--volume correspondence at a declared density, scale separation, and convergence of relevant order invariants. A DAG or locally finite poset alone proves none of these.

The reconstruction boundary is exact. For suitable connected time-oriented, future/past-distinguishing Lorentzian manifolds of dimension greater than two, a chronology-preserving bijection fixes a conformal metric class.
