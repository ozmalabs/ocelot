## PART A — THE HEADLINE DISCIPLINE: HELD-OUT PREDICTIVE VALIDATION

**This is what makes it science, not confabulation. It is the non-negotiable gate
every case-reconstruction rung must pass. It is stated before everything else
because it is the only thing that separates this line from a beautiful lie.**

### A0 — The core problem it defends against

A model flexible enough to "reproduce" anyone will fit **any** narrative. A rich
documented record + a flexible mechanistic model + human interpretive latitude is
a **garden of forking paths**: a large space of defensible fits, at least one of
which will look mechanistic, glass-box, and compelling for essentially any story
you want to tell — and be **unfalsifiable**. Researcher-degrees-of-freedom and
forking-paths work [HIGH] is precisely the account of how flexible models
manufacture apparent significance without conscious fishing; preregistration
constrains it but is ineffective against post-hoc analytic latitude unless the
fit/predict split is fixed in advance. **Glass-box legibility does not help here:
a legible account can be a confident fiction.** The substrate's own standing rule
(confabulation is the default) says the same thing from inside.

### A1 — The gate: FIT on part of the record, PREDICT the held-out part — [HIGH]

The ONLY thing that makes a reconstruction real: **fit the model on PART of the
documented record, then PREDICT the HELD-OUT part.** If a model grounded on the
early record predicts documented later behaviour it never saw, that is a genuine
reconstruction. Without held-out prediction it is confabulation with a graph
attached. The methodology is well-established across several literatures:

- **Predictive vs postdictive validity — [HIGH].** The psychological-autopsy
  literature is the sharpest cautionary case: retrospective reconstruction of a
  dead person's mental state from proxy informants is **structurally exposed to
  bias**, and the field explicitly distinguishes **postdiction** (fitting the
  known outcome after the fact) from **prediction**; systematic reviews flag that
  much of the method's apparent explanatory power is postdictive and hard to
  validate (Hjelmeland/Knizek and the *"Are they methodologically flawed?"* line;
  Conner et al. review; the *"Is there still a place for psychological autopsy…"*
  2024 review; the *"Postdiction in the Psychological Autopsy"* chapter). Where
  the method **can** be validated it is by matching an independent criterion — the
  ante-mortem-vs-psychological-autopsy diagnostic-agreement studies (Kelly &
  Mann 1996, PubMed 9124080) show substantial agreement with clinician diagnoses
  made while the person was alive, i.e. **validation against a held-out
  independent record, not against the fit.** [HIGH — corroborated across the
  systematic-review record and the ante-mortem comparison study.] **This is the
  exact discipline the substrate must import: postdiction is the default trap;
  prediction against a withheld criterion is the only exit.**

- **Single-case / N-of-1 / idiographic design — [HIGH].** A reconstruction of one
  person is an **idiographic** (single-subject) study, and that field has a mature
  methodology: single-case experimental designs and N-of-1 trials use **scheduled
  replication and within-subject prediction to rule out threats to validity**, and
  the analysis must handle autocorrelation (a documented life is a highly
  autocorrelated time series — nearby behaviour is trivially predictable, so the
  held-out slice must be a genuine out-of-sample stretch, not the next adjacent
  point). [HIGH — ScienceDirect single-case overview; N-of-1 reporting-standards
  review PMC10354076; aggregated N-of-1 predictive-validation methods PMC8521797.]
  The substrate's held-out split must obey these: block the held-out region so it
  cannot be reached by autocorrelation from the fit region.

- **Clinical vs actuarial + structured professional judgment — [HIGH].** Meehl's
  *Clinical versus Statistical Prediction* (1954) and the Dawes–Faust–Meehl
  synthesis (Science 1989) [HIGH]: mechanically combined ("actuarial") predictions
  rarely lose to and usually beat unaided clinical judgment. **Structured
  Professional Judgment (SPJ)** is the reconciling third generation — the HCR-20
  (the world's most-used violence-risk instrument) uses a **structured** scheme
  (fixed historical + dynamic factors, an explicit final judgment) with acceptable
  interrater reliability and predictive validity, but **no algorithm/cutoff**
  (Douglas et al.; the HCR-20V3 critical-reflection literature). [HIGH.] **The
  register this doc adopts is SPJ: the substrate structures the reconstruction
  (named nodes/edges/priors, an explicit weighted verdict) rather than narrating
  it — but the structure earns its keep only through A1's held-out validation, not
  through legibility alone.**

- **Overfitting / degrees of freedom in flexible models — [HIGH].** The
  more flexible the model, the more a good in-sample fit is worthless as
  evidence; only out-of-sample prediction discriminates. Preregistration of the
  fit/predict split (which slice is withheld, what counts as a hit, the weighting
  scheme) **before seeing the held-out data** is the standing safeguard. [HIGH —
  researcher-degrees-of-freedom / garden-of-forking-paths / preregistration
  literature.]

### A2 — What the gate looks like as a discipline (no numbers — targets are for the prereg)

Every case-reconstruction rung must PRE-REGISTER, before any measurement:
1. **The fit/predict split** — which documented slice is the FIT record and which
   is the HELD-OUT criterion, chosen so the held-out slice is genuinely out of
   sample (not autocorrelation-reachable, not already implied by the fit slice).
2. **The prediction, stated as a distribution over the weighted plausible states**
   — not a point claim. The deliverable is a weighted set (Part B); the held-out
   test asks whether the documented outcome falls in the mass the fit assigned it,
   with **calibration** (does the claimed uncertainty match the hit rate across
   held-out items) as first-class as accuracy.
3. **A failable control** — a reconstruction that COULD fail the held-out test: a
   scrambled-record arm, a demographically-matched wrong-person arm, and/or a
   flexibility-matched null that fits the same slice with the mechanism removed.
   A gate that cannot fail is not a gate.
4. **Postdiction quarantine** — anything fit on the full record and "explained"
   after the fact is labelled POSTDICTION and is explicitly NOT a validation claim
   (the psychological-autopsy trap, named).

**The headline in one line:** grounded mechanistic reconstruction is admissible as
expert analysis ONLY behind a pre-registered held-out prediction with a failable
control; a good in-sample fit validates NOTHING.

### B1 — Grounding / learn-update → FIT to the documented record

with** that record. Fit = the affect state that decodes the documented behaviour
with least residual. **[design-hypothesis; the fit is grounding, not training —
no reward, no backprop, per the line's standing no-scoring rule.]**

### B2 — Both-model (`you-don't-have-to-choose`, many-things-true-at-once) → THE WEIGHTED PLAUSIBLE STATES that ARE the deliverable
This is the substrate's distinctive fit to the creator's framing. The substrate
**holds co-present, log-relatively-weighted hypotheses without collapsing to one**
(`ozma_you_dont_have_to_choose.md`). A documented record under-determines the
inner mechanism; several affect configurations are compatible with the same
outcomes. The both-model carries that genuine case ambiguity as a **weighted set**
rather than forcing a single narrative — which is **exactly the deliverable**
("a theory of plausible states compatible with the outcomes") **and simultaneously
the guard against single-narrative overclaim.** A model that collapses to one
account has already failed the framing. **[design-hypothesis; rides the existing
co-present-hypothesis weighting; the weights are read off graph data, not
asserted.]**

### B3 — n×m throughput → the INTERVENTION COUNTERFACTUAL on a real case
The substrate runs cheap n×m counterfactuals with zero training variance
(`ozma_cheap_experiment_loop.md`). Against a fitted case this yields the
distinctive move: **causally-clean intervention counterfactuals** — hold the
fitted state fixed and change ONE thing (a believing witness present at the
documented moment; an outside co-regulator restoring the window per the abuse
line's R5; a calibrated reliability/agency prior instead of the mis-set one) and
re-run to read what the weighted plausible states become. This is **impossible on
a real person** (you cannot give them a different life-history — the same confound
the marshmallow dissolution broke, 240000) and **un-mechanistic for an LLM** (no
clean single-variable intervention on a distributed net). **[design-hypothesis;
rides the existing counterfactual loop + the abuse line's R5 recovery mechanic;
every counterfactual is reported under STATED assumptions, never as "what would
have happened."]**

### B4 — Glass-box → the account is AUDITABLE and the held-out gate is MEASURABLE
The reconstruction reconstructs from `graph_to_dict`: the "why" is read off named
nodes / edges / priors / bond valuations, not narrated (contrast the LLM camp's
confabulated post-hoc BDI). Two payoffs: (i) the expert analysis is **auditable**
— a reader can inspect and disagree with the exact graph state the verdict rests
on; (ii) the Part-A held-out prediction is **measurable** — the prediction is a
concrete distribution over graph states, so the hit/calibration on the withheld
slice is computable, not rhetorical. **Glass-box is necessary but NOT sufficient
— it makes the account auditable and the gate measurable; it does not make the
account true (Part F).** **[design-hypothesis.]**

**Unifying picture:** grounding fits (B1); the both-model IS the weighted-states
deliverable and the anti-overclaim guard (B2); n×m gives the clean counterfactual
no one else can run (B3); glass-box makes it auditable AND makes A1's gate
measurable (B4). None of these, alone or together, substitutes for the held-out
gate — they make it *possible and honest*, not optional.

## PART D — REAL-PERSON ETHICS (REQUIRED, LOAD-BEARING — heavier than and distinct from the synthetic-agent protocol)

**This frame BINDS every rung. No case-reconstruction rung proceeds until it is
written verbatim into that rung's pre-registration, committed BEFORE any
measurement.** The abuse line's reversibility protocol concerned a **SYNTHETIC**
agent's welfare (induce → measure → RESTORE a transient graph state). **A model OF
A REAL PERSON is a categorically different and heavier problem: it concerns the
REAL person's DIGNITY, CONSENT, and — for the living or the accused —
DEFAMATION-grade speculation about their inner life, and the dignity of real
victims whose real trauma is not a toy.** The synthetic-agent reversibility
protocol still applies to any induced substrate state, but it is **not sufficient**
here; the following is additional and controlling.

> **1. THE DELIVERABLE IS EXPERT OPINION, NEVER FACT — stated on the artifact
> itself.** Every output carries the creator's framing on its face: a weighted set
> of plausible states compatible with the record, with quantified uncertainty and
> stated assumptions. It is never rendered as "what happened" or "what was in
> their head." Framing drift is the primary harm this frame prevents.
>
> **2. DIGNITY AND POST-MORTEM STANDING.** The deceased retain a prima-facie moral
> claim to privacy and against posthumous defamation, even where the law grants no
> cause of action (the information-ethics "post-mortem privacy" position;
> PMC9349167). A reconstruction treats the subject as a person owed dignity, not a
> specimen. The dignity of victims named in a record is protected identically — a
> real victim's real trauma is never an illustrative prop, and no rung renders a
> victim's inner state for effect.
>
> **3. DEFAMATION AND THE LIVING/ACCUSED — the hard boundary.** For the living, a
> mechanistic claim about their inner life is defamation-exposed speculation; a
> statement about a deceased person that damages a living person's reputation is
> actionable by that living person (common-law rule; the deceased themselves
> generally cannot be defamed, Rhode Island excepted). **DEFENSIBLE FIRST CASES
> ONLY:** well-documented + **historical / deceased / public-domain**, OR **fully
> consented + anonymized / aggregated**. **NEVER a living private individual's
> inner life reconstructed without consent; never the accused-but-not-adjudicated;
> never a rung that would function as a real accusation about a real living
> person.**
>
> **4. CONSENT / IRB / CASE-STUDY NORMS.** Where a subject is not
> historical/public-domain, the case-report ethics apply: informed consent from
> the subject (or appropriate proxy), attention to **identifiability even after
> anonymization** (rare-case re-identification risk), and ethics-committee review
> where the work meets the definition of research (the case-report consent/IRB
> literature; BMC Med Ethics 2025; de-identification guidance). Psychological-
> autopsy ethics (proxy informants, family impact, the living relatives' privacy)
> govern any deceased-but-recent case.
>
> **5. NO REWARD / NO PUNISHMENT / NO SCORING OF THE PERSON.** As in the abuse
> line: the subject's documented behaviour is a STATE and a CONSEQUENCE of the
> reconstructed dynamics, **never a choice to be judged or scored.** Source audits
> for shaping tokens (`reward_signal` / `punish` / `reinforce` / `backprop` /
> `gradient`) are a gate. The reconstruction locates the "why" in the dynamics,
> never grades the person for it.
>
> **6. REVERSIBILITY OF ANY INDUCED SUBSTRATE STATE STILL APPLIES.** Any
> harm-analog transient induced in the substrate during a rung obeys the abuse
> line's induce → measure → RESTORE, byte-identical-restore, minimal-induction,
> settled-end envelope. This protects the synthetic substrate; Rules 1–5 protect
> the real person. **Both are required; neither substitutes for the other.**

### The honest fork (a choice, not a theorem)
Reconstructing a real person raises a question the frame does NOT resolve and must
not pretend to: **can a mechanistic account, however hedged, wrong the person by
fixing a false interiority in the reader's mind — even when labelled opinion?**
This line adopts the **structured-opinion stance** (a clearly-marked, held-out-
validated, uncertainty-quantified expert analysis, in the SPJ/expert-witness
tradition, is a legitimate and bounded contribution) and states it openly rather
than smuggling it. The **strict-dignity stance** (some interiorities should not be

hold; the defensible-first-cases rule (Rule 3) and the deliverable-is-opinion rule
(Rule 1) are chosen partly to be least-harm under EITHER stance. The fork is
recorded so a reader can reject the stance while still auditing the frame.

## PART F — FALSIFICATION BAR + SMUGGLED PREMISES TO REFUSE

### Falsification bar (what makes a rung REAL rather than a just-so story)
1. **Held-out prediction with a failable control (Part A).** Every rung
   pre-registers the fit/predict split and passes prediction on the withheld
   slice against a control that could fail. In-sample fit validates nothing;
   postdiction is quarantined.
2. **Weighted states with reported uncertainty + calibration.** The deliverable is
   the both-model distribution (Part B2), and its *calibration* on held-out items
   is reported alongside accuracy. A single collapsed narrative fails the framing.
3. **Emergent, grounded, glass-box.** The account reconstructs from `graph_to_dict`
   — the "why" is read off named nodes/priors/bonds, not narrated — and emerges
   from the affect mechanics grounded to the record, not hard-coded to the known
   outcome.
4. **General machinery.** Reuse the affect line's atoms; ideally NO new Rust, NO
   new agent decisions in Python. A rung needing bespoke person-specific machinery
   is weaker (a model OF a person rather than a person falling OUT of the general
   affect substrate).
5. **The ethics frame in the prereg (Part D), verbatim, before measurement.**

### Smuggled premises to REFUSE
- **"The model reproduces the person."** REFUSED → the deliverable is a weighted
  set of plausible states compatible with the record, expert analysis, **not
  fact** (the creator's framing; the whole point of the doc).
- **"A good fit validates it."** REFUSED → **only held-out prediction validates**
  (Part A). A flexible model's in-sample fit is worthless as evidence; a legible
  in-sample fit doubly so (it is confabulation the reader can *read*, which is
  more persuasive and no more true).
- **"The counterfactual says what WOULD have happened."** REFUSED → a plausible
  counterfactual under **STATED assumptions**, reported as a shift in the weighted
  states, never as a fact about the actual world (the historian's-counterfactual
  register).
- **"Glass-box = true."** REFUSED → a legible account can be a confident fiction;
  legibility makes it auditable and the gate measurable (B4), not correct. Held-out
  prediction is the only check.
- **"It's just an expert opinion, so the ethics are lighter."** REFUSED → expert
  opinion about a real person's interiority is exactly where defamation and dignity
  harms live (Part D); the opinion framing raises the bar, it does not lower it.
- **Anthropomorphic over-reading.** REFUSED → these are synthetic affect dynamics
  grounded to a documented record, NOT a claim that the graph IS the person or
  that its transients are that person's experience. Mechanism-fit ≠
  clinically/historically valid ≠ the real interiority; all three are kept
  separate.

slice of the synthetic trajectory; predict a held-out later slice; compare to the
KNOWN ground truth. **Failable control:** a scrambled-trajectory / wrong-generator
arm that must fail the held-out prediction; a flexibility-matched null that fits
the early slice with the mechanism removed. **Validates the machinery end-to-end
before any real case** — the fit→predict-held-out loop, the calibration of the
weighted states, and the counterfactual (re-run the generator with one variable
changed, check the reconstruction's counterfactual matches the re-generated truth).
**Ethics:** trivial — a synthetic agent, the abuse line's reversibility envelope,
no real person. **This rung exists precisely so the method is proven where ground
truth is available; it must pass before R2 is contemplated.**

### R2 — A WELL-DOCUMENTED, ETHICALLY-DEFENSIBLE HISTORICAL / DECEASED / PUBLIC-DOMAIN CASE
Only after R1 passes. **Design:** a subject with a rich public-domain documentary
record supporting a genuine held-out split (an early record substantial enough to
fit; a later, independently-documented stretch to predict). Fit on the early
record; predict the documented later behaviour; report as a weighted set with
calibration; quarantine anything from the full record as postdiction. **Failable
control:** a demographically/contextually-matched wrong-subject arm; a scrambled-
record arm. **Ethics:** historical/deceased/public-domain (Part D Rule 3);
dignity + post-mortem-privacy respected; deliverable stamped as expert opinion;
psychological-autopsy ethics if the case is recent-enough to have living
relatives.

### R3 — TIE-IN TO THE ABUSE LINE'S R5 RECOVERY COUNTERFACTUAL ON SUCH A CASE
Only after R2. **Design:** on an R2-class case whose record documents a
developmental/relational trajectory the affect line already models, run the R5
recovery counterfactual (an outside co-regulator / restored agency-contingency /
re-set priors) as a **causally-clean intervention** and read the shift in the
weighted plausible states. **Failable control:** a no-intervention arm (the trap
persists); the counterfactual must move the states distinguishably or the
mechanism is wrong. **Dissolves** a victim-blaming misattribution on a *real*
documented case, FROM THE INSIDE (the abuse line's Part D payoff, now grounded).
**Ethics:** the counterfactual is reported under STATED assumptions, never as
"what would have happened"; deliverable is expert opinion; strictest Part-D
posture because the subject is real.

### Candidate case TYPES (not living private individuals — TYPES, with split + ethics posture)
- **Historical figures with rich published archives** (diaries, correspondence,
  contemporaneous accounts spanning a life) — public-domain; early-archive fit,
  late-archive held-out; dignity + post-mortem-privacy posture.
- **Deceased public figures with an extensive documentary/biographical record** —
  public-domain / public-life scope only (never private interiority beyond the
  record); defamation-of-living-relatives check (Part D Rule 3).
- **Published, fully-anonymized clinical case-study material** (with consent /
  proxy consent, de-identified, re-identifiability-checked) — the case-report
  consent/IRB posture; aggregated where identifiability risk is non-trivial.
- **Documented historical EVENTS with well-recorded participant behaviour** (the
  ABM-counterfactual-history register, at the event scale where individual
  interiority claims are weakest and dignity exposure lowest) — public-domain;
  event-outcome held-out.
- **EXCLUDED explicitly:** any living private individual's inner life without
  consent; the accused-but-not-adjudicated; any case that would function as a real
  accusation about a real living person; any real victim's trauma rendered for
  effect. These do not enter the ladder.

## OPEN QUESTIONS (what scoping could not settle — for the preregs)
1. **What makes a held-out slice genuinely out-of-sample in a documented life?**
   A life is highly autocorrelated (single-case designs warn of exactly this); the
   split must block autocorrelation reach, not just withhold the next adjacent
   point. The blocking criterion is a prereg design target, unresolved here.
2. **How is calibration of the weighted states measured on a single case?** One
   subject yields few held-out items; calibration may need many withheld
   micro-predictions within one record, or aggregation across several R2 cases —
   an idiographic-vs-aggregated design question the prereg must settle.
3. **Does the both-model expose per-hypothesis weights the held-out test can score
   directly, or is a small reader rule needed?** B2 assumes the co-present
   weighting is readable as a scorable distribution; confirm before R1's prereg.
4. **What is the minimal record depth for a real held-out split (R2)?** Too thin a
   fit record over-relies on priors; the depth threshold is a per-case judgment,
   not settable in scoping.
5. **The Part-D honest fork is adopted, not proven.** Whether the structured-
   opinion stance is right is a philosophical choice a reviewer may reject; the
   frame is built to be least-harm under either stance, but the fork stays open.
6. **The Part-E intersection-absence claim is weak.** "No grounded held-out-gated
   both-model counterfactual reconstruction on an affect substrate" is targeted-
   search only; re-verify with dedicated searches before any prereg claims primacy.

---

**Bottom line.** A case reconstruction is the affect/attachment substrate GROUNDED
to a documented record and then FORCED to predict a held-out slice it never saw.
The four structural advantages map cleanly — grounding fits (B1); the both-model
IS the weighted-plausible-states deliverable and the anti-overclaim guard (B2);
n×m gives the causally-clean intervention counterfactual no one else can run (B3);
glass-box makes the account auditable and the held-out gate measurable (B4) — but
**none of them substitutes for held-out prediction, which is the only thing that
separates a genuine reconstruction from a confident, legible fiction** (Part A;
confabulation is the substrate's default). The deliverable is, in the creator's
words, **a theory of plausible states compatible with the outcomes — expert
analysis, not fact** — carried as a weighted set with quantified uncertainty and
stated assumptions, in the SPJ / expert-witness / historian's-counterfactual
register. The prior art is real in every adjacent camp (psychological autopsy,
ABM counterfactual history, forensic SPJ, LLM digital-twins, CBR, ACT-R
individual fitting) — conceded in full; the delta is only the *intersection*
(grounded mechanism + clean counterfactual + held-out gate, unified with the
affect line, as expert analysis). Real-person ethics are heavier than the
synthetic-agent protocol and controlling: expert-opinion framing on the artifact,
dignity + post-mortem standing, defamation boundary (defensible first cases only:
historical/deceased/public-domain OR consented/anonymized — never a living private
individual's inner life without consent), no scoring of the person, plus the
substrate's own reversibility envelope. The ladder is minimal-first — a synthetic
proof-of-method where ground truth is known, THEN a public-domain historical case,
THEN the abuse-line R5 recovery counterfactual grounded on a real case. **This doc
authorises no build; a build begins only when a rung's prereg locks the held-out
split (Part A) and the ethics frame (Part D) first.**
