## The recovery theorem’s floor changes the claimed bound

The formal manuscript’s Proposition “Conditional recovery result” says that, if `A_i − ΔD_i − H_i ≥ ε > 0` while `B_i > 0`, backlog “falls by at least ε per interval until it reaches zero.” Stage-2 Proposition C2 repeats the same statement and proof. **Confirmed:** the ledger instead gives `B_{i+1} ≤ max(0, B_i − ε)`. When `0 < B_i < ε`, the next backlog is zero and the actual fall is `B_i`, which is less than `ε`.

A positive net adaptation increment still reduces a positive backlog to zero in finite intervals. The proposition must distinguish ordinary intervals from the terminal one—for example, state a decrease of `min(ε, B_i)` per interval, or claim an `ε` decrease only while `B_i ≥ ε`. The same repair is needed in both artifacts so the paper’s statement that the result is “exact” remains justified. The Stage-1 T3 does not repeat this invalid quantitative lower bound; it only claims that a measured intervention can reduce `B`.

## Stage-1’s conclusion has not been advanced to the formal-paper lifecycle

The Stage-1 notes identify themselves as a research record rather than a formal paper. Their closing “Defensible next claim,” however, says “A future Paper 10a can argue only conditionally,” while the formal manuscript is present, registered in `paper_metadata.json` and `paper_metadata.tex`, and listed in the papers README. **Confirmed:** this is a temporal/status disagreement among the artifacts rather than a substantive expansion of the claim.

Update that sentence to refer to the formal companion now available, or to a future empirical case paper if that is the intended next work.
