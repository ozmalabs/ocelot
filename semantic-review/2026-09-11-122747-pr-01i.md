The GE gap remains prior to action selection. Lovelock’s restricted classification may constrain a candidate only after GE realizes the relevant physical arena and independently proves its metric-theory restrictions; it cannot supply the arena, its observables, or the restrictions itself.

The compared external routes are conditional constraint results rather than foundations from an untyped residual.
