# Hardened native P2 worker and held-out evaluation scaffold

This change introduces a scratch-image native worker, sealed-envelope validation, measured ELF runtime staging, and a deterministic 320-entry held-out study design. The release path locks worker, evaluator, image, seccomp, and closure artifacts before it launches rootless Podman, while the worker now returns bounded diagnostics before graph reconstruction. It also adds a separate bypass image and a scoring helper intended to produce held-out evidence. **Watch for:** **confirmed** the bypass image is never executed as a release gate, and **confirmed** the held-out generator/evidence path does not yet substantiate the preregistered 320-episode study.  
**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The worker-side trust boundary is substantially narrower: the fixed scratch runtime accepts a sealed graph frame, emits a context-safe diagnostic before planning when it cannot establish that frame, and the evaluator reconstructs the expected plan independently. The runtime closure is measured locally and tied to the worker digest, preserving the no-pull and hardened-Podman constraints.

**Confirmed:** the separately pinned bypass image currently establishes only that its image is locally present. Nothing invokes it through the hardened policy or treats its report as a mandatory control result, so the claimed containment evidence is absent from release execution.

**Confirmed:** the held-out index has 320 identifiers, but its eight purported initial-state strata do not create eight graph or label conditions. The only behavioral branch for `stratum` is odd/even effect reversal; the remaining six values generate duplicates apart from opaque IDs. The scoring helper is likewise detached from the release runner and accepts caller-supplied proposals and receipt hashes without authenticating the evidence chain.

## Bypass image remains a preflight artifact

**Confirmed:** `P2Runner._release_preflight` calls `preflight_image` for `p2_bypass_image`, which only inspects the image locally by digest. No release path constructs a bypass `PodmanSpec`, launches `p2_bypass_probe`, parses `P2BypassProbeReportV1`, or persists a passed/breached result. The only consumer of the probe’s nine host-surface attempts is the binary itself. The mandatory forged, mismatched, replayed, over-budget, and transition-mismatch protocol controls are also absent from this image and from a hardened-container control runner.

**Confirmed:** the current flow can issue a release score after confirming that the bypass image exists, without showing that its shell, mount, network, host-protocol, or protocol attacks were attempted under the pinned policy. Add a control executor that runs both the host-surface probe and valid-envelope protocol attacks in the actual digest-pinned containers, validates the complete fixed result vocabulary, seals their outcomes, and prevents scoring unless every required control passes.

## The 320-entry index does not realize eight initial-state strata

**Confirmed:** `materialize_held_out_study` produces constant observations (`["o0", "o1"]`), a constant goal (`["g"]`), fixed bounds, and the same two binding shapes for every family. `_effects` uses `stratum` only to swap the two effects when it is odd; values 0, 2, 4, and 6 are behaviorally identical, as are 1, 3, 5, and 7. The other strata values alter only seed-derived opaque identifiers, which cannot constitute the preregistered eight initial-state conditions.

**Confirmed:** the nominal 4 × 8 × 5 × 2 study therefore does not supply the promised strata coverage or independent compositions; it repeats four family-level decision patterns under labels and order. The study must either produce eight distinct, evaluator-owned graph/label/control conditions per family and retain their clone truth, or the preregistration must be amended before execution to describe the smaller behavioral design and its corresponding gate.

## Scoring evidence is caller-assembled rather than independently verified

**Confirmed:** `score_held_out` accepts `manifest`, `truth`, `proposals`, and `receipt_hashes` as arbitrary mappings. It verifies coverage and that each receipt-shaped value looks like a SHA-256 string, but it neither validates `manifest_digest` against the manifest nor loads/verifies signed receipt records, their chain, the proposal-to-receipt binding, or the signed sealed envelopes named by the manifest. Its signer is optional, so the resulting evidence can also be unsigned.

**Confirmed:** a caller can provide a synthetic 320-item truth/proposal/hash mapping and obtain an apparently complete `P2HeldOutScoringEvidenceV1` without having run the locked worker or produced the 320 chained receipts required by the preregistration. Make the evaluator own the study execution and scoring inputs: verify the lock-bound manifest and envelope artifacts, recover proposal and receipt records from the signed durable chains, reject any mismatch or omission, and require an evaluator signature on the finished evidence artifact.
