## Residual derivative is a declared information force, not an imported physical one

The derivation contract introduces \(\mathcal Q_I\), a preparation/readout map into that differential information-state, and a residual functional \(\mathcal R_I\). It defines \(\mathcal F_I(q)=-D\mathcal R_I(q)\in T_q^*\mathcal Q_I\) only where the derivative exists, with a separate response map required before treating the covector as an update direction. The manuscript states the same H-IF construction and expressly bars treating the result as newtons without the Newtonian translation map. **Confirmed:** this supplies the requested declared state and residual derivative while preventing a type-only relabeling of gravity.

## Clock and accelerometer now enter through one typed bridge

Both artifacts declare \(\Theta:(q,\mathcal O_t)\to(\tau,\mathcal A_{\rm acc})\), where \(\tau\) is an operational physical-clock reading and \(\mathcal A_{\rm acc}\) is a declared proper-acceleration/support observable. This is carried into the Newtonian, covariant, source, and geometry translation maps rather than identifying a cycle count, coordinate acceleration, or residual value with proper time or accelerometer output. **Confirmed:** the clock/accelerometer type correction is present and consistently used.

## Physical recovery is split into independent obligations

The candidate must prove a Newtonian readout \(K_{\rm N}=d\mathbf p/dt\), a covariant exchange readout \(K_4=Dp_\mu/d\tau\), a dimensioned source map \(L\) with compatible exchange accounting, and a geometry/observable map \(\Phi\). The manuscript additionally keeps matter coupling, the test-body/free-path derivation, source dynamics, and universal coupling outside those maps rather than inferring one from another. **Confirmed:** Newton/four-force, source, geometry, and matter recovery are independently typed; a force/exchange output is not treated as a rank-two source, and neither source nor geometry is treated as a free-path derivation.

## Gravity recovery remains conditional and physically bounded

T0/T1 and the manuscript's conditional gravity-derivation target demand the flat and load-shaped recovery conditions, a shared implemented dynamics, source/exchange closure, clock and tidal observables, universal coupling, and weak-field/precision limits. They also reject circular use of a metric, geodesic principle, stress tensor, or force law as an input to an alleged passive-continuation derivation. **Confirmed:** physical gravity recovery is conditional rather than represented as complete.

## Local attribution does not become a momentum/gravity identity

The history-bearing record and inference map are required to attribute a present passive continuation to momentum transport or load-shaped gravity. A local accelerometer can distinguish free from supported motion only in its declared domain; it does not itself determine curvature, source structure, or the explanatory attribution, while extended tidal, source, and cross-frame evidence remain relevant. **Confirmed:** the non-identity of momentum and gravity, plus the history and local-accelerometer limits, are retained in both artifacts.
