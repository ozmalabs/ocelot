# Grammar-relative shape trajectories for Paper 10a revision 10

Revision 10 corrects the MDL–cost-floor grounding across Paper 10a and its Stage-1 and Stage-2 records. Outcomes are now declared shapes or shape trajectories rather than preserved invariants; a declared grammar defines both the generable shape space and a grammar-relative lower envelope. A grammar change can alter both, and only a declared shape comparison map can record the resulting floor step. The records retain the account-versus-realised-grammar formulation of the same GE compression pressure without equating description length to audited cost or treating a grammar step or transition as empirically established. **Watch for:** no actionable findings.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

The correction makes cross-grammar comparison explicit: a declared map connects a shape under the old grammar to the comparable shape under the new one before a lower grammar-relative audited envelope may be called a floor step.

MDL remains a compression criterion for an account and its generative grammar, while the audited ledger measures realised transformations. Their correspondence is structural and conditional, not numerical evidence of a cost decline, realised substrate, grammar change, or civilisational transition.

The two-lens distinction, outcome and allocation boundaries, evidence limits, physical-substrate condition, Anthropocene qualification, and unreleased status remain intact. Agriculture remains scoped as evidence of historical productivity and input-composition change rather than universal or costless production.
