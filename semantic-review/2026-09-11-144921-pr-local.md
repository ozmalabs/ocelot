# Separate analytical classification from continuance ranking

Paper 10a revision 8 and its Stage-1 and Stage-2 records implement a two-stage architecture. The analytical lens defines the current state space and conditionally classifies trajectories as terminal discontinuity or viable non-isomorphic successors without ranking either branch. A separate continuance interpretation explicitly ranks continuance above terminal discontinuity and, within continuance, directs evaluation toward lower measured human suffering \(S^H\) and continuing human participation \(P^H\). The interpretation is a value input rather than an inference from GE, the cost ledger, or the partition; requested scope, evidence, substrate, Anthropocene, and release-status limits remain stated. Watch for: no actionable concerns.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

The formal paper, Stage-1 record, and Stage-2 ledger make \(\mathcal M(R_0)\) a classificatory analytical state space and reserve the continuance lens for ordering its already classified possibilities. Changing the interpretive lens leaves the analytical classification unchanged.

The ordering requires declared definitions and measurements for civilisation, humanity, suffering, participation, population, horizon, and threshold. Cost evidence remains audited and outcome-normalised, GE substrate status remains unverified, Anthropocene terminology remains analytical rather than geological, and the paper remains unreleased.

## The manifold maps possibilities without assigning their value

Paper 10a §1.1 defines \(\mathcal M(R_0)\) as the declared state space of reproduction arrangements, ledger variables, and measured constraints, limiting “manifold” to a classificatory term. Given \(G(R_0)\), it maps terminal discontinuity and viable non-isomorphic successor regimes without a value ordering. Stage 1’s “Two lenses” statement and Stage 2 D4/D5 preserve that premise-bound, exhaustive terminal/successor classification.

## Continuance is a declared interpretive input with measurement obligations

Paper 10a §1.2 separately records continuance above terminal discontinuity and, within continuance, lower protocol-measured \(S^H\) with continued \(P^H\) as the ideal direction. It expressly excludes GE, the cost ledger, and the transition partition as sources of that ordering, while requiring definitions of civilisation, humanity, suffering, participation, population, horizon, and threshold. Stage 1 and Stage 2 D5 repeat that a different lens changes the ordering, not the analysis.

## The application remains conditional and evidence-bounded

The three records retain the requested limits: \(G(R_0)\) is not derived from observed cost data; audited costs require declared outcome, reliability, access, allocation, and externality boundaries; and no universal floor, forecast, policy instruction, or demonstrated net social gain is claimed. The GE graph substrate remains an unverified, independently reproducible same-boundary scenario rather than evidence for \(G(R_0)\) or a transition branch. “Anthropocene transition/end” remains an analytical umbrella rather than a formal geological assertion, and Paper 10a remains unreleased.
