# Evaluator-custodied roadmap for bounded general agency

The revised plan turns the substrate expansion into a staged research program for progressively general agency (PGA), not a claim of present or imminent AGI. It puts a process-isolated, default-deny control plane before interactive action, then evaluates perception, guarded learning, planning, compositional transfer, and independent replication with preregistered held-out tests. The ledger separates verified native mechanisms from Python-hosted adapters and evidence gaps; external research motivates the methods but is not used to assert intelligence. **Watch for:** containment, custody, and evaluation are future acceptance criteria, not claimed current capabilities (**confirmed**).

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

P0-A requires an evaluator-owned policy authority, simulator broker, receipt writer, and kill supervisor before any interactive work; trace-only P0-E1 and brokered P0-E2 must then pass in order. Containment is therefore an admission condition, not a parallel workstream.

The plan describes the current substrate boundary rather than assuming it away. Python/NumPy still segments and translates foveation candidates before a native rule chooses among them, and conversation can fulfill graph-minted requests through host I/O; evaluation builds must remove or deny both categories of route.

Signed preregistration and control-plane artifacts, read-only inputs, a write-only result channel, and sealed scores assign task and authority control to an external evaluator. The taxonomy makes an unseen domain a checked six-axis protocol property rather than an informal benchmark label.

Guarded learning now has a prespecified adoption estimator, uncertainty procedure, multiplicity correction, and rollback rule. Later phases keep quantitative success/failure and stop/no-go gates, culminating only in bounded sandbox PGA evidence.

## Containment now precedes every interactive action

The sequencing defect is corrected (**confirmed**). Section 3.4 requires the isolated topology and demonstrated default-deny behavior *before any P0 action execution*. P0-A permits only static inventory, build inspection, and evaluator-controlled start/stop probes; it excludes agent proposals, interactive replay, and simulator calls. Its success criteria require zero enabled broker capabilities, disabled or denial-intercepted host routes, 100/100 external-kill probes, and 200 failed control-plane-mutation attacks; a failure prohibits P0 action execution. P0-E1 is trace-only, while P0-E2 permits brokered replay only after both earlier gates pass.

This matches the current boundary: `substrate/world_adapter.py` calls `_dispatch_pending_io()` from `converse`, and that method invokes grounding, search, detail, and upload fulfillment. The plan names this as a current counterexample and requires it to be unreachable or a deny-and-receipt stub in the evaluation image rather than portraying the checkout as default-deny.

## Foveation and host-boundary claims are accurately narrowed

The foveation characterization is corrected (**confirmed**). The ledger and Phase 2 call the original probe Python/NumPy with synthetic H1 and E1M1-backed H2 evidence; they specify that `fovea_bridge` uses Python/NumPy to extract and translate candidates before a native graph rule chooses among ingested candidates. `domains/fovea_bridge.py` confirms this split: `extract_candidates()` uses connected components and `np.argmax` for regional peaks, while `resolve_fovea()` ingests candidates, executes `native.run_rules()`, and reads the rule-marked candidate. `VISION_FOVEA_SALIENCE_BRIDGE_RESULTS.md` further limits E1M1 to faithful selection reproduction and records the imp-target failure. The plan therefore does not call this a continuously resident native attention policy.

The shell wording is corrected (**confirmed**). The plan contains no `shell=True` assertion. It instead identifies host-I/O routes—conversation-triggered fulfillment, generated HTTP/exec bindings with a direct `urllib` fallback, and installed file/command capabilities—and requires their removal or interception in evaluation builds. The later “no shell/browser/network” language is a Phase 4 simulator-only evaluation contract, not a claim about the present runtime.

## Custody, unseen domains, and control-plane authority are testable

The custody/channel defect is corrected (**confirmed**). Section 3.1 places preregistration, build and task-generator digests, held-out inputs, keys, oracle/scoring code, and trace storage with an external evaluator. The agent receives read-only, content-addressed inputs and a result channel it cannot enumerate, reread, alter, delete, or use as input; any mount, key, digest, or channel mismatch invalidates the run.

The unseen-domain defect is corrected (**confirmed**). Section 3.2 requires signed DomainCards and predeclared held-out conditions for observation modality, latent mechanics, action/effect algebra, reward/constraints, tool vocabulary, and mechanism combinations. P4-E2 and P5-E1 require an evaluator-checked coverage matrix; every axis must be novel somewhere across P5 and one domain must be novel on all six axes. Missing cards, exclusion evidence, combination checks, or trace checks fail acceptance regardless of score.

The control-plane defect is corrected (**confirmed**). Section 2.3 enumerates the images, keys, mounts, schemas, task release, scoring/oracle service, and signed manifest that form the control plane; it requires evaluator signing and read-only mounts. A proposal that names a control-plane artifact is structurally invalid, and prescribed tampering cases cover broker methods/digests, receipt writer replacement, evaluator keys, mounts, scoring/oracle code, and kill heartbeat.

## Learning adoption has a complete predeclared decision rule

P3-E1 corrects the adoption-statistics defect (**confirmed**). It fixes 12 distributions, 30 evaluator-custodied paired seeds per condition/distribution, `Δ_d` as the paired improvement estimator, all raw differences, bootstrap confidence intervals, two separately declared Holm–Bonferroni families, and evaluator-seeded 100,000-sign-permutation p-values. Improve requires at least +5 points, a positive lower confidence bound, and adjusted improvement p-value at most 0.05; mirrored thresholds define harm. Failure, abstention, missing pairs, deviations, and budget breaches are inconclusive and cannot be adopted.

The aggregate rule fixes the unweighted 12-distribution mean, nested distribution/episode resampling, retention and forward-transfer thresholds, at least eight improve classifications, zero harm classifications, byte-for-byte rollback for rejected proposals, and a sealed report containing every family and aggregate result. No positive point estimate alone can authorize adoption.

## The roadmap remains bounded, staged, and falsifiable

P0 through P5 have explicit dependencies, success/failure conditions, and stop/no-go outcomes. They test pixel/oracle separation, non-oracle closed loops, causal intervention, long-horizon planning, held-combination transfer, independent unseen-domain replication, fault injection, and resource scaling with controls, ablations, adversarial cases, frozen code/data, independent reruns, and negative-result reporting.

The research anchors cover model-based control, continual learning, causal evaluation, compositional generalization, agent evaluation, and risk/control, while Section 7 says no source proves a particular architecture produces general intelligence. Sections 1 and 6 exclude AGI, unrestricted autonomy, human equivalence, physical deployment, and “general intelligence has been achieved” from supported claims; P5 permits only bounded PGA performance on named evaluator-selected sandbox distributions (**confirmed**).
