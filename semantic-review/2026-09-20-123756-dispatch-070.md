### Attestation-gated activation and complete commitment binding

**Confirmed.** `TrackARunner.run()` validates the adapter’s exact `VerifiedAttestationBinding` registration and closed startup-evidence map before calling `activate_attested_capabilities()`. The activation commitment carries the immutable selection and digest, lock/manifest/worker-contract digests, all provisioned input/access digests, the provisioning digest, and the attestation binding digest. The runner rejects a returned capability whose activation echo differs in any field or whose attestation object is not the exact adapter-issued binding. This directly addresses both REVIEW-070 activation findings.

The dependency order is pre-launch provisioning, attestation validation and activation, fresh-process input rehydration, timing verification, seed derivation, task/control execution, receipt validation, result-seal verification, and final signing. No task or seed is built before the timing verifier succeeds; the public result exposes public-input and access commitments while the signed final binding retains private-store and rehydration commitments.

The closure receipt points to all three exact receipt hashes, and the impasse seal repeats those links plus the closure-receipt and chain digests. The runner persists, reads back, and verifies the seal before returning the impasse.
