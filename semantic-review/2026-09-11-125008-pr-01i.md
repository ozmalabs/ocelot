Because the declared trace is written as indexed events \(e_t\), an observable that can depend on trace construction or recording order need not be fixed by the support poset and typed event content. Declare that order as physical/operational data, or require invariance under order-preserving isomorphisms of the typed relation/trace before calling it gauge-like. A shared formulation preserves the ledger’s conditional claim rather than making bookkeeping invariance unconditional.
