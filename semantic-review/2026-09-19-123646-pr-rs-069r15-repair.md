# RS-069R15 provenance evidence boundary repair

The R15 repair separates local build provenance from the remote image digest observation and from remote provenance/attestation and immutability claims. The corrected candidate binding preserves the R14 image digest and push result while projecting remote provenance and immutability as unknown because no corresponding observations exist. The loader now rejects unsupported remote-provenance and immutability claims, and the focused regression suite covers the corrected projection, stale approval, and fail-closed cases. The historical append-only ledger remains byte-for-byte unchanged and its hash chain still validates. **Watch for:** **confirmed** — the validator checks the shape of supplied remote evidence but does not verify that its lookup or evidence digest corresponds to an actual attestation artifact; **confirmed** — remote provenance/attestation and repository immutability remain unknown and must not be promoted by downstream consumers; **confirmed** — the master runsheet remains `RS-069R15=IN_PROGRESS`, so this is proposed `PASS_REVIEW`, not master acceptance.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Local provenance is now represented independently from registry identity: the local provenance digest remains verified, while the remote digest is supported only by the Artifact Registry digest-describe observation. The corrected binding and runner no longer turn local provenance into a remote provenance claim.

The projection is fail-closed around unsupported evidence. A remote provenance claim requires a structured lookup record tied to the image digest, and an immutability claim requires an observation other than a digest describe; the current corrected artifact supplies neither and therefore records both states as `null`.

The R14 operation boundary is preserved as historical evidence rather than rewritten. The final ledger events retain the successful push/digest-describe operation and the stop after operation 1, while the stale approval artifact prevents the corrected binding from being authorized for another run.
