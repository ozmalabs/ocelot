## High-level view

GE supplies only the information-realization residual, while H-EM introduces a separate electromagnetic state/clock, local gauge, charge/current, response, observable, covariance, interface, and exchange contract. The exchange conclusion needs the same premise discipline used elsewhere in the addition. Maxwell’s equation determines the field stress divergence, but matching matter-side balance requires a diffeomorphism-covariant matter action and matter equations, with declared boundary accounting; EM1–EM4 alone, as labelled, do not state all of that.

QED remains a further quantization programme. The gravity/information-force and local-history limits survive intact, and the fail-closed release gate confirms the document remains unreleased.
