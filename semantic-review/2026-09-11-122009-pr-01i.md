# Conditional four-dimensional Lovelock selector for GR recovery

Revision 9 adds a bounded selector result to the conditional GR-recovery programme: a GE selector reaches the four-dimensional Lovelock classification only after independently proving the metric-only, local/natural, two-jet, symmetric, off-shell divergence-free, background-free restrictions and a nonzero Einstein coefficient. The result is correctly limited to \(E_{ab}=aG_{ab}+b g_{ab}\), leaving physical constants, signature, topology, boundary data, matter, and universal coupling outside the classification. Residual minimization remains a selection problem rather than an independent derivation of Einstein--Hilbert gravity, and the available alternatives reappear when the restrictions are relaxed. The formal paper, Stage-2 ledger, and Stage-1 audit state the same boundary; the pre-existing EM, gravity, momentum, history, and unreleased-status limits remain intact. Watch for: no actionable concern found in the requested scope.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

The selector extension converts a broad “minimum residual” aspiration into a proof obligation: it must establish the full Lovelock hypothesis bundle before GR5 can apply. The classification controls only the bulk metric tensor in the stipulated four-dimensional class; it neither supplies the geometric, coupling, boundary, or calibration inputs needed for a physical GR realization nor rules out EFT, nonlocal, extra-field, or metric-affine constructions outside that class.
