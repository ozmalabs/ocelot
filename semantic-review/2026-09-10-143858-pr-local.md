# Bounded cross-frame perspective grounding

01g adds a distinct companion layer: it recasts the Total Perspective Vortex as a failed demand for a view from nowhere, then proposes an inspectable bridge between frames and a conditional cost/risk account for changing self-model-supporting organisation. It does not duplicate 01b’s self-model argument or 01f’s consciousness claim, and it uses 03’s reuse-versus-alteration form in a new cross-frame setting. The manuscript consistently excludes clinical classification, truth validation by difficulty, and direct possession of another frame’s experience; the adjacent-literature citations are bounded as analogous machinery, not proof of the formalism. **Watch for:** **confirmed** the bridge consumes F-local states without specifying their W-indexed representation, and **confirmed** the risk/cost definitions do not yet define a comparable order or consistent support condition for their stated tests.  
**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

01/01b provide the situated-frame and self-model premises; 01g owns the attempted cross-frame construction, its non-totalising guard, and the conditional transition burden. TPV is demystified as an impossible demand rather than a mental state or experiential credential.

The bridge must remain a construction in W, but its transition signature passes P_F(x) and M_F(F) directly into a W procedure. Without an explicit record/report/translation representation of those F-local objects, the formalism does not enforce the no-direct-access boundary it states.

The imported Narrative Momentum cost form needs a narrower statement, and the risk hypothesis needs a domain, order, and comparison protocol. Its claim to hold support fixed conflicts with changing remaining support, so neither the ordering nor the proposed falsifier identifies a stable controlled comparison.

## The W-indexed bridge needs W-indexed inputs

**Confirmed** — `papers/formal/01g_graphic_equalisation_total_perspective_vortex_grounding.tex`, “Jumping off the tower: the cross-frame bridge,” lines 233–267. The bridge is defined as a W construction with admitted evidence, a translation procedure, and declared loss. But the transition takes `P_F(x)` and `M_F(F)` as arguments and emits a W-indexed representation. Earlier definitions make both inputs organisations F constructs under F’s aperture (lines 127–135 and 159–163), not objects W can inspect. The bridge tuple does not define an embedding, report, encoding, or W-model for either input.

Define the transition over `M_W(F)`, W-admitted records of `P_F(x)`, or an explicit transmission/translation map sourced in `E^W_{F,K}` and `Pi^W_{F,K}`, with its losses. This makes the no-possession claim enforceable by the signature.

## Risk cannot yet be ordered or experimentally compared

**Confirmed** — `papers/formal/01g_graphic_equalisation_total_perspective_vortex_grounding.tex`, “Transition cost and self-model risk,” lines 314–344; “Failure modes, calibration, tests, and falsifiers,” lines 365–386. `d`, `c`, `r`, and `s` have informal meanings but no domains, normalization, or order; `Phi_W` is called an ordering or conceptual quantity without a monotonicity condition. `1-r` and `1-s` assume subtractable, compatible scales even though the paper denies a universal numerical scale.

The hypothesis says support conditions are fixed while lower remaining support `s` increases risk (lines 328–334); the falsifier repeats that `S_W` is fixed while asking whether decreases in remaining support order burden (lines 382–386). Since `s` is introduced as remaining grounding/support and `S_W` is W’s supplied support, the controlled comparison changes an element it declares fixed.

For each grounding, define a baseline support context separately from the support factor deliberately varied, define an order for each measured or ordinal variable, and require `Phi` to be monotone in those orders. The falsifier can then compare matched transitions with fixed class and background conditions.

## The cost form is additive despite the stated caveat

**Confirmed** — `papers/formal/01g_graphic_equalisation_total_perspective_vortex_grounding.tex`, “Conditional transition cost,” lines 292–305. The manuscript imports 03’s additive cost decomposition and then says the form is not a claim that every grounding is additive. But the displayed equality makes total transition cost the sum of a reuse cost and a non-negative alteration increment for every chosen transition class to which it applies.

Scope the formula to groundings whose cost currency admits addition, or formulate the general claim as an ordinal relation—altering relevant carried organisation has a higher conditional burden than matched reuse—and present the sum as one operationalisation.

## Release registration stops before the release gate

**Likely** — `tools/release_papers.py`, `CLEARED` list at lines 38–59. The new manuscript appears in revision metadata and the papers README, but the release tool generates only its `CLEARED` allowlist and states that future documents remain unreleased unless added. 01g is therefore absent from generated PDF/website releases despite its formal-paper registration.

If this commit is intended only to draft the paper locally, leaving it absent is deliberate. If it is intended to publish 01g alongside the other formal companions, add it to the allowlist and update the curated “Where to start” material only if it should be an entry point.
