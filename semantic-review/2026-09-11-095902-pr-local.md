# Passive continuation as a bounded momentum–gravity recovery programme

Paper 01i turns the inherited reuse-versus-alteration vocabulary into a deliberately limited programme: passive continuation means zero *additional* alteration cost only within a declared class, while physical momentum transport and gravitational free propagation remain recovery targets. It preserves the key physical distinction that momentum is not a force and that ideal GR free fall is not a locally felt gravitational force. The manuscript withholds momentum conservation, geometry dynamics, geodesics, source laws, equivalence, and quantum gravity pending typed maps, symmetries, couplings, observables, and empirical limits. **Watch for: [high, confirmed]** the load-shaped target simultaneously permits a non-metric “stated alternative” and requires the metric-connection geodesic equation, so the promised alternative branch is not formally typed.  
**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The manuscript separates the internal class-relative cost result from the flat and load-shaped physical regimes that a future realization would have to recover. Its flat contract names a cotangent-bundle momentum map and then uses contravariant four-momentum without first supplying the flat metric and connection that make that conversion and transport equation meaningful. **[medium, confirmed]**

The curved contract refuses to get a source law from geodesic motion, but its scope has to choose: either it is a metric-geodesic recovery target, or it supports explicitly different gravitational structures with their own free-path equation, source type, clocks, and observables. It cannot call an alternative admissible while making the Levi-Civita-style equation mandatory. **[high, confirmed]**

“In-frame” is framed as a local operational underdetermination rather than a force identity. The wording should preserve the distinction already made in the notes: a specified local accelerometer can operationally read proper acceleration, even though that result alone cannot infer curvature, source, or distinguish a homogeneous gravitational description from uniform acceleration. **[medium, confirmed]**

The generated metadata and fail-closed release gate handle 01i coherently: the consistency check covers 25 papers and the release dry run excludes the new paper. The human-facing formal-record index still says 24 papers and omits 01i, so its registration and stated count disagree with the actual corpus and release policy. **[medium, confirmed]**

## Verified strengths

Passive continuation is repeatedly bounded to a declared alteration class and explicitly denied to mean no activity, time, interaction, support cost, or cross-class total cost. The manuscript faithfully retains the steering constraint: momentum and gravity are not the same force, and in-frame inseparability is local operational underdetermination requiring an observable and inference model. Its four-way ledger, source/exchange requirement, UFF/EEP and clock/PPN constraints, and falsifiers prevent the paper from claiming conservation, GR geodesics, source dynamics, equivalence, or quantum gravity. The prior-art discussion is appropriately scoped to targets and constraints rather than presented as a bridge for the project vocabulary.

## Metric-geodesic language cannot stand in for an alternative theory

**[High, confirmed]** The abstract says a load-shaped realization can establish \(u^\nu\nabla_\nu u^\mu=0\) “from a metric or stated alternative,” while G1 permits “a Lorentzian metric and connection, or an explicitly testable alternative.” The displayed equation is defined only after a manifold, tangent bundle, connection, and a clock have been supplied; it is not the common target of a genuinely non-metric alternative. The paper uses the target as the condition for calling the curved regime recovered.

Make the contract disjunctive. A metric branch should require \((M,g,\nabla)\), state whether \(\nabla\) is Levi-Civita or otherwise constrained, and recover the geodesic equation in its test-body domain. An alternative branch should name its propagation equation, source object, clock/accelerometer observables, gauge or transformation rules, and empirical equivalence or difference. The source-map notation has the same problem: “\(T^{\rm eff}_{\mu\nu}\) or stated alternative” must be replaced by a precisely typed alternative, not a placeholder alongside a tensor-specific Bianchi discussion.

## Flat transport needs a declared Minkowski transport structure

**[Medium, confirmed]** Section 6 declares \(P_4:\State\to T^*M\), which is a covector-valued map, while F1--F6 and the abstract require \(dp^\mu/d\tau=0\), a contravariant-vector equation. In a flat special-relativistic realization this can be repaired by declaring \((M,\eta,\nabla^{(\eta)})\), setting \(p^\mu=\eta^{\mu\nu}p_\nu\), and stating the proper-time/affine-parameter and mass-shell conventions. None of those objects is yet a project result; they are required premises for the recovery target.

Without that repair, F1--F6 are not a complete, independently checkable target as written.

## Local operational underdetermination must leave local accelerometry intact

**[Medium, confirmed]** The in-frame remark says a bare record does not decide whether its realization is “momentum transport, coordinate acceleration, proper acceleration, curvature, or source response.” The research notes and derivation ledger are more precise: after an accelerometer observable has been supplied, a local observation can distinguish a free ideal test body’s zero proper acceleration from a supported body’s nonzero proper acceleration. What remains underdetermined in the local equivalence domain is the curvature/source interpretation and the distinction between a homogeneous gravitational description and uniform acceleration without extended or comparative inference.

Revise the phrase to distinguish an *unspecified record* from a *specified local operational measurement*. This retains the intended steering: momentum and gravity remain physically distinct; their separation is not supplied by a bare in-frame reading without the requisite model and inference. It also avoids making “in-frame” sound like a denial of the manuscript’s support-force and tidal observables.
