## History attribution needs the ledger’s typed-map guard

**Major — confirmed.** In `papers/formal/01i_graphic_equalisation_passive_continuation_momentum_gravity.tex`, Definition “History-bearing attribution record” (§Scope and terminology) places `I^W` in the tuple but gives neither its type nor what it maps from and to. Proposition “History required for physical attribution” requires a “declared inference map from that record to the relevant physical observables,” and its proof says history is necessary but insufficient without the map. The definition never makes the map’s typed physical-attribution contract a prerequisite.

`papers/derivations/01i_passive_continuation_momentum_gravity_derivation.md` §4.1 instead defines a *typed* history-bearing record, requires the map to connect the record to typed physical observables, source/support models, and comparison conditions, and rejects both a snapshot and history without that map. Its §5 “History-bearing attribution” row gives `I:(H, O_local, O_extended, O_source) ->` stated momentum/load, curvature, or source attributions; §7 P-11 and §8 item 13 make the contract a prerequisite and a failure condition.

Define `I^W` with its record and local/extended/source-observation inputs, admissible attribution outputs, uncertainty/compression and comparison-domain conditions, and physical types. State that contract in the formal definition and proposition so the manuscript preserves the ledger’s result: history is necessary for attribution in this programme but cannot establish it without a typed declared inference procedure.

## Proper acceleration does not become an attribution test

The formal paper’s “What in-frame inseparability does and does not say” remark, §§Standard physical targets and Recovery conditions and falsifiers, confines a local accelerometer to determining proper acceleration and distinguishing a free test body from a supported one in its stated domain. It excludes inference of curvature, source structure, or momentum-versus-gravity explanation. The ledger uses the same boundary in §4.2, §6.2, §8 item 13, and the final claim ledger: tidal, source, support, and cross-frame information belong to the separate history-bearing inference procedure.

## The programme does not collapse momentum into gravity

The formal Scope, abstract, H-PC hypothesis, recovery conditions, and conclusion reject “one force” language: momentum is state/charge-like, force is specified transfer/change, and ideal GR free fall has zero proper acceleration while support and tidal effects remain observable. The derivation repeats this limit in §§1, 7, 8, and 10.

The common-law claim remains conditional on a concrete realization closing F1–F6, G1–G8, source/exchange, universal-coupling, and empirical-limit obligations; the ledger’s T1 adds P-1 through P-12. The paper is marked draft, and `papers/README.md`, “Version and release status,” says conditional 01i remains excluded from the fail-closed release allowlist.
