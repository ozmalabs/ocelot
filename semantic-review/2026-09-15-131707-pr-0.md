# Graph-native two-segment structural discovery

This change adds a fixed two-segment discovery demonstration in which `ad_*` rules select the next concept by graph-resident traversal rank, derive a five-field residual signature, and hand recurrent evidence to the existing native recurrence and shape-decode ladders. The focused demonstration runs all eight preregistered cases and the focused migrated suites pass: 15 passed with no skips when invoked through `.venv` with `PYTHONPATH=.`. The generated positive artifact begins with the unrelated `self_design` axis only and gains a binding-signature axis after settlement, so the demonstrated axis is not preinstalled in that run. Watch for: **confirmed** the reported adapter audit is fabricated rather than observed, and **confirmed** the declared all-label bijection does not transform several labels or the held-out request. The implementation supports a tightly bounded local accommodation of one fixed relation shape; it does not establish general intelligence or unconstrained concept invention.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Trajectory selection, residual reification, structural recurrence identity, and minting are graph-native in the demonstrated route. The adapter supplies raw fixture facts and invokes the rule fixpoint; it does not call the historical Python trajectory or residual helpers, and the focused tests replace those helpers with raising stubs. The positive artifact also shows the minted binding axis is added after initial graph construction rather than present at boot.

The evidence boundary is weaker than the preregistration claims. A hard-coded audit dictionary is asserted as if it measured prohibited adapter writes and callback use, while the label-scramble case only remaps a subset of training-side strings and leaves the held-out request unchanged. These gaps prevent the test suite from proving the no-injection boundary or full label-independent transfer it claims.

## Native trajectory and residual path

**Confirmed:** the discovery loop's behavioral decisions are encoded in the seed rules rather than delegated to the historical controller. `ad_expand_segment_candidates`, `ad_rank_min_candidate`, and `ad_choose_min_rank_candidate` create and choose candidates from `trajectory_next` by `traversal_order`; `ad_derive_segment_signature`, `ad_derive_residual`, and `ad_reify_single_residual` construct the canonical five-field residual and recurrence evidence. The adapter in `domains/autonomous_discovery.py` inserts the fixture's concepts, semantic relations, policy, and request, then calls `run_rules`; it does not invoke the patched legacy routes. `tests/test_autonomous_discovery_migrated.py` patches the legacy trajectory, residual, signature, and concern functions to raise, and all eight cases passed under that condition.

The recurrence ledger preserves the canonical structural key rather than aggregating by predicate: `mrl_open_candidate` and `mrl_post_evidence_once` compare `recurrence_key`, while `mrl_derive_persistence` counts linked evidence. The different-structure counterfactual changes the second tuple's role and quantum, and the successful run records two persistence-one candidates rather than one aggregate. The held-out consumer compares the candidate evidence witness's relation head and two roles against the operation request; its role and head mismatch cases genuinely alter those compared graph facts and correctly remained unlicensed in the generated assertions.

## The audit does not measure the prohibited boundary

**Confirmed:** `submit_preregistered_case` assigns `state["audit"] = {"raw_discovery_submissions": 1, "prohibited_writes": 0, "callback_invocations": 0}` unconditionally in `domains/autonomous_discovery.py`. The runner serializes that dictionary verbatim, and the test merely compares it to the same literal. Nothing counts calls, installs the preregistration's promised raising ask/LLM/network/tool callbacks, or prevents/checks adapter writes of `ThoughtSegment`, residual, evidence, witness, candidate, observation, or axis nodes.

The source inspection and legacy-function monkeypatches are useful evidence for this narrow route, but the current telemetry cannot establish the stronger claim that no prohibited Python decision or callback occurred. Instrument the adapter/substrate boundary so only raw types and edges can be authored before settlement, provide real callback sentinels, and assert their observed counters.

## The label-scramble transfer case is only partial

**Confirmed:** the preregistration describes a bijection over entity IDs and all opaque/surface labels, but the implementation applies `opaque.get()` only to a limited set of values in `_add_raw_case`. `relation_a`, `relation_b`, `request_train`, and `setpoint` have no entries in `opaque_bijection`, so remain unchanged. More importantly, `submit_held_out_operation` hard-codes `request_id="held_out_test_z"` and `display_label="positive_like_display"` and never consults the bijection. The passing case therefore demonstrates invariance to a subset of training labels, not the declared end-to-end label permutation.

Apply the bijection to every label named by the preregistration and assert that each altered fact differs while the graph topology and semantic fields remain isomorphic. That would turn this from a partial relabeling smoke test into the stated counterfactual transfer control.

## Demonstrated novelty is local and artifact-backed

**Confirmed:** the generated `discovered_axis_positive/initial_graph.json` contains one `CapabilityAxis`, `self_design`, and no binding-signature axis. Its settled graph contains one additional axis with `capability_keys` equal to `[["rel:binding","bind","ITEM","SLOT",1]]`; the rerun graph retains the same two axes. The precovered-shape counterfactual also reaches recognition with zero new episode axis edges, which is the relevant novelty control.

This is evidence for novelty within the fixed seed closure and one preregistered relation schema. It does not support a claim of general intelligence or unconstrained concept invention: the fixture provides exactly two training concepts, one graph-resident five-field semantic relation per concept, a fixed two-step policy, and a preselected structural licensing rule.

## Reproducibility requires an undeclared import-path setup

**Confirmed:** executing `python3 scripts/run_autonomous_discovery_demo.py --fixture ... --output ...` from the repository root raises `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'domains'`. The same runner and its 15 focused tests pass under `PYTHONPATH=. ./.venv/bin/python`; no skips were reported. The checked code must either be installed as a package or the runner must establish the repository root itself before importing project modules, otherwise the claimed artifact command is not directly reproducible.
