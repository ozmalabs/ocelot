# Narrative information-force derivation contract

The contract defines information force as the residual derivative \(\mathcal F_I=-D\mathcal R_I\) on a declared differential information-state space, with separately required Newtonian, covariant, source, and geometry bridges. Physical-gravity recovery remains conditional on flat and load-shaped recovery, history-bearing attribution, universal coupling, and empirical limits; it does not identify momentum with gravity or overextend local accelerometry. **Watch for:** **medium severity, confirmed** — the derivation types the proper-time map as \(\Theta:\mathfrak C\to\tau\), but the paper’s information-force contract leaves \(\Theta\) as an untyped bundle of “clock and readout conditions,” so its clock obligation is not independently specified at the same level as the force, source, and geometry maps.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

An information realization produces a residual covector first; distinct bridges then establish any physical force, source, or geometry meaning. The required flat and load-shaped recoveries are conditional premises, not identities supplied by the Narrative vocabulary.

Local accelerometry distinguishes free and supported motion only in its domain, while momentum-versus-load attribution requires the declared history, source/support, and comparison record. 01i remains a conditional programme excluded from the release allowlist.

## The formal contract leaves the clock bundled into other bridges

**Medium severity, confirmed.** H-IF says it must supply “measurement-preserving physical state and clock maps” at `papers/formal/01i_graphic_equalisation_passive_continuation_momentum_gravity.tex:284-285`, but IF2 only passes \(\Theta\) into \(K_{\rm N}\) and \(K_4\), then calls it physical-clock and readout conditions (lines 289-297). IF3 likewise only consumes it as an input to \(L\) and \(\Phi\) (lines 301-305). It does not declare \(\Theta\)’s domain, codomain, clock observable, coordinate-time/proper-time relation, or accelerometer-readout conditions.

The derivation does: its map ledger at `papers/derivations/01i_passive_continuation_momentum_gravity_derivation.md:236` declares \(\Theta:\mathfrak C\to\tau\), ties it to a physical clock, and requires derived velocity, acceleration, and an accelerometer observable. Its IF2 chain then consumes \(\Theta\) at lines 325-334. G2 later requires a causal/proper-time observable in the paper, but does not provide the independently typed bridge promised by H-IF. Add the derivation’s signature and operational requirements to the paper’s contract, then have IF2–IF3 consume that declared map.

## The residual and physical meanings are otherwise separated

H-IF introduces \(\mathcal Q_I\), \(Z\), and \(\mathcal R_I\) before defining \(\mathcal F_I\in T_q^*\mathcal Q_I\) at `papers/formal/01i_graphic_equalisation_passive_continuation_momentum_gravity.tex:265-280` and `papers/derivations/01i_passive_continuation_momentum_gravity_derivation.md:296-318`. \(K_{\rm N}\), \(K_4\), \(L\), and \(\Phi\) have distinct target roles, and direct insertion of the residual into a stress-energy or metric equation is prohibited.

## Recovery, attribution, and release remain bounded

The paper requires H-IF, IF2–IF3, F1–F6, G1–G8, H-PC, history-bearing attribution, universal coupling, and weak-field/precision limits before the claimed gravitational regime is recovered (`papers/formal/01i_graphic_equalisation_passive_continuation_momentum_gravity.tex:309-322`); derivation T0 matches this at lines 343-354. Both retain momentum/gravity non-identity and the local accelerometer boundary (formal lines 164-177; derivation lines 144-207 and 440-442). `papers/README.md:129-130` explicitly keeps conditional 01i excluded from the fail-closed release allowlist.
