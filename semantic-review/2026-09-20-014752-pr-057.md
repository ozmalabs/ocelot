# Immutable issuance snapshots for credential evidence

DISPATCH-057 hardens verifier-owned capability receipts and credential-readiness evidence against mutation after issuance. Both registries retain the exact issued object, an issuance-time snapshot of every declared field, and the original public commitment; later validation requires all three to remain consistent, so `object.__setattr__` followed by resealing cannot upgrade altered evidence. The focused boundary suite and an independent attack harness also preserve the valid evidence path and show that mutated inputs are rejected before any backend call. The repository-only boundary remains intentionally unable to execute until external signed-environment and approval prerequisites are supplied.

Watch for: **confirmed** operational execution remains BLOCKED because the active packet has no cryptographically verified short-lived credential evidence and approval is disabled; this is an external prerequisite, not a defect in the snapshot fix.

### Issuance snapshots close the legitimate-object reseal gap

`CapabilityReceipt` and `VerifiedCredentialReadinessEvidence` are still verifier-created exact types, and their registries retain the original object rather than only its numeric identity. Each registry entry now also retains the complete issuance snapshot and original seal. Registration validation compares the current object fields and seal with that snapshot and recomputes the commitment against the original seal, so changing a field with `object.__setattr__` and recomputing the public digest fails even when the object began as a genuine issued instance.

The receipt factory rejects the mutated-and-resealed receipt before it reaches `executor.validate_credential_binding`; the IAM boundary rejects mutated-and-resealed evidence before the reviewed executor is asked to perform any operation. This preserves the fail-closed trust boundary identified by REVIEW-057 rather than relying on the public seal alone.

### Valid promotion and executor reachability

The independent harness built a real fixture, verified that its receipt and evidence were registered, successfully promoted fresh evidence from `unresolved` to `verified`, then applied field mutation plus a recomputed seal to each issued object. The receipt attack raised `CredentialEvidenceError` with no backend calls; the evidence attack raised `IAMBoundaryError` with no backend calls. The focused suite also covers the valid reviewed-boundary success path and confirms the normal request/receipt flow remains intact.

### Local-only protection and remaining gates

The focused run completed without changing the protected packet, approval, prestate, R18R10/R18R12 ledger, historical packet, or `P5_MASTER_RUNSHEET.md`; before/after SHA-256 inventories were identical. No cloud, IAM, GCS, KMS, credential, or retry operation was performed.

`validate_iam_boundary()` continues to report `ready_for_executor: false`, `mutations_performed: false`, and `applied_state: false`. The **confirmed** remaining prerequisites are a credential evidence record bound to the current V2 packet/provider/authority/exact capability account, externally supplied signed-environment evidence with a cryptographically verified short-lived credential, and an enabled fresh approval plus reviewed executor path. Until those exist, execution must remain blocked.
