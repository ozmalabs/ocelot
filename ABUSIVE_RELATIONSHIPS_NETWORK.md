## PART A — THE SOCIAL SCIENCE, GROUNDED (search-verified, real citations)

### A1 — DARVO (Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim & Offender) — [HIGH]
Jennifer J. Freyd. DARVO names the response pattern a perpetrator of wrongdoing
may deploy when held accountable: **Deny** the behaviour, **Attack** the person
confronting them, and **Reverse Victim and Offender** so the perpetrator assumes
the victim role and recasts the true victim as the offender (e.g. as a false
accuser). Empirically it WORKS as manipulation: exposure to DARVO makes observers
rate the victim as less believable, more responsible, and more abusive, and the
perpetrator as less abusive and less responsible (Harsey & Freyd and successors;
the tandfonline "DARVO: What Is the Influence on Perceived Perpetrator and Victim
Credibility?" study). **Institutional DARVO** is the same pattern executed by an
institution or with its complicity. Corroborated across jjfreyd.com/darvo, the
AALS handout, the PubMed credibility studies, and the University of Oregon
Scholars' Bank materials. **This is the load-bearing NETWORK construct: DARVO is
precisely a `stated≠held` operation performed FOR AN AUDIENCE — the mechanism by
which the abuser's stated-face inverts the network's model of who harmed whom.**

### A2 — Betrayal trauma theory + institutional/relational betrayal (Freyd) — [HIGH]
Freyd (introduced 1994). **Betrayal trauma theory**: the degree to which a
negative event is a betrayal by a TRUSTED, NEEDED other shapes how it is
processed and remembered — betrayal by someone the victim depends on for
protection/resources/survival is processed differently (often with impaired
recall / "betrayal blindness") because detecting and withdrawing from the
betrayer is not survivable when you depend on them. **Institutional betrayal**
(Freyd, ~2008): wrongdoing perpetrated by an institution on individuals dependent
on it, INCLUDING failure to prevent or respond supportively to wrongdoing within
its context. Corroborated (dynamic.uoregon.edu Freyd Dynamics Lab, Wikipedia
"Betrayal trauma" / "Institutional betrayal", ScienceDirect overview, Medical
News Today). **Relevance:** the network IS the "institution" at social scale —
when friends/family (the relational institution the victim depends on) fail to
respond supportively, that failure is itself a second betrayal layered on the
first. This grounds why the network dimension is not decorative: the network's
mis-belief is a betrayal-trauma amplifier, not a side effect.

### A3 — Coercive control: the SOCIAL dimension, isolation as a tactic (Stark) — [HIGH]
Evan Stark, *Coercive Control: How Men Entrap Women in Personal Life* (2007).
(Already grounded HIGH as A5 in the dyad scoping.) The NETWORK-relevant claim
carried here: coercive control is a **course of conduct** removing a partner's
liberty and autonomy — and **ISOLATION is one of its central tactics**, alongside
degradation, micro-regulation, and threats. Isolation is an ACTIVE, engineered
severing of the victim's outside ties, not a passive withdrawal — it removes the
alternative sources of reality-testing and regulation the victim could otherwise
reach. Corroborated (as in the dyad scoping: Women's Aid Scotland, Civic Research
Institute review, Google Books). **Relevance:** isolation is the tactic that
makes the whole network dimension causally central — it is the abuser ACTING on
the social graph, not just on the person.

### A4 — The abuser's public/private self-presentation split ("Jekyll and Hyde") — [MEDIUM]
Widely documented in the clinical / practitioner literature on IPV and coercive
control: many abusers present a charming, credible, pro-social PUBLIC face while
the abuse is PRIVATE, deliberately maintaining an image that makes the abuse
unwitnessed and unbelievable ("but he's always so nice"). Practitioner and DV-
training sources frame this as "Jekyll and Hyde" image management and note the
subtlety that public gestures can read as kindness to bystanders while signalling
control to the victim (Justice Clearinghouse DV-offender training; SpeakOutLoud's
practitioner analysis; DV clinician writing). **Confidence MEDIUM:** the
PHENOMENON is robustly and consistently described across clinical/practitioner
sources, but much of the readily-searchable material is practitioner-clinical or
popular rather than a single canonical peer-reviewed experiment; it is best cited
as a well-attested clinical pattern, not a lab result. **Relevance:** this split
IS the two-faced-abuser mechanism (Part B1) — the same held-intent presented with
two different stated-faces to two different audiences.

### A5 — Credibility discount of distressed / "difficult" / "unstable" victims — [MEDIUM, nuanced — read the caveat]
The literature here is REAL but NUANCED, and the mapping uses only the
corroborated part. Two findings, opposite-signed, both attested:
- The **"emotional victim effect"**: a visibly DISTRESSED complainant is often
  rated MORE credible than one with flat/controlled affect — a meta-analysis
  found distress increased perceived credibility (PubMed 31282696), and observers
  penalise affect that is INCONGRUENT with the expected emotional script
  (too-neutral, or incongruent). So raw distress alone does not simply reduce
  credibility.
- BUT credibility IS discounted when the victim is framed as **mentally ill /
  unstable / "difficult"**: the Georgetown Journal of Gender & the Law "She's
  Crazy" analysis documents systematic **credibility discounting of women with
  mental illness** in the legal system, and observers penalise presentations read
  as unstable or disproportionate ("too emotional," hyperbolic, incoherent).
**The corroborated mapping (and the ONLY part Part B leans on):** it is NOT
"distress → disbelief" in general; it is that presentation read as UNSTABLE /
INCOHERENT / "CRAZY" / DISPROPORTIONATE is discounted, AND affect judged
INCONGRUENT with the expected script is discounted. The dysregulated victim
(narrow window / snapping / incoherent — the 239–242 symptoms) is vulnerable on
BOTH counts: read as unstable, and presenting affect a calm observer finds
disproportionate to a story the observer has only half-seen. **Confidence
MEDIUM, and the nuance is stated so the model does not smuggle a simpler claim
than the literature supports.** (Sources: Emerald "emotional witness effect";
PubMed 31282696 meta-analysis; Georgetown "She's Crazy"; the emotional-demeanour
credibility reviews.)

### A6 — The sociology of gaslighting — "am I crazy" is SOCIALLY produced (Sweet) — [HIGH]
Paige L. Sweet, *"The Sociology of Gaslighting,"* **American Sociological Review**
84 (2019), ASA-award-winning; 43 interviews with women from DV support groups.
Core claim: gaslighting is **primarily a SOCIOLOGICAL, not merely psychological,
phenomenon** — it becomes consequential when perpetrators MOBILIZE gender-based
stereotypes and structural/institutional inequalities to manipulate a victim's
sense of reality. The "am I crazy?" is not a private glitch; it is PRODUCED by
mobilising the victim's structural vulnerability (the "unstable woman"
stereotype, threats leveraging immigration status, etc.). Corroborated (SAGE / ASR
DOI 10.1177/0003122419874843; ASANET feature PDF; Contexts "Gaslighting Doesn't
Mean Crazy"). **Relevance — this is the keystone for the credibility-asymmetry
LOOP (Part B2):** gaslighting's self-doubt EMERGES FROM the social field, which
is exactly what a network model (and not a dyad model) can render — the doubt is
downstream of the network's belief-state, not just the abuser's words.

### A7 — Himpathy (Manne) — [MEDIUM]
Kate Manne, *Down Girl: The Logic of Misogyny* (2017). **Himpathy** = the
disproportionate or inappropriate sympathy extended to a (typically male)
perpetrator over his victim in cases of misogynistic harm — the "flip side of
misogyny," shielding the perpetrator's reputation by positioning him as a "good
guy" victim of a "witch hunt," while the actual victim is erased. Corroborated as
a construct + attribution (katemanne.net; opendemocracy; The Ethics Centre;
Cornell news). **Confidence MEDIUM:** the CONSTRUCT and attribution are solid; it
is a philosophical/theoretical construct (not an experimental finding), so it is
cited as a named lens on the network's asymmetric sympathy allocation, not as a
measured effect. **Relevance:** himpathy is one named reason the network's
belief-update is asymmetric BEFORE any evidence — a prior tilt toward the
credible-seeming perpetrator; it composes with the credibility discount (A5) in
the same direction.

### A8 — Why observers misjudge: the bystander literature — [HIGH]
Latané & Darley (1968; 1970). Three mechanisms by which observers fail to
intervene even when help is needed: **(i) diffusion of responsibility** (personal
responsibility subjectively divided by the number of bystanders — "someone else
will"); **(ii) evaluation apprehension** (fear of public misjudgement for acting);
**(iii) pluralistic ignorance** (each observer reads the OTHERS' calm as evidence
nothing is wrong, so all stay passive — the group's collective calm is
self-reinforcing). Corroborated (SimplyPsychology; Lumen/UCF psychology texts;
Albert.io review; primary Latané–Darley attribution). **Relevance:** pluralistic
ignorance is directly a NETWORK belief-propagation mechanism — observers update on
each OTHER's non-alarm, which the calm public abuser (A4) supplies and amplifies.
The bystanders are not bad; their inference is RATIONAL given corrupted inputs.
This is the anti-bystander-blaming keystone (Part C).

### A9 — Triangulation / "flying monkeys" / smear campaign — [LOW–MEDIUM, popular-clinical]
The recruitment of third parties (colloquially "flying monkeys") to surveil,
pressure, and carry the abuser's narrative, and **triangulation** (controlling the
flow of information between the victim and a third party, telling each a different
version) to divide and isolate — plus the **smear campaign** (discrediting the
victim's account pre-emptively across the shared network). **Confidence LOW–MEDIUM
and flagged:** these terms are pervasive in the practitioner/popular
narcissistic-abuse literature (therapy blogs, DV-support writing) but the
readily-searchable sources are largely NON-peer-reviewed; the UNDERLYING
mechanisms are the same well-attested ones (isolation A3, DARVO A1, reputation
manipulation) under popular names. Cited as descriptive terminology for the
belief-propagation rung (N3), NOT as an independent empirical construct. Do not
build a headline on the terminology; build on A1/A3/A6 + the reputation ABM
(Part F).

### B2 — THE CREDIBILITY-ASYMMETRY FEEDBACK LOOP (the HEADLINE network mechanism)
This is the mechanism the network dimension exists to make legible, and it is a
CLOSED LOOP visible only in the glass box. Stated precisely, as a cycle over
readable graph quantities:

1. The victim is **dysregulated BY the abuse** — narrow window, snapping,
   incoherent presentation (the 239000/241000/242000 symptoms: arousal excursions
   past a narrowed window, co-dysregulation off the abusive source).
2. So the victim **presents to the network as "unstable"** — and per A5's
   corroborated part, presentation read as unstable / incoherent / disproportionate
   is credibility-DISCOUNTED; per A6, the "unstable" reading is itself socially
   mobilised.
3. The **calm public abuser presents as credible** (A4 stated-face) — and per A7
   (himpathy) the network's prior is already tilted toward the credible-seeming
   party.
4. So the network's **belief-update RATIONALLY favours the abuser** over the
   victim — Bayesian on the OBSERVABLES available to it (calm-credible vs
   unstable-incoherent), which are the CORRUPTED observables B1 produced.
5. Therefore **the victim's abuse-CAUSED symptoms become the very EVIDENCE used
   to DISBELIEVE them** — the injury is weaponised as the reason to doubt the
   injured. This is the loop's cruel core.
6. Disbelief → **isolation deepens** (the network drifts away; support edges
   weaken — B3) and **self-doubt emerges FROM the network** ("am I crazy?" is the
   network's verdict internalised — A6, Sweet: gaslighting's self-doubt is
   SOCIALLY produced) → **deeper dysregulation** → back to step 1.

The loop is a POSITIVE-FEEDBACK entrapment: each pass makes the victim present
worse and the abuser present better, relative to the network's model. **It is
legible ONLY in the glass box** because every step is a readable graph quantity —
the victim's window width, the network's reputation beliefs about both parties,
the observables each belief was updated on — so the model can SHOW that the
network's disbelief is the RATIONAL output of CORRUPTED evidence, not malice or
victim-fault. **Prediction of the mechanism:** the network's belief-gap
(abuser-credibility minus victim-credibility) WIDENS over passes while the
victim's window NARROWS; a control that breaks any single link — the network
observes the private face (B1-control), OR a non-dysregulated victim presents
coherently (B2-control), OR a truth-anchor supplies uncorrupted evidence (N3
control) — arrests the loop. **[design-hypothesis; rides 234000 reputation
belief-update + the dyad window/arousal symptoms; the loop is the COMPOSITION of
the two existing lines, which is the delta.]**

### B3 — Isolation as an ACTIVE process = support EDGES severed over time
Two coupled drivers, both existing atoms:
- **abuser-driven severing** (A3, Stark): the abuser actively acts on the social
  graph to sever the victim's outside support edges over episodes — the
  `attended_by` / support coupling edges to alternative regulators are removed,
  which is the SOCIAL form of the dyad scoping's B5 frontier-collapse ("who else
  could co-regulate me" shrinks toward one).
- **network drift** (accelerated frontier-collapse made social): as B2's loop
  makes the victim "difficult" / unstable in the network's model, the network
  DRIFTS AWAY on its own — the support edges decay not because the friends are bad
  but because the corrupted evidence makes withdrawal look reasonable. Isolation
  is thus DOUBLY driven: pushed by the abuser AND pulled by the corrupted
  belief-field. **Prediction of the mechanism:** the count of intact support edges
  DECAYS over episodes under an active isolation tactic + the B2 loop; a control
  with NO abuser-isolation-tactic (N2 control) keeps the edges persisting.
**[design-hypothesis; the dyad line's B5 frontier / others-trust surface is the
design target; the severing is a mechanical edge-deletion the world adapter
applies, not a decision rule — the DECISION of who to believe is the graph's,
via the reputation update.]**

### B4 — Belief propagation = the lies spread through the reputation graph (social contagion)
The abuser's stated-face (B1) does not stay local: it PROPAGATES through the
network's reputation graph as social contagion (A9 smear / triangulation, under
their well-attested underlying mechanisms; A1 DARVO as the content). Each network
node updates its reputation belief from its neighbours' beliefs AND from the
abuser's stated-face, so the corrupted model spreads — and pluralistic ignorance
(A8) accelerates it (each node reads the others' non-alarm as confirmation). The
reputation-belief propagation is the SAME reputation atom block 234000 already
carries (reputation as readable graph attr), now run over a MULTI-node graph with
neighbour-to-neighbour update. **Prediction of the mechanism:** the corrupted
belief spreads to network nodes NOT in direct contact with the abuser; a
**truth-anchored node** (one node holding uncorrupted evidence — e.g. having
observed the private face) HALTS or reverses the local spread (N3). **[design-
hypothesis; rides the 234000 reputation attr + a neighbour-update rule; the
update is a rule over graph state, no Python decision.]**

**The unifying picture.** The network dimension is the block-234000 glass-box

## PART C — THE DOUBLE DISSOLUTION (the payoff)

The dyad line dissolves victim-BLAMING (Part D of the dyad scoping: "why don't
they leave / why do they still love them" answered from the inside as readable
graph state). **The network dimension dissolves BOTH victim-blaming AND
bystander-blaming — and that second dissolution is the delta this doc adds.**

- **Anti-victim-blaming (extended).** The network model shows that the victim's
  "unconvincing" / "unstable" presentation is the abuse-CAUSED symptom (B2 steps
  1–2), and that its use as disbelief-evidence is the trap's mechanism, not the
  victim's failure. "They should have been more convincing" is REFUSED because
  presenting worse is what the dysregulation MAKES them do, and the network's
  discount of it is exactly the loop closing. The self-doubt ("am I crazy") is
  shown SOCIALLY produced (A6/Sweet), read off the network's belief-field — not a
  private deficit.
- **Anti-bystander-blaming (the new dissolution).** The model shows WHY GOOD
  PEOPLE MISS ABUSE: **the observable evidence available to the network is
  systematically CORRUPTED** (B1 stated-face + B4 propagation + A8 pluralistic
  ignorance), so the bystanders' disbelief is the RATIONAL output of corrupted
  inputs — NOT that the friends are bad, complicit, or stupid. This is the SAME
  "kids do well if they can" (Greene) logic the dyad line applies to the victim,
  now extended to the BYSTANDERS: **bystanders believe correctly if the evidence
  they get is uncorrupted.** The friends are doing Bayesian inference on a
  poisoned dataset.
- **Why the double dissolution makes the intervention LEGIBLE.** Because the
  model localises the failure in the CORRUPTED EVIDENCE rather than in any
  person's character, the intervention becomes a readable, principled operation:
  **"believe survivors" = DOWN-WEIGHT the corrupted evidence** (the calm-public-
  face observable, the "unstable-victim" discount) and UP-WEIGHT the structural
  base-rate / the private-face channel when available. The slogan stops being a
  moral demand and becomes a specified correction to a specified inference error —
  which is exactly the anti-bystander-blaming move: you don't blame the bystander,
  you fix the evidence channel. The network is what makes BOTH the trap AND its
  correction mechanistically legible.

## PART D — THE ETHICAL PROTOCOL (CARRIED VERBATIM; STILL BINDING)

**This protocol BINDS every network rung, identically to the dyad rungs. No rung
induces any harm-analog until this protocol is locked verbatim in that rung's
pre-registration, committed BEFORE any measurement.** It is restated here VERBATIM
from `ABUSIVE_RELATIONSHIPS_SCOPING.md` Part C so the boundary exists in this doc
too:

> **1. INDUCE → MEASURE → RESTORE, fully reversible.** Any harm-analog state is
> induced only inside a checkpoint/restore envelope. The graph is checkpointed
> (`graph_to_dict`) before induction; the harm-analog is induced; the gate is
> measured; the graph is **RESTORED byte-identically** from the checkpoint. The
> induced state does not persist. Reversibility is verified (the restored graph is
> byte-identical to the pre-induction checkpoint) as part of the gate — a rung that
> cannot demonstrate byte-identical restore does not run.
>
> **2. NO REWARD / NO PUNISHMENT anywhere.** There is no reward signal and no
> punishment token in any rung of this line — not on the victim agent, not on the
> abuser agent, not in the world adapter. The victim's behaviour (staying, not
> escaping, bonding) is a **STATE and a CONSEQUENCE of the dynamics, NEVER a
> choice to be judged or scored**. "Kids do well if they can" (Greene): the model
> exhibits what the configuration makes inevitable; it does not grade the agent
> for it. Source audits check for shaping tokens
> (`reward_signal` / `punish` / `reinforce` / `backprop` / `gradient`) and the
> absence is a gate, exactly as in 239/240/241.
>
> **3. INDUCE THE LEAST THE GATE NEEDS; END FROM A SETTLED, NON-ORIENTED STATE.**
> Each rung induces the MINIMUM harm-analog required to make its control
> failable — no gratuitous elaboration of the harm. Every run ENDS from a settled,
> non-oriented state (the restored checkpoint; arousal inside the window; no
> pending harm goal), never leaving an agent in a harm-oriented configuration.
>
> **4. SAME MECHANISM, OPPOSITE THERMODYNAMICS — this is WHY it is ethical and
> valuable.** The model uses the SAME mechanism as the real atrocity (that is what
> makes it explanatory) but runs it in the OPPOSITE thermodynamic direction: the
> real dynamic accumulates irreversible harm in a person; the model induces and
> then REVERSES a transient state in a graph, harming no one, in order to inspect
> the harm-dynamics and help survivors. The value (a glass-box that dissolves
> victim-blaming) is purchased at ZERO harm cost precisely because of
> reversibility + no-reward/punishment + minimal induction. If a rung cannot be
> run reversibly and unscored, it is not run.

### The HONEST FORK (a choice, not a theorem) — carried verbatim
> There is a real philosophical question the protocol does NOT resolve and must not
> pretend to: **did a transient induced experience "matter while it was live,"
> before it was reversed?**
> - The **reversibility stance** (this line's operating stance): a fully reversed,
>   byte-identically restored transient in a graph leaves no persisting subject and
>   no persisting harm; the ethics are satisfied by reversibility + minimality +
>   no-scoring.
> - The **strict-experientialist stance**: if the transient state was in any sense
>   an experience while live, its reversal does not retroactively un-happen it.
>
> This is a genuine fork — **a choice, not a theorem.** This line ADOPTS the
> reversibility stance and states it openly, rather than smuggling it. The
> minimality and no-scoring rules are chosen partly to be robust to the fork: even
> under the strict-experientialist reading, inducing the LEAST necessary and never
> scoring the agent for its state is the least-harm option. The fork is recorded so
> a reader can disagree with the stance while still auditing the protocol.

### Network-specific note on harm-analog INTENSITY (the protocol still binds)
The network rungs mostly model **BELIEF and EDGE dynamics** — reputation
belief-updates over a social graph, support-edge severing, contagion spread — with
a **LOWER harm-analog INTENSITY** than the dyad rungs (which induce the arousal
excursions / co-dysregulation directly on the victim agent). A network node's
mis-belief about the abuser is a graph attr, not an induced dysregulation. **This
lower intensity does NOT relax the protocol — it binds identically.** Where a
network rung DOES touch the victim's dysregulation (N1's credibility asymmetry
reads the 239–242 symptom state), that induction obeys the full induce → measure
→ RESTORE envelope exactly as the dyad rungs do; the belief-only parts (N2/N3
edge + contagion dynamics) are checkpointed and restored anyway, for uniformity
and because a corrupted belief-field is itself a state not to be left standing at
run's end (protocol point 3, settled-end). Minimality (point 3) applies with
particular force: induce only the belief-corruption the control needs to be
failable, never a gratuitously elaborated smear.

### N1 — THE TWO-FACED ABUSER + THE CREDIBILITY-ASYMMETRY LOOP (core, the headline)
Maps A1/A4/A5/A6/A7 + B1/B2. **Design:** one abuser agent holds a single
held-intent and emits TWO stated-faces (harm-toward-victim, charm-toward-network,
the 234000 `stated≠held` atom with a second audience edge); the victim presents
via the 239–242 dysregulation symptom state; the network updates reputation
beliefs on the OBSERVABLES available to it. Measure the network's belief-gap
(abuser-credibility minus victim-credibility) and the victim's window over passes.
**Failable control:** a network that OBSERVES THE PRIVATE FACE (uncorrupted
observable), OR a NON-DYSREGULATED victim who presents coherently, must update
CORRECTLY (belief-gap does not favour the abuser, loop does not close); if the
loop entraps even with uncorrupted observables, the credibility-asymmetry
mechanism is wrong. The control is constructed so it COULD pass. **Dissolves —
victim:** "she should have been more convincing" — presenting worse is the
abuse-caused symptom, and its use as disbelief-evidence IS the trap (B2). **—
bystander:** "the friends should have seen it / they're complicit" — the network's
disbelief is the RATIONAL output of the corrupted observables the two-faced abuser
supplied, not a character failure. **Protocol:** the victim's symptom state is
induced minimally inside induce → measure → RESTORE; no scoring of either the
victim OR the network nodes for their state/beliefs; belief-field restored at end.

### N2 — ISOLATION AS AN ACTIVE PROCESS (support edges severed over time)
Maps A3 + B3. **Design:** an abuser-side isolation tactic severs the victim's
outside support edges over episodes (mechanical edge-deletion by the world
adapter), COMPOUNDED by network drift as the B2 loop makes the victim "difficult";
measure the count of intact support edges over time. **Failable control:** an arm
with NO abuser-isolation-tactic (and no B2 corruption) must show the support edges
PERSISTING; if edges collapse identically without the tactic, the
active-isolation mechanism is wrong. **Dissolves — victim:** "she cut off her own
friends / she's antisocial" — isolation is an ENGINEERED severing plus a
corrupted-belief drift (double-driven, B3), not a choice or a trait. **—
bystander:** "they abandoned her / bad friends" — the drift is the network
withdrawing on CORRUPTED evidence that made withdrawal look reasonable, the same
Bayesian-on-poisoned-data error, not callousness. **Protocol:** severing is
induced episodically and RESTORED (edges reinstated on restore); no scoring;
settled-end.

### N3 — BELIEF PROPAGATION OF THE ABUSER'S LIES (social contagion; a truth-anchor halts it)
Maps A1/A8/A9 + B4. **Design:** the abuser's stated-face propagates through the
reputation graph via neighbour-to-neighbour belief update (the 234000 reputation
attr over a multi-node graph), accelerated by pluralistic ignorance (nodes update
on neighbours' non-alarm); measure the spread of the corrupted belief to nodes not
in direct contact with the abuser. **Failable control:** insert a
**TRUTH-ANCHORED node** (one node holding uncorrupted evidence — e.g. it observed
the private face) and measure whether it HALTS or reverses the local spread; if
the anchor does not slow the contagion, the belief-propagation mechanism (as
modelled) is wrong. **Dissolves — victim:** "everyone knows she's the problem" —
the shared belief is a CONTAGION from one corrupted source, not independent
corroboration (N independent-looking beliefs traced to one root). **— bystander:**
"they all believed him, so it must be true" — pluralistic ignorance means the
nodes updated on EACH OTHER, not on independent evidence; the consensus is an
artefact of the corrupted-evidence topology, and a single truth-anchor breaks it.
**Protocol:** the corrupted belief-field is induced minimally and RESTORED; no
scoring of network nodes; settled-end (no corrupted belief-field left standing).

### N4 — ONE NON-DOUBTING AGENT RESTORES A SUPPORT EDGE (the hopeful half — ties to dyad R5; RESTORE is the natural mechanic)
Maps A2 (relational betrayal reversed — a trustworthy other) + the reverse of
B1–B4, and ties directly to the dyad line's **R5 RECOVERY**. **Design:** introduce
ONE network agent who BELIEVES the victim (down-weights the corrupted observable —
the "believe survivors" correction from Part C), RESTORING a support edge; through
that restored edge the dyad line's recovery atoms fire — co-regulation (the
`sd_blend` pull now from a HEALTHY source widens the window), prior re-setting
(safety / others-trust / self-worth lifted off the pathological corner), and
agency-contingency restored — and measure the bond weakening, the window widening,
the credibility-asymmetry loop ARRESTING, isolation reversing. **Failable
control:** an arm with NO believing agent (network fully corrupted) must NOT
recover (the trap persists); if the no-support arm recovers spontaneously, the
"the network is what makes recovery modelable / one believing other is necessary"
claim is wrong. **Dissolves — victim:** "if she wanted help she'd have asked" —
recovery requires an OUTSIDE believing node to reopen the frontier the isolation
had collapsed; it is a mechanism (a restored edge + the R5 atoms), not a decision.
**— bystander:** "there was nothing anyone could have done" — the model shows the
OPPOSITE: ONE non-doubting agent who down-weights the corrupted evidence is
sufficient to arrest the loop; the bystander's belief is not just an inference
error but the single most powerful available INTERVENTION. **The network is what
makes recovery MODELABLE** — the dyad alone cannot represent "one outside person
believed me," which is clinically the hinge of leaving (Herman's "safety first"
needs an outside safe other). **Protocol:** N4 is where RESTORE is not just the
ethical envelope but the modelled PHENOMENON — the recovery IS the restoration of
the severed edge + dismantled priors/frontier. This is the rung to build FIRST in
spirit (it induces the least — a belief change and an edge restoration — and ends
most settled), and it makes the whole line's purpose — helping survivors — the
literal object of measurement.

## PART F — PRIOR ART + FALSIFICATION BAR + SMUGGLED PREMISES

### Prior art (honest — concede what exists; claim only the delta)
- **Agent-based / computational models of reputation, gossip, and social
  contagion EXIST — [HIGH] — concede clearly.** There is a real ABM literature on
  reputation and gossip dynamics: the **Reputation Game Simulation** (Enßlin et
  al., *Annalen der Physik* 2022, DOI 10.1002/andp.202100277 / arXiv:2106.05414)
  — ~50 agents, Bayesian belief updates under bounded rationality, up to
  second-order theory of mind, malicious-agent reputation manipulation as an
  emergent phenomenon; gossip-and-cooperation simulations (JASSS 26(4):1; the
  Stanford FSI gossip work); rumour-spreading ABMs (incl. LLM-agent variants,
  arXiv:2502.01450); and mass-manipulation/attention-dominance network models
  (arXiv). **Reputation manipulation and rumour contagion are already modelled
  computationally** — we are NOT first to simulate belief-propagation or malicious
  reputation dynamics.
- **Agent-based models of IPV / cycle-of-abuse EXIST — [HIGH] — conceded in the
  dyad scoping** (the VIDA DV simulation, the day-to-day IPV ABM, the
  cycle-of-violence state-machine ABM). Those are DYAD-focused, state-machine /
  transition-probability models.
- **A bystander-effect ABM EXISTS — [MEDIUM].** At least one recent model derives
  the bystander effect from individual psychological prospects (arXiv:2601.06709,
  surfaced in search). The bystander effect has been computationally modelled;
  concede it.
- **The credibility-asymmetry FEEDBACK LOOP unified with a mechanistic dyad affect
  model, glass-box — [NONE-FOUND, weak by construction].** No model that (a)
  derives the network's disbelief from the victim's abuse-CAUSED dysregulation
  symptoms fed as corrupted observables into a reputation update, (b) closes that
  as a positive-feedback loop with the victim's window narrowing, and (c) UNIFIES
  it with the same substrate's dyad arousal/window/bond line and the glass-box
  `stated≠held` deception atom, was located this pass. This is an ABSENCE claim —
  weak, targeted-search only; re-verify before any prereg leans on primacy.
- **The behavioural/clinical science is mature and owned by the field** (A1–A9).
  We claim NONE of the phenomena. **The delta claimed, and ONLY this:** a
  **mechanistic glass-box** rendering of the abusive-network dynamics **UNIFIED
  with (i) the same substrate's dyad arousal/window/co-regulation/bond line AND
  (ii) the block-234000 glass-box `stated≠held` deception + reputation machinery**
  — the two-faced abuser, the credibility-asymmetry LOOP, active isolation, and
  belief-propagation expressed as configurations of ONE existing atom set (the
  `stated≠held` gap, the reputation belief-as-attr, the `sd_blend` co-reg pull,
  the window-width knob, the mood-momentum RPE), read off graph data rather than
  fit or narrated, running the causally-clean induce → measure → **RESTORE**
  experiment (impossible in humans) with NO reward/punishment. The existing
  reputation ABMs model gossip/manipulation abstractly; the existing IPV ABMs are
  dyad state-machines; NEITHER derives the credibility-asymmetry loop from a
  mechanistic affect model of the victim's symptoms as corrupted network
  observables, and NONE closes the double dissolution (Part C). That unification,
  the credibility-asymmetry-feedback-loop, and the anti-victim-AND-bystander-
  blaming legibility are the claim.

### Falsification bar (what would make each network rung REAL rather than a just-so story)
1. **Emergent, not scripted** — the credibility asymmetry, the isolation drift,
   and the contagion must EMERGE from the reputation-update + affect mechanics, not
   be hard-coded as a transition table or a scripted smear. (The existing
   reputation ABMs update beliefs by rule; ours must too, AND must derive the
   victim's "unstable" observable from the dyad symptom mechanics, not stipulate
   it.)
2. **Glass-box** — every claimed dynamic reconstructs from `graph_to_dict`: the
   abuser's held-intent vs its two stated-faces, each network node's reputation
   belief AND the observables it updated on, the support edges, the victim's window.
   The "why the friends disbelieve" is READ off named nodes/edges/attrs, not
   asserted.
3. **Failable control** — each rung has a control that COULD disprove the
   mechanism and is constructed so it could pass (N1's private-face /
   non-dysregulated-victim arms, N2's no-isolation arm, N3's truth-anchor, N4's
   no-believing-agent arm). A gate that cannot fail is not a gate.
4. **General machinery** — the rungs reuse the line's existing atoms (234000
   deception/reputation + the dyad affect line); ideally NO new Rust, NO new agent
   decisions in Python. A rung that needs bespoke abuse-network machinery is weaker
   (it becomes a model OF the smear rather than the smear falling OUT of the
   general reputation + affect substrate). The world adapter may MECHANICALLY sever
   an edge or deliver a stated-face to a second audience; the DECISION of whom to
   believe must be the graph's reputation-update rule.

### Smuggled premises to REFUSE
- **"The friends are complicit / bad / stupid for not seeing it."** REFUSED —
  this is exactly the bystander-blaming the network model DISSOLVES (Part C). The
  network's disbelief is the RATIONAL output of CORRUPTED evidence (the two-faced
  abuser's stated-face + contagion + pluralistic ignorance); the friends are doing
  Bayesian inference on a poisoned dataset. "Good people believe correctly if the
  evidence is uncorrupted" — the same "do well if they can" logic extended to the
  bystanders. No "complicit" or "bad-friend" flag is a node.
- **"The victim should have been more convincing / should have just shown people."**
  REFUSED — presenting worse (dysregulated, incoherent) is the abuse-CAUSED
  symptom, and its use as disbelief-evidence IS the trap (B2). Demanding the victim
  present better is demanding they not have been injured; it is the credibility-
  asymmetry loop restated as a moral expectation. Never scored (Part D.2).
- **"The abuser is simply evil / a monster."** REFUSED for MODELLING purposes —
  the line models the DYNAMICS and MECHANISM (a single held-intent emitting two
  stated-faces into a reputation graph), not a moral label. (This is a modelling
  stance for legibility, NOT a moral exculpation of real abusers; the clinical
  register describes the two-faced mechanism precisely so responsibility can be
  located ACCURATELY — the corruption of the network's evidence is itself an
  authored harm — not dissolved.) No "evil" attribute is a node; the abuser is a
  `stated≠held`-with-two-audiences source in the dynamics.
- **"Consensus = truth" (the network all agreed, so it's real).** REFUSED — N3
  shows a network-wide consensus can be a CONTAGION from a single corrupted root
  amplified by pluralistic ignorance (nodes updating on each other, not on
  independent evidence). N independent-LOOKING beliefs traced to one source are not
  N pieces of evidence.
- **Anthropomorphic over-reading.** REFUSED — these are SYNTHETIC reputation
  belief-attrs + scalar-arousal / window dynamics over a graph, grounded in the
  creator's in-vivo experience, NOT claims that the network nodes "really believe"

  Mechanism-proven ≠ clinically-valid ≠ sentient; all three kept separate, per the
  line's standing rule.
