# Theory revisions — standing input to the papers and the book

**Source:** `~/paper_revision_notes_time_seldon_momentum_photon_eye.md`
(25 items, plus a paper-by-paper priority note). These are revisions to the
*theory*, not to this book, and they arrived after Parts I–VI were drafted.
This document records which are applied, which are pending, and — for two of
them — what they corrected in an already-written chapter.

The notes' own priority: **Papers 1–3 carry the largest conceptual revisions.**
Paper 1 needs the non-primitivity of time, the epistemic boundary, and the
separation of discrete sufficiency from fundamental discreteness. Paper 2 needs
the unknown-count / present / determinability structure and blind spots. Paper 3
should recast momentum as present structure. The photon-graph eye material
belongs in its own later paper; these notes are its theoretical foundation.

### The correction §5 forced

Chapters 4 and 7 both used the phrase **"unknown is absence"**, taken from the
substrate's own working vocabulary. Under the three-way distinction that phrase
conflates two different states:

- **NULL** — the dimension is not instantiated at all;
- **unknown** — the dimension exists, the value is unresolved;
- **resolved** — a realised value.

An unvisited cell is *unknown*, not NULL: the spatial dimensions are
instantiated and what is missing is the value. The two behave differently under
projection, and the difference is operational rather than terminological — an
unresolved value is something an agent can go and resolve; a NULL dimension is
not something it can look for.

Both chapters now say **"unknown is a state in its own right"** and chapter 7
states the three-way distinction where it is first used. The original phrasing
was not wrong about what it was arguing against (a default zero); it was
imprecise about what it was arguing *for*.

## Pending — for chapters not yet drafted

**Chapter 17 (Seldon manifolds)** takes the largest share:

- **§14, §15** — the temporal ratchet exists independently of observers.
  **Seldon manifolds are optional observer/agent machinery** for representing
  reachable unresolved continuations. *Nature does not have to run a Seldon
  manifold in order to evolve.* Narrative, narrativium, unknown counts and
  inferred futures are first-class only in frames that represent the relations
  defining them — they are not universal substrate requirements. This should
  shape the chapter's opening, because the natural reading of Part VII is the
  over-strong one.
- **§4, §6, §7** — the present defined structurally as the collapse boundary at
  which the instantiated unknowns relevant to that collapse have resolved
  (`U_F = 0`, with NULL dimensions excluded from the count); future distance
  recast as unresolved structure rather than geometric resolution, `U_F(h) → 0`
  as the horizon approaches the present; and an observer-relative determinability
  `D_F(h) → 1`. **Avoid saying the universe becomes more deterministic** — what
  changes is how much determining structure is resolved in-frame.
- **§16** — blind spots, as above, at manifold grain.
- **§12, §13** — replace the left-to-right temporal metaphor. Causation has two
  components: the temporal ratchet through successive collapses, and structural
  depth through containing frames. Roughly a 45° change of causal vector, not a
  90° replacement of time by structure. Depth comes from **superframes**, not
  from historical frames, and may extend without a privileged terminal frame.

**Chapter 18 (narrative momentum and narrativium)** — §18/§19 as applied in
ch. 14, but as the chapter's spine: prior deformation leaves present structure
that constrains reachable continuation, which is the same form in both cases.
Chapter 13's desire paths are already written this way and can be cited as the
measured instance.

**Chapter 19 (the open problem)** — §17: an agent can genuinely operate over
multiple reachable, unresolved futures *in its frame*, while those futures remain
structurally determinable from sufficiently deep present-frame or superframe
structure. This preserves both sides of the dichotomy rather than eliminating
either, and it belongs with the composition that is named-and-unbuilt.

**Figures.** PAPER-02-1's caption should adopt §14/§15's framing — the tube is
observer machinery, not a thing nature runs — which strengthens the existing
THEORY-FIGURE marking rather than replacing it.

## One note for the book's own register

Several of these revisions move claims in the direction of *claiming less*: the
substrate is not asserted to be discrete, reality is not asserted to be a graph,
the universe is not said to become more deterministic, and higher machinery is
not a universal requirement. That direction is worth preserving in the prose
wherever a chapter has to choose, because the over-strong reading is always the
available one and is what a hostile reader will assume was meant.
