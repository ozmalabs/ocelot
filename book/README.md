## The book, in one paragraph

One book, nineteen chapters plus introduction, prologue, one front-matter
chapter and an epilogue. Its spine is **residual** — what does not fit, and
what a system does with it. Its antagonist is **a number standing where a
shape belonged**. It is **narrative-led**: the engineering leads, the axioms
arrive in Part V, and the ending is a named cut rather than a conclusion,
because the substrate has no natural ending and neither did the person running
it.

## Standing decisions (do not relitigate without cause)

1. **One book**, not two volumes. Length is handled by merging, never by
   dropping a thread.
2. **Narrative-led part order.** Theory late. Claim still stated up front in
   every chapter — narrative-led is not claim-withheld.
3. **The eye is the flagship**, and it is *an eye*, not "vision" — the scoping
   is what lets the diagnosed cortex null sit inside the chapter's scope.
4. **Retractions, intervals, nulls and ch. 12's ethics protocol are uncuttable.**
5. **The author's standing is never argued in the text.**
