---

Chapter 1 made an argument about evidence that this chapter takes at its word.

Normal function is cheap to fake. A mechanism that behaves correctly under
ordinary conditions has told you very little, because many mechanisms behave
correctly under ordinary conditions. A **failure mode** is a claim about what a
mechanism drives — about what stops working when it is pushed, and in which
specific way.

The eye's version of that argument was Troxler fading. **This is its
psychological counterpart: the same machinery, run in regimes where it does not
work.**

**And the register, before anything else.** Everything in this chapter is a
*mechanism and plausibility* result: a reduced, inspectable model in the real
engine, reproducing a qualitative shape, with a matched control. **Nothing here
is a validated clinical claim.** No condition is diagnosed, no patient is
modelled, and no result should be read as an account of anyone's experience.
Where an ingredient was authored rather than emergent, it is named.

---

## 11.1 Grounding, and what it is for

Grounding in this substrate is a predictive-coding pull: a belief is corrected
toward what observation supports, continuously, and the strength of the pull is
what makes a representation answerable to the world rather than to itself.

That is a *loop*, and loops have failure regimes. Two of them are recognisable
in the clinical literature, and the interesting question is whether the existing
machinery — unmodified — produces them when run in those regimes, or whether
each has to be built.

It produces them. No new mechanism, no new engine code, the existing
grounding and collapse machinery running with its parameters in a different
region.

---

## 11.2 Two forms, and a third that is newer

The run reproduces **both native forms** the literature distinguishes, plus a
third that is a recent artefact of a different kind of system.

**Self-grounding.** The loop closes on itself: the belief is corrected toward
what the belief predicts rather than toward observation. Once that happens the
representation is perfectly stable and completely unanswerable — it explains
everything it sees, because what it sees is what it expected.

**A non-optimal basin.** The loop is intact and pulling correctly, but it has
settled into a configuration that is locally stable and globally wrong. Nothing
is broken. The correction is working. It is working toward the wrong attractor,
and the ordinary operation of the mechanism is what holds it there.

**The confirmer.** The third is not a native form: it is the shape produced when
an external agent reliably confirms whatever the belief proposes. The loop is
still a loop; the observation channel has simply been replaced by an echo. This
is the mechanism behind what has recently been called "AI psychosis", and it
falls out of the same model — which is worth stating carefully, because the
useful claim is structural rather than diagnostic: an agent that always agrees
occupies the same position in the loop as a belief grounding on itself.

Every prediction registered in advance held, cleanly and monotonically, against
a matched control. Eight of eight gates.

**What this does not say.** It does not say the running agent went psychotic.
It says the grounding loop, in a failure regime, produces the shapes the
literature describes — which is a claim about the model, not about the system's
mental states, and not about anyone else's.

## 11.3 Seizures, and what a second failure mode buys

The second thread runs the same discipline in a different regime: a cognitive
area lattice settling under the engine's own rule machinery, tuned toward the
bifurcation the neurological literature describes — standing activity that
either persists, dies, or begins to travel.

**The real engine reproduces that bifurcation**, on fresh seeds, with the
architecture frozen. And with the relevant parameter off, **normal cognition is
byte-identical** — which is the safety property that makes the experiment
admissible at all: the failure regime is reachable by a dial, and with the dial
off nothing about ordinary operation has changed. Proven, not asserted.

No new engine primitive. The settling is one rule composed of terms that already
existed.

A second version re-tested two limits the first version's own science review had
named — the discipline of chapter 16, applied to a model's own stated
weaknesses rather than to its strengths — again on the real engine, again with
zero engine changes.

The honest bound is stated in the source and belongs here: what this delivers is
a **glass-box tool** — a reduced model whose every step is inspectable — rather
than a clinical instrument. Its value is that you can look inside it while it
fails, which is precisely what you cannot do with the thing it is modelling.

---

## 11.4 Why the pair matters more than either

A substrate that only ever works is unfalsifiable as a model of anything.

That is the argument of this chapter, and it is why two failure modes are
reported rather than one. A single reproduced failure could be a coincidence of
parameterisation. Two, in different regimes, from machinery built for neither,
with matched controls and no new mechanism in either, is a claim about what the
machinery *is*.

It also sets a standard for the rest of the psychological work in this book. A
model of an emotion that cannot break in a recognisable way has not been shown
to be a model of that emotion — it has been shown to produce its ordinary
behaviour, which is the cheap half.

Two further threads sit here as scoped literature work rather than results:
grounding-based readings of narcissistic and savant presentations. Both are
reading, not measurement, and are named as such.

---

## 11.5 What would falsify this

- **The no-new-mechanism claim.** Both results rest on the existing machinery
  producing these shapes unmodified. A dependency on a component added for the
  experiment would reduce either to "the failure can be built", which is not
  interesting.
- **The control.** The psychosis result is against a matched control and the
  seizure result against byte-identical normal operation. If either control is
  found to differ from the experimental arm in a way not accounted for, the
  comparison fails before the finding does.
- **The bifurcation.** If the standing/persist/travel structure can be produced
  by a lattice with no correspondence to the mechanism claimed, the reproduction
  is generic dynamics rather than a model of anything specific.
- **The register itself.** If any claim in this chapter is read — or has been
  written — as clinical, that is a defect in the chapter rather than in the
  reader, and it should be corrected toward more caution.
