# 12 · Coercive Control

*Draft. Sources: `docs/research/bigville/ABUSIVE_RELATIONSHIPS_LINE_SUMMARY.md`
and the fifteen rung documents it cites, plus the five scoping documents locked
before them. Register per `BOOK_VOICE.md` § register guards — clinical,
anti-sensational, mechanism rather than moral labels. Every number is copied
from an already-gated rung's own results document.*

---

Before any of the work in this chapter was designed, a different line had
already measured the thing all of it turns out to elaborate.

That earlier work was about development and co-regulation: how one person's
nervous system helps settle another's, which is ordinary, well-supported
affective science. It measured a specific configuration — what happens when the
person doing the regulating **is also the source of the dysregulation**.

The answer was that the coupling runs the wrong way, and does not return.

Not "returns slowly". The regulation loop, which works when a settled other
helps an unsettled one, inverts when the other is the cause: the system converges
on a state it does not leave. That result was measured for its own reasons, in a
line not built for this one, and it is the mechanism most of what follows
elaborates.

**This chapter reports fifteen pre-registered experiments on the dynamics of
coercive control** — in a dyad, then in a network, then under the question of
whether any of it requires intent. It is a mechanism study. It makes no claims
about individuals, assigns no moral labels, and does not model anyone's
experience. What it models is a coupling, and what it reports is how that
coupling behaves under conditions that are stated in advance.

The purpose is scientific and clinical understanding. It is grounded in the
author's own experience of trauma and the care system, which is stated here once
because it is the reason the work was undertaken, and is not the evidence for
anything in it.

## 12.1 The protocol, in full

This section is not a preamble to the results. It is a result, and it belongs
in front of them.

Every one of the fifteen preregistrations carries the same four-point protocol,
verbatim, locked before any rung ran:

1. **Induce → measure → RESTORE.** The induced state is reversed, byte-identically,
   and the restoration is checkpoint-verified as a gate. A rung that could not
   restore would not pass.
2. **No reward, no punishment, anywhere.** Source-audited on every rung for
   shaping tokens — reward signals, punishment, reinforcement, gradients. The
   audit is a gate, not a review.
3. **Induce the least the gate needs.** Every run ends settled and
   non-oriented.
4. **Same mechanism, opposite thermodynamics.** The real dynamic accumulates
   irreversible harm in a person. The model induces and reverses a transient
   graph state, harming no one. The mechanism is the same shape; the direction
   of accumulation is not.

Each preregistration also carries what the documents call the **honest fork** —
the open question of whether the reversibility stance is sufficient, or whether
a strict-experientialist stance would say that a system in which such states can
be induced at all requires more than reversibility. That fork is stated in each
document rather than resolved by assertion, and it is not settled here either.

The ethics gates and the scientific gates were passed **independently**. A rung
whose ethics gates failed would not have run, and none did.

Two things about this deserve stating plainly. First, the protocol is stronger
than what most work of this kind carries, and it was locked before the results
that would have made it inconvenient existed. Second, a protocol is not a
justification: it does not make the subject matter safe to be careless with, and
the register of the source documents — clinical, unsensational, no vivid
scenarios — is part of the same discipline rather than a stylistic preference.

## 12.2 The bar that shaped every rung

One methodological constraint runs through the whole line, and it is what makes
the results mean anything:

> **No rung may invent a bespoke "abuse mechanism".**

Every scoping document carries this as a falsification bar. If a rung needed a
new mechanism built specifically to produce the phenomenon it was studying, that
rung would have demonstrated only that the mechanism could be built.

The line closes with **zero new engine primitives and zero new decision logic
across all fifteen rungs**. Everything is composition of atoms that already
existed for other reasons: arousal, appraisal, windows of tolerance, bonds,
co-regulation, reputation, belief update.

This is the strongest single fact in the chapter. The dynamics below are not
modelled; they are what these ordinary components *do* in particular
configurations.

## 12.3 The dyad

Five rungs, each with a control that could have failed. [CC-1] sets out all
fifteen at once.

**R1 — the bond strengthens under intermittency.** Three arms at identical dose:
intermittent harm-and-affection, consistent affection, consistent harm. Bond
strength 265.4 [255.9, 275.0] intermittent, against 237.7 [237.4, 238.1]
consistent-affection and 205.9 [205.0, 206.9] consistent-harm.

The mechanism is the **swing**, not the mean: what strengthens the bond is the
harm-to-relief transition, which is consistent with the partial-reinforcement
extinction effect and was not built in. And the preregistration's own
sensitivity analysis showed that both consistency controls *could* win under a
weaker parameterisation — so the ordering was a real test rather than a
foregone one.

**R2 — the cycle sustains itself.** A constant per-tick tension increment on the
abuser's own relaxation oscillator produces a limit cycle with arousal standard
deviation 3.876 [3.873, 3.879]. The control, with constant regulation, sits at
an *exact* fixed point: 0.000 [0, 0]. No external periodic driver was supplied.
The cycle is not imposed; it is what a relaxation oscillator does.

**R3 — a single anchor narrows the window.** With the outside regulator removed
so that only one relationship remains, the window of tolerance collapses to its
floor, 5.0 [5, 5], against 10.0 [10, 10] when an outside regulator is present.
Anchor distance 48.87 [48.85, 48.89] against 54.85 [54.83, 54.87]. Both
sub-gates pass.

**R4 — control fails to get learned.** With escape non-contingent, the
contingency prior sits at 0.256 [0.253, 0.259] and the attempt rate at 0.031
[0.031, 0.032]; with escape contingent, prior ≈ 1.0 and rate 1.000 [1, 1].

The rung explicitly follows the modern reframing of this literature: it is not
that helplessness is learned, but that **control fails to get learned**. And it
produced an unscripted finding — the non-contingent arm attempts escape exactly
twice, then freezes into a permanent absorbing state. Nobody wrote that. It
falls out of composing a choice over appraisals with a belief update that only
receives evidence when an attempt is made: two failures, and the belief no
longer supports a third attempt, so no further evidence ever arrives.

**R5 — recovery, and the line's first partial result.** With outside support
restored and all four dyad mechanisms composed: bond weakening by more than 50×,
window widening, escape attempts resuming — three interval-separated passes.
The fourth metric, anchor distance, is a **null**: 49.96 against 50.06, intervals
overlapping.

It is reported as a partial rather than smoothed into a clean headline, and the
null is mechanistically explained rather than excused: the outside pull is
measurably working, but it is small, and the abuser's own large arousal
excursions swamp the raw distance arithmetic. That explanation was
**pre-registered as the likely outcome before the confirmatory run**, not
constructed afterwards.

## 12.4 Why leaving is not the end of the question

Two rungs address what the first five do not. R4 modelled whether escape
*works*. Neither it nor anything else modelled whether escape is *worth it* when
it does work, or what happens after a genuine recovery when a residual bond is
still standing.

**R6 — a correct decision that lands on staying.** One new subtracted term is
added to R4's own unmodified appraisal: the cost borne by dependents. At zero
dependent cost the rung reproduces R4's landed numbers exactly — a
harness-neutrality check that says the addition changed nothing else. At high
dependent cost, attempt rate falls to **0.000 [0, 0]** against 1.000 [1, 1].

The important number is a different one. The contingency prior **stays at its
uninformative 0.4998** throughout. It is never even disconfirmed.

Which is to say: this decision is not a belief that escape will fail. The
appraisal is entirely correct about controllability, and it lands on staying
anyway, because the cost falls on someone else. That is a different mechanism
from learned non-contingency, and conflating the two — treating staying as
evidence of a distorted belief — gets the person wrong.

**R7 — recovery is not protected.** A two-by-two factorial: post-separation
contact during a honeymoon phase, and resource collapse after leaving. Return
propensity with both drivers 0.7278; honeymoon only 0.3921 [0.3882, 0.3959];
collapse only 0.3145 [0.3123, 0.3167]; neither, exactly 0.0000 [0, 0]. All five
sub-gates pass.

Both drivers together exceed either alone. A trajectory that genuinely recovered
is not thereby protected from a later pull back, and the two pulls are
different: one is affective, one is material.

## 12.5 The network

The dyad line describes two people. The rungs that follow put them in a
population, and this is where the dynamics stop being about a relationship.

**N1 — the credibility asymmetry.** One person presenting two faces, with a
network updating its beliefs from what it observes. The belief gap widens from
204 to 2371 across the run. An insider with access to the private face sits at
−1144.20 — the reverse sign. A control in which the victim is not dysregulated
holds at exactly 0.00.

One design decision carries the literature's actual nuance. The credibility
discount is keyed on a *structural* fact — that the presentation is incoherent —
rather than on raw distress. Distress alone is not discounted; a presentation
read as unstable is. That distinction is the finding, and getting it wrong would
have made the rung a caricature.

**N2 — isolation is super-additive.** A two-by-two of active severing and
ordinary network drift, measured as intact support edges: both drivers 0.00 [0,
0]; active severing alone 4.00 [4, 4]; drift alone 1.90 [1.86, 1.94]; neither
8.00 [8, 8]. The combination is worse than the sum, and it was measured as
super-additivity rather than assumed — a design hazard that would have made the
result true by construction was found and fixed before the design was locked.

**N3 — corruption without observation, and a truth anchor that reverses it.**
In an eight-node topology, nodes with **zero direct observation** of the abuser
reach a corruption of 2231.38 [2230.35, 2232.41]. Belief travels through the
network without any of it coming from the source.

Then the arm that matters: with a truth anchor present, the local belief gap
does not merely slow — it **reverses sign**, to −953.57 [−954.55, −952.58].

And a mechanism that arrived free: pluralistic ignorance — everyone privately
doubting and publicly conforming — falls out of plain neighbour-belief averaging
with no explicit term. It was measured rather than assumed, and it answered an
open question the scoping document had left standing.

**N4 — one believing agent.** The network's recovery rung, and the most
integrative in the line. One mechanical world event converts a single already-severed
support node into an insider *and* an outside co-regulator, and restores its
edge. It required **zero new rules**.

Bond 9.58 against 511.25. Window 12.47 against the floor of 5.00. The believer's
belief gap −1312.08 against 1539.89 — a full sign reversal. Intact edges 4.00
against 1.00. Four of four, cleaner than the dyad's own recovery rung.

The unscripted finding is the one worth carrying out of this chapter: restoring
that single edge measurably slowed the drift of the **other, unconnected nodes**
as well. The mechanism is not mysterious once seen — the victim's own reduced
dysregulation feeds every node's belief update, not only the believer's. One
person believing her changes what the rest of the network sees.

## 12.6 Does the loop require intent?

The network line's abuser presents two faces. The obvious question is whether
that presentation has to be *chosen* — whether the credibility asymmetry needs
a deliberate strategist, or arises from someone choosing nothing at all.

Scoping the question properly produced a correction to the line's own prior
work, and it is the clearest instance in this book of a method catching
something a conclusion would have hidden.

**The rule everyone had been reading as deliberate was an unconditional
broadcast.** N1's charm presentation fires every tick regardless of held state:
no choice over alternatives, no evaluation against a benefit. Nothing in it is
goal-directed.

So N1 had never been a positive example of intent. Which means the originally
scoped pair of rungs — designed to show the pattern arising *without* intent —
could not have tested that at all. They would have compared one non-intentional
mechanism against another and concluded something about structure.

A rung was added to close the gap **before the question could honestly be
asked**: a genuine, inspectable, scored example of concealment chosen because it
serves a goal.

- **U0 (the positive control).** Concealment selected over honesty by an
  appraisal in which the cost of honesty scales with true harm. Correlation
  between frustration and concealment 0.76 [0.762, 0.763]; the goal-blind
  control is not merely uncorrelated but **100% degenerate**.
- **U1 (context-dependent affect).** No divergence node at all — the network
  simply samples the true state only away from the dyad. Context-gated belief
  gap 378.1 [372.17, 384.03] against an always-observed control at 149.1
  [143.27, 154.93].
- **U2 (a sincere self-account).** One held value, no hidden "true" value, and a
  declared minimisation constant. Minimised 3054.47 [3051.99, 3056.95] against a
  zero-discount control at −659.32 [−661.02, −657.62] — sign-flipped.

**The loop does not require intent.** A person who is not choosing anything, who
holds no second private value and is sincere in their own self-account, produces
the same network asymmetry — provided the world sees them in one context and
someone else in another.

U0 reused the same appraisal template as the developmental rung of chapter 10,
verbatim, with no new engine code. The mechanism that models a child waiting for
a marshmallow, unmodified, is the mechanism that models concealment chosen
because it pays.

## 12.7 What reaches people, versus what happened

The last rung takes up a statement survivors report making — that physical abuse
would in some ways be easier than coercive control alone, because it is harder
to get anyone to believe the latter — and asks whether it is quantifiable rather
than only assertable.

Reading the existing machinery produced two findings before any design was
possible. The network's ordinary nodes had **no channel at all** for a discrete,
victim-independent trace — only the abuser's continuous presentation and the
victim's own continuous state. And the two conduct rungs do not actually differ
in whether an incident occurs; both use the same driver. So the honest axis is
not *what kind of harm* but, entirely network-side, **whether an incident's
trace reaches anyone**.

The design is the leanest in the line. The dyad is reused byte-identically
across both arms — same abuser, same seed stream, same victim — so harm is
matched by construction rather than by tuning a dose. The single variable is
whether one observable is delivered, keyed on the abuser's own existing incident
count, discounting *his* credibility. It never boosts the victim's claim, a
deliberate separation of "there is now evidence of what he did" from "she is more
believable".

Belief gap with the incident visible 2248.17 [2246.72, 2249.62]; invisible
2369.46 [2368.09, 2370.83] — which matches N1's own standing number of 2370.81,
a discipline check on the whole composition.

Interval-separated, and here is the limit, reported rather than buried: **the gap
narrows and does not flip sign.** At the disclosed weight actually used, the
abuser remains net-favoured even when the incident channel is visible. A pilot
sweep across two orders of magnitude found the separation held at every value
tested.

The grounding is bounded too. The UK's own 2021 review of the coercive-control
offence records evidentiary insufficiency in 35% of such cases against 24% for
domestic abuse generally, which is the empirical shape the result matches. But
there is a live academic dispute over whether the incident-versus-pattern
distinction is even the right lens. And in the course of the reading, a domestic
violence advocacy source was found naming the survivor phrase itself as a myth
to avoid perpetuating. That was found unprompted, and it is recorded here for
the same reason it was recorded there: the result is about what a network's
evidence channel does, not an endorsement of the framing that prompted the
question.

## 12.8 What this does not establish

The chapter's own boundaries, stated as flatly as possible.

**These are not clinical findings.** They are mechanism-and-plausibility results
of the kind chapter 11 also reports: a reduced, inspectable model in a real
engine, reproducing a qualitative shape. Nothing here has been validated against
clinical data, and no rung claims it has.

**No individual is modelled.** The components are appraisals, arousal, windows,
bonds and belief updates. A person is not any of those, and the correspondence
between a rung's arms and a real situation is a matter of shape, not identity.

**The population is small and synthetic.** Eight nodes is a topology, not a
society.

**Where a phrase came from a person, its provenance is stated.** Two rungs exist
because the question was asked directly, and both say so.

What the chapter does establish is narrower and, taken carefully, more useful:
that this family of dynamics — bond under intermittency, cycle, window collapse,
non-learning of control, credibility asymmetry, isolation, contagion without
observation, and the reversibility of each — is what a set of ordinary,
independently-motivated components does when connected in particular ways. No
part of it needed a mechanism built for the purpose.

## 12.9 What would falsify this

- **The single finding underneath.** If a regulating other placed on the source
  of dysregulation can be shown to couple correctly and return under this
  machinery, the substrate result all fifteen rungs elaborate is wrong, and they
  go with it.
- **Reuse.** If any rung is found to depend on a component that was in fact
  added for it, that rung's claim reduces to "the mechanism can be built".
- **The intent result.** If the non-intentional arms can be shown to smuggle in
  a functional equivalent of intent — a hidden divergence the design intended to
  exclude — §12.6's conclusion fails.
- **The recovery results.** R5's null and N4's four-of-four are the line's most
  optimistic findings. If recovery under this machinery turns out to depend on a
  parameterisation not disclosed in the preregistrations, both weaken.
- **Reversibility.** The ethics protocol rests on byte-identical restoration
  being a complete account of what was induced. The honest fork in every
  preregistration is the standing statement that this is a position rather than
  a proof.

*Ends. The finding to carry forward is N4's unscripted one: one node changing
what it believed measurably changed what the nodes it was not connected to
believed. In a chapter about a mechanism that isolates, the recovery mechanism
turned out to propagate too.*
