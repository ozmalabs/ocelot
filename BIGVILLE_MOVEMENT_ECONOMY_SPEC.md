# BIGVILLE — MOVEMENT + ECONOMY as graph-native ACTIONS — SPEC

**Status: spec / scoping (nothing built). 2026-07-29.** The next rung(s) after
247000 (law-enforcement) + 248000 (30-cast + scale). Turns the proven *social/
political* drama on a **static** stage into a **living town** where agents MOVE
with autonomous agendas and act in an ECONOMY — all graph-native, reusing existing
faculties, staying proximity-local (scale-safe).

---

## 0. The frame — why this is more than "more verbs"

Today bigville people scheme, infer, accuse, back a side, journal, report, witness,
enforce, file motions, and vote — but their **positions are seeded once and never
change**, and the Stardew-style affordances (buildings, shops, plots) are inert map
tiles. Three payoffs make movement+economy the RIGHT next rung, not decoration:

1. **Dynamic epistemics.** Perception is already occlusion-gated. Once people MOVE,
   *who witnesses what becomes contingent on where they go* — the constable's beat,
   the reporter's route, a villager who happens past John's office window. The
   credibility/enforcement drama stops being a function of seed placement and
   becomes **emergent and contingent**. This is the deep coupling: movement feeds
   the already-proven witness→credibility→enforcement chain.
2. **Autonomous agendas.** Drives (hunger, etc.) give every villager a life
   *independent of the John/Mary plot* — the baker's day is baking and selling. The
   "no NPCs, everyone a full mind" thesis becomes **materially** real, not just
   stated.
3. **Material stakes.** Employer/worker, seller/regular-customer, debtor — a second
   relationship layer over the social one; feuds and loyalties now have money in
   them.

**The capstone — this is what makes it NOT a "bullshit social simulator."** A
narrated social sim (Smallville and its descendants) has an LLM *describe* stress; the
consequence is theatre with no material substrate. Bigville's consequences are
**grounded, propagating, and felt** — the project's grounding thesis applied to a
society:
- **Grounded** — coins, goods, energy, positions, sight-lines are a real substrate a
  decision *touches* (getting drunk really costs the coins).
- **Propagating** — a failure travels the SUPPLY/DEPENDENCY graph, UPSTREAM as well as
  down: the drunk fishmonger can't buy fish → the fisherman goes UNPAID → the
  fisherman's own livelihood is hit → his suppliers/family feel it. That graph is
  sparse, so propagation is the substrate's native O(edges) flood — the SAME sparse
  propagation that keeps scale linear. The cascade FLOWS along edges; it is not
  authored.
- **Felt** — being stiffed changes the fisherman's actual AFFECTIVE state (frustration,
  worry for his own household), which FEEDS BACK into behaviour: refuse further credit,
  demand cash upfront, badmouth the fishmonger — landing in the credibility/social
  layer. **Economy → affect → society is a closed loop**, so emotional weight is
  INCURRED, not described.

**THE DEEPER CAPSTONE — why it MATTERS: real stakes, AGENCY *and* STRUCTURE (creator,
2026-07-29).** A simulation matters only with REAL stakes — outcomes that genuinely turn on
what the participants do, AND on forces beyond any of them. Bigville must have BOTH, and
grounding is what makes each real (not narrated):
- **Controllable — agency is real.** An agent's choices measurably change ITS OWN fate.
  *Test:* replay with the agent choosing differently; if its fate changes, the outcome was
  (partly) in its control.
- **Uncontrollable — structure is real.** Emergent MARKET/SYSTEM forces no individual
  commands (the bank-hoards-all fixpoint, the liquidity crunch, the carrying-capacity
  ceiling, a rate hike that dooms mortgagors who each did nothing wrong) — the participants
  can only WATCH. *Test, and it DISTINGUISHES structure from agency:* sweep the affected
  agent's every personal policy — if they ALL lead to the same fate, the force is
  STRUCTURAL; then apply a STRUCTURAL intervention (a tax, a rate cut, a jubilee, breaking
  the bank) and show the fate changes. That PROVES it was out of any individual's control,
  changeable only systemically.
- **Why this is the point:** the stakes are GROUNDED (real coin/home/food/life →
  homelessness, starvation, leaving, death) and EMERGENT (unscripted), so bigville is a
  **glass-box MODEL OF THE AGENCY/STRUCTURE BOUNDARY** — for any fate you can measure
  whether it was chosen or structural, and run the structural counterfactual a real society
  never permits. **This is the gate that makes it MATTER, and it is FALSIFIABLE.** R9's
  redistribution-ON/OFF gate is it, instantiated: no individual mortgagor escapes the
  fixpoint by any personal choice; only the structural tax/redistribution flips everyone's
  fate. Ethics: crises are INDUCED then RESTORED (the relational-ethics protocol — no
  reward/punishment); the stakes are reversible-but-real, the value is the understanding.

**COLLECTIVE ACTION is the HINGE from agency to structure — and it EMERGES, it is not built
(creator's correction, 2026-07-29).** The capstone above says a structural force changes ONLY
by a structural lever, not by any individual — but WHO pulls the lever? A COMBINATION.
Collective action (a rent strike, a run on the bank, a vote to remove John, a mob, a guild, a
movement) is how the powerless COMBINE to move what no individual can — the emergent BRIDGE by
which agency REACHES structure, the answer to "who changes the unchangeable." **It is NOT a
mechanism to build; it EMERGES** from ingredients already present: aligned interests CREATED by
the structural forces themselves (every mortgagor hurt identically by a rate hike; everyone
below the fixpoint), ToM (recognize the shared plight), communication (coordinate), the
backing/coalition machinery (the John/Mary factions are ALREADY proto-collective-action), and
norms/reputation (to overcome FREE-RIDING, the classic obstacle — social pressure on those who
won't join). **So it is MEASURE-FIRST:** induce a shared structural grievance (a rate hike, a
famine, an enclosure) and MEASURE whether a coalition FORMS and ACTS — genuinely emergent (a
leader bears the first cost, reputation punishes defectors), NOT scripted. If it emerges, agency
reached structure; if it does NOT, that is a FINDING (a missing ingredient). A Coalition is READ
OFF as an emergent cluster in the backing/interest graph (minted when it forms + acts), never
scripted.

**THE 3.5% RULE — the flagship CONVERGENT-VALIDITY target (creator, 2026-07-29).** The concrete,
falsifiable, QUANTITATIVE thing the emergent hinge can be measured against: Chenoweth & Stephan's
empirical regularity that no nonviolent campaign in their dataset (~1900–2006) FAILED once it
mobilized ~3.5% SUSTAINED ACTIVE participation. It is observational (one history per country, no
replay, confounds: what-counts-as-participation, selection, reverse causality). Bigville can do
what that literature cannot: **give the threshold a MECHANISM (percolation/tipping on the
backing-graph + reputation + free-riding — at some participation fraction the coalition is
visibly winning, the cost of joining drops below the cost of standing aside, and free-riders
cascade in), SWEEP it** (threshold as a FUNCTION of repression [247], inequality, cohesion, and
the information channel [free press R21 lowers it? captured raises it?] — a curve, not a magic
number), and **test the nonviolent-advantage finding** (Chenoweth's other headline: nonviolent
succeeds ~2× violent — bigville has BOTH channels [R15 violence + nonviolent collective action]
and can reproduce the gap endogenously or fail to). **TWO VALID MODES — the firing line is PROVENANCE HONESTY, NOT tuning (creator's correction,
2026-07-29).** Tuning is an instrument, not a sin; the two modes answer DIFFERENT questions and
each is valid IF reported as what it is:
- **FORWARD (sweep, no target):** pre-register the mechanism, sweep participation + conditions,
  REPORT whatever threshold falls out (3.5%, 15%, or none). Near-3.5% emerging WITHOUT being tuned
  for = convergent validity (the mechanism independently reproduces a real law); a different value
  = a discrepancy to explain.
- **INVERSE (calibrate to a target):** TUNE the conditions until the threshold lands ≈3.5% and
  report THE CONDITIONS — "these are the conditions REQUIRED to achieve 3.5%." A VALID result (the
  inverse/calibration problem; the condition-set is the PREIMAGE — the manifold move,
  `[[ozma_case_reconstruction]]`), and arguably the MORE policy-relevant question: not "does a
  magic number exist" but "what does it TAKE — how much repression, inequality, press freedom — to
  enable a successful nonviolent movement?"
- **The ONLY fabrication is misreporting PROVENANCE:** claiming untuned emergence when it was
  dialed in, or hiding that the conditions were fit. Both modes are legitimate; lying about WHICH
  one you ran is the offense.
**SMALL-N / LAW-OF-LARGE-NUMBERS CAVEAT:** at HUNDREDS of residents (3.5% of 200 ≈ 7 PEOPLE) the
town lacks the LLN — any threshold estimate is NOISY, INDICATIVE not precise ("succeeded at 3.2%
vs 4.1%" is mostly sampling variance). Report it as a DISTRIBUTION over seed-replays + a CI
(`[[ozma_population_is_distribution_over_seeds]]`), POWER it by averaging many runs or scaling the
town up, and NEVER claim a sharp match to a decimal the population can't support (the "power the
gate before the endgame" lesson, harder here because the town is smaller than the effect is fine).
Gate belongs with R10 (the punctuated-equilibrium capstone) — prior art situated against the
ACTUAL literature (the 3.5% threshold is contested — fuzzy, the necessary-vs-sufficient framing
often overstated), never overclaimed.

**THE DYNAMICAL SIGNATURE OF CORRECTNESS — PUNCTUATED EQUILIBRIUM (creator, 2026-07-29).**
The TELL that the whole living town is working is a specific time-series shape: **STABILITY
→ RAPID CHANGE → STABILITY** (punctuated equilibrium / self-organized criticality). It is
the right tell because of what it RULES OUT — the five ways to be fake or broken: (1) drift
to a DEAD fixpoint (the degenerate bank-hoards-all attractor — grinds to a halt and STAYS
halted); (2) SMOOTH change (linear, not complex); (3) white NOISE (no real equilibria);
(4) FROZEN (a diorama); (5) a clean PERIODIC limit cycle (too regular to be adaptive).
Punctuated equilibrium is the ONLY signature of a genuine complex adaptive system with real
structure — and it is the DYNAMICAL FORM of the agency/structure duality above: the stable
stretches are individual agency + local negative feedback holding a METASTABLE equilibrium;
the punctuations are the STRUCTURAL forces discharging — a regime shift no one chose (a bank
run, a famine, a removal, a cascade). **The substrate connection is EXACT:** the engine is
settle → residual → re-settle at the micro scale; punctuated equilibrium is that at the
MACRO scale, and the seizure work (un-dissipated residual RECRUITING → a cascade,
`[[ozma_seizure_as_undissipated_residual]]`) is literally the micro-mechanism of a
punctuation. And `compute ∝ surprise` gives a SECOND independent measurable: the COST profile
is punctuated too — cheap during stable stretches (low surprise), expensive at transitions.
See R10 — this is the integrative capstone gate, above any single mechanism.

**AFFECT MUST CLOSE THE LOOP — FELT, NOT REGISTERED — AND THE FAILURE MODES MUST BE REACHABLE
(creator, 2026-07-29): "a real simulation has to have failure modes; the sadness or grief has
to be FELT, and have impacts on behaviour."** The anti-diorama-of-feelings criterion, and it is
a HARD requirement, not a flourish. A REGISTERED emotion (a valence/arousal number sitting on a
node) is decoration; an emotion is FELT only when it ENTERS THE DECISION MACHINERY and changes
what the agent does next — grief must be a TERM in the `decision_value` Argmax, the same selector
that picks every other action (the sibling of `[[ozma_affect_gates_learning]]`: affect gates
BEHAVIOUR, not only learning). If a grieving resident makes the same economic choices as a
content one, the grief is NARRATION and the town is a bullshit simulator again. **"Impact on
behaviour" is specific**, routing into already-built machinery: WITHDRAWAL (stops attending /
working → income drop → the R7 spiral), SELF-MEDICATION (drinking — stamina/coin/health cost,
R14), a LOWERED SNAP THRESHOLD (grief makes the 242 frustration-snap cheaper → R15 violence more
reachable), INABILITY TO CO-REGULATE (the grieving parent can't hold the child, 239 → the window
narrows for the next generation through the priors). **The failure mode that makes it REAL is the
NEGATIVE SPIRAL** — grief → worse decisions → worse outcomes → more grief (the R3 collapse-spiral
at the affective level), which must be REACHABLE and ENDOGENOUS (falls out of the coupling, not
scripted) but ESCAPABLE by the SAME mechanism (co-regulation / counselling / a friend / the
window widening, 239). Whether an agent spirals to breakdown or recovers is then a MEASURABLE
function of whether support was present — the mechanistic form of `[[ozma_caregiver_validated_models]]`
("kids do well if they can"): the collapse is not a WILL to fail, it is the narrowed window
making the regulated choice UNREACHABLE (dissolves victim-blaming, `[[ozma_abusive_relationships_line]]`).
**The FALSIFIER is the affect-blind control:** ablate the affect→`decision_value` coupling and
re-run — if behaviour does not change under grief, the emotion is PROVEN decoration; the gate
passes ONLY if grieving agents measurably behave differently, the difference produces the spiral,
and co-regulation bends it back. **The ethics and the escapability are ONE knob:** felt grief is
a real harm-analog, so inducing it binds the induce → measure → RESTORE protocol — and the
RESTORE *is* the co-regulation that widens the window. See R17.

**THE ROOM WHERE IT HAPPENED — occlusion is real for the AGENTS, TRANSPARENT for the OBSERVER
(creator, 2026-07-29).** The deepest research payoff of the whole social layer, and it is an
ASYMMETRY held deliberately: every privacy mechanism (the private council session R20, Mary's
counsel R9, the conspiracy whisper R19, the unwitnessed eviction R16, John's inner malice) is
faithfully OPAQUE to the other townsfolk — their epistemic limits are modelled, not cheated —
while the OBSERVER holds a full readout of not only what was SAID in the closed room but the
STATE of every actor in it: the HELD decision (vs the public address — the 234 gap READ, not
inferred), `w_supergraph`, spite, the exact `decision_value` that tipped the vote, who backed
whom. In real politics the private meeting is doubly opaque (no outsider knows what was said; the
participants' true motives stay hidden), and the historian must RECONSTRUCT the room from a lossy
surviving record, a reconstruction that can never be checked against the thing itself — political
history is structurally unfalsifiable. Bigville KEEPS that occlusion for the agents and REMOVES
it for us. Two consequences: (1) every "what did they really decide / what was his real motive"
— permanently speculative about a real closed room — is a LOOKUP here; (2) it makes bigville the
CONTROLLED VALIDATION GROUND for the case-reconstruction machinery (`[[ozma_case_reconstruction]]`,
"expert analysis is NOT a fact"): hand the reconstructor ONLY the noticeboard minutes + the
townsfolk's partial observations, ask it to reconstruct the room, and SCORE its distribution
against the ground truth we hold. The historian's problem is not merely dissolved for the
observer — it becomes a BENCHMARK.

## 1. Architecture — REUSE, target ZERO new Rust

Every DECISION (where to go, whether to buy, what to produce) is a graph RULE; the
world adapter only applies the resulting position/resource change as mechanical I/O,
ticks, and reads state for the viewer. **No Python `if agent_hungry and near_baker:
buy` — that is a rule.** (Source-audit gate, as every bigville rung.)

Reusable seeds already in the tree (confirmed present):

| need | reuse |
|---|---|
| navigate the grid to a target | `quanta_navigation` / `grid_navigation` / `spatial_belief_core` / `spatial_map` / `nav_intent` |
| the motor act (next hop) | `motor_core` / `motor_intent_core` / `embodied_motor` |
| a need becomes a goal | `drives` / `drives_estimation_control` / `core_emotions` / `goal_construction` / `goal_derivation` / `actor_goals` |
| goods, prices, exchange, wages | `economics` / `net_economy` / `langsim_build` (the 243000 economy substrate) |
| perceive after moving | the scaffold's existing occlusion-gated LOS/earshot graph query |

The bigville grid is *already symbolic* (typed tiles), so navigation is a **graph
query over the persistent map-belief** (unknown = absence), exactly like the
perception gate — NOT a pixel/photon path. That is why this should need no new Rust:
it is composition of existing Terms + seed rules + mechanical adapter I/O. **If a
genuinely new primitive is unavoidable, add a Rust Term and justify it — but the
target and the design constraint is: none needed.**

## 2. The action space (concrete)

**MOVEMENT**
- An agent holds a Goal (from a drive or a social/political goal) carrying a spatial
  target (bakery / John's location / home / the square).
- Navigate: flood the persistent map-belief toward the target via
  quanta/grid-nav → next hop (a motor ControlResponse) → position update (adapter
  I/O). Respects walls/solids and the config-space body (people = 1×1).
- **After each move the occlusion query re-runs at the new position** → perception,
  and therefore knowledge, is now DYNAMIC. This is the load-bearing coupling.

**COMMUNICATION — utterance LOUDNESS and the OBSERVE-vs-HEAR split (R19)**
- Every utterance carries a LOUDNESS scalar that scales the existing EARSHOT radius
  (the scaffold already runs an occlusion-gated LOS/earshot query): an utterance at
  loudness L delivers its CONTENT only to hearers within `radius(L)`, occlusion-adjusted.
  A whisper → tiny radius; a shout → large. No new Rust (loudness parametrizes earshot).
- **Two channels off ONE act.** SIGHT (line-of-sight to the speaker) delivers the
  COMMUNICATION ACT — "X is speaking to Y" is a VISIBLE event — while HEARING (inside
  `radius(L)`) delivers the CONTENT. So a whispered exchange in plain view is
  *observed-as-conversation* but its content NEVER enters a distant witness's graph.
  Knowing-a-conversation-happens and knowing-what-was-said are SEPARATE facts.
- **Loudness = seed axis + choice.** BASE and MAXIMUM loudness are per-character seed
  parameters (population variation — the quiet child, the booming publican;
  `population_is_distribution_over_seeds`); the ACTUAL loudness of a given utterance is a
  `decision_value` choice in `[0, max]` (drop to a whisper, shout to your ceiling) — but
  no one can exceed their own max. The teacher can project over a room the children cannot.
- **Consequences fall out, not scripted:** genuine SECRECY (two people conspire in public
  by whispering — observable, content-private), genuine PRIVACY (Mary's counsel spoken
  quietly in an occluded room does not leak even to someone who sees the meeting — her
  privacy code becomes a PHYSICAL fact of the hearing model, not an honour promise), and
  the CLASSROOM chatter dynamic (R18/R19).

**ECONOMY**
- Goods as graph nodes (grain, bread, ale, coin…); inventory as edges
  (`agent -has-> Good{qty}`).
- Needs = drives: a drive above threshold mints a goal to satisfy it (hunger →
  "get food").
- **PREFERENCE + satisficing (the value trade-off — graph-native, no new mechanism).**
  A need is satisfiable by SEVERAL goods, and each villager holds a heterogeneous
  PREFERENCE weighting over them (apples ≻ bread for one, ale ≻ bread for another) —
  seeded ~Normal per villager, another axis of the population distribution (like
  charm / `w_spite`). The CHOICE of which good to pursue/buy is the substrate's own
  `decision_value` Argmax over reachable options:
  `value(good) = preference[villager][good] · need_urgency − travel_cost(distance to
  a source) − price(good)`, Argmax over the options the villager currently perceives
  or knows. **Satisficing EMERGES** — when the apples are too far their `travel_cost`
  dominates and bread wins *despite* the lower preference; the villager gets the best
  REACHABLE option, not its ideal. It is the SAME Argmax as John's `ss_choose_burn` /
  Mary's ToM decode (`core_emotions` value economy), NOT a Python `if hungry and
  apples_far: bread`. Glass-box: "wanted apples, took bread — the orchard was across
  town." (Distance couples this to MOVEMENT (R1) and its cost to the pathfinding
  budget; price couples it to the ECONOMY (R3).)
- Production requires SKILL: output = f(SKILL[agent, activity], energy, inputs). A
  tradesperson at their workplace with inputs produces (Marjorie Dough bakes; the
  Crane brothers farm; Cask Barleigh brews) — but **yield scales with a per-(agent,
  activity) competence**, seeded heterogeneously (the fisherman high FISHING, the
  farmer high FARMING). An agent CAN attempt any activity, but off-specialty yield is
  much lower (the farmer can fish, badly). **This grounds SPECIALIZATION** —
  comparative advantage (each does what they are best at) is WHY the supply network
  exists at all; it is EARNED from the skill distribution, not seeded. (Skill is
  DYNAMIC — learnable by doing and by teaching; see R6.) A rule: `at workplace ∧ has
  inputs ∧ skill → mint output ∝ skill, consume inputs`.
- Exchange: **LOCAL only** — a buyer and seller *co-located* at a shop exchange
  coin↔good at the SELLER'S price. **Prices are SET BY THE SELLER** — a `decision_value`
  call (maximise profit vs clear stock vs undercut a rival vs gouge under scarcity), NOT
  a global auctioneer and NOT a fixed constant: a FREE-market, seller-priced economy
  where competition, price wars and gouging EMERGE from individual pricing decisions (no
  invisible-hand third party). A rule matching (buyer need ∧ seller stock ∧ co-location ∧
  coin ≥ the seller's price ∧ the buyer accepts). **NEVER a global market clear** (see §4
  scale trap).
- **Supply NETWORK (multi-tier), not just shop↔customer.** A producer's inputs are
  bought from a SUPPLIER: the baker buys grain from a farmer, the fishmonger buys fish
  from the fisherman, the brewer buys grain too. So most tradespeople are ALSO buyers
  up the chain — a sparse dependency graph (`supplies`/`buys_from` edges). A node's
  failure propagates ALONG it (native O(edges) flood; sparse ⇒ stays linear): no coins
  at the fishmonger → the fisherman goes unpaid → the fisherman can't restock line/bait
  → his own output falls → *his* customers feel it. Nothing scripted; it flows the graph.
- **CARRYING CAPACITY — harvest supply vs population demand MUST balance, and the balance
  is MEASURED, not hand-forced.** The town's harvest rate (labourers × skill × yield) must
  meet consumption (heads × need-rate); that equilibrium is the town's carrying capacity.
  **Do NOT secretly re-tune to force sustainability** (the diorama failure mode) —
  parameterise harvest/labour/consumption, MEASURE the net daily flow per good (the
  carrying-capacity margin), tune the DEFAULT seed to a VIABLE regime (a living town, not
  a dying one), and KEEP the knobs so imbalance can be INDUCED as a demonstrated crisis
  (famine, migration, the dream→leave collapse §2.6). Imbalance is a FINDING, not a bug.
  **Partly SELF-balancing:** scarcity → the seller's price rises → labour/production shifts
  toward the scarce good, and labourers can RE-ALLOCATE by learning a needed trade (R6) —
  division of labour correcting itself; whether it self-corrects under a shortage is
  gate-able (induce scarcity → does labour move, or does the town collapse?). Carrying
  capacity must grow with N toward 200 (the harvest base scales with the town).
- **MONEY: conserved in every transfer, but the LIVE dynamic is CIRCULATION vs POOLING —
  and interest ALONE is a degenerate attractor.** Every exchange/salary/rent/tax/interest/
  repayment is a TRANSFER of existing coin, so `Σ coin` is conserved (gate: invariant
  EXCEPT at an explicit MARKED mint/burn — seeded only if we model growth). Conservation is
  the invariant kind the substrate holds well (`[[ozma_discovers_conservation]]`). **The
  creator's fixpoint insight (2026-07-29):** a STRICTLY fixed supply + interest with the
  bank as a pure SINK drives a DEGENERATE attractor — the bank ends up with (almost) all
  the coin and trade SEIZES (debt-deflation / usury: nobody repays principal+interest from
  a pool that never grows → default is STRUCTURAL). **This is a real phenomenon to
  DEMONSTRATE, and the resolution is CIRCULATION, not abandoning conservation:** the bank is
  NOT a sink — it is Scrooge's ENTITY who draws a salary he SPENDS (+ bank running costs),
  so interest-in flows back OUT; and the FISCAL loop (taxes → public purse → mayor/constable
  salaries + public works → spent back) redistributes. So the live, MEASURED quantity is
  money VELOCITY / distribution: does coin CIRCULATE (sustaining) or POOL at the bank/wealthy
  (seizure)? Both are valid findings; the counter-flows (institutional SPENDING + taxes, and
  optionally a marked mint for growth) are the knobs. Free seller-pricing over this is a
  genuine closed economy where inflation/deflation, velocity and liquidity crises are
  OBSERVABLE — and the interest-only, no-redistribution regime is the crisis arm.
- **FISCAL — taxes, the treasury, and public salaries (how the mayor gets paid).** Public
  institutions are funded by TAXES on landowners + workers, at a RATE SET BY THE COUNCIL
  (a political `decision_value` / vote — the fiscal lever, TWIN of the bank's interest rate).
  Revenue flows to a PUBLIC PURSE (`Treasury` node) that pays the MAYOR's salary, the
  CONSTABLE's wage (public enforcement, 247), the SCHOOLMASTER's salary (public education,
  R18) and public works — money RE-ENTERS circulation when those salaries are spent. The
  public payroll is now THREE names — mayor, constable, schoolmaster — so a tax-cutting council
  defunds not just enforcement but EDUCATION (a structural lever with a GENERATIONAL fuse: the
  cohort schooled under a closed classroom comes of age under-capable, R18). Same principal/agent structure as the bank: the mayor draws
  a salary from the purse he administers → a corrupt John can capture the council, inflate
  his salary, or funnel public funds to Scrooge (their "understanding") — a corruption
  Mary's lawful fight is partly ABOUT. **The town has TWO institutions, TWO levers, TWO
  salaries, TWO money-flows:** the BANK (Scrooge / interest / pulls coin IN) and the
  COUNCIL-MAYORALTY (the council / taxes / redistributes coin OUT). Their balance — the
  interest drain vs the fiscal redistribution — IS the town's political economy, measured
  (velocity), not tuned.
- **HOW the council actually governs — TWO-TIER MEETINGS + the NOTICEBOARD (R20).** Governance
  is not an instantaneous lever-set; it runs on SCHEDULED council meetings (an R3-time cadence)
  with TWO tiers: a PUBLIC meeting (townsfolk ATTEND by choice; the mayor's ADDRESS; visible
  discussion — the legitimacy layer) and a PRIVATE meeting (council only, OCCLUDED — R19: the
  town sees it convene but does not hear it) where the DECISION is actually made (the collective
  `decision_value` over the council backing-graph — the tax rate, land policy, a prosecution).
  The gap between the tiers is the agency/structure question MADE MEASURABLE: does the public
  discussion move the private decision (agency reaches the lever), or is the public meeting
  theatre over a decision already made (structure)? — a correlation you compute. It is also the
  234 `stated ≠ held` atom at INSTITUTIONAL scale (public address vs private decision). The
  decision is then WRITTEN DOWN by ONE council member (an owned, potentially-distorting act — the
  minutes are a RENDERING, not ground truth) and POSTED on the town-hall NOTICEBOARD — a
  PERSISTENT, SPATIAL, WRITTEN public channel (distinct from ephemeral acoustic speech). The
  noticeboard is HOW "laws become known" (the 247 promulgation mechanism), and reading it is
  GATED: you must go to the town hall (R1) and be LITERATE (R18) — so a law can be officially
  posted yet genuinely UNKNOWN to an illiterate or distant resident (ignorance-of-the-law becomes
  a real, UNEQUAL state — a structural inequity).
- **THE CHURCH — funded by DONATION, the THIRD institution (voluntary, NO lever).** St.
  Ewe's runs on voluntary giving: the devout TITHE (a committed recurring fraction — a
  `decision_value` of devotion), others DONATE WHEN THEY CAN (discretionary surplus).
  Devotion is a per-villager seed axis (tither / occasional / none). Church income funds

**FINANCE / ASSETS — housing tenure, the bank, interest (the DEEPER economy).**

  the plot (build on another's = trespass); a mortgage is secured on the LAND+building and
  foreclosure transfers the LAND (the ultimate collateral); and it is politically contested — a
  LAND TAX (Georgist, a council lever R9), ENCLOSURE of the commons (the land-grab that CREATES
  a landless class), council allocation. **Deep point:** land concentrates HARDER than money
  (non-produced + finite), so it is the DEEPEST inequality root and the PUREST structure — if
  all land is owned the landless cannot acquire any by ANY personal effort; only a STRUCTURAL
  lever (a land tax, a grant, enclosure-reversal) opens it — the agency/structure boundary (§0)
  at its bedrock.
- **Housing is an ASSET** (`House{value}`) held under one of three TENURES, seeded per
  character → a wealth/class structure from the start: **OWN outright** (no housing
  cost, holds equity), **MORTGAGE** (owes the BANK principal + interest, pays each
  cycle, builds equity slowly), or **RENT** (pays a LANDLORD — another character —
  each cycle, builds NO equity). Owner/landlord accumulate; the renter never does.
- **The BANK is a BUILDING + an ENTITY run by a banker AGENT — NOT a magic third
  party** (same principle as in-game enforcement, 247). A `Bank` node with its OWN
  funds/reserves (deposits + capital), housed in a building on the map (opening hours,
  occlusion — R3/perception apply), RUN BY a full banker agent — **Scrooge McBankFace**.

  the BANKER's `decision_value` call (fair, or predatory if his seed is greedy / if John
  has an understanding with him): monetary policy with a FACE. **The bank's funds are
  SEPARATE from the banker's personal funds** — Scrooge draws a (healthy) SALARY (a
  bank→banker transfer each cycle) and his personal wealth accrues from the salary, NOT
  directly from the reserves. This is the principal/agent structure: the banker's
  incentive (grow his salary + personal wealth) can DIVERGE from the bank's health (a
  greedy Scrooge sets a high salary + predatory rates → drains reserves → more
  foreclosures that enrich the bank he draws from). And because the reserves are
  separate, the BANK can go INSOLVENT (defaults + withdrawals deplete reserves) even
  while Scrooge is personally rich — a first-class systemic event (the macro fishmonger
  cascade: a bank run / credit freeze hits every mortgagor and depositor at once).
- **Interest IS the substrate's CROSS-FRAME multiplicative base.** A mortgage balance
  grows by `(1+r)` per period — a MULTIPLICATIVE step along the time dimension (the
  `scale2` / log-base `DimensionGenerator`, per the frame/continuum invariant in
  `CLAUDE.md`). The rate `r` is INTENSIONAL (a rule you APPLY); the balance is OBSERVED
  STEPPED, at integer payment periods ("you can apply the rate, but any given balance
  is stepped"). Graph-native — no float lives *in* a frame; the continuous rate lives
  in the cross-frame map.
- **Rent / mortgage payment** = periodic transfers on the day/night cycle (reuse R3).
  Miss them past term → the BANK FORECLOSES (seizes the house) or the LANDLORD EVICTS →
  **HOMELESSNESS** (a severe state → drives/affect/social concern). LEVERAGE AMPLIFIES
  the cascade: a goods shock (the fishmonger's bad day, §2 debt) becomes a HOUSING
  catastrophe via the mortgage — the owned-outright character weathers the same shock.
- **Rentier / systemic power.** A landlord can raise rent / evict → power over a tenant

  over every mortgagor at once. Macro-power, above the interpersonal drama.

## 2.5 — TIME, ENERGY & THE DAY/NIGHT CYCLE (the async substrate)

Agents and the world run **ASYNCHRONOUSLY** — not a flourish, the substrate's
DEEPEST commitment (the global clock is a lie; per-sensor epochs; per-area ticks;
compute ∝ surprise — `[[ozma_global_clock_is_the_lie]]`, `[[ozma_time_is_per_sensor_async]]`).
Each agent is its own async process on its own tempo; the world (day/night phase,
store cycles) is ANOTHER async process agents perceive and react to on their own
schedules. There is NO global heartbeat everyone obeys. Reuse the per-sensor-epoch /
cortex-tick-as-settlement machinery (blocks 188000/177000). **This async structure is
WHY 200-real-time is conceivable** — no lockstep; work only where the action is.

- **Energy budget (per-agent).** A stamina resource seeded per character (the farmer
  has high stamina; a frail elder low) + modulated by status (hunger drains it faster). MOVEMENT and ACTIONS deplete
  it. **As it nears threshold the agent SLOWS** — the per-tick movement/action budget
  shrinks (graded degradation, like the arousal/window 241000). A SECOND resource
  economy (energy) alongside goods/coin; each character must MANAGE it day to day.
- **Sleep = an autonomous DECISION + replenishment.** The agent DECIDES when to sleep
  — a `decision_value` trade-off (sleep now vs finish this task vs unsafe spot vs
  how-late-it-is), NOT a global "night → everyone sleeps." Restful sleep (home/safe)
  REPLENISHES the budget.
- **Exhaustion collapse (a failure mode with social consequence).** Push PAST the
  threshold → involuntary collapse-sleep WHEREVER you are, and it is NON-restorative —
  a spiral (exhausted → collapse → poor sleep → still exhausted), the temporal twin of
  the window-breach/snap (242000: crossing a threshold → a qualitatively worse state).
  A collapsed villager is a WITNESSED event → OTHERS' CONCERN fires (reuse the
  co-regulation/affect machinery, 239000) — self-management couples to the social-affect
  layer.
- **Dark reduces sight.** The day/night phase modulates the occlusion-gated sight
  query's RANGE (short at night). **Epistemics become TIME-DEPENDENT** — less is
  witnessable in the dark, so the abuser literally schemes under cover of darkness and
  the credibility/enforcement drama (245/247) becomes time-contingent (who witnesses
  John depends on WHEN as well as WHERE).
- **Store hours = an EXPECTATION, distinct from a LAW.** Shops run on fixed cycles
  (open ~8am…). The town HOLDS a prediction "the stall opens at 8" — a held prediction
  — and a passed-out fishmonger VIOLATES it → SURPRISE / residual (the substrate's
  predict-and-residual loop, `[[ozma_held_prediction_decay]]`, at the SOCIAL level).
  A DIFFERENT normative register from the law rung (247000): a LAW violation → censure
  (ENFORCED); an EXPECTATION violation → surprise/concern (PREDICTED, not enforced) +
  a downstream supply gap. Two registers, both graph-native, neither Python.
- **Emergent cascade (the fishmonger — nothing scripted).** He drinks → exhaustion
  collapse at 5am → doesn't open at 8 → the town's expectation is violated (surprise)
  AND the fish supply is gone → fish-preferrers hit the preference trade-off (§2) and
  satisfice elsewhere → an economic ripple, maybe social friction. A causal chain that
  FALLS OUT of async-energy × preference × economy × expectation — read straight off
  the graph. This is the "living town": contingent, glass-box, unscripted.

## 2.6 — IDENTITY, LIFE-GOALS & DREAMS (the soul layer — why they are not utility-maximizers)

Two mental objects beyond the multi-tick goals of §2 — both graph-native, both what stop
a villager being a bare utility function:

- **LIFE-GOAL (long-horizon, GROUNDED, identity-orienting).** A goal at a COARSE
  time-quantum whose payoff is a lifetime away (become mayor; retire wealthy; restore the
  family name; raise good children) — the ROOT of the goal hierarchy, not an actionable
  step. It ORIENTS sub-goal selection over a long horizon (a "become wealthy" character
  saves over leisure) and is part of IDENTITY (who I'm trying to become — anchored on the
  self-worth prior + the self-model). Grounded: a real, if long, path. Reuse the
  hierarchical goal machinery at the coarse-time root (the extensive-frontier orientation,
  "always one more step toward it").
- **DREAM (ungrounded, high-valence, an ESCAPE not a plan).** "I could leave all this
  behind and join the navy." UNGROUNDED (no path from here in the map-belief — imagined,
  not decoded; beyond the grounding boundary), high-VALENCE, low-ACTIONABILITY. Its
  function is AFFECT REGULATION, not attainment: when the present turns aversive (debt,
  foreclosure, drudgery, being stuck) the dream is INVOKED and gives temporary relief
  (valence up / arousal down) WITHOUT changing the material state — the escape valve
  (cousin of intellectualisation's residual-quarantine: relief without resolution). The
  OPPOSITE of §2 satisficing (which takes the reachable): the UNreachable option held
  anyway for its affective work.
  - **The collapse.** A dream can become a GOAL: aversive valence past a threshold (the
    present unbearable) OR a grounded PATH appearing (a recruiter in town) → the dream
    COLLAPSES into an actionable goal and the villager ACTS (leaves) — the collapse
    machinery. A latent goal carrying latent agency; a quiet character's dream collapsing
    into departure is an unscripted story.
  - **Why it's the soul.** A pure utility-maximizer has no dreams (always takes the best
    reachable). The ungrounded, UNeconomic escape fantasy is the HUMAN part — it colors
    how a character ENDURES, INTENSIFIES with material hardship (couples to R4/R7), and
    gives every villager latent agency. Reuse imagination (counterfactual-self), the
    grounding boundary (marks it ungrounded), affect regulation, and collapse.

## 2.7 — THE OPEN WORLD: travellers, the inn, and SCOPE-GATING (a window into a bigger world, affordably)

Bigville is not a closed box — it is a WINDOW into a larger world. TRAVELLERS come through:
some bring GOODS bigville can't produce (imports) and SELL them; others pass through, stay at
the INN, and buy food + lodging (external demand). Each traveller is a FULL agent with their
own story (no NPCs even for visitors) and MAY REOCCUR (a returning peddler, a seasonal trader —
remembered across visits via persistence).

- **SCOPE-GATING is the load-bearing architecture — it IS the substrate's compute-∝-surprise /
  frontier mechanism.** An agent runs ONLY when IN SCOPE (in/near bigville, the active area);
  out of scope it does NOT run — it is DORMANT (persisted graph state, ~0 compute), REACTIVATED
  from that state (byte-identically) on re-entry. The world beyond scope is the FRONTIER
  (unknown = absence, first-class — you do NOT simulate what you cannot see); travellers
  MATERIALIZE from the frontier on entry and DISSOLVE back into it on exit. This is the SAME
  mechanism that makes night cheap (sleepers ~0 work, R3), the fovea cheap, and the per-area
  dirty-index bounded (compute where the action is). **It makes an UNBOUNDED world AFFORDABLE:**
  compute stays ~linear in the IN-SCOPE population, NOT the total world (the 248000 scale thesis,
  spatial frontier) — a 200-person bigville + an unbounded traveller world costs only the
  in-scope agents.
- **The OPEN ECONOMY eases the closed-money degeneracy.** External trade is a real money
  SOURCE/SINK: a passer-through buying supper at the inn INJECTS coin; a peddler selling imports
  moves coin across the boundary. So the town's money is NOT a strictly closed pool — external
  trade is the MARKED external FLUX (`Σcoin` conserved internally EXCEPT at the logged
  external-trade points — travellers ARE those marked points, per the §2 money model). The
  natural, realistic ease on the interest-fixpoint (a closed pool never grows; an open one
  exchanges with the outside), AND it gives bigville access to goods it CANNOT produce (not
  autarkic).
- **Travellers as full agents:** each has a story/goal (a peddler after profit, a pilgrim, a
  fugitive, a family moving on), a held state, and relationships that build across reoccurring
  visits (the innkeeper who knows the regular trader) — "no NPCs" extended to the transient
  population.
- **GIANTVILLE — the nearby CITY, the frontier made concrete.** The closest big city to
  bigville is GIANTVILLE; it anchors the bigger world (bigville is a TOWN near a CITY). Three
  couplings: (1) **News from the frontier** — travellers bring NEWS of Giantville (prices,
  events, opportunities), a TESTIMONY channel residents comprehend (244/246) and fold into
  belief — but SECOND-HAND (ungrounded, lower-confidence than a visit; distortable by the
  teller → credibility applies). The town's picture of the wider world is a CONFABULATED
  COMPOSITE of a few residents' grounded memories + travellers' rumor, glass-box (you can see
  whose belief is knowledge and whose is hearsay). (2) **Grounded-by-visit vs HEARSAY** — a
  resident who VISITED Giantville GROUNDS it (a held belief from experience); one who only
  heard of it holds a ROMANTICIZED, ungrounded image — the grounding boundary applied to a
  PLACE (meaning bounded by experience; the dream feeds on the ungrounded image). (3) **The
  DREAM of moving there = R8 meets R11** — for a resident stuck in bigville, "move to
  Giantville" is the archetypal ungrounded escape DREAM (§2.6); its COLLAPSE = they LEAVE →
  walk off the edge of scope → go OUT OF SCOPE (dormant, dissolve into the frontier). "Leaving
  for the big city" IS the dream→departure→out-of-scope pipeline; it ties to emigration (people
  leave in a downturn → the town shrinks). They may REOCCUR as a returning traveller with news,
  having now GROUNDED Giantville (the going made the dream a real life OR shattered it against
  reality).

## 2.9 — THE LIFE CYCLE: birth, rearing, health, death, inheritance (the society becomes an ORGANISM)

The missing KEYSTONE: a society REPRODUCES itself across generations. This wires the DORMANT
developmental-emotion arc (attachment/Harlow, the three foundational priors, co-regulation,
affect-gates-learning — the deepest work in the tree, blocks 233-242) into the town, makes
INTERGENERATIONAL inequality possible (inheritance), and gives the concentrating forces their
RESET (death → the punctuated equilibrium the validity gate needs).

- **BIRTH.** A family may have a CHILD — a new agent seeded partly from its parents (NATURE:
  temperament params ~parents, the population-distribution). Births → population up → housing/
  food demand → construction/carrying-capacity.
- **REARING = the developmental arc, FINALLY EMBODIED.** A child is RAISED by caregivers: it
  acquires its three foundational PRIORS (world-safe / others-trust / self-worth) and emotional
  temperament from the QUALITY OF CARE (co-regulation 239, the window-of-tolerance, Harlow
  contact-comfort 233) — NATURE (inherited params) + NURTURE (the care it actually gets). Well
  cared for → secure priors; neglected/abused → mis-set priors (the affective-disorder family);
  the caregiver's OWN regulation shapes the child. The whole developmental line, at last in a
  society.
- **COMING-OF-AGE.** The child matures into an adult — acquires skills (R6 apprenticeship),
  forms its own goals/dreams, enters the economy; the childhood priors are the deep setpoints it
  carries for life.
- **HEALTH / VITALITY = ONE mechanism (aging + illness/injury unified).** A HEALTH variable
  declines CHRONICALLY with age (slow secular decline of stamina/skill/capacity) and ACUTELY
  with illness/injury (a sudden, recoverable drop — the R3 collapse-spiral shape); care/rest/the
  DOCTOR restore it; overwork, hunger, a bad winter deplete it. **DEATH = health at zero** (age /
  illness / injury / starvation / exhaustion). One variable, two timescales — NO separate illness
  mechanism.
- **DEATH → INHERITANCE.** The estate (LAND R13, house, wealth, the trade) passes to HEIRS
  (children/kin), or is DISPUTED, or has no heir (reverts to the commons/council).
  **Inheritance is the DEEPEST inequality dynamic** — the landed stay landed, debt is inherited,
  you inherit your class. And **death is the RESET the concentrating forces need:** a wealthy
  owner dies → the estate DISPERSES (many heirs), CONCENTRATES (primogeniture), or is SQUANDERED
  (a failed heir) — the perturbation that makes PUNCTUATED EQUILIBRIUM (R10) possible; without
  death the fixpoint is IMMORTAL.
- **VIOLENCE — a simple SCALAR (nothing deeper), the inter-agent route to harm and DEATH.** A
  violent act carries an AMOUNT (scalar severity). Minimal consequences: the VICTIM loses HEALTH
  (`−amount` — the acute arm of the R14 health variable, an inter-agent illness/injury); the
  AGGRESSOR loses STAMINA (`−cost` — R1/R3 energy; violence is tiring, so it can't be endless).
  Health → 0 by violence → **DEATH** (R14 — murder/manslaughter, the FIFTH route to death).
  **Enforcement is an EVIDENCE-AND-PROOF pipeline, not omniscience (247).** The constable learns
  of an act by WITNESSING it (line-of-sight) OR by being TOLD — a REPORT is a testimony Message
  from the victim or a bystander (a plain communication act). On a report he INVESTIGATES:
  accumulates EVIDENCE (testimonies, corroboration, and the victim's health-drop itself as
  PHYSICAL evidence) and forms a BELIEF by ABDUCTION — decode the evidence against held
  generators of what-could-have-happened (the case-reconstruction / manifold-of-projections move
  — a RECONSTRUCTION, not a fact, that can concentrate or stay diffuse). "SUFFICIENT PROOF" is
  that belief crossing a confidence threshold (a `decision_value`); only then does he PROSECUTE
  WITH THE COUNCIL (the STATE, 247/R9), which ADJUDICATES as a collective decision over its
  backing-graph → WARNING (small amount / stupid fight) or PUNISHMENT (large amount — magnitude,
  not the trigger, sets the severity, à la the 242 snap threshold). **Occlusion is GRADED, not
  binary:** victim testimony + the physical health-drop can convict an UNWITNESSED act; the
  unseen-abuse case escapes only when the victim won't/can't report AND no other evidence exists
  (genuine INSUFFICIENT PROOF — the abuse-line dynamic, for the RIGHT reason + its ethics
  protocol). The constable can be WRONG both ways (a true act acquitted for want of proof; a
  FALSE report the investigation must be able to REJECT — the false-accusation refusal), and
  justice is CORRUPTIBLE structurally: prosecution runs through the council, and John RUNS it (he
  can block a true prosecution or push a false one — emergent). **The TRIGGER is a
  value-decision** — the outward-facing form of the 242 frustration-SNAP, of spite (`w_spite`,
  John), or of DESPERATION (the starving turning to force) — anger/dysregulation/malice/need
  channeled into a physical act. Minimal by design (a number + a few deltas), but it adds bodily
  STAKES (you can be hurt or KILLED) and enables the town's darkest outcomes.

## 3. The staged ladder (each sub-rung separately gate-able)

Do NOT build this as one monolith. TWENTY-THREE sub-rungs, disjoint seed blocks; the async
time/energy layer of §2.5 threads through them:

- **R1 — MOVEMENT + ENERGY budget (block 249000).** Agents navigate to a seeded goal
  via the reused nav faculty; occlusion re-queries at the new position; **each move
  DEPLETES the per-agent energy budget, and the agent SLOWS as the budget nears
  threshold** (graded — the per-tick move budget shrinks).
  - **HEADLINE gate (the coupling):** movement CHANGES what is witnessed — a villager
    that moves onto a line-of-sight to John's office WITNESSES the scheme it could
    NOT witness from its seed position, and the existing witness→credibility (245) /
    enforcement (247) chain fires differently as a result. Movement is proven by its
    effect on the *already-proven* social layer, not by a position delta alone.
  - **Energy gate:** distance covered per tick DECREASES as the budget depletes (the
    slow-down is graded, measurable); a rested agent covers more than an exhausted one
    over the same wall-clock. **Failing controls:** no goal → no purposeful movement
    (idle/stay); a wall on the straight line → the path routes around it, no teleport;
    goal at current cell → zero moves; energy-cost ablated → no slow-down (cost is
    load-bearing).

- **R2 — NEEDS → GOALS + PREFERENCE/satisficing (block 250000).** A drive above
  threshold mints a spatial goal; WHICH good it targets is the `decision_value`
  Argmax over reachable satisfiers weighted by seeded preference minus travel-cost
  (§2). Reuse `drives` + `goal_construction` + `core_emotions` value economy;
  per-villager preference weights seeded ~Normal.
  - **Gate (drive):** a hungry agent forms a food goal; **ablate the drive → no goal**
    (load-bearing, à la Harlow 233000). Two competing drives → the higher wins (reuse
    `conflicting_goals_default`).
  - **HEADLINE gate (preference/satisficing — the flip on distance alone):** a villager
    that PREFERS apples to bread, held constant, picks APPLES when the orchard is near
    and BREAD when the orchard is moved far — the choice flips on travel-cost alone,
    preference unchanged. **Failing controls:** preference ablated (equal weights) →
    picks the nearest satisfier regardless; travel-cost ablated → always picks the
    top-preference good however far (proves cost is load-bearing); a villager seeded
    to prefer ale makes a *different* choice from the apple-preferrer in the same town
    (preference is per-villager, not global).

- **R3 — DAY/NIGHT + the async temporal substrate (block 251000).** An async
  day/night world phase; **dark reduces the sight-query RANGE** (perception becomes
  time-dependent); **autonomous SLEEP decision** (sleep-when-you-choose; restful sleep
  replenishes the budget); **exhaustion COLLAPSE** (past-threshold → involuntary
  non-restorative sleep) that is WITNESSED → others' CONCERN fires (reuse 239000
  co-reg/affect). Agents async, not lockstep (reuse per-sensor-epoch 188000).
  - **HEADLINE gate (dark gates witnessing):** the SAME scheme at the SAME place is
    witnessed by DAY and NOT by night (sight range shrinks) — the 245/247 chain fires
    time-contingently; flips on the day/night phase alone.
  - **Gates:** sleep is CHOSEN (an agent low on energy + safe + late elects to sleep;
    a busy agent defers) — **failing control:** remove the sleep option → the agent
    runs to collapse. Exhaustion collapse is NON-restorative (post-collapse energy <
    post-restful-sleep energy — the spiral) and DRAWS concern from a witness (concern
    absent if the collapse is occluded/unwitnessed — ties to R3-dark + perception).
  - **Async correctness:** removing the global lockstep must not change any 245/247/248
    landed number when day=constant + energy=∞ (the async substrate reduces to the
    synchronous scaffold in that limit — a byte/decision-identity check).

- **R4 — ECONOMY: production + LOCAL exchange + supply NETWORK + debt + store hours as
  EXPECTATION (block 252000).** Tradespeople produce FROM inputs bought up-chain from
  suppliers (baker←farmer grain, fishmonger←fisherman fish); co-located agents exchange
  coin↔goods; when short of coin, a seller may extend CREDIT (an `owes` obligation
  edge); shops open on fixed cycles, an EXPECTATION the town holds (not a law).
  - **Gate:** a hungry agent WITH coin navigates (R1) to the baker (R2 goal) *during
    opening hours* (R3 clock), buys bread (local exchange), hunger drops. **Failing
    controls:** no coin → no purchase (unless credit extended → `owes` edge); no baker
    at-post → no bread; baker with no grain → no production; **stall CLOSED (or the
    keeper collapsed/asleep) → no exchange even if present.**
  - **Supply + debt gate (the material chain):** the fishmonger with no coin CANNOT
    buy fish → the fisherman goes UNPAID (or is owed) → the fisherman's stock/output
    falls next cycle → *his* customers are affected. A DEFAULT flips the creditor's
    affect (frustration/worry, 204000/239000) and his next credit decision (refuses /
    demands cash). **Failing controls:** debtor pays → no default, no affect flip, no
    supply gap; sever the `supplies` edge → the downstream shortfall does NOT appear
    (proves the propagation is along the graph, not scripted).
  - **Expectation gate (predict-and-residual, NOT censure):** the town HOLDS "stall
    opens at 8"; a keeper who collapsed at 5am → stall shut at 8 → the expectation is
    VIOLATED → SURPRISE registered by would-be customers (distinct from a LAW violation,
    which would draw censure). Control: keeper opens on time → no surprise.
  - **Scale gate (LOAD-BEARING):** re-run the 248000 scale probe with movement+economy
    ON → realized interaction-ops must stay ~LINEAR (slope < ~1.5), because exchange is
    LOCAL. A global market-clearing implementation would measure ~2.0 — the **falsifier**;
    an economy-on quadratic is an all-pairs bug to fix, not a wall.

- **R5 — THE LOOP CLOSES + emergent cascades (block 253000).** work → wage → buy →
  consume → sleep sustains the town (or finds a real breakdown); the temporal, economic
  and social layers interact.
  - **Gate / measurement (measure-first, not pass/fail):** over a multi-day run, does
    the town SUSTAIN, or surface a real failure — a shortage, a hoarder, a keeper who
    drinks and doesn't open? Glass-box: read one villager's whole DAY (Marjorie: wake →
    bake during hours → sell → buy grain → tire → sleep → journal). **The fishmonger
    cascade as a registered demonstration (the anti-bullshit gate):** induce a keeper's
    exhaustion collapse → show the FULL emergent chain, every hop read off the graph,
    nothing scripted — (a) missed opening → expectation-surprise; (b) DOWN-chain: no
    fish → fish-preferrers satisfice elsewhere (§2 preference) → ripple; (c) UP-chain:
    coins spent on drink → can't pay the fisherman → fisherman UNPAID → his affect flips
    (worry for his own household) → he refuses the fishmonger further credit / badmouths
    him → the credibility/social layer (245) moves. **The gate that makes it non-bullshit:**
    every consequence is a real state change that PROPAGATES along the dependency graph
    and CHANGES an affective state (economy→affect→society closed), not a narrated
    description. A sustained loop AND a surfaced breakdown are both results.

- **R6 — SKILL, LEARNING-BY-DOING & TEACHING (block 254000).** Skill (per-(agent,
  activity) competence, seeded) makes production yield specialised (R4/§2); this rung
  makes it DYNAMIC. Reuse the capability-graph competence, the learning loop
  (dynamic-quantization / affect-gates-learning front end), and the
  grounding-from-a-teacher / `teach` facility (the language-bootstrap loop).
  - **Learning-by-doing:** repeated practice nudges the skill up (diminishing returns) —
    the substrate's own learning loop pointed at a trade (it already learns from
    experience: Galileo, respawn-cost). The farmer who keeps fishing slowly improves.
  - **Teaching (skill transfer):** a skilled agent co-located with a practising learner
    TEACHES → accelerated skill gain (the grounding-from-a-teacher loop). Teaching is
    the teacher's DECISION (`decision_value`: help vs create a competitor who undercuts
    me — the fisherman may REFUSE to protect his trade). It can be COERCED (John's
    power/enforcement lever, 247) — but a coerced teacher may SABOTAGE (teach badly on
    purpose = `stated!=held` applied to pedagogy, the 234 deception atom).
  - **HEADLINE gate:** the farmer's fishing yield << the fisherman's (skill scales
    yield); **ablate skill -> yields equal** (skill load-bearing). **Learning gate:** the
    farmer practising fishing -> yield RISES over runs; **ablate learning -> flat**
    (load-bearing). **Teaching gate:** fisherman teaches farmer -> faster rise than solo
    practice; the fisherman REFUSES when the learner is a competitive threat (willingness
    is a real decision, not always-yes); a COERCED teacher transfers but DEGRADED vs a
    willing one (coercion + sabotage readable — reuse 234 `stated!=held`).
  - **Payoff:** the town's economic structure is CONTESTABLE and dynamic — a farmer can
    learn to fish and break the fisherman's supply monopoly; knowledge is power, hoarded
    (refuse to teach) or extracted (coerced). Specialization is EARNED, not fixed.

- **R7 — FINANCE: housing tenure, the bank, interest, rent (block 255000).** The deeper
  economy (§2 FINANCE). Three tenures (own/mortgage/rent); a BANK institution issuing
  mortgages and setting its own rate; interest as the cross-frame multiplicative base;

  - **G-tenure-diverges:** over a multi-day run, owner/landlord equity RISES, the renter's

  - **G-leverage-amplifies (HEADLINE):** a character whose income collapses (reuse the
    fishmonger cascade) MISSES mortgage payments → the bank FORECLOSES → homeless →
    affect/social-concern fires. **Failing control:** the SAME income collapse on an
    OWNED-OUTRIGHT character → keeps the house (leverage is the amplifier, isolated).
  - **G-interest-is-policy:** the bank RAISES the rate → mortgagors' payments rise → more
    defaults/foreclosures; lower it → fewer. The rate is a DECISION (a predatory/
    John-controlled bank extracts more; a fair one less) — `decision_value`, not a
    constant. **Control:** rate held → default count flat.
  - **G-bank-is-an-entity + salary (funds separate):** the `Bank` reserves ≠ Scrooge's
    personal coin; a SALARY transfers bank→Scrooge each cycle and his personal wealth

    wealth flat while the bank still operates. Source-audit: no abstract third party —
    every rate/lending/salary decision is the banker AGENT's rule.
  - **G-greedy-banker (failing control):** a GREEDY Scrooge (high salary + high rate)

    Scrooge's personal wealth (he can stay rich while the bank fails).

    `principal·(1+r)^k − payments`; `r` is applied intensionally, never stored as an

    the map, not the frame).
  - **G-rentier-power:** a landlord raising rent / threatening eviction moves a tenant's
    affect + backing (John-as-slumlord = economic coercive control) — ties to 239/247.
  - **Payoff:** class and inequality are MECHANICAL (tenure → wealth divergence), a
    financial shock is a HOUSING shock (leverage), and there is now MACRO-power (the
    bank's rate) above the interpersonal drama.

## 4. Pre-registered design RISKS (bind the prereg of each sub-rung)

- **The SCALE × TIMING frontier — a MEASUREMENT, not a target (creator, 2026-07-29).** How big a
  town at what tick rate is EMPIRICAL — the scaling run produces a Pareto curve (residents ×
  tick-rate on commodity hardware) and we pick an operating point ON it, per purpose. **The demo
  scale is EARNED, reported as part of every claim** (the eval-subset-is-part-of-the-claim lesson,
  applied to population), never assumed. Two forces PULL APART and are resolved by TWO operating
  points on ONE curve: statistical power wants THOUSANDS (so 3.5% ≈ 35 people not 7 — the §0 LLN
  caveat sharpens) → a big, faster-than-real-time BATCH town for the numbers; a live demo wants
  HUNDREDS in REAL-TIME (watchability, live-adaptation framing) → a small real-time town. Same
  substrate + seeds, different point on the frontier. **The scaling run is also a TEST of the
  compute thesis** (`compute ∝ surprise`, R11 `G-scope-gating`, the RETE incremental matcher
  192000–195000): does per-tick cost track ACTIVE bindings/surprise or HEADCOUNT? If headcount →
  an O(N) problem to fix; if surprise → a large-but-quiet town is cheap and only the punctuations
  are expensive — the SAME cost signature as punctuated equilibrium (cheap stable, costly
  transitions, §0). The thing that makes it affordable and the thing that makes it valid are ONE
  measurement seen twice. Pre-register: measure cost-vs-headcount AND cost-vs-activity separately.
