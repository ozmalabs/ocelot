# DEVELOPMENTAL SAFETY ONTOLOGY — RESULTS

**Date:** 2026-08-04

## Outcome

The developmental path now represents causal closure, experience retention,
contextual release, caregiver protection, recoverability, and unbuffered
exposure as separate facts. Caregiver handoff is no longer permission to act.
No float was added to graph cognition.

## Registered gates

- **DS1 passed.** A deterministic adverse-tail receipt with support loss and
  high load is retained despite receiving no success or release credit.
- **DS2 passed.** Developmental programs and transitions carry separate
  `experience_retained` and `contextual_release_eligible` state. Caregiver
  protection can authorize a retained program in that context without erasing
  or globally relabelling the program.
- **DS3 passed.** All absent, scrambled and contingent caregiver arms complete
  the same 108 self-authored actions and master the same two early
  capabilities.
- **DS4 passed.** Caregiver support is a separately reported exafferent world
  force. It does not change the child command or receive an efference copy.
- **DS5 passed.** Calibration assistance is controlled by command experience
  relative to somatomotor field size. Later assistance is controlled by
  capability attempts relative to its body-derived evidence window.
- **DS6 passed.** The focused integer and physical suite passes 59 tests.

## Three-arm developmental assay

`results/caregiver_safety_ontology_ablation_v7.json` uses the same Walker2D
body, fixture, seed and curriculum in all arms.

| arm | self-authored actions | retained | protected / unbuffered | contingent passive models | mastery |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| caregiver absent | 108 | 108 | 0 / 108 | 0 | 2 |
| caregiver scrambled | 108 | 108 | 14 / 94 | 0 | 2 |
| caregiver contingent | 108 | 108 | 14 / 94 | 7 | 2 |

The scrambled arm records all eight mismatched passive events, refuses body-map
handoff credit, and nevertheless develops cold. Protection remains available
because caregiver presence and caregiver contingency are independent facts.
The contingent arm actively reverifies all seven passively mapped channels;
the absent arm correctly reverifies none.

This assay shows that caregiver help is not required for these milestones. It
does not show that the help is behaviorally irrelevant: the short curriculum
has insufficient tail risk and all arms reach the same early frontier.

## Matched consequence assay

`results/caregiver_protection_physics_v1.json` applies the same downward
body-weight pulse and the same tonic child command to two identically seeded
upright worlds. Timing comes from observed support-channel and joint counts.
The caregiver-buffered arm receives 35.06 N·s of external support and finishes
0.000621 m higher than the unbuffered arm. This is a small but discriminative
physical consequence difference. It demonstrates intervention, not a claim of
human injury prevention or biomechanical realism.

## Failures found during implementation

The first corrected run emitted cold actions but mature gait logic relabelled
them, preventing developmental receipt closure. The second completed 14 cold
calibration actions but exposed another hidden-home dependency: current-sensed
reference binding still required caregiver handoff. Both were corrected.
Situated reference binding is now independent of caregiver evidence, and both
scaffolded and unscaffolded attempts retain fresh motor-babble agency.

The protective world model currently supplies one body-weight of upward force
during graph-authorized actions. This cleanly separates child command from
caregiver intervention, but a future caregiver model should learn catching,
graded support, repositioning and withdrawal as its own adaptive controller.
