# P4-R0 local preregistration

P4-R0 is a deterministic public-fixture local engineering test, **not P4-E1/E2 evidence**. It always reports `classification: local_readiness_only`, `custody: local`, `adopted: false`, and `promotion_authority: false`, then exact-rolls back. It cannot satisfy P3 or P4 gates.

Locked inputs: `fixtures/p4_r0_public_bundle_v1.json` (exactly two public deterministic simulator-only domains), `fixtures/p4_r0_mechanics_arms_v1.json` (the five declared executable mechanics arms), fixed local test-key identity, schema versions, Containerfile/seccomp/native-export/image digests in the generated lock, and a lock-bound catalog containing every materialized fixture record and arm digest. Runtime never hardcodes a fixture key: the evaluator resolves the requested record and arm only from these catalogs and signs a `P4R0RuntimeSelectionV1` envelope containing run ID, fixture/record/selection identity, arm identity/digest, and selection digest. The selected record still derives an opaque run-scoped fixture ID; the record and arm digests remain run-independent.

Each selected arm contributes locked mechanics and a trace contract to native setup. `graph_planner` and `search_budget_matched` require causal/replan provenance; `reactive_no_plan` uses a direct transition with no causal/replan node; `no_causal_model` retains planning without causal nodes; and `reset_memory` clears provenance and fails closed with a memory-reset impasse. Assertions are made over native graph transitions and signed trace/outcome bindings, not over arm labels.

The evaluator binds the signed runtime selection, record, arm, and run-derived ID into task release, replan, proposal/grant/action/outcome receipt context, signed evaluator outcome transport, native request, terminal/impasse/result receipts, and rollback receipts. Proposal, reservation, transport, and trace digests transitively include `selection_digest` and `arm_digest`; native state persists and compares these fields on every observation and outcome. The scratch worker reads the image-owned bundle and catalogs, verifies the runtime envelope signature and both catalog digests before native setup, independently verifies the selected record, and consumes the selected arm mechanics. Production launch remains image-only: no host mounts, no host fixture reads, `--pull=never`, `--network=none`, read-only root, dropped capabilities, no-new-privileges, private PID, fixed UID, seccomp, measured closure, and fixed image-owned trust/input paths.

Limits remain: 8 steps, 1 information intervention, 1 reversible mistaken action, 1 replan budget, 12 branches, 2 hypotheses, 8 actions/risk/transactions, 64 KiB frames, 3 seconds, no worker persistence. The lock carries the executable mechanics and trace contract for each declared arm; no score comparison is evidence.

Negative corpus: fixture substitution; opaque selection-ID mismatch; raw bundle/record digest mismatch; selected-record mismatch; label scramble; unsupported or reordered transition mechanics; forged/altered signed outcome; expired/replayed grant or outcome; missing/unsupported/unresolved manifold; budget exhaustion; contradiction; malformed/unknown output; host-I/O/control-plane/rollback probes; and containment substitution. All terminate no-go or impasse and restore the pre-run graph snapshot exactly. Any source, seed, fixture, tool document, Containerfile, image, seccomp, or limit change requires a new lock and invalidates outputs. Adoption, durable learning, remote access, and aggregation into P3/P4 evidence are prohibited.
