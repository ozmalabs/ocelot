The check establishes the stated property only for the one four-variable formula and the proposed extension on that instance: all 16 assignments were examined, and the extension was total and single-valued there. It is finite, instance-specific evidence.

It does not establish that every residual CNF has a total extension. That universal claim ranges over formulas not included in the check. Nor does the check establish that solving such formulas is easy: no runtime, cost measure, or broader set of instances was evaluated. The report should state both the result's scope and what it does not establish, as the research guide requires ([SCIENCE_RESEARCH.md §7.1](docs/SCIENCE_RESEARCH.md#71-writing-docsresearchtopicresultsmd)).
