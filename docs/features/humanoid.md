# Humanoid / embodiment

Embodied motor control — balance, walking, manipulation, damage adaptation —
for a simulated humanoid body, built on a body-agnostic sensorimotor layer
shared with the ground-vehicle and aerial control work
(`docs/features/flight-control.md`).

## The shared sensorimotor architecture

The ground, aerial, and humanoid controllers share **one** graph-native
sensorimotor layer (`seeds/embodied_sensorimotor_core.json`, generated by
`scripts/gen_embodied_sensorimotor_core_seed.py`). It does not know or care
whether the body it's controlling is a car, a drone, or a person — it learns
from opaque effectors, delayed physical receipts, and uncertain multimodal
observations. The simulator boundary only transduces physics (position,
orientation, velocities, contact, sample period, sensor latency, actuator
activation, energy/power/thermal readings); prediction, sensor fusion,
attention, interruption, credit assignment, consolidation, and replay are
all Rust-executed graph rules on top of that boundary, not per-body Python
logic. Biological constraints are translated into graph mechanisms rather
than simulated anatomically — e.g. "delayed proprioceptive/vestibular
feedback cannot be read as the current body state" becomes an
`EmbodiedBodyBelief` that advances a prior and precision-weights each
delayed sensor receipt, the same predictive-coding move the vision eye
makes for retinal input.

Older, body-specific seeds (per-limb, per-joint priors) now depend on this
shared core through `embodied_motor_priors` rather than duplicating it.

## What's landed

- **Balance and walking** — graph-native humanoid balance adaptation and
  walking, including a landed fix where a previously-off "balance term"
  (`locomotion.support_geometry_preview_authority`) was switched on with a
  single protocol-line change and measured, rather than redesigned.
- **Damage and wear adaptation** — a multi-version arc (v1 through v4 in
  `docs/research/humanoid/`) of hidden-damage navigation: the body
  continues operating and re-adapting its control policy as components
  degrade, without being told what broke.
- **Nonholonomic control** — wheeled/legged bodies with motion constraints
  that can't move directly sideways; a two-version arc building the graph
  representation for that constraint class.
- **Generic effector assembly learning** — the motor substrate discovers
  coordination structure from a body's wiring and verified action receipts,
  without requiring a named humanoid skeleton, named muscles, or a
  predefined gait; the same representation covers joints and wheels.
- **Developmental motor curriculum** — situated, child-like motor
  development (a staged curriculum rather than a single end-to-end
  controller), and a CMU-motion-derived topology bootstrap that distills
  observed human movement into output-null graph topology (it does not
  imitate poses or retain floating-point kinematics — its purpose is
  giving the analysis-by-synthesis loop a better starting prior).
- **Affordance vocabulary** — a general base vocabulary for what a body can
  *do* with an object, grounded rather than hand-listed.
- **Contact physics** — continuum and wrench-based contact modeling for
  manipulation.

## Where the code lives

- Seeds: `seeds/embodied_sensorimotor_core.json`,
  `seeds/embodied_motor_priors*.json`, `seeds/effector_compensation.json`
  and neighbors.
- World adapters / scripts: the `humanoid_*` and `embodied_*` families
  under `scripts/` and `domains/`.
- Gates: `tests/test_effector_compensation_migrated.py`,
  `tests/test_embodied_adaptation_migrated.py`, and neighbors.
