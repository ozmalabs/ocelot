# Research assistant evaluation design

Status: proposed pilot, not a preregistered confirmatory study. No comparative
results or claim of benefit are available. Freeze tasks, source revisions,
answer keys, scoring form, model settings and analysis in a separate committed
preregistration before any held-out measurement. Pilot cases are burned and
must not be reused for confirmation.

The decision is whether structured research memory improves research answers
enough to justify its cost. Compare the same model and source access under:
(1) source files and ordinary notes, (2) source files and a generic claim and
evidence ledger with no GE-specific fields, and (3) the research helper with
its full frame ledger. Give each arm equal source access, tool privileges and
token/time budgets. A separate source-access-only arm is the ordinary-notes
baseline; record all prompts and tool calls. Randomize assignment/order and
use independent blinded graders. The unit is the independently run task,
not the number of claims within one answer. Account for pairing and repeated
model runs in the preregistered analysis; report uncertainty and all failures.

A pilot should cover at least: a finite check versus a universal mathematical
claim; evidence that contradicts a broad claim; an Ocelot experiment with a
failed or missing control; a stale source or unavailable artifact; and an
inapplicable frame field. Include material from both repositories and
non-GE examples. The answer key must cite fixed source content and specify
acceptable uncertainty, counterevidence, and scope. Plant contradictions only
in clearly marked synthetic fixtures, never in original research records.
Keep held-out questions and grading keys inaccessible to the assistant during
runs. Establish scorer agreement and revise the rubric using pilot material.

Record per task source-claim accuracy, unsupported assertions, recall of
contradictions and limitations, citation reopenability and hash match,
completion, tokens, latency and tool cost. Publish the denominator, missing
runs, confidence intervals and the exact analysis, not just a favorable
aggregate. Predeclare a primary outcome and a practically meaningful change,
sample-size justification, exclusion policy and decision rule in the eventual
preregistration. A null, degradation, source access failure or inconclusive
sample must be reported as such; no tool or theoretical contribution follows
from a successful synthetic check alone.

A subsequent four-case paired exploration was run; its cases are now burned.
It found no measured helper advantage on that small set, with weak grader
agreement on citation reopenability. See the [results](../../../evaluation/research_assistant/exploratory_20261114b/RESULTS.md)
for the data and limitations. It did not meet the requirements for a
confirmatory held-out evaluation.
