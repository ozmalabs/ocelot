# Navigation outcome-ranker integration v1 — capture amendment

The capture preserves the three policy orderings, held-out candidate utility table, candidate trials, and training-outcome transfer. During result audit, I found that the runner did not record the per-policy ordering latency required by the preregistration. This omission does not change the ordering, held-out scores, or attempt counts, but it means the experiment does not provide the preregistered ordering-time measure or a complete cost comparison. The source-locked runner and raw capture are preserved unchanged; no timing values are reconstructed after the fact. Treat the result as valid for outcome/order/utility evidence only. A follow-up cost panel must add timing before its source lock and include branch, scoring, and adoption costs if claiming economic advantage.
