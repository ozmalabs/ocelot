# Ousiometric representation comparison contract v1

Status: preregistration design contract for Phase 28. This contract does not
claim that VAD and PDS are interchangeable psychological constructs, that the
paper's claims generalize outside English lexical evidence, or that a frame
improves the substrate. Fixed frames are disabled by default. All agent-side
selection, acquisition, and action decisions remain native graph/Rust work.

## 1. Claims and estimands

The investigation separates four hypotheses:

1. **Basis utility under a bound:** a named axis-aligned metric/aperture may
   alter learning and prediction efficiency when finite work is imposed.
2. **New information:** a full-rank coordinate change cannot add observations;
   any apparent information gain must be traced to new witnessed evidence,
   not to a rotation.
3. **Acquired significance:** an experience-built frame is supported only by
   source-bound, observed outcome witnesses and independent fresh validation.
4. **Sampling geometry:** differences caused by lexical or event frequencies
   are distribution effects and must be separated from differences in world
   mechanisms and distinct outcome support.

The sole proposed behavioral primary is paired normalized learning-curve AUC
for the registered `PDS-bounded` versus `VAD-bounded` arms at equal raw evidence,
three-coordinate precision, aggregate frame/decoder bits, candidate cap, native
work, episode horizon, and verifier policy. A positive result requires an
absolute improvement of at least 0.10, a 99% paired world-bootstrap lower bound
above zero, and Holm-adjusted p ≤ .01 across the two registered world families.
Both world families must satisfy the direction and safety gates. If compatible
semantic calibration is unavailable, this comparison is relabeled
`published-rotation-bounded`; no VAD/PDS psychological or semantic claim is
permitted. The protocol may not switch its primary after development outcomes.

The full-information reference is a conformance control, not an efficacy
contrast. Unquantized full-rank arms use the same source rows, predictor,
precision, and transported metric. Their predictions and distances must agree
within the transform's preregistered numerical tolerance. `learned-3D`, one
seeded `random-basis` per replicate, `frozen-space`, fixed-metric and
transported-metric conditions are declared secondary mechanism controls.
`learned-flex` is a capacity curve and is not a primary equal-capacity arm.

## 2. Calibration, units, and labels

An operational feature encoder must be fitted from common observable features
using development-only anchors. Every fixed-frame arm receives the exact same
raw feature records and anchor membership. Lexical labels used to align or
orient the frame count as information and are excluded from training and
sealed concept clusters. No PDS, VAD, hidden factor, answer, or correct-axis
label enters an experience-built arm. Without documented compatible units and
a full-rank map, conditions are described only as rotations of shared numeric
coordinates. No published psychological axis name is assigned to arbitrary
world features.

The paper supplies the approximate full-rank VAD→PDS relationship in Eq. 4,
 derived by a full three-dimensional SVD followed by a −π/4 rotation of the
goodness/aggression plane. Its printed coefficients are rounded (approximately
`[[.50,.48,.72],[-.72,.69,.04],[.48,.55,-.69]]`). A frozen copy of those
displayed coefficients with a computed inverse defines a valid mathematical
full-rank map for round-trip and predictor-invariance tests against that map;
those tests do not reproduce the article's original empirical coordinates.
The exact source ratings and preprocessing are separately required for
psychological lexical calibration. The NRC VAD v1 (2018) source matches the paper's stated 20k-word
instrument more closely than v2.1, but its data license is non-commercial
research/education and prohibits redistribution. The later v2.1 source is a
distinctly versioned instrument and cannot silently substitute for v1.

The GPADS five columns do not count as five independent coordinates. The
registered PDS view uses its declared P/D/S columns, preserving the source's
structure orientation (high = unstructured); the five-column file contains
redundant views of a three-dimensional construction. A derived VAD↔PDS map
must pass rank, conditioning, inverse-round-trip, and held-out conformance
checks and retain source normalization metadata. If exact original ratings or
reproducible preprocessing are unavailable, psychological lexical
calibration is unsupported; the mathematical full-rank rotation diagnostic
can still be reported under that narrower name.

## 3. Evidence and equal budgets

All primary arms receive identical ordered raw episode observations, action
opportunities, world-owned receipts, source-event deduplication, prediction
targets, verifier access, and total paid interactions. Each bounded arm has
three coordinates, the same coordinate precision, fixed aggregate storage
bits including forward/inverse maps and decoder, same candidate cap, and one
shared `ThinkingBudget`. Failed proposals, map and decoder descriptions,
exceptions, residuals, exact checks, native rule work, bytes, and elapsed time
are charged. A separate flexible-dimensional arm reports all those costs and
is not treated as equal-capacity evidence.

Observed world factors remain private to the generator/scorer. Agent inputs
contain observable features and actual receipts only. Repeated occurrences do
not become independent support: source event IDs are deduplicated, and support
is counted by distinct verified outcome witnesses. Uniform type, measured
token frequency, and shuffled-frequency presentations are analyzed separately;
rare adverse controls remain visible regardless of mean performance.

## 4. Geometry and unknown behavior

For a full-rank map `y = T x`, prediction comparisons transport the predictor
and metric through `T⁻¹`. L1 and quadratic distances are compared under both
transported and fixed metrics. Fixed axis-aligned quantization/aperture may
change rankings; this is reported as bounded basis utility, not new
information. A lossy projection, dimension drop, five-versus-three coordinate
comparison, singular inverse, nonfinite value, missing source unit, stale
epoch, or unverified frame returns a typed unknown/rejected result and is not
scored as a successful comparison.

Unknown and missing are never coerced to zero. Mismatched owner, source,
evidence status, frame, units, or epoch cannot match. Candidate proposals stay
non-authoritative until native verification succeeds on fresh contexts. No
host adapter chooses an action or frame. The simulator supplies only measured
observations and verifies the action selected by the graph.

## 5. Decision and safety boundary

The primary behavioral analysis uses 200 paired streams per world family,
paired world-level bootstrap intervals with 10,000 resamples, Holm correction
across the two family contrasts, alpha .01, and the margin above. Every
registered arm, failed run, timeout, abstention, and missing lexical concept
is retained. Lexical agreement is a separate secondary measure and cannot
rescue a failed behavior primary.

Lexical danger is asserted/inferred language evidence. It may request
additional measurement but cannot certify an action, alter verified physical
consequence receipts, replace a time-indexed manifold residual tube, lower a
confidence/approval gate, or override a host veto. Reassuring language cannot
hide projected escape; frightening, negated, quoted, or ironic language cannot
prove physical danger. Only an independently verified consequence and the
existing nontradeable host boundary govern action authorization.

## 6. Compatibility and runtime scope

Phase 28 implementation is opt-in, investigation-profile-only, self-load-only,
and disabled by default. It may use existing native Terms and graph data; a
new Rust primitive requires a separate written semantic-gap finding. The
Python world adapter only translates input events, calls native ticks, and
reads graph-selected actions/receipts. Default and conversation profiles,
existing frame semantics, safety contracts, and approvals do not change.

This experiment can establish bounded utility for its declared synthetic
worlds and lexical comparison coverage. It cannot establish universal qualia,
causal independence from orthogonality, human parity, real-world danger, or
general competence. No default/core representation promotion follows from a
positive lexical or bounded-metric result alone.
