## Design and allocation

The panel contains `permutation_relabel_4` and `integer_matrix_basis_2`, each
in forward and reverse directions. Each of the four pairing/direction cells
uses 200 heldout seeds, each evaluated in `fresh`, `source_exposed` and
`mismatched_source` arms on the same generated target world. This is 2,400
primary records. Another 100 wrong-frame and 100 nonconjugate control records
are run, for 2,600 total. Heldout seeds occupy the fixed 6,000,000 block; the
5,000,000 block is reserved for development. World and arm order, seeds,
training/heldout rows and control assignment are generated by the frozen
runner.

The primary criterion is at least 25% fewer target exact checks for the
source-exposed arm than fresh, with no more than a 2 percentage-point solve
loss. A comparison is eligible only if both arms verify the same target
effect. Missing matched outcomes produce `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, never a zero or a
pass. Wrong-frame and nonconjugate controls must have zero adopted transfers.
All paired records must have the same world hash, clean agent-input audits,
and actual outcomes. External ARC/LS20 ingestion and a protocol-matched human
comparison are unavailable and remain `UNSUPPORTED`/`UNAVAILABLE`.

The development adapter currently gives fresh no source map and therefore no
matched solved outcome. This limitation is frozen; the panel may establish
mechanism safety and control behavior without establishing transfer benefit.
No changes to thresholds, arms, seeds, exclusions or scoring are allowed after
the heldout draw. Any implementation change invalidates this registration and
requires a new preregistration before a new heldout allocation.
