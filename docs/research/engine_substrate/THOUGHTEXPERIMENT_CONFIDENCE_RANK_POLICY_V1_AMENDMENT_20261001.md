# Confidence ranking policy v1 — observed discriminator amendment

The preregistered expectation that the current raw-count policy would still choose the bad candidate after a verified negative was not supported. In all three workers, the independent calibration probe first selected episode_scoped_offer_guard and returned [1,0]. After that failure was recorded, the existing policy selected dedup_identity_fix first and passed [1,1] in one trial. The branch-local Beta(1,1) smoothed-mean Program made the same selection and reached the same outcome.

This is retained as a negative comparative result: the treatment did not improve first choice or trial count over the current policy once verified negative evidence was available. The current selector appears responsive to the failure count under this exact candidate-cost difference. The failed preregistered discriminator is not removed from the gate set, and no treatment superiority claim is made. The three-worker panel still validates branch-local Program replacement, equal evidence across arms, source/native lock integrity, independent-oracle labeling, and primary isolation. It does not establish calibration, robustness to other priors or candidate costs, or natural-task transfer.
