# Substrate induction surface and integration inventory (2026-09-26)

## Why this inventory

The preceding nonlinear GenInfer experiment tested one operation family: one-step geometric transformation induction in `shape_decode.rs`. A wider source scan finds several other native inference Terms and graph-authored mint paths. They are not interchangeable, and results for one family must not be generalized to the entire substrate. This inventory classifies their input class, output representation, seed integration, evidence, and remaining tractability/capability gap.

## Synthesis: solutions and gaps

The substrate is not empty at structure acquisition. It has tested, narrow methods for integer linear maps, higher-order integer translation dynamics, discrete motion-layer decomposition, recurring relational-shape abstraction, and bounded executable program search. Some of these methods mint durable graph data and demonstrate later reuse; others are direct operators, explicitly gated seeds, or offline host-side mechanisms.

The unresolved capability question is composition across those acquisition surfaces: whether ordinary production residuals route to a suitable learner, whether a candidate representation is validated independently, whether mint/adoption is safe and durable, and whether held-out tasks improve over frozen, retrieval, or bounded-template baselines at measured cost. `RatioInduce` is specifically semantically incomplete and unintegrated. `MetaShapeMint` is real symbolic minting but its result is not executable code. `TimeInfer` is a genuine higher-order learner, but its evidence concerns discrete integer-vector transition classes. The nonlinear GenInfer result narrows only the one-step geometric matrix learner.

## Literal seed-manifest census

A reproducible read-only scan of all **826** current `seeds/*.json` manifests records exact objects whose `type` equals one of the inference Term names. It finds `TimeInfer` in six manifests; `DimInfer` in two; `GenInfer`, `DepthInfer`, `BlinkBridge`, and `MetaShapeMint` in one each; and no literal seed invocation of `RatioInduce`, `SupportInfer`, or `SynthEnumerate`. Exact setting/status token mentions for the GenInfer, TimeInfer, DepthInfer, and BlinkBridge gates each occur in one manifest. This census proves only that serialized seed Terms/settings are present or absent. It does not establish seed loading, rule reachability, default flag values, or that a normal runtime producer emits the required input status. The output includes every matched seed/path and SHA-256 for the census script and all scanned manifests: [runner](../../../scripts/substrate_induction_seed_census_v1.py), [capture](../../../evaluation/reports/substrate_induction_seed_census_v1_20260926.json).

## Runtime activation census

A three-arm clean-adapter capture closes part of the manifest-to-runtime gap for the shape-growth path. `WorldAdapter()` loads 17 core seeds and neither `shape_decode_loop` nor `meta_shape_growth`; the selected conversation evaluation profile loads 70 seeds including `shape_decode_loop` but excludes `meta_shape_growth`. The same two `teach()` observations remain `pending` in both. Explicitly loading the two-seed growth dependency closure (`shape_vocabulary`, `meta_shape_growth`) changes the outcomes to `residual` then `recognized` and mints one symbolic MetaShape, `ms_is_a_slot_a_slot_b`. A follow-up reproducing the daemon's actual fresh-boot branches corrects the production interpretation: daemon `full` loads both growth seeds (729 of 740 selected manifests succeed; 11 fail) and reaches residual→recognition with one new MetaShape, while daemon `chat` and `embodied` profiles omit them and remain pending. All four inspected learner flags are absent in all of these arms. These are activation results on authored examples, not natural production prevalence or evidence that symbolic MetaShape minting creates executable operators. See [v1 results](SUBSTRATE_INDUCTION_RUNTIME_ACTIVATION_CENSUS_V1_RESULTS_20260926.md) and [daemon v2 results](SUBSTRATE_INDUCTION_DAEMON_ACTIVATION_CENSUS_V2_RESULTS_20260926.md).

## Next gates

1. **Integration census:** for every learner row above, lock the exact seed set, activation flags, producer/input status, default path, and whether normal runtime observations can reach it. Distinguish direct-call tests from seeded closure and host-only code. The daemon's three fresh-boot branches and the selected conversation evaluation profile now have activation evidence. Next audit actual production call sites that invoke `teach()` or `meta_shape.observe`, and measure compatible residual prevalence plus false-mint/transfer behavior before proposing activation changes.
2. **Adoption/value gate:** evaluate MetaShapeMint on independently held-out concepts and distractor populations, scoring retrieval and downstream transfer, false mint, forgetting, and graph growth. It is the most mature Rung 4 symbolic path; measure its decision quality before expanding its mint vocabulary.
3. **Program synthesis gate:** trace a production residual through search proposal, independent verification, adoption, reuse and rollback; compare against a fixed grammar/retrieval baseline and report search/latency tails.
4. **RatioInduce correction gate:** resolve the representation semantics and construct an actual population-to-held-out-reconstruction path before treating it as a learner or integrating it.
5. **Dynamics generalization gate:** keep `TimeInfer`/`DepthInfer` in their measured integer/synthetic envelopes until an independently authored sensor/task stream demonstrates downstream prediction and calibrated failure outside those fixtures.

These gates are complementary. Passing one mechanism gate cannot be used to claim general tractability or general learning capability.
