# Phase-0 novel-shape and novel-location response v1 results

The source-locked capture passed all schedule, freeze, and execution-integrity gates across three new seeds. The 90 frozen probes include seen square/plus controls, held-out X morphology at the two trained locations, and the same X at two untrained locations. The analyzer independently recomputed activations from the saved weights: all 90 activation vectors match the native values within `1e-6`, all 90 recomputed argmaxes match native winners, and learned weights remain exactly unchanged during the frozen probe phase.

All 30 seen-shape probes were location-correct (15/15 square and 15/15 plus). The unseen X was also location-correct in 30/30 probes at the trained sites. At the novel sites `(5,12)` and `(12,5)`, however, 0/30 X probes had a winning unit whose field was centered within one cell of the presented site with spread below three cells. Median winning-field distance from the novel target was 5.10 cells and median spread 6.57 cells. Thus the prior morphology-tolerance result replicates on new seeds, while zero-shot location transfer fails under the registered criterion.

The pattern is consistent with learned location-specific receptive fields that tolerate a new local morphology, rather than translation-invariant shape features. That interpretation is limited to a two-site map, four units, fixed binary masks, three seeds, and the selected novel coordinates. The substrate may generalize differently after training a broader spatial map or using a different representation; neither is measured here. This does not establish semantic shape recognition or natural-image capability.
