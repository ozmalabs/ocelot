**Reason:** v1.3 established graph-attributed proposal utility on the two held-out adjacent navigation states, but the preregistered candidate digest treated engine-generated `content_hash`, `revision`, and `rule_uid` fields as semantic rule content. Positive controls require the graph and host `flip_comparison` candidates to match under this projection. Negative controls alter each of the four retained behavior dimensions (target key, match, guard, effect/operator/value or ordering) and must yield a nonmatch. Never overwrite the v1.3 result or call its failed raw-digest prediction a pass.

Capture a new source lock, source-stable orders without held-out utility outcomes, inspect graph attribution and no-cue abstention, then score only if those gates pass. A pass supports generalization across the tested cardinal displacements on this authored rule family; it does not establish obstacle reasoning, task discovery, general proposal diversity, or self-improvement.
