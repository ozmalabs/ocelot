# Thoughtexperiment checkpointed and isolated combined observer v1.2.12 results

Three source-locked full-matrix workers pass corrected analysis across ten fixtures. Combined event streams equal event-only reports, and total sparse residual receipts match whole-graph diffs on every fixture. The same-event branch now inserts an authored hypothesis node before a phase checkpoint, then performs rule closure. Its total `conclusions` receipt matches the full diff from before the hypothesis, while `derived_only` matches the full diff from the checkpoint baseline. This validates the two residual scopes on this branch fixture.

The capture also contains 36 separate-arm processes (three replicates × three fixtures × four modes), launched before the coordinator retains the large matrix output. Every process has a distinct PID and stable source lock. RSS/PSS are sampled after graph construction and immediately after each mode returns; values therefore reflect retained end-state memory deltas, not transient peak allocation or native allocation alone.

On the report-object cap workload, median post-run RSS/PSS delta is 2,924 KiB for plain closure, 13,956 KiB for full-snapshot exact diff, 118,164 KiB for event-only tracing, and 187,433 KiB for combined capture. Combined retains about 69 MiB more than event-only on this authored stress fixture. Median wall times are 6.13, 19.85, 165.32, and 481.52 ms respectively; combined is 2.91× event-only. On the small direct fixture, combined delta is 588 KiB vs 520 KiB event-only, with 0.189 vs 0.129 ms wall time. Same-event writes show 588 vs 520 KiB and 0.196 vs 0.131 ms. These measured costs make observer co-activation concrete, while remaining workload-specific. The v1.2.11 high-water readings (751,016 KiB in every arm) are not usable for comparison: probes followed large capture aggregation and inherited the coordinator's process high-water state. V1.2.12 moves probes first and adds current RSS/PSS sampling.
