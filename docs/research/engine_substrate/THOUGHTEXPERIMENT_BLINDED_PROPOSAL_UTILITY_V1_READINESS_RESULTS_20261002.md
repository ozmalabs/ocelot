# Thoughtexperiment blinded proposal utility v1 — executable readiness audit

**Decision:** no-go for panel capture; 5/7 readiness checks pass.

The new read-only audit reads the four admitted fixture captures and the pre-capture protocol. It confirms that all four fixture artifacts enumerate at least three distinct candidates and record held-out successes and failures, with source stability recorded. However, the old shape-axis rank trace only rehearsed 2 of its 6 candidates before stopping at the first repair; its other four variants have no held-out utility score in that capture. Recurrence has 8/8 candidate outcomes, navigation 6/6, and control-kind 6/6. The v1 protocol and v1.1 information-boundary amendment are present, and no held-out panel order or score capture has begun.

Two gates fail:

- Each prior fixture capture has three workers; the confirmatory protocol requires four fresh workers per family.
- None of the four existing captures records the common candidate-level cost fields for enumeration, ranking, branch creation, scoring, and adoption verification.

These are evidence gaps, not evidence of poor utility. The older captures were admission and integration studies, not the confirmatory panel, so their worker and cost data cannot be retroactively pooled. The panel must independently score all six shape candidates on its locked held-out set. A source-locked common runner, separate order and scoring stages, and cost-reconciling analyzer remain necessary.
