# GenInfer repeated-predicate recovery factorial results

The 75-call factorial is deterministic within every cell. All 15 unperturbed controls recover `[1,1,0,2]` with no residual. Under the 12 perturbed conditions, the decomposition is accepted in 11/12 cells, but the generating matrix is recovered in only 4/12. Both outcomes depend on which two points share a predicate: the uniform +1 and +2 perturbations preserve the generating matrix for `adjacent_01` and `opposite_03`, but select `[-1,1,0,2]` for `adjacent_12`. Unequal perturbations produce alternate matrices with residuals in three cells, an alternate matrix with no residual in one cell, and refusal in one cell. Thus `explained=true` is not a reliable proxy for generator recovery, and residual support alone does not make the decomposition identifiable.

The v1 fixed-work gate failed as written: one perturbed cell (`adjacent_12 × unequal_1_3`) consistently reports 14,640 checked candidates while the other cells report 6,560. Its larger observed coordinate range changes the data-derived search bound. The original [v1 analysis](../../../evaluation/reports/substrate_geninfer_repeated_predicate_recovery_v1_analysis_20260926.json) preserves the failed gate. The [v1.1 amendment](SUBSTRATE_GENINFER_REPEATED_PREDICATE_RECOVERY_V1_1_AMENDMENT_20260926.md) relaxes only this invalid fixed-count invariant; the [amended independent analysis](../../../evaluation/reports/substrate_geninfer_repeated_predicate_recovery_v1_1_analysis_20260926.json) verifies all inputs, positive controls, repeat determinism, and per-cell stable positive checked counts while reporting the 14,640 value. This is descriptive amended analysis, not a new confirmatory capture.
