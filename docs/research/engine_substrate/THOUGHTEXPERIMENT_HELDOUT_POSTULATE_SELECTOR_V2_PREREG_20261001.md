# Held-out postulate selector v2 preregistration

**Question.** Does the held-out `sift` scale-cancellation derivation survive the normal open-gap selector, and does selector output remain invariant under receipt adoption?

## Method

Repeat the v1 full-boot, controlled `batch_normalization` ownership rewire, and held-out `sift` structural transfer across three fresh workers. For each Direct and receipt arm, call the real `synthesize_gaps()` path twice with the same explicit harvested shape and structural lineage: derivation-only (`prune=False`) and normal selector (`prune=True`). Retain full gap records from both modes, along with the surface-only control from v1, canonical graph parity, and stable source/seed fingerprints.

## Gates

Require the v1 identity, lineage, shape, canonical graph, structural-hit, and surface-control gates to continue passing; require Direct/receipt derivation-only outputs equal; require Direct/receipt selector outputs equal; report whether the target scale-cancellation gap is retained and the complete selector output. The selector may validly retain or prune the gap; do not make novelty or utility claims from either outcome alone.

## Limits

This measures the existing selector's outcome on one held-out authored Concept under a controlled source-edge rewire. It does not establish real-world utility or learned improvement. A retained open gap is only a candidate; validation against an independent utility oracle remains necessary.
