# Scheduler checkpoint transfer v1 results

The first source-locked transfer check passed on two independent small synthetic workload families: productive CAN coactivation and retinal percept synthesis. Four entry states (fresh and post-closure in each family) passed exact graph, rule, scheduler, global-counter, per-rule-counter, and final-state parity. All six independent analysis checks passed. The post-closure states had non-empty dirty-rule and cursor state, but neither family exercised a non-empty dirty-node set.
