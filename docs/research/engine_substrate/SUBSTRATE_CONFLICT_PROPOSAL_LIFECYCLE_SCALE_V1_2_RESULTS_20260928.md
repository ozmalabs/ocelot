# Conflict proposal lifecycle and ambiguity-aware resolution scale v1.2 results

The v1.2 source-locked panel passes all gates in three fresh workers for N=2/4/8/16/32/64 and unique/tied maxima. Proposal generation produces exactly N proposals and edges; repeat closure reaches fixed point with zero progress and graph writes. Unique maxima are selected with no ambiguity flag. Tied maxima reach fixed point with `ambiguous=true` and no selected proposal. The v1.1 silent tie selection is therefore a construction-level gap that existing graph-native Terms can address; no engine change was needed.

Generation remains linear in this fixture: progress is N and candidate tests are 2N. The ambiguity-aware resolver's total Term evaluations are 80, 198, 578, 1,914, 6,890, and 26,058 for unique maxima at N=2 through 64; tied cases use 58, 140, 400, 1,304, 4,648, and 17,480. These counts grow near quadratically over this range. MaxOver evaluations are 3N for unique and 2N for tied, while repeated `Count(Filter(...))` evaluation accounts for substantial additional Term work. Wall-clock values are retained in raw capture but treated as descriptive only.

This result supports a stable ambiguity-aware resolver for this authored proposal graph, and exposes its cost profile. It does not establish an asymptotic bound, natural tie frequency, semantic score quality, learned proposal generation, or tractability of rung-three induction. The benchmark is conditional on this explicit Term composition and current locked build.
