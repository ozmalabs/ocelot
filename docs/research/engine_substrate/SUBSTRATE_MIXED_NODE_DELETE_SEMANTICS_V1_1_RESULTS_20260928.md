# Mixed-topology public node deletion v1.1 results

**Evidence grade:** three fresh source-locked native workers; all 7 runner gates and 8 independent analysis checks pass. The initial runner and first analysis assertion defects are disclosed in the [v1.1 amendment](SUBSTRATE_MIXED_NODE_DELETE_SEMANTICS_V1_1_AMENDMENT_20260928.md), with their captures preserved separately.

Each worker constructs an attributed graph with two incoming edges, three outgoing edges, one self-loop, four outside nodes, and one unrelated edge. Six edge types appear overall; five are incident to the deleted node. In all three workers, public `remove_node` leaves exactly the three surviving outside nodes and the unrelated edge. Every incoming/outgoing/self-loop lookup becomes false, the unrelated lookup remains true, and the post-delete canonical graph exactly matches an independently filtered pre-delete snapshot. The hook contract is one `node_deleted` event and no `edge_deleted` events.

This closes the mixed-type, incoming-edge, self-loop, attribute, and unrelated-edge semantic corner cases for one deterministic fixture. It does not measure timing, active-rule dirty propagation or cursor invalidation, mutation-log output, concurrent readers, or natural workload frequency.

Reproduce with `python3 scripts/substrate_mixed_node_delete_semantics_v1.py --lock`, then the runner and `python3 scripts/analyze_substrate_mixed_node_delete_semantics_v1.py`.
