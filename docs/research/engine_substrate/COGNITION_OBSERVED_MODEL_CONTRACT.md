# Observed world-model and experiment contract

P18 learns only from source-bound observations and independently verified action/outcome receipts. A world owns its transition equations, hidden state, goal and receipt verification. The learner receives ordinary observations, legal action affordances, action records, and observed results; it must never ingest world equations, hidden transition classes, target feature labels, or completed answer hypotheses.

The supported model class is a bounded categorical action-conditioned feature transition. A candidate is generated from repeated matching transitions on distinct objects/episodes, carries all supporting receipts and source/model/frame epochs, and remains a portfolio when observations do not identify one model. A counterexample is retained and disputes the candidate. A model view is usable only while its receipt, owner, source and epochs remain current; unrepresented or irregular dynamics produce abstention.

The independent experiment loop permits the graph to select among legal, funded probes only when current source-grounded candidate predictions disagree. The host/world boundary checks legality and transports the selected action; it does not classify observations, rank hypotheses, or fabricate a result. The world executes the action and returns a receipt verified from its own transition function. That observed result is ingested as new evidence, and a model/prediction must change in value or become disputed, not merely increment an audit version.

The observation-built evidence panel is limited to finite categorical object-mechanism and partially observed temporal worlds. Comparators receive the same observations, legal actions, interaction ceiling and resource allowance: learned-model, held-vocabulary search, passive observation and fixed probe. Structural controls cover ambiguous, missing and irregular observations. A positive claim requires either at least a 20 percentage-point held-out effect/continuation prediction gain or at least 25% fewer interventions with solve loss no greater than two points, plus at least 100 actual simulator-result-linked refinements. Unsupported model classes must abstain.

This contract supports finite simulator consequences only. It does not establish unrestricted causal induction, physical validation, calibrated probabilities, general-purpose scientific discovery or autonomous robotics.
