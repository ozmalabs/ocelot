# Selected-probe adoption transfer v1 — results

**Status:** all 15 independent analysis gates pass across 18 fresh processes.

## Result

The live authenticated selected-probe adoption gate transferred from the earlier square/identity study to two separately authored scalar task families: parity and three-argument clamp. Each family includes a correct candidate on a broad, informative domain, a wrong candidate whose supplied domain aliases it with its challenger, and a wrong candidate on a domain where the selected probe distinguishes the pair.

Both correct broad-domain candidates were adopted in all three repetitions and matched every held-out: parity 41/41 and clamp 21/21. In both alias conditions, the evidence receipt alone was accepted, but the selector measured zero prediction entropy and the live route refused adoption in all six runs. In both distinguishing conditions, the selected input had one bit of prediction entropy; truthful evidence contradicted the candidate, the receipt was rejected, and no shape was adopted in all six runs.

The independent analyzer recomputed all selected probes, HMAC signatures, receipts, and held-out labels/predictions. It verified 18 distinct worker processes, full schedule completion, source/native/seed stability, and all six expected condition outcomes. This is transfer evidence for a bounded gate, not for general hypothesis learning.

## Limits and next step

The panel uses one host, two authored task families, one caller-supplied challenger per case, fixed admissible domains, and a bundled analytic oracle. It does not measure how often natural tasks provide a useful challenger/domain pair, whether the substrate can generate those independently, provider truthfulness, or large-scale learning cost. The adopted construction is still supplied as runnable source; this is guarded example acquisition rather than autonomous invention.

Next, vary challenger-set size and quality, add independently authored task families and noisy/conflicting signed labels, and report correct adoption, false adoption, and refusal under a predeclared task-level deployment rule. Keep the gap between authenticated identity and truthful labeling explicit.
