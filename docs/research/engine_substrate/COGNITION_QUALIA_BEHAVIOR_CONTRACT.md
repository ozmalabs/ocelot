# Qualia behavior and significance contract

P17 closes the behavioral loop from source-bound episode evidence to a later prediction/action proposal and back to an independently verified consequence. A `QualiaDimension` alone is not evidence of behavioral use. A prediction must cite its owner-scoped standing dimension, the fresh feature observation and the acquisition witnesses used to derive it. The consumer requires exact owner, feature key/family, source/model/frame epoch and value-kind agreement. Missing, unknown, stale, contested or wrong-owner inputs produce no proposal; unknown is never converted to a zero-valued feature.

Training episodes provide only observed features, action records, and independently verified outcome receipts. They may not contain target-dimension, expected-class, answer, hidden-action or future-outcome fields. The scorer owns the mapping from a fresh input/action to the observed result. Prediction and action proposals are emitted by native graph rules. A second native rule authorizes only proposals tied to the funded request, current standing axis and an explicit legal-action context; a host adapter may transport that authorization through the P16 world receipt interface but may not classify the input or choose the action.

The frozen arm receives the same number and order of observed episodes and the same compute ceiling, but its learned-axis policies stay disabled. The acquired arm may form a dimension only from recurring source-bound observations and fresh outcome validation. The shuffled arm receives the same evidence volume with feature labels permuted before exposure. The no-experience arm receives no training episodes but receives the same fresh task. All arms see the same fresh task and legal action affordances.

Experiential significance is categorical and context-bound. It is derived only from verified consequences after an action, retains positive and adverse outcome witnesses, and may affect later attention/action proposal ranking only when support recurs across distinct episodes. No fixture supplies a positive significance label to the learner. A counterexample is retained with its source and receipt, immediately contests dependent standing influence, and blocks further action proposals from that axis. A context split proposal requires repeated, verified, context-distinct evidence for both consequence mappings; it does not erase earlier episodes or autonomously promote an unsupported replacement axis.

The registered P17 panel uses 200 fresh later-task worlds for each of `tool_contact` and `social_request_repair`, with acquired, frozen, shuffled and no-experience arms. Each world has equal exposure, action set and compute ceilings across arms. The primary measure is correct later prediction/action against the independent receipt oracle; the registered gain threshold is 20 percentage points for acquired versus each control. Orthogonal and reversal controls have 100 cases per family. Required structural gates are zero wrong-owner/stale-source acceptances, zero actions after axis knockout or contestation, zero target-field leaks, and complete paired allocation. Failure of efficacy stays failure even if candidate/significance-node counts rise.

P17 supports only the finite categorical worlds and externally verified simulator consequences named here. It does not establish real-world qualia, consciousness, human-relative significance, open-world action, calibrated affect, or any capability level.
