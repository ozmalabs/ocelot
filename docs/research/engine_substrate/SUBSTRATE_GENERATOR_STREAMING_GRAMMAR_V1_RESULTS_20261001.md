# Streaming generator conditional-grammar probe v1 (2026-10-01)

The source-locked panel compares eager and streaming candidate-cohort handling across 12 paired workers: triangular and square tasks at grammar size 5/6, Euler at size 4/5, two repetitions each. The operator grammar adds `Lt`, `Gt`, `Eq`, and ternary `IfThenElse` to the arithmetic operators. All workers completed; every arm reached `max_size`; and every pair has exact ordered signature, pool-size, explored-count, distinct-count, and held-out target-support parity. Analyzer source/build and factorial coverage gates pass.

Streaming lowers Python-traced peak allocation in every grouped cell: median eager/streaming ratios range from 1.09× to 3.83×. Its median wall ratios remain near one (eager/streaming 0.87×–1.02× by group), with two paired repeats too few to infer a small speed difference. This supports lower temporary Python allocation through a ternary cohort but does not support a latency improvement. The tracer excludes native Rust allocations and affects absolute times; the workload remains three authored task adapters under one expanded grammar.
