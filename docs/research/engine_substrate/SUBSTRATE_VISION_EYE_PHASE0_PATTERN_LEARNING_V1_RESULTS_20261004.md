# Phase-0 spatial pattern learning transfer v1 results — 2026-10-04

The source-locked runner and independent analyzer pass all gates on 12 fresh graphs. With stable 5×5 burst patches, receptive fields localize at both `(5,5)` and `(12,12)` in all three initialization seeds: 6/6 cases have at least one unit with spread below 3 cells and centroid within 1 cell of the target. The temporally shuffled controls preserve exactly 25 active cells per step but localize in 0/6 cases.

Independent analysis recomputes receptive-field measures from the stored 256 synapse weights per unit. Median best-unit spread is 2.00 cells for fixed inputs and 6.48 for shuffled inputs; fixed-input best-unit centers are numerically at the target centers, while shuffled best-unit centers remain near the retinal center. This is evidence that the authored native rule path can acquire a location-specific receptive field from a stable activity pattern and does not do so from matched-count spatially shuffled activity on this panel.

Median native closure time per step is 7.29 ms (fixed) and 7.14 ms (shuffled). Input updates differ: stable arms perform a median 25 attribute writes total, while shuffled arms perform 180,427; median fact-update time is 4.24 μs versus 113 μs per step. These are one-host descriptive timings. The assay uses an authored 16×16 retinal graph and direct RetinaCell burst facts, not natural images or the complete production sensory path. It measures a selected learning mechanism, not self-improvement or general induction.
