# Recurrence budget calibration v1 — capture amendment

The first v1 training and held-out captures are retained as a censored pilot, not a valid completed gate. The independent analyzer found that the runner supplied candidate descriptors using `candidate_id` while `select_candidate_from_graph_evidence` requires `proposal_id`; consequently every training selection abstained. The first standalone analyzer also lacked the local Rust binding path. The per-candidate closure outcomes remain useful pilot observations, but they cannot support the preregistered selection claim.

A corrected v1.1 runner maps opaque candidate IDs into the selector's required proposal descriptor schema. Its protocol, runner, analyzer, source/native lock, orders, and scores are frozen as new versioned artifacts before the confirmatory capture. The v1 pilot files remain unchanged.
