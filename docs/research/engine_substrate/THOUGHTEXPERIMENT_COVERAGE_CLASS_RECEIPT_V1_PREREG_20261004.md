# Thoughtexperiment coverage-class receipt v1 preregistration

## Question

Can a coverage-qualified behavior-class decision from the frozen policy study
be recorded inside a shared-page CoW thoughtexperiment branch and returned to
the primary graph as a sparse, revision-checked receipt without changing
primary state before return?

## Frozen cases and procedure

Use only the five panels where the source-locked coverage-aware policy chose
`behavior_class`. Build each primary graph from the panel's initial candidate
Terms, fork a shared-page snapshot, and journal a new class receipt plus links
to its returned candidate nodes. Require the frozen policy analysis to mark
all returned candidates exact on its declared 6,560-case holdout before
constructing the return. Extract the `derived_only` delta and apply it to the
unchanged primary at its captured graph/rule revision.

Capture full primary-before, branch-after, and primary-after graph states so
an independent analyzer can recompute the receipt diff. Require the primary
to remain unchanged before return, branch/primary graph parity after return,
all rule slots unchanged, exact receipt membership, and stale-revision retry
rejection with no further primary mutation. Record fork, journal, extraction,
and apply wall times as descriptive measurements only.

## Limits

The class decisions and candidate pool are inherited from a frozen authored
numeric benchmark. This measures transport and isolation of an accepted
finite-class receipt through the CoW graph lifecycle; it does not repeat
induction, establish natural-task coverage, or authorize persistent rule
activation. Five panels on one host are a mechanism demonstration, not a
reliable throughput estimate. No production state is modified.
