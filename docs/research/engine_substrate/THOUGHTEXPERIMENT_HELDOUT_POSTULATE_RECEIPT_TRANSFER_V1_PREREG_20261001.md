# Held-out postulate receipt transfer v1 preregistration

**Question.** Does the identity-corrected `scale_cancellation` shape transfer invariantly through the actual `synthesize_gaps()` operator onto a separate held postulate in its source Microtheory?

## Fixture and comparison

Run three fresh full-seed workers. Use the corpus-derived `batch_normalization` two-owner collision and perform the same controlled ownership-row rewire in Direct and receipt branches. Select the distinct existing held Concept `sift` from the `computer_vision` Microtheory as the held-out postulate. Harvest the `scale_cancellation` shape from each branch. Call `synthesize_gaps()` at order 1, with the explicit one-shape library, `record=False`, `prune=False`, `substantive_structural=True`, and zero-hop structural scope. Also run a surface-only control with `substantive_structural=False` on each arm.

## Gates

Require three fresh PIDs; unique `sift` postulate and stable fixture identities; source/seed fingerprints stable; exact canonical graph parity; exact harvested shape parity with one source owner candidate; `sift` lineage contains `computer_vision`; Direct and receipt structural gap outputs equal and include a derivation through `scale_cancellation`; both surface-only controls exclude that derivation. No timings.

## Limits

This tests a separate authored postulate through the production synthesis operator but uses a controlled owner-edge rewire and disables openness pruning to isolate derivation. It is evidence of source-lineage transfer behavior, not evidence the derived capability is novel, useful, or improves held-out task performance.
