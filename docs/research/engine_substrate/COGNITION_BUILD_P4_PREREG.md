### D2/D3 specification decisions

Numeric shape signatures use the frozen eight-coordinate legacy contract in `COGNITION_SIGNATURE_CONTRACT.md`: ordered forward differences, ordinal ranks, zero padding, full source-coordinate retention, and rejection of malformed/non-finite/out-of-bound inputs. Outcome ids make signed evidence idempotent; negative evidence cannot ground a reading. Equal best candidates stay unresolved until exact verification.

Signature descriptors are schema-versioned and owner/source/frame/epoch scoped. Missing is distinct from zero, dimension identity and value kind must agree, and stale source epochs invalidate projections. Temporal windows contain only measured frames, require stable owner/window identity and regular sampling, and refuse irregular or synthesized frames.
