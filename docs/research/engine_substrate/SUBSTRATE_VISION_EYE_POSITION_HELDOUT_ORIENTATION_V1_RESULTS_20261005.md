# Position-held-out orientation representation v1 results

The source-locked capture and independent analyzer passed all schedule, source, geometry, freeze, and held-out split checks across six fresh workers. Each worker completed 9,600 balanced training presentations (400 per orientation at each of 12 positions), then recorded native outputs for 32 masks at all 16 positions. Horizontal and vertical masks each contain five pixels and share the exact centroid at every location. The four corner positions were excluded from learning and from readout fitting.

The preregistered external nearest-class-mean readout scored **31/48** on held-out positions after phase-0 learning, versus **29/48** using the initial random WUnit weights. By seed, learned versus initial scores were 6/8 vs 6/8, 4/8 vs 5/8, 5/8 vs 4/8, 7/8 vs 6/8, 5/8 vs 4/8, and 4/8 vs 4/8. Learning improved three seeds, declined one, and tied two; aggregate correct-only vs initial-only probe outcomes were 8 vs 6. This is a small, inconsistent difference and does not establish reliable position-invariant orientation learning. Centroid-only and active-count-only tie-breaks each score 24/48, as expected. A translation-aware raw-pixel template scores 48/48, confirming that the labels are recoverable from the bar geometry.

The result measures transfer utility in WUnit activations with an external decoder. Orientation labels are not installed into the graph, and no learned in-substrate classifier was evaluated. The direct RetinaCell burst interface bypasses natural-image perception. This is evidence against claiming that the previously measured retinotopic map already supplies a strong transferable orientation representation under this setup; it does not rule out other architecture settings or representations.

A post-hoc seed-cluster uncertainty analysis finds three seeds improved, one declined, and two tied. The exact two-sided sign-test result is `p=0.625`; the percentile interval from all 46,656 six-seed bootstrap resamples for the mean learned-minus-initial accuracy difference is `[-4.2, 10.4]` percentage points. Paired probe outcomes are 8 learned-only correct, 6 initial-only correct, 23 both correct, and 11 both incorrect. These data do not resolve a learning benefit. Probe outcomes are not treated as independent replicates. See the locked [uncertainty protocol](SUBSTRATE_VISION_EYE_POSITION_HELDOUT_ORIENTATION_V1_1_UNCERTAINTY_PROTOCOL_20261005.md), [analyzer](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/analyze_vision_eye_position_heldout_orientation_v1_1_uncertainty.py), and [report](../../../evaluation/reports/vision_eye_position_heldout_orientation_v1_1_uncertainty_20261005.json).

Training median closure time was **8.26 ms per step** across workers. This is a small one-host synthetic panel; it is not a general learning rate, natural-task estimate, or self-improvement result. The next learning gate should improve or replace the unsupervised representation path and repeat a preregistered factorial panel with broader positions/orientations and a decoder fixed in advance.
