# Thoughtexperiment blinded proposal utility v1 — fixture admission audit

**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Decision:** no-go for confirmatory capture; four task contexts now have candidate-utility evidence, but common panel worker, blinding, and full-cost requirements remain unmet.

## Audit finding

Before the navigation integration, the ranking and experience-transfer reports established two authored correction-task families:

1. **Shape-axis correction:** repair an authored rule that repeatedly creates duplicate axes. The graph-authored ranking study found that structural-cost-first ordering tries an unsuccessful signature-to-residual edit before the successful `dedup_identity_fix` ([ranking results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_GRAPH_AUTHORED_CORRECTION_RANK_V1_RESULTS_20261001.md)).
2. **Recurrence-offer idempotence:** repair an authored rule so each candidate receives exactly one offer. The v2 cross-family study creates a task where the episode-scoped guard suppresses the second valid offer and the candidate-scoped repair succeeds ([transfer results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_CROSS_FAMILY_IDEMPOTENCE_TRANSFER_V2_RESULTS_20261001.md)).

The automatic outcome persistence and contamination studies reuse these two task families. The v1 cross-family transfer uses the recurrence task where both cold and experience arms already choose the successful candidate first, so it measures evidence consumption but no utility gain. V2 creates a measured two-to-one trial reduction, but still uses the same two families, an explicitly authored shared `idempotent_mint_guard` class, a host-supplied candidate space, and a host-created training transfer. The v2 runner enumerated eight candidates but stopped rehearsing after the first successful candidate; a later admission audit scores the complete generated set. These reports cannot be counted as four independent evaluation families.

The recurrence admission audit now confirms eight stable, distinct candidate descriptors from the unchanged generator, scored on disjoint fresh training and held-out episodes across three workers. `dedup_identity_fix` passes both splits in all workers; the other seven variants fail held-out utility. Its independent review passes 12/12 gates. This closes the per-family candidate-diversity and utility-separation check for recurrence, but does not establish ranking benefit or complete cost ([admission results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_RECURRENCE_CANDIDATE_ADMISSION_V1_RESULTS_20261002.md), [protocol](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_RECURRENCE_CANDIDATE_ADMISSION_V1_PREREG_20261002.md), [runner](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_recurrence_candidate_admission_v1.py), [lock](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_recurrence_candidate_admission_v1_lock_20261002.json), [raw](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_recurrence_candidate_admission_v1_raw_20261002.json), [review](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_recurrence_candidate_admission_v1_analysis_review_20261002.json)).

A new source-locked grid-navigation polarity study adds a third independently task-shaped repair fixture: comparison directions are fault-injected in `hop_descend_gradient`, the graph-authored composer chooses `flip_comparison` from a residual cue, and an independent native action oracle scores four held-out directions. The candidate set has six variants, with only the comparison flip passing (4/4 per worker); three fresh workers pass all 16 corrected review gates ([results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_NAVIGATION_POLARITY_REPAIR_V1_1_RESULTS_20261002.md)). This is useful candidate-utility evidence, but it exercises the graph-authored structural composer, not outcome-conditioned `rank_candidates`; the task cue explicitly includes `flip`, and the fixed candidate generator also places that variant first. It therefore does not yet supply a utility advantage or outcome-ranking transfer cell for the blinded ranker panel.

A follow-up source/native-locked integration now exercises the production graph-resident rank Program on the navigation candidates. Across three fresh workers, disjoint Up/Left training outcomes move `flip_comparison` ahead of the cold structural-cost choice for held-out Down/Right cases. Experience rank uses one trial versus two for cold rank, but ties default order, which already tries the successful flip first. The independent reviewer passes 13/13 outcome and integrity checks. The runner omitted preregistered per-policy ordering latency; an amendment discloses this, and no economic comparison is claimed. This establishes candidate-specific outcome use on a third task context, not a gain over default, cross-family transfer, or autonomous outcome collection ([integration results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_NAVIGATION_OUTCOME_RANK_INTEGRATION_V1_RESULTS_20261002.md), [preregistration](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_NAVIGATION_OUTCOME_RANK_INTEGRATION_V1_PREREG_20261002.md), [amendment](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_NAVIGATION_OUTCOME_RANK_INTEGRATION_V1_AMENDMENT_20261002.md), [runner](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_navigation_outcome_rank_integration_v1.py), [lock](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_navigation_outcome_rank_integration_v1_lock_20261002.json), [raw](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_navigation_outcome_rank_integration_v1_raw_20261002.json), [review](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_navigation_outcome_rank_integration_v1_analysis_review_20261002.json)).

A fourth independently task-shaped candidate-utility fixture now comes from the `control_kinds` seed. The source-locked task faults the effect-side string-match comparator in `classify_kind_family`. Six candidates are generated; only `flip_comparison` passes both held-out control-name classifications, while the original rule passes all four train/held-out cases and the fault fails all four. Three workers pass 14/14 independent checks. This is a bounded lexical task whose expected labels are determined by the supplied `FamilySignal` table; it establishes a fourth task context, not natural-language generalization or autonomous diagnosis ([results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_CONTROL_KIND_CLASSIFICATION_REPAIR_V1_RESULTS_20261002.md), [protocol](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_CONTROL_KIND_CLASSIFICATION_REPAIR_V1_PREREG_20261002.md), [runner](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_control_kind_classification_repair_v1.py), [lock](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_control_kind_classification_repair_v1_lock_20261002.json), [raw](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_control_kind_classification_repair_v1_raw_20261002.json), [review](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_control_kind_classification_repair_v1_analysis_review_20261002.json)).

The source-locked candidate-surface inventory adds an important scope check: the standard core fixture has 159 active rules and 545 syntactic candidate instances, but only `sd_mint_quantum` receives the task-shaped `dedup_identity_fix`. The other instances are generic edge deletions, match guards, comparison flips, and attribute-key substitutions. Thus the obstacle is not merely finding enough syntactic variants; validated task utility is concentrated in one rule family. The inventory is read-only and provides no evidence that those generic variants solve useful tasks ([candidate-surface results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_CORRECTION_CANDIDATE_SURFACE_V1_RESULTS_20261001.md)).

I re-executed its worker once against the current checkout. It again enumerated 159 rules and 545 candidates, matched the earlier per-rule inventory exactly, and preserved the source graph and rules. However, `domains/self_correction.py` no longer matches the v1 capture's source hash. Therefore this is a current one-process diagnostic, not a replacement three-worker source-locked inventory; a current three-worker refresh should accompany the later fixture-admission lock.

## Ranker transfer-interface audit

Before the panel lock, the production ranker source shows that prior outcomes match by exact target/label, then by an explicit `outcome_class` when one exists. Shape-axis and recurrence share the authored `idempotent_mint_guard` class; navigation and control-kind have no cross-family class and use distinct target rules. Under the preregistered other-family-only experience arm, the latter two families should have the same outcome counts as frozen rank. Keep the current class assignments fixed and report this as a scope boundary; assigning new classes after seeing results would invalidate the panel.

A source-locked, two-stage control-kind probe confirms this null-transfer prediction. It provides all eight recurrence training records to the actual graph ranker; each control-kind candidate receives zero prior counts and experience order equals cold rank in all three workers. Both rankers put a failing low-cost attribute swap first, while default order puts the held-out-successful comparison flip first. A separate scoring process evaluates the exact serialized bundles only after order capture; independent review passes 20/20 checks ([results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_CROSS_FAMILY_RANKER_NULL_TRANSFER_V1_2_RESULTS_20261002.md), [v1.2 amendment](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_CROSS_FAMILY_RANKER_NULL_TRANSFER_V1_2_AMENDMENT_20261002.md), [runner](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_cross_family_ranker_null_transfer_v1_2.py), [lock](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_cross_family_ranker_null_transfer_v1_2_lock_20261002.json), [order capture](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_cross_family_ranker_null_transfer_v1_2_orders_20261002.json), [scores](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_cross_family_ranker_null_transfer_v1_2_scores_20261002.json), [review](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_cross_family_ranker_null_transfer_v1_2_analysis_review_20261002.json)). This confirms that shared task/outcome labels are an operative limit of the ranker rather than merely a theoretical concern.

## Shape-axis full-candidate follow-up

A source-locked four-worker follow-up now scores all six unchanged-generator candidates on disjoint train and held-out observation signatures. Two variants pass the direct held-out graph oracle in all workers: dedup_identity_fix and swap_term_leaf:key->signature; the other four fail. This repairs the 2/6 utility-coverage gap in the earlier stop-on-success trace, but remains four replications of one authored task form, not a fifth independent family or a transfer result ([results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_SHAPE_AXIS_FULL_CANDIDATE_ADMISSION_V1_RESULTS_20261002.md), [protocol](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_SHAPE_AXIS_FULL_CANDIDATE_ADMISSION_V1_PREREG_20261002.md), [runner](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_shape_axis_full_candidate_admission_v1.py), [lock](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_shape_axis_full_candidate_admission_v1_lock_20261002.json), [raw capture](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_shape_axis_full_candidate_admission_v1_raw_20261002.json), [review](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_shape_axis_full_candidate_admission_v1_review_20261002.json)).

## Five-policy control-kind integration pilot

A separate four-worker pilot now runs experience rank, frozen rank, default, seeded random, and target memoization with orders written before held-out scoring. Independent review passes 14/14 checks. Experience receives 20 train-only records from the other three families; target-mismatched records leave its six candidates at zero priors, so experience equals frozen rank and needs two trials in all workers. Default and the same-target memo choose the held-out-successful flip first; random needs 1, 3, 5, and 1 attempts. All arms eventually succeed. The captured timings include fresh setup and scoring and omit isolated adoption costs, so no economic win is claimed. This is one family and does not substitute for the four-family panel ([results](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_FIVE_POLICY_CONTROL_KIND_PILOT_V1_RESULTS_20261002.md), [protocol](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_FIVE_POLICY_CONTROL_KIND_PILOT_V1_PREREG_20261002.md), [runner](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_five_policy_control_kind_pilot_v1.py), [lock](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/thoughtexperiment_five_policy_control_kind_pilot_v1_lock_20261002.json), [orders](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_five_policy_control_kind_pilot_v1_orders_20261002.json), [scores](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_five_policy_control_kind_pilot_v1_scores_20261002.json), [review](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_five_policy_control_kind_pilot_v1_review_20261002.json)).
