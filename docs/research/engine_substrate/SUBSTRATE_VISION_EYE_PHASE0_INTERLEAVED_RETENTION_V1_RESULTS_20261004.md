# Phase-0 visual interleaved retention v1 results

The source-locked capture passed its six-worker, source-stability, exact-exposure, active-count, progress, and Argmax-cache gates. The preregistered retention outcome did not pass: blocked acquisition retained both 5×5 locations in 3/3 seeds, while one-step alternation retained both in only 1/3. The two interleaved failures (seeds 1 and 2) ended with best receptive-field spread about 5.33 cells and centers several cells from either target; the passing seed localized both with 2-cell spread. Independent scoring recomputed all receptive-field metrics from the captured 256 weights per unit.

Median closure time was similar (7.14 ms/step blocked, 7.17 ms/step interleaved). Input-update cost was not similar: blocked schedules changed the active patch only twice (75 total cell writes per worker), while one-step alternation changed 50 cells on nearly every step (399,975 writes) and took median 0.127 ms/step versus 0.0048 ms/step. Thus the learning contrast is valid at equal exposure counts, but the treatment also introduces repeated input transitions and much higher host-side update work. This result indicates temporal switching sensitivity in this particular phase-0 rule setup; it does not establish general interference, capacity, natural-image learning, or self-improvement. A follow-up should vary alternation block length and separately control/measure transition count before attributing the effect to forgetting versus input-transition dynamics.
