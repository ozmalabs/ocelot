# GenMint process-restart reuse v1.1 analysis amendment

**Status:** analysis/capture-schema amendment, no change to task cells, arms, repeats, held-out inputs, success criteria, or runtime implementation.

The first 90-worker capture completed with no worker failures, but the initial analyzer expected matrix/layout/arm/replicate labels at the row top level while the runner retained them in the nested training record and randomized schedule. That analyzer did not produce a valid final report. The raw attempt is preserved as `genmint_process_restart_reuse_v1_initial_pilot_20260930.json` with its corresponding initial lock. The confirmatory run was repeated after binding labels from each training record to the preregistered schedule; all 90 workers completed under the refreshed lock.

A second analysis clarification separates training acquisition from restart persistence. Enabled training can report `recognized_transformed` without acquiring the exact intended matrix, as already observed in the v3 matrix-family study. Such a cell is counted as an acquisition failure, not as a restart failure or successful persistence. The persistence route gate applies to every held-out call only when the exact training-acquired generator exists. An independent amended analyzer also distinguishes that route from coincidentally correct predictions produced by another generator.

The original randomized panel and checkpoint protocol are unchanged. The final capture preserves the source/native lock, exact 90-cell schedule, and fresh consumer process IDs. The v1.1 analysis reports the acquired-generator conditional score separately from all other routes and keeps the one failed acquisition stratum visible. This amendment supports the corresponding [v1.1 analysis](../../../evaluation/reports/genmint_process_restart_reuse_v1_1_analysis_20260930.json); the [final raw capture](../../../evaluation/reports/genmint_process_restart_reuse_v1_20260930.json) and [lock](../../../evaluation/substrate_capability/genmint_process_restart_reuse_v1_lock_20260930.json) remain the authoritative execution artifacts.
