# Harvest-shape receipt lineage v2 preregistration

**Question.** After source attribution is keyed by the selected Concept/Rule node identity, does Direct versus receipt adoption preserve harvested lineage metadata and structural transfer output?

## Method

Reuse the v1 full-boot `batch_normalization` ownership rewire and actual `harvest_shapes()` plus `differentiate()` path. Run three fresh workers. Compare complete `scale_cancellation` shape objects, including source-node identity and owner candidate fields, and compare `differentiate()` output under the same empty text and machine-learning-at-hop-zero lineage. Also require exact canonical graph equality and capture stable source/seed fingerprints.

## Gates

Require distinct PIDs, stable selected owner/target IDs across workers, exact canonical graph equality per worker, identical Direct/receipt target shapes, identical nonempty Direct/receipt structural transfer results, stable source fingerprints, and the exact selected donor's owner resolution (the selected source Concept node has one owner candidate). No timings.

## Limits

The branch rewire is controlled and the postulate lineage is a fixed structural counterfactual. Passing establishes receipt-invariant provenance and firing on this corpus-derived source. It is not a natural-task performance result or a general proof of every ownership relation.
