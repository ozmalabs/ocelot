# Thoughtexperiment recurrence graph-selection v1.1 — results

**Candidate-selection subgate passes; full preregistered tractability gate fails.** Four source/native-locked workers independently expose the same eight candidates. For each candidate and worker, the runner executes four fresh training episodes; the sample unit is one recurrence episode containing two due keys. The native Term selects `dedup_identity_fix` as the unique candidate with four correct training episodes in all four workers. After orders freeze, the chosen candidate passes all four held-out episodes per worker. The seven alternatives score below the training maximum and each fails at least one held-out episode. The corrected analysis passes 15/16 checks and identifies one specific failure: all candidate episodes do not reach fixed point within the 100-call budget.

The closure failure is concentrated in five alternatives: `drop_match_edge` and four `swap_term_leaf` variants hit the `run_rules(100)` cap in all 16 training and 16 held-out episodes each. That is 80 capped runs in each split, 160/256 candidate episodes total. Two other alternatives and the selected `dedup_identity_fix` do reach fixed point; one of those alternatives still fails the offer-count oracle. A cap hit means progress remained at the runner's work limit; it is not proof of nontermination. The selected candidate reaches fixed point throughout and preserves exactly one offer for each due key.

Mean graph evidence aggregation plus native-Term selection was 0.801 ms per worker; candidate enumeration averaged 109 ms per worker; corrected training and held-out candidate episode runtimes average 459 ms and 467 ms. An earlier analysis divided the worker-aggregated time by the total across four workers, understating the per-episode values by four; the raw capture is unchanged ([correction](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_RECURRENCE_EARLY_REJECTION_V1_AMENDMENT_20261002.md), [corrected analysis](../../../evaluation/reports/thoughtexperiment_recurrence_graph_selection_v1_3_analysis_20261002.json)). These small synthetic-fixture timings include boot and multiple closure sweeps and do not establish general scaling. The full gate's fixed-point requirement remains failed; the closure cap behavior is itself a tractability finding for poor proposals, and should drive the next engine work toward termination-aware candidate budgets and better early rejection.

The sample unit is corrected explicitly in the protocol: one fresh recurrence episode contains two separately keyed due candidates, with four train and four held-out episodes per candidate/worker. The source lock and order/score hashes bind the capture. The amended analysis separates selection success from closure-within-budget failure ([analysis amendment](THOUGHTEXPERIMENT_RECURRENCE_GRAPH_SELECTION_V1_1_AMENDMENT_20261002.md)).
