# Open process search and strategy contract

P19 searches over a bounded typed expression grammar: `Compose`, `Inverse`, integer `Power`, state guards, and explicit subgoal nodes. The current native proposal seed implements `Compose`, `Inverse`, `Power(2|3)`, exact observed-initial-state guards, and the intermediate state of a composition as its subgoal. Other guard predicates, recursive depth expansion, and reusable subgoal ordering remain incomplete; the bounded `max_depth` is a policy field, not evidence those constructors are implemented. Operator arity/type checks derive from held process nodes; candidate names are labels only and never dispatch a recipe. Every attempt, including invalid or unsupported expressions, consumes the same shared search budget and remains in the work ledger.

The supported exact evaluator is the native finite process algebra from P16. A candidate stores its full expression tree, exact effect fingerprint, parent process IDs, source/model/frame epochs, observed-state guard, subgoal state, work cost and verification result. The P16 executor's verifier-side digest receipt hashes those graph-authored fields and parent source identities; it excludes the candidate display name. The candidate-specific human approval binds that digest, tree, parameters, finite effect, guard and current domain epochs. The executor recomputes the digest and native effect before every world transition, then records the observed receipt. Unknown or invalid composition never becomes an identity. Model-based transitions remain separate from exact finite algebra and require current P18 model receipts.

The P19.03 addition currently lifts an observed ordered trace to a different state and goal only when object count and source/model/frame/probe epochs match; each step remains checked by native process application. This establishes one bounded unseen-state transfer fixture, not learned subgoal order, object-count generalization, or long-horizon strategy search. Those behaviors still require implementation and evidence. Exact-context replay remains a comparator, not evidence of strategy generalization. Temporarily worsening paths are not pruned by the trace executor, but successful temporary-regression transfer is not yet demonstrated.

Novel operators continue through expression-digest, proof, candidate-specific approval, adoption and rollback gates. No historical approval transfers to a new expression. The opt-in P19 adoption policy consumes only a human approval bound to the current candidate, and revocation or any expression/parameter/guard/domain change aborts dependent tasks. An unapproved proposal may be evaluated in a non-actionable local verification context, but cannot reach a world transition until the exact adoption record and independent action authorization both pass.

P19 evidence covers finite permutation effects and irreversible object/tool constraints only. A supported novelty claim requires a verified held-out non-template expression and strategy use under an unseen binding. Results must report work/cost frontiers and every failed search. This contract does not claim unbounded synthesis or general reasoning.
