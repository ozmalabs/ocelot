# P28 representation decision

Status: **retain the existing representation; do not promote the Eq. 4 frame.**

## Evidence

The frozen [P28 results ledger](COGNITION_OUSIOMETRIC_RESULTS_V1.md) reports
the complete 2,400-row allocation over 200 paired seeds in each of two
registered simulator families. The sole primary contrast, Eq. 4 rotation
versus shared basis under the fixed L1 condition, had a mean AUC difference of
zero in both families, 99% paired-bootstrap intervals [0, 0], and
Holm-adjusted one-sided paired sign-flip p-values of 1.0. The preregistered
benefit criterion failed in both families. Every control arm had the same
outcome curve per seed as the primary arms.

The adverse score-outcome rates were equal between primary arms. The raw
receipt audit also found identical all-action adverse rates between these
arms, including target-calibration receipts. That risk comparison is specific
to the simulator and does not establish real-world safety. Native runtime and
source provenance passed, full-rank geometry controls passed, failures and
abstentions were retained, and the existing future-manifold/native-stop
boundary remained intact.

There is no frozen lexical checkpoint or token-to-concept bridge, so external
lexical scores were not opened. The frame names therefore describe a
published rounded rotation of shared numeric coordinates; they do not provide
compatible psychological calibration or show that a new coordinate contains
new information.

## Decision

Keep the current substrate representation and its default behavior. Do not
enable or globally promote the Eq. 4/PDS-labelled frame, change the default
metric, or claim a representation benefit. The registered result gives no
bounded-basis utility in these worlds and provides no basis for attributing
the exact outcome parity to a semantic coordinate system.

The low social-repair success rate limits sensitivity in that family. It does
not turn the zero contrast into a positive or general result. Further work is
justified only by a separately frozen experiment with a concrete supported
feature-to-axis calibration or a task where the existing verifier exposes a
measurable representation-dependent decision. Any optional local frame would
remain disabled unless that new evidence passes its own cost, compatibility,
behavior, and risk gates.

This is a bounded negative decision, not proof that no representation change
could help in other environments. It does not update any L1–L7 capability
level or close P22's independent multi-generation lineage, resource, freeze,
or held-out evaluation gates.
