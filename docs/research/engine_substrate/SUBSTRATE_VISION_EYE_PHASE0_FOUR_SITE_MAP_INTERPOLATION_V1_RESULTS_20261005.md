# Phase-0 four-site map and held-out interpolation v1 results

The source-locked capture passed all schedule, runtime, and freeze gates: three new seeds completed 16,000 one-step training closures each, with exactly 4,000 exposures at each of four locations; all 80 probes per worker ran with weights unchanged. Independent analysis reconstructed all 240 activation vectors and all 240 native winners from captured weights and masks.

The intended four-site representation did not emerge reliably. Seeds 12 and 14 localized receptive fields to only two of the four trained sites; seed 13 localized none. Across the 60 trained-site probes, only 20 winners met the exact localization criterion (<1-cell center error, <3-cell spread). Across all 180 held-out-site probes, zero winners met it. A weaker nearest-trained-region score was correct on 50/180 held-out probes (27.8%), with median winner-field distance 4.61 cells and spread 6.42 cells. This is substantially below the two-site result and shows that the 2-site success does not scale directly to four sites under the same 4-unit, one-step, eta=0.005 regime.

Median closure cost was 6.96 ms/step and median input-update cost about 0.060 ms/step during training. The weak outcome is therefore not explained by a large closure-time increase; the failure is in learned representation quality under this schedule. It does not prove a hard two-location capacity limit: site count, per-site exposure, sequential competition, learning rate, and schedule are entangled. The next gate should vary site count and schedule with paired seeds, retaining a two-site positive control and per-site field ownership measures.
