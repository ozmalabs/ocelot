# Phase-0 spatial feature geometry transfer v1 results

The source-locked capture passed all runtime and schedule checks for six workers: three fresh seeds (6, 7, 8) each completed both conditions, exact 8,000-step schedules, equal 4,000 exposures per location, expected active counts/write counts, progress, and cache checks. The independent analyzer recomputed centers and RMS spreads from all captured weights; source, runner, and analyzer integrity checks pass.

The 5×5 solid-patch control localized both sites in 3/3 seeds. The mixed-geometry arm—3×3 filled square at `(5,5)` and five-cell cross at `(12,12)`—also localized both sites in 3/3. The target-centered fields' observed spreads were exactly 1.155 cells for the small square and 0.894 for the cross in every seed, matching the uniform input masks' analytically expected RMS spreads. This shape-spread comparison is a post-capture exploratory diagnostic, not a preregistered pass criterion; the amendment states its calculation. The result supports local learning of both position and the spatial second-moment scale for two distinct activity geometries in this fixed task.

Median closure time was 7.03 ms/step for the solid control and 7.45 ms/step for mixed geometry. The mixed arm used fewer input writes because its two patterns contained fewer active cells, so its lower input-update time is not evidence of better efficiency at matched workload. This is not a shape classifier: the centers are supplied by the task and scoring checks receptive-field localization, not identity recognition. It does not demonstrate semantic class transfer, natural-image capability, or general induction.
