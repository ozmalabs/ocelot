# Phase-0 sequential location acquisition and retention v1 results — 2026-10-04

All source and independent-analysis gates pass for six workers: three initialization seeds crossed with both location orders. Every stage uses 4,000 closures and exactly 25 active retinal cells. After stage 1, all six populations localize the first trained position. After stage 2, every population localizes both positions, regardless of order: acquisition of the second location is 6/6 and retention of the first is 6/6. Independently recomputed best-unit spread is 2.00 cells at both positions, with centers numerically at the requested patch centers.

Median closure time is 7.21 ms per step in stage 1 and 7.30 ms per step in stage 2. This indicates no large measured closure-cost increase from retaining two learned locations in this small fixture, but it is one host and one rule family. The result supports bounded sequential acquisition and retention of two spatial patterns; it does not establish larger codebook capacity, natural-image recognition, robustness to noisy/interleaved distributions, or general induction.
