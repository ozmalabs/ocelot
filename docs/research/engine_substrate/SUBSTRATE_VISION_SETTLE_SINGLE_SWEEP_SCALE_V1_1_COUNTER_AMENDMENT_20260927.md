# Vision settling single-sweep scale v1.1 counter amendment

The original v1 driver compared the full scale-audit snapshot byte-for-byte. Inspection of its preserved raw capture shows that the only varying global field is `deferred_queue:fb_samples:drain_ns`, a measured duration. All rule candidate, Term evaluation, and clause counters are exact across the three workers. V1.1 compares the complete per-rule and per-clause counters and every global counter except keys ending in `_ns`; the excluded duration remains in the raw capture and is summarized as timing evidence. No input, workload size, seed, application, or semantic gate changes. The size cap at `n_side=10` remains as documented in [the size amendment](SUBSTRATE_VISION_SETTLE_SINGLE_SWEEP_SCALE_V1_1_AMENDMENT_20260927.md).
