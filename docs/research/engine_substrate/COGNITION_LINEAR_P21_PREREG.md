# Linear P21 preregistration — resource-bounded retention and self-curriculum

**Protocol:** `linear-p21-v1`  
**Frozen against parent commit:** `f9212d28`  
**Status:** prospective; no held-out rows have been accessed at freeze.  
**Purpose:** test whether native offline sleep improves retention beyond replay-only under charged memory and whether residual-selected curriculum improves delayed novel-task learning over equal random and fixed selectors. The study uses deterministic simulator worlds and does not establish broad cognition.

## Frozen questions and criteria

Sleep primary: paired mean normalized delayed learning-curve AUC for full sleep minus replay-only, across all three fixed capacities. AUC uses the fixed 0/1/2 target-interaction checkpoints and trapezoidal integration normalized by the two interaction intervals. It passes at a gain of at least `0.10` with new-task accuracy loss no greater than `0.02`. Curriculum primary: paired delayed novel-task accuracy for residual selection minus the mean of random and fixed selection, at the same capacity/workload. It passes at a gain of at least `0.10`. The two primaries are classified independently. A valid negative estimate is `FAIL`; an incomplete or structurally invalid panel is `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. No subgroup or resource-based rescue is permitted.

## Worlds, arms and allocation

Sleep streams acquire two observed thermal-transition receipts, enter the idle interval, receive eight real latch-interference actions, undergo the assigned native lifecycle, then retrieve evidence into a fresh P20 native learner before a delayed unseen thermal task. The four paired arms are full sleep, replay-only, matched-compute awake rehearsal and no offline treatment. Every arm uses the same byte-charged memory adapter, eviction rules, fresh learner, retrieval boundary and primitive-action ceiling. Source receipts cannot be read from a retained host copy. The awake comparator solves real native tasks and must remain within five percent of full-sleep native rule steps.

Curriculum streams use the same three eligible evidence items and native task bridge in residual, randomized-rank and fixed-rank modes. Each generated task executes two authorized actions in a fresh thermal world; observed receipts train the native learner before the delayed probe. Paired arms must match target actions and native rule steps. Capacities are 2,048, 4,096 and 8,192 serialized bytes, fixed before treatment.

The frozen held-out draw contains 200 sleep streams and 200 curriculum streams at each capacity: 2,400 sleep arm rows and 1,800 curriculum arm rows total, plus 100 structural controls. Development readiness used two streams per capacity per panel and two controls. Exact allocation content and hashes are embedded below.

## Resource accounting and controls

Every sleep row records the charged memory audit, evictions, interference actions, retrieval and decode work, world receipts, native rule steps, shared-budget debit, graph footprint and elapsed time. Curriculum rows record selected task, observed training receipts, target actions, native rule work, remaining budget and graph bytes. Controls exercise over-capacity rejection and unavailable reads and verify answer-field isolation. Hidden answer/source copies, sealed evaluation IDs or leaked labels, capacity read-through, missing receipts, unmatched work and unauthorized actions invalidate the affected panel. All failures remain in the denominator.
