# Native full-rank GenInfer comparison v1.1 results

All preregistered gates pass across 96 fresh workers: eight fixture families × three independent repetitions × two binaries × audit off/on. Source and protocol hashes match, each arm has one stable distinct binary hash, and source manifests are identical between arms. The analyzer was frozen before the capture and its hash matches the capture.

The feature-enabled engine acquires the registered 1-D reflection and all three distinct 3-D integer matrices through normal closure, mints graph-resident generators, and reuses them on held-out observations with exact recognition receipts. Across the three 3-D geometry families, median audit-off training closure is 1.46 ms with the solver versus 0.65 ms in the feature-off arm; held-out medians are 0.79 versus 0.47 ms. The 2-D baseline and solver arms match status, coverage, residual, receipts, generators, and closure work across all repetitions. Keep the feature opt-in: this is a narrow capability gain at a measurable added cost, not a general speedup.

The rank-deficient, nonlinear, and duplicate-ID controls remain unrecognized and mint no generator in either arm, in both training and held-out phases. Native full-rank counters reconcile in every audited phase. This confirms the refusal boundaries on these authored fixtures but does not establish broad false-acquisition rates.

Scope clarification: the “nonlinear” control here applies the static within-frame warp `z := z + x*y`. It tests refusal by the full-rank linear matrix inducer. It does **not** test the separate `TimeInfer` order-N trajectory extrapolator, which fits finite differences over per-pair offsets when every consecutive frame pair is a rigid translation. The latter has dedicated positive evidence in [Generic Order-N results](GENERIC_ORDER_N_RESULTS.md), including order-2 and order-3 predictions; arbitrary within-frame nonlinear map induction is a different capability question.

The next identity-alignment gate was executed at N=8/32/128. The sorted join passes exact semantic/refusal parity, but closure timing is effectively at parity in this range; see [sorted-join v1 results](GENINFER_FULL_RANK_SORTED_JOIN_SCALE_V1_RESULTS_20261006.md). The next measurement adds direct key-field comparison counters and extends through N=2,048. Natural frequency of full-rank frames, dimensions above three, rank-deficient induction, and CPU/memory cost on natural tasks remain open.
