# Phase-0 dense-map interpolation v1 results

Six source-locked workers (seeds 24–29) trained nine WUnits on a 3×3 grid of 3×3 burst patches, with 4,000 presentations per site. Each then received three frozen probes at each of the nine trained sites and sixteen untrained positions between grid centers: 450 probes total. Every seed localized all nine trained sites; all 162/162 trained probes selected a field centered within one cell of the presented site with spread below three cells.

At the 288 held-out probes, exact coordinate localization was 0/288. However, nearest-trained-site-region prediction was correct on 288/288 probes, in every seed. Winning fields had a median center-to-probe distance of 2.83 cells and median spread of 1.15 cells. The learned representation therefore supports highly reliable discrete region selection across the sampled grid gaps, while its narrow site-specific fields do not represent the intervening coordinate itself. This resolves the earlier four-site result's low nearest-region accuracy for a denser map, but does not establish continuous interpolation, robustness to other grid origins/features, or natural-vision utility.

**Validation amendment.** The raw v1 capture's `passed` flag is false because the runner mistakenly expected 225 probes/worker (`75 * REPEATS`) instead of the preregistered 75 (25 sites × 3 repeats). Raw rows contain exactly 75 probes and exactly three per site for each of six workers; every other original runner gate passes. The corrected independent v1.1 analyzer recomputes the probe matrix, every activation/winner, source-lock equality, and frozen-weight preservation; all eleven amended checks pass. The raw capture is unchanged, and this validator defect and correction are documented in the [v1.1 amendment](SUBSTRATE_VISION_EYE_PHASE0_DENSE_MAP_INTERPOLATION_V1_1_AMENDMENT_20261005.md).
