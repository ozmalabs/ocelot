# Phase-0 held-out shape response v1.2 results

The corrected source-locked run passed all capture gates on three fresh seeds. Each worker trained for 8,000 steps on alternating 3×3-square and five-cell-plus inputs at two locations, then ran 20 frozen probes. The analyzer independently reconstructed receptive-field centers/spreads and exact activity dot products from the captured weights. All 60 recomputed activation vectors match native activations within `1e-6`, all 60 recomputed argmax winners match the native current-step winner, and the learned receptive-field weights are exactly unchanged by the probes.

Seen-shape controls were location-correct in 30/30 probes: 15 square probes and 15 plus probes. The held-out five-cell diagonal X—never presented during training—was location-correct in 30/30 probes across both trained sites and all three seeds. Median winning-field spread was 1.155 cells for square probes, 0.894 for plus probes, and 1.025 for held-out X probes; median winner-center distance was effectively zero in all three groups. This supports transfer across these three simple spatial morphologies at already-trained locations. It does not establish shape identity classification, new-location transfer, or natural-image recognition.

The attempt history is retained. V1 stopped before a capture because the probe labels did not map to the training-mask names. V1.1 fixed those names but wrote the freeze flag to the runtime Eye node instead of `EyeParams`; its raw capture correctly fails the freeze and probe-count gates, and its probe winners are not treated as frozen transfer evidence. V1.2 uses `set_params`, verifies `EyeParams.frozen == 1.0`, and confirms exact weight preservation. No failed-run thresholds or outputs were overwritten.
