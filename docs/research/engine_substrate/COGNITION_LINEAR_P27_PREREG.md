## Question and fixed tasks

Measure whether persistent cognition-on state accumulated by evidence selection, native process search, or both improves exact next-state prediction over the same frozen ancestor on fresh, independently seeded simulator worlds. This is a finite simulator study; it does not test human equivalence, physical grounding, broad process synthesis, or general capability.

The two registered task families are `cyclic_relation` (7-state permutation) and `branching_relation` (9-state permutation), generated by the P26 `EvidenceSearchPool` world builder. Every scored world is independently seeded and receives the same family-specific action interface. Training and evaluation use only draws congruent to 3 modulo 6, the generator’s fixed transferable relation. Training draws are [3, 9, 15, 21, 27, 33, 39]; the three common ancestor receipts use [3, 9, 15]; scheduled training draws are [[21], [27], [33, 39]]. Each scored outcome is an externally verified simulator receipt after applying `act_0` to the fixed family initial state. The evaluator strips the expected outcome and scorer-only fields from the native query before prediction.

The held-out test panel contains 200 independently seeded worlds per family, each scored at the same g0–g3 checkpoint sequence. A separate 200-world-per-family validation panel is scored only at g3. Test and validation use the registered draw list in the freeze record and have distinct generated-world seeds. Development worlds and labels are excluded from both panels.

## Arms, schedule, and authority

- Arms: `frozen_ancestor`, `evidence_selection_only`, `process_search_only`, `combined`. Each owns an isolated cognition-on learner graph; no state is copied between arms after the common baseline.
- Checkpoints: `g0`, `g1`, `g2`, `g3`. Trigger on cumulative work `[0, 1000, 2000, 4000]` or task-access markers `[1, 2, 4]`. Capture no-change, failed-search, and pending-authorization intervals with their actual state and counters.
- Every arm uses one shared native ThinkingBudget of capacity 32.0. Search is limited to 32 attempts per lineage and 1 per task; candidate adoption is disabled.
- Maximum cumulative native-rule work: 250000. Maximum complete dynamic graph size: 2000000 serialized bytes. Report realized resource vectors per family, arm, and checkpoint; no efficiency claim is made when costs differ.
- No simulator transition, approval, or world receipt may result from candidate search. Held-out and validation labels never reach training, search, selection, checkpoint readiness, or predictor queries.

## Estimands and decision rules

Primary contrasts are combined minus frozen ancestor for paired g3 exact-success difference and the linear checkpoint slope over g0–g3 with x values `[0, 1, 2, 4]`. Secondary contrasts compare each of evidence-selection-only and process-search-only against the frozen ancestor. Bootstrap resampling is by paired world cluster with 20,000 draws, deterministic seed base `27010251`, and Holm correction across all family × arm × outcome tests.

A registered family contrast passes only if final gain ≥0.10, checkpoint slope ≥0.05, both one-sided 99% lower bounds exceed zero, and Holm-adjusted p<0.01 for each outcome. Missing predictions remain visible and score as failures in the denominator. Flat, ceiling, floor, missing, failed-search, and no-adoption outcomes are retained. Validation is descriptive and cannot rescue a failed primary.

## Integrity controls and resource reporting

The 100 frozen controls comprise 34 held-out selector-denial checks, 33 duplicate-receipt replay checks, and 33 candidate-approval-boundary checks, assigned round-robin by control index. Each must pass: held-out selector access is denied before selector views are materialized; replay creates no duplicate evidence; and the adoption policy remains disabled with zero world transitions, approvals, and adoption receipts. Report all controls and every missing/pending record.

Seed roots, concrete training/test/validation world seeds, control roots, and generator split offsets are encoded in the machine-readable allocation below. P27 root/derived seeds are checked for collision and against the prior numeric envelopes 26–34m, 41–44m, 70–132m, and 150–170m. The allocation digest and all source/runtime bindings are part of the freeze record.
