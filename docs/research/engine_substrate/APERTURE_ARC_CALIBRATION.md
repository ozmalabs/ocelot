# What this arc is, and what it is not

Written after the fact, because the individual documents each carry honest bounds and the *tone*
across them still overclaims. Words like "headline", "decisive" and "the sharpest result" appear in
my own commit messages for findings that are, psychologically, unremarkable.

## The psychology is not new, and none of it is a discovery

Everything the aperture arc reproduces is already well described:

| measured here | already known as |
|---|---|
| moving someone's aim so harm cannot be detected | coercive control, grooming, goal substitution |
| the victim selecting correctly by their current lights | why survivors report not knowing at the time |
| stress shortening the horizon | tunnelling under scarcity and threat |
| an unresolvable residual detected but not resolvable | learned helplessness, and its distinction from ignorance |
| keeping the frames under which one is never wrong | motivated reasoning |
| a shared frame neither party can lift alone | folie à deux |
| reasoning failing where regulation works | every clinical account of dysregulation |
| a detector reporting incidence, read as authorship | the frame-up; and the con whose delivery mechanism is the mark's own competent investigation |

No clinician would find any row surprising. **The contribution is not the phenomena.**

The last row has a popular statement precise enough to be worth naming: *The Punisher* (2004), whose
plot is §§7–9 in sequence. Howard Saint is not deceived at the content level and makes no error —
evidence is arranged for him to find, he investigates competently, reasons correctly, and kills his
own wife and closest friend. **The plan requires him to detect**; a Saint who noticed nothing would
be safe. That a film can carry the mechanism is the calibration point rather than a decoration on
it: the structure is common knowledge, and what the substrate adds is where in the pipeline it acts.

## What is actually being claimed

Three things, all narrower:

1. **Parsimony.** No term was added to produce any of these. They fall out of machinery built for
   other reasons — a tube, an aperture, a residual, an admissibility grammar — and in one case
   (`APERTURE_PATHOLOGIES.md` §1, motivated inattention) out of a one-line guard that reads as
   diligence. A model that needed a *manipulation* term to produce manipulation would be worth less.
2. **Distinguishability.** The arc separates things that present identically from outside: ignorant
   / stupid / stubborn as three fields rather than three degrees; two aetiologies of folie à deux
   that answer to opposite interventions; three ways to be moved, of which the horizon defends
   against one. That is diagnostic structure, not new phenomenology.
3. **Some consequences that are uncomfortable and follow whether or not one likes them** — listed
   below, because they are the part worth keeping.

## The unpleasant ones

* **Detection is not defence.** The fixed-aim agent knows it is not being served from round 2 and is
  still displaced by 7.2 at round 12. A residual buys the possibility of correction, not correction.
* **There is no error to correct.** A re-aimed agent is not mistaken. It selects correctly against
  the aim it now holds, so there is nothing in the present for it or anyone else to catch.
* **Forgetting is the vulnerability.** The only thing that recovers a moved aim is the retained prior
  aim. Discard your own history and you lose the sole instrument that detects having been re-aimed.
* **Stress does not make you gullible, it makes you manipulable.** The horizon shortens, and capture
  becomes indistinguishable from help *for correct reasons*.
* **Teaching and manipulation are one operation.** The substrate has no second primitive, which is
  the papers' claim; the aperture seed measured it before the claim was written, and could not
  furnish a distinction if asked.
* **Where the aim is controlled, nothing bounds the drain** — because the stopping condition is the
  residual, and there is no residual to have.

## What would make it more than this

Nothing here touches humans. Every fixture is hand-built, one-dimensional, deterministic, n=1, and
`arousal_window_coreg`'s standing caveat — an authored affect model, not a clinical one — is
inherited by everything downstream of it and is not discharged by any result in the arc. The
distinguishability claims are the ones that could in principle be tested against real data, and none
has been.

**Correct reading: a substrate built for manifold projection reproduces a set of known
psychological phenomena without being given a term for any of them, and separates several that are
usually conflated.** That is worth having and is not a contribution to psychology.
