# Held-out postulate receipt transfer v1 results

**Result:** the identity-corrected scale-cancellation shape transfers to a separate existing postulate through `synthesize_gaps()` identically under Direct and receipt adoption; the surface-only control does not transfer it.

## Outcome

Three fresh full-boot workers pass all eight gates. Each loads 731 seeds with 11 recorded failures and selects the unique `sift` postulate at node 21992. Its lineage includes `computer_vision` (ID 21985). The scale-cancellation donor is `batch_normalization` node 15504, with one exact owner candidate: `computer_vision`.

Under the actual `synthesize_gaps()` path with the one-shape harvested library, order 1, no graph recording, openness pruning disabled, and same-Microtheory structural matching, both Direct and receipt arms derive the same order-1 gap with capability keys `speaker normalization`, `formant ratio`, `vocal-tract-length normalization`, and `f2/f1`, via `scale_cancellation__from__batch_normalization`. Both surface-only controls return no derivation through that shape. Whole-graph canonical parity, shape parity, and complete structural-gap output parity hold in all workers; source and seed fingerprints are stable.

## Interpretation

This is a held-out transfer across distinct existing Concepts: the donor is `batch_normalization`, while the postulate is `sift`. It demonstrates the provenance correction preserves a structural transfer through the production synthesis operator and that the transfer depends on the owner lineage, not lexical overlap. It does not establish that the derived gap is useful, novel, correctly prioritized, or improves an external task; pruning was disabled to isolate derivation.

## Limits and next step

The ownership rewiring is controlled and full-corpus-authored, not a naturally occurring task trace. Eleven seed failures bound full-boot coverage. Next enable the normal selector, compare retained/pruned gap outcomes, and replay a held-out task with a blinded utility oracle. Also audit `source_node_id` and `source_mt_candidates` consumers and retain the separate public graph-order contract work.
