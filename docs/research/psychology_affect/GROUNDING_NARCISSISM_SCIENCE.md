# The thermodynamic / basin-energy model of narcissism vs the science — an honest adjudication

**Register:** skeptical reviewer, not advocate. Same discipline as the seizure
adjudication: a unification earns its keep by COMPRESSION (MDL), not by a novel
prediction — but I must still separate (a) genuinely novel, (b) re-statement in new
vocabulary, (c) wrong / unsupported. "Under-explored, therefore we can contribute" is
NOT "clever, therefore new." Synthetic-plausible ≠ empirically supported, and I flag
which is which throughout. Where I could not verify a specific finding I say so rather
than fabricate.

---

## 0. The claim, compressed

Narcissism modelled as **grounding to a non-optimal basin** in a predictive-coding /
free-energy substrate, with a basin-DEPTH × escape-ENERGY thermodynamic framing:

1. The SELF-model is the **ungrounded reference point** of the frame. Ungroundedness
   yields two things *at once*: it CAN be set high (nothing pins it) and it is UNSTABLE
   (nothing holds it) → **grandiosity and fragility are ONE property**.
2. An inflated self-model is a **high-residual / high-free-energy** configuration held
   away from where observation would put it ("ball up a hill") → needs **continuous
   external energy** (admiration / supply / control).
3. The self-model **cannot absorb negative prediction-error internally** (absorbing it =
   fixed point descends = collapse), so behaviour is **error-routing**: (a) export error
   outward (devaluation, reattribution, rage — sub-belief, not conscious lying); (b)
   import energy (supply-seeking; insatiability = the standing cost of holding the
   configuration; "injury" = supply cut → residual re-charges).
4. Grandiose vs vulnerable = **two energy strategies over one basin** (pump-in vs
   test-avoidance).
5. Change-resistance has no willpower appeal: the treatment demand (let the self take the
   error and descend) IS the collapse the structure exists to prevent.
6. It slots into a **family**: self-grounding = psychosis; symmetric = folie à deux;
   collective = cult / folie à N; asymmetric-coercive = abusive relationship. Narcissism =
   the self-grounding basin whose **stability requires other people** (supply + error-sink).

## 1. Psychodynamic / clinical (Kohut, Kernberg, the mask model)

**The package the model claims to unify was already unified here — ~50 years ago.**

- **Kohut** (self-psychology, 1971/1977; narcissistic rage, 1972): the grandiose self is
  a normal archaic structure the individual is *fixated* on after parental empathic
  failure; it is *intrinsically fragile* (it defends against fragmentation of the self);
  it depends on **selfobjects** — mirroring / idealisation — for cohesion; withdrawal of
  that supply produces **narcissistic injury → rage or emptiness/depression**. That is
  points 1–3 and 5 of the claim almost verbatim, minus the energy vocabulary. "Supply,"
  "injury," and "fragility-under-grandiosity" are Kohutian terms of art.
- **Kernberg** (object-relations, 1975): the grandiose self is a *defensive* fusion
  (ideal-self / ideal-object / real-self) erected against oral rage and envy; it protects
  against an underlying devalued self; therapy must "expose the defensive purpose of the
  grandiose self." That is point 2 (held-away-from-truth, propped up) and point 5
  (treatment = confront the defense = the feared descent).
- The two disagree on origin (Kohut: deficit/empathic failure; Kernberg:
  conflict/aggression) — a heterogeneity the single-basin picture flattens (see §5).
- **Mask model** ("grandiosity masks fragile low self-esteem"): this is exactly the
  model's point 1, and it is **empirically CONTESTED**. Kuchynka & Bosson's review "Where
  is it now?" decomposes it into three testable hypotheses:
  - *discrepant* self-esteem (high explicit / low implicit) — **weak, inconsistent
    support**; several studies fail to find covert low self-esteem under grandiose
    narcissism;
  - *unstable* self-esteem (high but labile/reactive) — **some support**;
  - *contingent* self-esteem (high but conditional on agentic success) — **best
    supported**.
  Support is notably **weaker in clinical samples**, and a rival "modest mask"
  investigation of vulnerable narcissists' *implicit* self-esteem is mixed.

**Verdict for §1:** points 1, 2, 3, 5 are **re-statements** of Kohut + Kernberg in
energy/predictive-coding vocabulary. And the load-bearing "grandiosity and fragility are
ONE property (fragility intrinsic)" rests on the mask model — whose strong form the data
do **not** cleanly support. A recent *independent, non-computational* convergence worth
noting: the phenomenological "existential fracture model" of NPD (Sun, 2026, *Frontiers in
Psychiatry*) also derives grandiosity (= "delusional exemption from mortality"), fragility
(= dependence on "external mirroring"), and error-export (grandiose-aggression vs
vulnerable-victimhood as "divergent responses to identical core anxieties") from one
rupture — reached from Heidegger/Jaspers, not free energy. Convergence from two directions
is mild evidence the *shape* is real; it is not evidence the *energy formalism* adds
anything.

## 2. Personality / social-psych (empirical): Morf–Rhodewalt, grandiose/vulnerable

**Morf & Rhodewalt (2001), the Dynamic Self-Regulatory Processing Model, is the closest
prior art and it is very close.** Their thesis: "a grandiose yet vulnerable self-concept
underlies the chronic goal of obtaining continuous external self-affirmation," maintained
by *intrapsychic* (attributional, self-serving) and *interpersonal* (self-promotion,
other-derogation) self-regulatory strategies that paradoxically **undermine the very self
they try to build**. Map to the claim:

| Claim | Morf–Rhodewalt |
|---|---|
| import energy (supply-seeking, insatiable) | "chronic goal of continuous external self-affirmation" |
| export error (devaluation, reattribution) | interpersonal self-regulation: other-derogation; intrapsychic: self-serving attribution |
| structure defeats its own repair | "self-regulatory efforts are counterproductive… undermine the self they seek" |
| grandiosity + fragility co-present | "grandiose *yet* vulnerable self-concept" |

So the **loop** (import energy + route error to protect a self-value that cannot take the
hit) is **already an explicit, cited, verbal model in mainstream personality psychology.**
The energy/thermodynamic vocabulary is new dress on this loop.

**Grandiose vs vulnerable as "two strategies over one basin":** partly consistent, partly
wrong.
- Jauk et al. (2017, *Frontiers in Psychology*, "The Higher the Score, the Darker the
  Core"): grandiose and vulnerable narcissism are **essentially uncorrelated in the normal
  range (r ≈ .02)** but **correlated at the high/pathological end (r ≈ .45)** — a
  breakpoint nonlinearity; at the top they "blend into an antagonistic core."
- Miller, Lynam, Campbell and colleagues: the two have **different nomological networks** —
  grandiose loads on extraversion/low neuroticism/boldness; vulnerable loads on
  neuroticism/introversion/negative affect.
- So "one basin, two thermostats" is a fair caricature **only at the pathological
  extreme**, where they merge; as a general account it **flattens** two traits that are
  statistically orthogonal and psychologically distinct at ordinary levels. The single-
  basin model is a model of *high/NPD-grade* narcissism, not of narcissism-the-dimension.
- **Developmental contradiction:** Brummelman et al. (2015, *PNAS*, "Origins of narcissism
  in children") — grandiosity is predicted by parental **overvaluation**, not by lack of
  warmth. Social-learning theory (Millon; Brummelman) says grandiosity is *learned
  entitlement from being over-praised*, not a defense propping a fragile self. That is a
  route to grandiosity with **no intrinsic fragility at all** — a direct challenge to
  point 1's "grandiosity ⇒ fragility, necessarily."

**Verdict for §2:** the self-regulatory loop is a **re-statement** of Morf–Rhodewalt. The
"two strategies over one basin" is **consistent only at the high end** and **contradicted**
as a general structural claim; the overvaluation route contradicts "fragility is intrinsic."

## 3. Computational psychiatry / predictive coding / FEP — *the under-developed layer*

This is where a genuine, if narrow, contribution can live — for the same reason as the
seizure finding: **there is a mechanism-rich FEP account of self-esteem, but essentially no
formal FEP account of NARCISSISM specifically.**

What already exists:

- **Self-esteem as a belief updated by social prediction error** is *established*. Will,
  Rutledge, Moutoussis & Dolan (2017, *eLife* 6:e28098) fit a computational model in which
  self-esteem fluctuates with **social prediction errors** (received minus expected social
  approval); updates track **vmPFC** activity, the PE itself ventral striatum / subgenual
  ACC. Will et al. (2020, *Translational Psychiatry*) show **low self-esteem = persistent
  negative expectations + reduced propensity to update** them from positive social PE.
  → Point 3's substrate ("self-value is a belief driven by social prediction error") is
  **not ours** — it is Will/Moutoussis, with a neural correlate.
- **Self-esteem as a precision-weighted, free-energy-grounded belief** is also *already
  written down*. "Feeling our place in the world: an active inference account of
  self-esteem" (2024, *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, niae007) casts self-esteem as a
  **sociometer** — a precision-weighted inferential belief about one's adaptivity within
  social hierarchy, grounded in variational free-energy minimisation. It mentions
  narcissism only in passing ("a violent need to defend the self due to fragility") and
  **does not develop a formal narcissism account.**
- **A high-precision prior that refuses downward prediction error and resists
  counter-evidence** is the standard **aberrant-precision account of delusion/psychosis**:
  Adams, Stephan, Brown, Frith & Friston (2013, "The computational anatomy of psychosis");
  the "delusion paradox" literature (Sterzer and colleagues; PMC10686356) — entrenched
  high-precision higher-order beliefs down-weight inconsistent prediction errors.

So the model's *mechanism* is assembled from three existing pieces: **self-esteem = social-
PE belief (Will/Moutoussis) + precision-weighting (active-inference sociometer) + high-
precision-prior-that-refuses-error (delusion literature).** The **specific composition** —
"narcissism = a *self*-value belief held at pathologically high precision that (i)
asymmetrically refuses *downward* social PE and (ii) discharges it by *active-inference
action selection* onto others (devaluation, reattribution) rather than model update" — is,
as far as I can find, **not stated as such in the FEP literature.** That composition is the
model's real computational-psychiatry contribution, and, like the seizure case, it lands in
a genuinely **under-developed** slot.

Two honest cautions:
- It is a **transfer** of an existing mechanism (aberrant precision) to a new target (the
  self-model), plus one added asymmetry (down-refuse / up-accept) and one added move
  (active error-export). Transfer + one asymmetry is a modest, not a large, novelty.
- "**Cannot** absorb error internally" is **too strong.** Narcissists are not psychotically
  delusional; grandiose narcissists demonstrably *do* update, tolerate some negative
  feedback, and sometimes respond to therapy. The defensible claim is a **high threshold /
  asymmetric low learning-rate for negative self-relevant PE**, not an absolute floor. As
  written, point 3 is a caricature; softened to "asymmetric precision," it becomes testable
  (§7).

## 4. The thermodynamic / energy-landscape framing

- **Attractor / energy-landscape models in psychiatry are real and measurable — but they
  measure a *different* quantity.** Energy-landscape analysis (Watanabe, Ezaki, Rees and
  colleagues) fits an Ising/Hopfield-style landscape to **neural state occupancy** from
  fMRI/EEG; depression appears as a **deepened, "canalised," entrenched basin** with
  reduced transitions (e.g. Frontiers in Psychiatry 2021, "Modeling Heterogeneous Brain
  Dynamics of Depression… Using Energy Landscape Analysis"; recent Nature Comms /
  Translational Psychiatry work on depression "system entrapment"). Here "energy" = negative
  log-probability of a measured brain state. That is **not** the same as the model's "free
  energy of a held-away-from-observation self-belief." Borrowing the word risks
  **equivocation** between two different energies.
- **The physics of the metaphor is loosely stated and, taken literally, mislabelled.** In a
  free-energy-minimising agent, a *stable* configuration is by construction a **low-energy
  attractor**. The narcissistic self-model is stable *for the agent* — it does not
  spontaneously collapse — so in the agent's **own generative model it is a deep basin, not
  "a ball perched on a hill."** It looks "high on a hill" only relative to an **external,
  veridical** model of the person's actual social standing. The correct reframing (and it is
  a *good* one, but must be stated right): the narcissist has warped its *own* landscape so
  the grandiose fixed point is locally low-energy; the "continuous external energy" it needs
  is **epistemic** — **active inference**: acting on the world (extracting admiration,
  controlling feedback, derogating rivals) to gather *confirming* evidence and to keep the
  self-prior's **precision** high. So "requires continuous energy input" is real, but the
  currency is **evidence-gathering and precision-maintenance, not thermodynamic work against
  a potential.** Stated as literal thermodynamics it is wrong; stated as active-inference
  epistemic cost it is correct and is the model's best single idea.
- **Is the high-free-energy claim measurable?** Only in the loose/proxy sense. You cannot
  read an agent's variational free energy off a person. You could *proxy* the maintenance
  cost with: self-esteem instability, supply-dependence (self-esteem variance conditional on
  admiration availability), and the physiological/affective cost of self-relevant threat.
  The neural energy-landscape method could in principle test whether self-relevant-threat
  states are high-cost / rarely-occupied — but no such study exists for narcissism (I did not
  find one), so this remains a **program, not a result.**

**Verdict for §4:** the energy-landscape framing is (i) a genuine, measurable idea in
psychiatry but about a **different quantity**; (ii) as applied here, a **metaphor** that is
**mislabelled if read as literal thermodynamics** and **correct if read as active-inference
epistemic cost.** Testable only via proxies; currently unmeasured for narcissism.

## 5–6. The four-part honest verdict

### (a) What is genuinely contributed
Not the individual pieces — every one is prior art (§1–§4). The candidate contributions are:

1. **The MDL move: grandiosity and fragility as ONE property derived from
   ungroundedness.** "The self is the frame's unpinned reference: unpinned ⇒ free to inflate
   (grandiosity) AND unheld ⇒ free to wobble (fragility) — the *same* property, two faces."
   Kohut asserts fragility-under-grandiosity; he does not *derive* it from a single
   structural fact. This derivation is genuinely economical and I did not find it stated this
   way. **BUT** it buys its elegance by *assuming* the contested mask (see (c)) — so it is an
   elegant compression of a premise that may be false for the grandiose pole.
2. **The composed FEP mechanism** (§3): narcissism = high-precision self-prior + *asymmetric*
   refusal of downward social PE + *active* error-export as action selection. This is the
   first-cut formalisation the literature lacks. Genuine but narrow (transfer + one asymmetry).
3. **The cross-syndrome FAMILY** (§6): psychosis (self-grounds a world-belief) / folie à
   deux (symmetric) / cult / folie à N (collective) / coercive relationship (asymmetric),
   with narcissism = the self-grounding basin **whose stability requires other people as
   supply + error-sink**, which is *why* it generates cult-leader and coercive-partner
   instances. I did not find this specific basin-topology family in the mainstream
   literature. It is the **most novel** move — and the **least supported**:
   **synthetic-plausible, untested, and at risk of being a metaphor** (see §7).

Net: a **modest but real** contribution = one crisp compression + one formalisable FEP
composition in an under-developed slot + one novel-but-speculative family. Not a large one:
the phenomenology it explains was already unified by Kohut and by Morf–Rhodewalt.

### (b) What is re-statement in new vocabulary
- "self held away from truth, needs admiration, injury on withdrawal" = **Kohut** (selfobject
  / mirroring / narcissistic injury), re-labelled with "energy/supply."
- "held-away, propped by defense, treatment = the feared descent" = **Kernberg** (grandiose
  self as defense).
- "import energy + export error, self-defeatingly, insatiably" = **Morf–Rhodewalt (2001)**
  dynamic self-regulatory loop.
- "self-value is a belief driven by social prediction error" = **Will/Moutoussis (2017/2020)**.
- "self-belief at high precision refusing error" = **aberrant-precision delusion account
  (Adams/Friston 2013; delusion-paradox literature)**, transferred to the self-model.
- "grandiose vs vulnerable = two strategies" = the standard **grandiose/vulnerable
  distinction**, re-labelled "two energy strategies."

### (c) Where it is WRONG / oversimplified / contradicted
1. **"Grandiosity and fragility are ONE property / fragility is intrinsic" is over-strong and
   partly contradicted.** The mask model is empirically contested; the discrepant-self-esteem
   (secret low implicit) hypothesis has weak/inconsistent support; grandiose narcissism is
   frequently linked to **genuinely high, stable, non-defensively-held** self-esteem, and its
   developmental origin can be **overvaluation** (Brummelman 2015), not fragility. The model
   explains a fragility that, for the grandiose pole, may not reliably be there. Its most
   elegant claim rests on its most contestable premise.
2. **The single basin flattens real heterogeneity.** Grandiose and vulnerable are orthogonal
   in the normal range (Jauk 2017, r ≈ .02) and merge only at the top (r ≈ .45), with
   different nomological networks. "Two thermostats on one basin" is right only at the
   pathological extreme. Kohut-origin vs Kernberg-origin, and vulnerable-vs-grandiose, are
   plausibly **different structures**, not one basin.
3. **"CANNOT absorb error internally" is a caricature.** Narcissists update, tolerate some
   feedback, and are not psychotic. The defensible form is **asymmetric high threshold**, not
   an absolute floor.
4. **Thermodynamic literalism is mislabelled** (§4): a *stable* self-model is a **low**-energy
   attractor in the agent's own landscape; the imported "energy" is epistemic
   (evidence/precision), i.e. active inference, not work against a potential. And "free
   energy" here ≠ the measured "energy" of energy-landscape psychiatry — an equivocation to
   avoid.
5. **The family (cult/coercion = same basin) is the least grounded** and closest to metaphor;
   it needs a differential prediction to be more than an analogy (§7).

### (d) Is it testable / falsifiable, and what distinguishes it?
Yes, in a limited but real way — because it shares parameters with Will/Moutoussis and
operationalises Morf–Rhodewalt:

- **Core operational prediction:** in a social-PE self-esteem task, grandiose narcissism =
  high self-prior + **asymmetric learning rate** (near-normal update to *positive* social PE,
  strongly attenuated update to *negative* social PE) + high self-prior precision +
  behavioural **error-export** (externalising attribution / derogation) selectively *after
  negative* feedback.
- **Falsifiers:** (i) if grandiose narcissists show **symmetric** updating / normal
  negative-PE learning rates, point 3's "refuses downward error" is wrong. (ii) If self-esteem
  variance is **independent of supply availability**, the "import energy / injury = supply
  cut" story is wrong. (iii) If cutting admiration does **not** produce a measurable
  negative-excursion/variance spike ("injury"), point 3(b) fails.
- **Distinguished from Morf–Rhodewalt:** M–R is verbal; this makes the **quantitative**
  asymmetric-precision prediction and a specific supply→self-esteem-variance coupling.
- **Distinguished from a plain FEP self-esteem model:** the added content is the
  **asymmetry** (down-refuse/up-accept) + **active** error-export as action selection + the
  **family scaling.**
- **The family claim** is falsifiable only if it makes a **differential** prediction — e.g.
  that a follower's self-esteem dynamics come to serve as the leader's **error-sink**
  (follower absorbs negative PE the leader exports; cut the follower → leader "injury"). Until
  it makes such a prediction it should be flagged **metaphor, not model.**
- **The energy-landscape claim** is testable only by **proxy** (instability +
  supply-dependence + threat-cost); the literal free-energy quantity is not readable off a
  person, and no narcissism energy-landscape study exists (unverified because absent, not
  because I failed to find it — I searched and found none).

## 7. Honest overall verdict

**Adds MODESTLY-but-genuinely, while being MOSTLY-a-restatement — with one over-strong claim
that is partly contradicted.**

- The phenomenology (grandiosity ↔ fragility ↔ insatiable supply ↔ injury ↔ change-
  resistance) was **already unified twice**: clinically by Kohut/Kernberg, empirically by
  Morf–Rhodewalt. The computational substrate (self-esteem as social-PE belief; precision-
  weighted sociometer; high-precision priors that refuse error) is **already built** by
  Will/Moutoussis, the active-inference sociometer, and the delusion literature. So most of
  the model is **re-statement in energy vocabulary.**
- Its **genuine additions** are three, in descending confidence: (1) the crisp MDL
  derivation of grandiosity-and-fragility as one property of an ungrounded reference —
  elegant but resting on the contested mask; (2) the specific FEP composition (asymmetric
  precision-defended self-prior + active error-export) filling a real, **under-developed**
  slot — the narcissism analogue of the seizure finding, where the FEP layer simply hasn't
  been written; (3) the cross-syndrome family — most novel, **least supported, currently
  metaphor.**
- Its **main error** is over-strength: "fragility is intrinsic / grandiosity and fragility
  are ONE property" and "cannot absorb error" overstate what the data support; the single
  basin flattens genuine grandiose/vulnerable heterogeneity; and the thermodynamics are
  mislabelled unless read as active-inference epistemic cost.
- It is **testable in principle** (asymmetric social-PE updating + supply→variance coupling),
  which is more than a pure metaphor offers — but those tests would largely **confirm or
  refute Morf–Rhodewalt-plus-precision**, so a positive result would not uniquely vindicate
  the *energy* framing.

**Confidence:** medium-high (~0.75) on the situating and on "mostly restatement + one
over-strong claim"; medium (~0.55) on exactly how much the FEP composition adds, because the
absence of a formal FEP-of-narcissism is an argument from *my* not finding one, and the
active-inference sociometer paper shows the field is moving toward it fast.

**The single strongest objection a real reviewer would raise:** *the model's most elegant
claim — grandiosity and fragility as one property, fragility intrinsic — is precisely the
psychodynamic mask model, whose strong form the empirical literature does not support;
grandiose narcissism is repeatedly found to travel with genuinely high, stable, non-defensive
self-esteem arising from overvaluation, so the model may be deriving, with great economy, a
fragility that for the grandiose pole isn't reliably present. Elegance bought by assuming a
contested premise is not compression — it is a restatement of the premise.*
