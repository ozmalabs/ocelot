## What was built

- **`seeds/seizure_dynamics.json`** (generated by `scripts/seizure_engine_seed.py`)
  — a DEFAULT-OFF seed with ONE combinational settlement rule,
  `sz_settle_next_residual`, expressed entirely with EXISTING DSL Terms
  (`Plus`/`Minus`/`Neg`/`Times`/`Min`/`Max`/`Sum`/`Neighbours`/`Attr`/`Lit`).
  **No new Rust Term.** It reads each area's current `residual` (+ neighbours'
  current `residual` over `projects_to`) and writes `next_residual`; the
  engine's native `fold_clock_edge` commits `next_residual → residual` at the
  clock edge (the double-buffered synchronous substep). One substep =
  one `(run_rules, fold_clock_edge)` pair.
- **Per-area settlement** (all quantities from the CURRENT buffer):
  `gen = alpha·min(max(0,R−c_gen),gen_cap)` (POSITIVE FEEDBACK, gain `alpha`);
  `acc = R + drive + gen`; `settled = tick·min(budget,acc)` (dissipation sink);
  `shed = give·tick·max(0,acc−c_gen)` (THE GIVE); `remain = max(0,acc−settled−shed)`
  (un-dissipated remainder); `push = g·tick·remain` (broadcast out);
  `received = Σ_{j~i} 0.25·push_j` (recruitment in);
  `next = clip(remain − push + received, 0, r_max)`.
  Broadcasting the **remainder** (post-settle-post-shed) is what makes the give
  gate recruitment: a high give leaves little to broadcast (dies), give→0
  broadcasts the whole overload (recruits → traveling wave).
- **`scripts/seizure_engine_lattice.py`** — the harness. A WORLD ADAPTER only:
  it sets the focal onset `drive` and the async per-area `ticking` clock each
  substep, ticks the engine, and reads `residual` back. Every dynamical term
  lives in the graph rule, evaluated in Rust. No numpy settlement.

Give sweep at `alpha=0.5, D=3, theta=1`: `give=1.0` → standing wave forms then

any give. The architecture above is frozen from this pilot and not touched
again. The confirmatory sweep runs on FRESH seeds (5–14).

## Registered predictions (committed BEFORE the confirmatory sweep)

**P1 — Overload condition is reachable / standing onset.** The synchronous
focal onset produces a STANDING wave: a rank-1 (single spatial profile × single
time course) fraction that is the dominant component over the onset window, and
onset-window front propagation ≈ 0. Substrate-independent; must hold.

**P2 — The native give is graceful.** With `give=1.0` the standing wave
DISSIPATES at ALL drive strengths `D` (final active → baseline). No seizure with
the give intact. A first-class CORRECT outcome, not a defect.

**P3 — The fold (the confirmatory threshold).** There exists `give*` such that
`give > give*` → the standing wave DIES (interictal) and `give < give*` →
PERSISTS (seizure). **Committed interval: give\* ∈ [0.50, 0.65]** (pilot centre
0.575, margin for seed variation). The confirmatory sweep on FRESH seeds 5–14
must place the median fold inside [0.50, 0.65].

**P4 — Traveling wave emerges (not scripted).** In the persisting regime, as
`give` drops below `give*`, a TRAVELING recruitment front advances monotonically
outward from the focus (front radius grows with linear r² > 0.9). Nothing scripts
propagation; it must fall out of `Sum`-over-neighbours recruitment in the engine.

**P5 — Matched control.** The interictal run and the seizure run differ ONLY in
`give` (identical `D`, `alpha`, `theta`, seed, lattice, seed manifest). If a
seizure requires changing the drive or per-run hand-tuning, that is a PLANT and
is reported as a failure.

**P6 — Variance decomposition (the anti-null).** Sweeping (`D × give`),
persistence must depend on `give` with the fold roughly INDEPENDENT of `D`
(a near-vertical boundary). If persistence instead tracks `D` with no independent
role for `give`, the reframe adds nothing over "too much input" — reported as a
NULL, not rescued.

**P7 — Good null / positive-feedback necessity (the anti-plant core).** With
`alpha=0` (positive feedback OFF) the lattice DIES at EVERY give, including
`give=0`. The seizure requires the positive-feedback term; the give alone cannot
produce it. (And `give=1` with `alpha` on cannot either — P2.) Both must hold, or
the seizure is an artefact of one dial.

**P8 — Quantitative contact (bonus, no tuning of the target).** With scale pinned,
report whether the traveling-wave speed lands near 0.48 mm/s and standing
duration near 300 ms. Ballpark contact is a bonus; a miss is reported and changes
no verdict.
