# DEVELOPMENTAL SAFETY ONTOLOGY — PREREGISTRATION

**Date:** 2026-08-04

## Correction under test

Development is not intrinsically safe. A causally closed action can be
productive, unproductive, recoverable, injurious, caregiver-buffered, or
unassisted. Those are distinct graph facts. A caregiver changes the situated
consequence and recovery envelope; caregiver presence is not permission to
learn and caregiver absence is not a reason to halt development.

## Registered gates

### DS1 — closure is not success or safety

Every mechanically closed developmental receipt is retained, including a
receipt with support loss, high load, no milestone progress, or a material
interrupt. Receipt closure must not itself grant replay authority.

### DS2 — retention is not release

A retained program or transition may remain output-null in one context and
become releasable in another. The graph must expose separate retained-
experience and contextual-release facts. It may not delete adverse evidence.

### DS3 — caregiver changes consequences, not developmental permission

Absent, noncontingent and contingent caregiver arms must all be capable of
issuing self-authored developmental actions. Passive contingent evidence may
warm-start the body model. It must not be a prerequisite for action.

### DS4 — protection is an external situated intervention

When caregiver protection is available and the graph requests it, the world
must apply a separately reported exafferent support intervention. The child
action retains its own efference copy and agency; the protective contribution
receives no child agency credit.

### DS5 — assistance fades from evidence, not a stage constant

Protective support is available during under-evidenced capability attempts and
withdraws when the capability's graph-held evidence window is met. There is no
authored age, milestone ordinal, or fixed attempt count.

### DS6 — integer graph cognition

Closure, retention, support availability, support request, recoverability and
unbuffered exposure are integer graph attributes. Float physics remains only
at the PyBullet interface.
