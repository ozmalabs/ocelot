## 6. WHAT THIS DOES **NOT** CLAIM (§8)

- **NOT that waves cannot produce orientation selectivity in principle.** It shows
  that **rung 7's decorrelation + rung 6's rule + 6400 steps at the frozen η**
  does not. A different learning rule, a longer horizon at a convergent η, or an
  anisotropic input are all untested.
- **NOT that rung 7's retina is worthless.** Its decorrelation is real and earned.
  **The finding is precisely that decorrelation is not the missing ingredient** —
  which is what rung 7 itself said in advance (*"a precondition is not a
  result"*).
- **NOT that the measure is sound.** Rung 8D found the opposite: **`osi()`'s
  argmax is a LOTTERY over exact ties** on flat curves (an aliasing degeneracy of
  the probe's discretisation). **The null survives it** because the lottery is
  noise, the floor is measured in-run, the unit is the seed, and n = 16 — but a
  tie-aware OSI remains the highest-value instrument work left.
- **NOT that the INTERACTION is null.** It is **underpowered by declaration**
  (needs n ≥ 59; not spent). It is moot here only because Δ_lateral is null:
  there is no lateral effect for it to attribute.
- **NOT that rung 6's negative is overturned.** **It DEEPENS.** Rung 6 diagnosed
  its own failure as the degenerate split carrying no independent correlation
  structure. Rung 7 removed exactly that. **The failure survived the fix**, so
  rung 6's diagnosis — though correct about its own channels — **was not the
  blocker.**
